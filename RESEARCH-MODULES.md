## Purpose

Reach a coherent, practically operable decentralized reciprocal mathematical commons. Separate difficult questions into small modules when that exposes a tractable claim or experiment. Do not require every heuristic to have an optimality proof, and do not confuse a locally correct module with a working composition.

## A module's research contract

The [placement investigation](research/modules/placement-separation/STATUS.md) has separately reviewed, unselected group/support, comparison, catalog, joint-envelope and bounded-domain sources. [COMPARISONS](research/registry/COMPARISONS.md) separates mechanism correctness from comparative cost and service. These are completed investigations, not instructions to restart them. The [research desk](research/NOW.md) identifies the current question and cycle; [WORK](research/registry/WORK.md) alone grants current write ownership. No optimization enters a comparison as free work; no resource certificate is an observed operating point.

[P's joint growth epoch](notes/checkpoints/CP-001P-joint-growth-epoch.md) supplies a separately reviewed finite operating case inside reciprocal-operability, not another network version. [SCENARIOS](research/registry/SCENARIOS.md) separates fixture/supply premises from consequences; the [handoff checklist](research/templates/review-handoff.md) requires complete held-cut reviews and coordinated repairs. Next continuation work starts from surviving day-24 state rather than resetting it.

For a substantive uncertain module, record:

1. **Question and boundary:** one decision or claim, with inputs, outputs and deliberately excluded work.
2. **Assumptions and dependencies:** exact inherited contracts, source pin, resource/availability conditions and trust boundary. Unproved assumptions stay named.
3. **Candidate mechanism:** enough detail to trace success, failure and cost. A finite refusal path alone does not establish useful operation.
4. **Claims by evidence grade:** conditional formal argument where suitable; source observation; explicit engineering hypothesis; heuristic proposal; future qualification. Do not blend them into one confidence label.
5. **Adversarial/ordinary countercases:** the smallest case that would break the claim, including honest error and practical inconvenience, not only malicious behavior.
6. **Exit criterion:** what closes this question at the present concept stage, and which stronger claims still require execution or measurement.
7. **Composition obligations:** what downstream consumers may rely on, what they must check independently, and what changes invalidate their assumptions.

This is a thinking aid, not a mandatory seven-section template for every paragraph. Reuse an existing contract by exact reference instead of copying its entire implementation story. Prefer one owner and one independent reviewer per nontrivial question; ask for targeted seam audits rather than unbounded “find every possible issue” reviews.

## Two compositional arguments to maintain

**Safety.** Let A be the existing receiver acceptance precondition and J its conditional mathematical judgment. Candidate events add inert data only; transfer events add unaccepted scoped bytes only; the unique acceptance transition still requires A. Under the inherited trusted-kernel/decoder/state assumptions, every acceptance event therefore still entails J for arbitrary broker transcripts. This does not imply identical accepted sets, successful discovery, privacy or affordable work.

**Practical reciprocal path.** For one declared local investigation, success requires a useful candidate in the consulted finite metadata scope, a reachable acquired complete selected evidence closure, compatible checking/policy/resources, an actual checked application and an actually supplied publication/custody path for any new contribution. These are compositional premises to exercise, not truths inferred from module names. If one fails, record the failed stage and its cost. Do not multiply independent-looking probabilities without justified dependence assumptions.

## Review and decision policy

- First construct a minimal successful paper trace. Then attack its most consequential assumption with a minimal counterexample.
- Close a discovered contradiction by repairing the mechanism or narrowing the unsupported claim explicitly. Do not merely attach “future work” to a contradiction and call the module operable.
- Use formal arguments for semantic/authority/integrity invariants where feasible. Use plausible heuristics and falsifiable cost/utility hypotheses where proof is inappropriate. Label the distinction.
- Evaluate alternatives against the same explicit resource and success criteria. Extra replicas, donated bandwidth or hidden correct-candidate lists are not free improvements.
- Audit changed interfaces and reverse-dependent claims. Reopen an otherwise settled module only when a changed assumption, new source fact or counterexample affects it.
- Freeze only a coherent selected checkpoint. Working proposals remain outside its integrity manifest. A frozen checkpoint records what was selected and reviewed, not infallibility.

Current implementation boundary is unchanged: Markdown specifications, read-only source inspection, arithmetic and document-integrity checks are allowed. No software implementation, executable models, native replay, artifact extraction or benchmarks begin without authorization.
