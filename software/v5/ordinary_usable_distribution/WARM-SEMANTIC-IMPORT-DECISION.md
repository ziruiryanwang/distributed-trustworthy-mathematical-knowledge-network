## Decision

**Do not create another proof-erased loader or acceptance backend.** The proposed
demand-scoped use of exact local theorem warrants is already implemented as
`grounded_cut`, including persistent root-scoped lookup, genuine recursive local
acceptance and ordinary full-proof custody. Its adopted `recursive01` outcome
checked two successive existing theorems with close/reopen and resolution, without
ancestral theorem-proof input on either cut-check path. This is a capability
result, not measured advantage or current-kit adoption.

The remaining investment question is full cost. A fixed-basis-restoring successor
has passed its minimal build and 42 native-free controls. The matched OLD / RESTORED /
NATIVE-sequential / NATIVE-batch comparison has a completed source review but no
executed result. Later priority changes did not authorize its automatic launch.
Current ordinary batching, selected smaller parents and approved Lean/Lake projects
remain the default. A new name for this architecture supplies no missing capability.

## Precise semantic relation

First correct the premise: a Lean theorem record is not universally opaque to
the native kernel. CP-001H and its independent source audit inspect the **same**
revision used here, `293d5d0c0c3f3dded4688b3ccd6a33939ac5102b`.
Native `constant_info::has_value(false)` includes theorem values; ordinary opaque
declaration values are excluded. The native converter can unfold a theorem proof
used as an `Acc` recursor major premise. Frontend theorem opacity is a different
policy. Replacing such a theorem by a signature can lose a Nat-valued computation
and make an old reflexivity proof refuse. No new runtime witness is claimed here;
the retained source/paper countercase already defeats blanket replay equivalence.

The implemented cut uses a different, useful relation. Let K be the exact earlier
warranted logical context, and H a freshly constructed scratch context:

- Start from the exact fixed foundation, including its authorized axioms and
  quotient/literal primitives. Do not inherit arbitrary owner declarations.
- For each selected prior theorem, bind its exact name, full ExportID, universe
  parameter list and complete propositional type to an earlier eligible K entry.
  Construct a safe axiom-shaped signature only in H. `requireProp` checks that
  the type has sort Prop through actual checked kernel addition; it is not a
  frontend guess. Universe instantiations and type dependencies remain exact.
- Retain concrete definitions and non-theorem opaques with full types, bodies,
  universes, safety/reducibility information and dependencies. Reconstruct complete
  inductive/constructor/recursor groups and compare exact generated inventories,
  including computation rules. Same-type substitutions are insufficient.
- Check the complete new theorem type and value in H. Exhaustive pre/post censuses
  classify every actual axiom as fixed basis or selected grounded signature; every
  other record must have its exact expected inventory. No scratch environment or
  `.olean` is returned as acceptance authority.

The interpretation J sends each temporary axiom to its exact earlier warranted
theorem in K and keeps the fixed/concrete meanings. Typing, universe substitution,
the conversion rules actually used in H, recursor/projection rules and proof
irrelevance must be valid under that interpretation. With the retained checked
proofs furnishing the assumptions, the new checked derivation gives a theorem
over K. Earlier-event ordering makes recursive use well founded. This is the
existing reviewed conditional soundness argument, not a new machine-checked
metatheorem or permission to trust a supplied signature.

It does **not** assert the converse, identical native converter success sets,
equal resource use, or unchanged frontend behavior. A cut refusal may still have
a valid ordinary proof. The actual first implementation accepts one new theorem;
concrete units precede signatures and may not depend on nonbasis theorem
signatures. New definitions/groups, interleaved concrete-to-theorem dependencies
and arbitrary semantic revisions are outside that slice. `GROWTH-AFTER-CUT`
already analyzes ordinary re-entry and prefers native-first authoring over
automatically expanding the cut checker.

## Authority, dependencies and public meaning

Initial derivation holds the real native owner lock, validates one selected
native-bootstrap context once, resolves its exact retained full units and derives
the compact summaries internally. A UnitID cannot authenticate a separately
supplied short type after its proof has been erased. The protected derivation
event, exact export binding and summary digest establish that association.

Each later event uses one committed map root for both selected membership and
whole-root new-name absence. It retains source/cut locks, current support and
withdrawal checks, exact full-group identities, actual clean worker completion,
durable commit intent and atomic summary/map/event commit. Reopen uses bounded
source-anchor metadata and indexed root lookup, not ancestral proof restoration.
Cross-branch signatures, caller-created summaries, a saved PASS or an arbitrary
module plan do not supply authority. Source withdrawal conservatively invalidates
dependent eligibility; no support change silently rehabilitates old state.

Three dependency meanings remain separate: computational/type dependencies needed
in H; exact original full-proof imports committed by portable V2 UnitIDs; and local
warrant/support dependencies of the cut event. Erasing proof-only imports from H
must not erase the latter two. The controller keeps the original **full new** proof
unit and exact imports for inert publication. A remote reader gets that evidence,
not the local cut report or its scratch axioms, and independently checks it under
its own acceptance route. Export semantics are preserved; cheap cold trust is not.

`#print axioms` also cannot be used as a shortcut authority test. In the scratch
environment it would report selected axiom-shaped theorem handles as assumptions
unless trustworthy imported metadata supplies their dependencies. Bundled
`Lean/Util/CollectAxioms.lean` follows types and full definition/theorem/opaque
bodies, or uses precomputed imported-module axiom entries. Ordinary approved
modules can therefore retain correct axiom diagnostics despite public proof
erasure. A fabricated cache entry cannot discharge a cut assumption. The actual
cut returns no scratch module for such ordinary use; full ordinary materialization
must retain/recover the real proof-and-axiom meaning. The fixed foundation's axiom
policy remains unchanged.

## Payoff boundary and stopping rule

Native snapshots already reuse checked deltas; they do not kernel-reprove all
old derivations. Their remaining explicit costs include selected lineage/binding
work, integrity reads, import and name scans. Cuts remove application theorem
proof ancestry from repeated checking only after paid summary/map construction.
They still pay fixed foundation work, selected concrete reconstruction, signature
checks, new proof checking, support checks, persistence, failures and custody.
The basis-restoring successor still loads fixed basis proof bodies.

The plausible winning region is separately timed durable admissions over a stable
small semantic vocabulary, while necessary old proof ancestry grows. Charge all
setup, metadata, failed cuts and mixed-declaration re-entry. Give ordinary routes
equal batching, caches, small eligible roots and incremental Lean support. A single
final native batch can be cheaper; a smaller parent cannot remove truly required
proof ancestry for free. Metadata-only savings may justify a simpler ordinary
lookup improvement rather than new mathematical authority.

No present source result establishes a current workload whose full-cost margin
justifies promotion. Reopen the **existing** cut comparison only for a concrete
repeated-use or memory-feasibility problem that batching/small contexts cannot
serve cheaply, and only when its result would decide adoption. Reuse prior semantic
and recursive evidence; do not repeat a proof-erasure demonstration or create a
second projection backend. Arbitrary cold ancestry remains outside this local
warrant argument, with optional certificates and explicit refusal retaining their
separate roles. The full v5 goal is not closed by this decision.
