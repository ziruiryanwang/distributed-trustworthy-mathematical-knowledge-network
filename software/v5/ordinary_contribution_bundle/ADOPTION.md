## Practical capability

After developing new concepts or consequences, use the already emitted public
contribute/complete report and evidence files to prepare an independent host's
bundle. The offline command preserves every listed unit, including supporting
definitions, validates byte/semantic/target bindings with existing inert codecs,
and generates the unchanged append format. It does not reopen an owner, reexport
the older corpus or produce another proof. Explicit full sharing and permitted
partial publication remain required.

Report paths and success flags provide no authority. Files come from the caller's
selected evidence directory and validated identifiers. Receivers still check the
math and obtain any missing exact imports. The bundle may not be dependency-complete;
older imports need separate retention when their previous provider may disappear.
Upstream authoring/context costs are unchanged. This is a usable handoff, not an
observed useful-growth speedup over expert composition of existing APIs.

## Validation

All eight focused groups passed on the extracted kit. The real concept-development
contribution retained 21 units/11 targets, including 10 support-only units; completion
retained 3 units/1 target, including 2 support units. Their 390,401 original evidence
bytes were preserved. Wrong identities/membership, malformed or missing evidence,
capacity, disclosure and output-boundary cases refused. Actual files and archive
entries were independently checked against the 268-payload selection.

The relocated public CLI function ran inside the isolated control worker; a
separate projector child and its outer exception wrapper were not exercised.
No Lean, owner, database, host-service or network campaign was repeated. Both
selected operations closed cleanly with zero stderr and no remaining runtime.
Known combined CPU was 3.671875 seconds, excluding development/review/model effort.
No rerun is required for this adoption, and no worldwide-release claim is made.
