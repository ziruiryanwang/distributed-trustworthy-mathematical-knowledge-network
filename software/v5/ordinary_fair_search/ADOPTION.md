## What is still central

The change removes a concrete first-provider starvation pattern and reduces
error-prone manual operator handoffs. It does not solve global discovery coverage,
arbitrary semantic matching, cheap universal cold verification, durable worldwide
availability, safe execution of arbitrary projects, or useful concept generation.
The current ordinary-source and receiver-checking routes retain their existing
limitations; new transport scheduling cannot silently strengthen them.

Next prioritize software consequences of the already justified concept-growth
work, with equally capable local baselines and full costs. Do not launch a larger
fairness benchmark, certificate campaign or another controlled math episode merely
because this checkpoint passes. License, independent security review and public
deployment authority remain unresolved; nothing has been published externally.
