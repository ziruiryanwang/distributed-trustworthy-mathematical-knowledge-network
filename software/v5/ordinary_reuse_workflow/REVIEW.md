## Bounds and two nonblocking wording improvements

`contribute_source` uses 32 groups, 4 MiB request payload, a separate 4 MiB
acquired-evidence total, 32 outputs, 1 MiB source, and additional candidate/admission
bounds. Each fetched unit and each request group is also capped at 1 MiB. The
recipe's general 32-group/4-MiB warning is correct but should state those per-unit
caps and distinguish the two aggregates for clearer fit diagnostics.

The phrase "selected closure" should not suggest replaying old theorem proofs:
`portable_owner_request` reconstructs singleton theorem signatures and follows
their semantic references, while definitions/generated groups may need fuller
material. It still fetches/validates complete retained selected units and checks
new outputs independently. This clarification prevents an overstrong cost claim;
it does not require a command or authority change.
