## Property and evidence

before TLS/application work. Handshake, HTTP work and incomplete cleanup all
retain their slots. There is no accepted-client task queue outside those K
records. An accepted socket consumes the finite `max_requests` attempt allowance
even when its TLS handshake fails; `requests` separately counts entered HTTP
handlers. Cleanup closes admission and cancels/closes owned work before awaiting
it, without renewing its saved deadline. These ownership and accounting rules
are the source argument; finite tests challenge their implementation.
