## What this buys for growth, and what it does not

The next loop establishes reusable concept/theory exchange, not a speedup. Charge
cold grounding, publication, discovery and failures, received dependency
checking, authoring, service and return; amortize grounding only over actual or
explicitly modeled later uses. Compare against equally capable persistent Lean
users who may directly share ordinary source libraries. Such a baseline may
already obtain this theory cheaply, so no avoided derivation cost may be invented.

After the loop, the consequential growth question is which *discovery or
composition advantage over that baseline* justifies the additional costs. A
conditional amortization argument can locate a nonempty benefit region; a
scaffolded example cannot establish causal discovery acceleration. Keep that
distinction explicit rather than collecting more successful theorem counts.
