## What follows, and what does not

The 265-group count alone establishes a necessary-condition failure for this
current explicit signature request. Rows 942, nodes 86914 and semantic edges 2571
are below their 4096/131072/32768 ceilings, but cannot cancel the group failure.
Raw native-unit bytes are exact; they are only **lower bounds on acquired portable
evidence bytes**. Thus 3638469 below 4 MiB, and 963145 below 1 MiB, do not prove those
evidence limits pass. Prepared group/header bytes are different and unmeasured.

Do not substitute 2651 for 265, declare all reuse impossible, infer a portable
manifest exists, or treat the failed producer as repaired. Local ordinary Lean
can use an approved installed Std package directly. A trusted-project route with
no explicitly acquired target does not prepare this F, but any commons-returned
new unit still needs exact grounded imports and actual independent admission.
This analysis does not establish that whole return workflow or grant a trust waiver.

The practical decision is to distinguish users needing approved package reuse
from users needing independent inert rechecking. Keep the former ordinary route
available; justify a separate intervention only for a concrete unmet latter need.
No cap increase, new foundation, module, certificate, retry or next target follows.
