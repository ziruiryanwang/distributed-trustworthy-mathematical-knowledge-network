Status: selected concept integration, with [independent integration review](../notes/audits/CP-001I-integration-cross-audit.md), [disposition](../notes/audits/CP-001I-AUDIT-DISPOSITION.md) and [CP-001I checkpoint](../notes/checkpoints/CP-001I-candidate-only-discovery.md). ## Product and authority boundary

Independent local investigators acquire, check, use and contribute formal mathematics. Lean 4 remains the initial basis. There is no required central server, universal theorem ledger, public mathematical task list, common root research goal or human-intention assessment. Political attribution is not part of acceptance. Operational authentication and finite accounting remain necessary where actual source/service authority depends on them.

This amendment selects an optional service allowing search metadata to be indexed without each indexer retaining and checking the corresponding proof closure. It does not weaken the qualified native-provider role: a provider claiming that role still satisfies the exact v5 eligibility, custody and serving contract. A candidate broker is a distinct optional service, not a renamed native provider.

There are three independently supplied resources: candidate metadata/search, complete evidence custody/service, and receiving-agent checking/application. One owner may supply several, but an allocation or successful action in one role does not discharge another role's obligations.

## What each observed event actually means

1. **Listed candidate.** A broker retained bounded descriptor bytes and one to four claimed same-R source leads at a local metadata cut. It may be wrong about hidden semantic bindings or future evidence service.
2. **Observed source read.** An actual authenticated source supplied specified exact CIDs/ranges under its own actual read scope during an observed interval. This does not establish the remainder of the closure or future service.
3. **Complete acquired evidence.** The receiver obtained the complete selected closure; checking, compatible realization, actual assumptions and current-use protection are separate further obligations.
4. **Reusable checked artifact.** The existing acceptance/current-use predicate holds under the receiver's adopted exact foundation, policy and qualified native interpretation.
5. **Checked application and new contribution.** Actual local arguments/transport/application check. A resulting new artifact is exported, checked, retained/served and made discoverable through its own funded path. Earlier listing or application does not automatically publish it.

No broker score, public popularity, signature or contact identity shortcuts these events. Human intention may shape a local investigator's choices, but enters none of these system-level mathematical acceptance tests. Heuristic ranking can be useful without proving optimality or mathematical novelty.

## Minimal direct service

An investigator uses an owner-selected known broker contact and authenticates that peer. Contact selection is optional and local; cold unfamiliar-broker discovery is not supplied by this lane. Existing direct native contacts and native routing remain available.

The service copies bounded STRUCT2 descriptor payloads. A broker need only validate visible syntax and exact claims, not fetch proof bodies, adopt the named foundation, certify hidden group shape or execute an extractor. Native-page collection separately negotiates its actual 3+7+8 reader contract; supporting cap 9 alone does not imply it.

Candidate identity is the existing typed descriptor ContentID d. Leads carry one to four distinct native source identities, bounded public endpoints and the same exact replay R visibly claimed by D. Lead updates do not alter d. There are no executable instructions, arbitrary URLs, exported source capabilities or recursive broker referrals in the row. Local LLMs must treat all metadata as data, not tool or policy authority.

Paid operations 21–23 submit one's own lead, withdraw one's own selected lead, or search an authorized immutable metadata cut. Search is a claimed-token conjunction with an optional exact target filter, not native pattern filtering. The shortest required-token posting and inclusive next-unprocessed boundary define the page order and progress. A full row that cannot fit and an interrupted test remain unprocessed.

Mutation comparison, no-op/conflict/policy decisions, row/posting updates and acknowledgment-cut selection occur in one authority-writer transaction. The selected comparison is explicitly value-CAS: absence or a canonical lead digest. ABA is allowed; withdrawal does not fence every older distinct unexecuted request. Quiescent local withdrawal requires settling one's own outstanding restoring intents. Another source's lead cannot be displaced by a self-submission.

Metadata disclosure authority is distinct from native read authority. Native acquisition always obtains the source's actual fresh public scope or independently issued holder-bound export. Expired, foreign or broker-copied capability bytes cannot authorize private reads. At the receiver, alternative proofs and realizations remain exact distinct checking cases.

## Finite lifecycle and honest capacity

The direct profile has explicit per-page row/byte/scan/CPU/time/memory limits and an aggregate owner-supplied stock/service envelope. Every live, reserved, uncertain or retiring allocation counts. Neither more keys nor a new accounting interval resets an existing obligation.

Snapshot slots include reservations before creation and retired roots until actual release. Created snapshots last at most 600 local seconds; the issuer may select less and reports the actual expiry. END retires new scan claims. MORE/PARTIAL, abandoned scans and lost first replies may retain orphan slots until original expiry. No close opcode, keepalive or auto-renewal is selected. Snapshot retirement and independently authorized immutable result delivery are different lifetimes; disclosure revocation and seal handling retain their exact NET2 fences.

The small economic case has 4000→4096 targets, eight known-contact search copies and two actual complete evidence sources. Under attained shared-core/increment sizes, six avoided discretionary native copies save 18 GiB of canonical native stock; this is not net fleet storage or a permission to delete promised custody.

Its 10000 one-page attempts/day are supplied by explicit timing as well as CPU/traffic: reservation starts spaced at least 8.64 seconds, at most 66 seconds of total slot residence including reservation, selected 60-second lifetime, last scan and reclamation, and no hidden preexisting/unrelated slot users. Thus `ceil(66/8.64)=8` slots suffice at paper grade, even if no page reaches END or reaches its caller. At 600 seconds, eight slots support only 1152/day sustained; CPU headroom cannot repair that stock-time conflict.

Per broker, the amended execution budget counts 10000 new queries (including failed new queries), zero continuations, 192 publication calls for two self leads per 96 new targets, and at most 400 further paid mutation executions, including failed/conflicting mutations and mutation retries: 10592 < 20000. CPU supply is 3192 ≤ 3600 seconds/day and traffic is approximately 10.7671 < 16 GiB/day under the stated allocations. Snapshot/root/spool memory, cleanup timing and all source/client native costs remain premises needing actual qualification. These are not measured rates or a successful-query guarantee. Larger workloads or longer useful scans require separately supplied resources and tradeoffs.

## Optional retry without research coordination

The local policy is invoked only by an already authorized investigator. It does not install a timer, scheduler, public research goal, remote job or automatic new account. Returned eligibility times are information, not authority.

An immutable completion observation is distinct from a stable retry obligation. Bucket identities omit invocation IDs, endpoint strings and broker snapshots but retain exact source/role/F/R and actual stage/data/scope as appropriate. Repeated failures of the same obligation can escalate cooldown; an unrelated good header cannot clear failed dependency bytes. A refusal under an old scope does not prohibit genuinely fresh negotiation. New actual stages recheck applicability and authority before dispatch.

TRY_ONE is a planner result containing one local one-shot ticket. The sole acquisition caller consumes it durably before dispatch; duplicate delivery cannot create a second trial. Exact settlement updates a failure count/deadline at most once. Uncertain work remains fenced and charged. Cache eviction never erases the work ledger or returns trial tokens.

The policy admits at most 32 visits, 128 lead inspections and 32 reserved trials per episode, one trial at a time, at most 8 existing service calls per tranche, additionally constrained by the parent's actual resources and horizon. Transient cooldowns cap at one hour under stable qualified time; no eventual source recovery is inferred. Earliest eligibility is the minimum over usable leads of each lead's maximum applicable deadline. Nontransient policy/resource/horizon barriers are reported separately.

Conditional round-robin fairness concerns a fixed retained set of at most four eligible leads under continuing admitted selections. It is not fairness across all mathematics, all agents or churn, and does not compel an investigator to continue. Broker-operation failures never silently populate native-source buckets merely because one NodeID runs both roles.

## Compositional claims and nonclaims

**Safety implication.** Under inherited cryptographic/decoder/reconstruction/kernel/local-state assumptions, arbitrary inert broker transcripts add no acceptance transition. Candidate events add hints; scoped acquisition adds unaccepted bytes; the existing sole acceptance transition checks the unchanged native predicate. Therefore each acceptance retains its conditional mathematical judgment. Malicious brokers can still censor, bias or waste paid work; this argument says nothing about identical outcomes or practical usefulness.

**Honest finite search.** With an honest fixed authorized snapshot, actual retained roots and sufficient admitted progress before retirement/expiry, the scan returns its matching prefix without boundary skips and an END certifies exhaustion of that local base posting above its starting boundary. There is no cryptographic proof of completeness against a dishonest broker and no global absence result.

**Conditional reciprocal path.** Complete usable evidence, compatible local foundations/realizations, a matching consulted candidate scope, actual source read authority, sufficient complete acquisition/checking/application resources and a funded publication/custody path permit a checked contribution to support another checked contribution. These are substantive premises to trace, not an assertion that an arbitrary useful theorem will be found or survive indefinitely.

**Conditional economic opportunity.** Role separation can avoid discretionary native replication when adequate source service/custody is independently retained. False leads, long-lived snapshots, cold acquisition, concentrated source load, larger proof closures or full broker-route renewal can defeat it. The million-target ungrouped routing stress calculation is a reason not to select that lane, not a hidden larger-capacity operating mode.

No global synchronized knowledge state, universal usefulness oracle, guaranteed exponential useful growth, complete mathlib interoperability, actual native memory fit or deployed throughput is established by this checkpoint. The ultimate project goal remains active.
