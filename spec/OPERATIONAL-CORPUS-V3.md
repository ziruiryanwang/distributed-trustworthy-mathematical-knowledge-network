# Operational mathematical commons v3: complete evidence and unfamiliar reuse

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: selected integrated concept/specification. Independent reviews and their bounded dispositions are recorded in the [CP-001F audit disposition](../notes/audits/CP-001F-AUDIT-DISPOSITION.md). No implementation, native execution or performance qualification is claimed.

## Product remains unchanged

Downloadable decentralized software lets independent local investigators acquire exact existing mathematics, check and use it locally, and contribute reusable new mathematics. Initial formal language is Lean 4. There is no required central server, global theorem ledger, common research goal, human-intention assessment, or unsolved-mathematics task registry.

Formal correctness is judged under explicit qualified foundations and complete selected evidence. Discovery, usefulness estimation, allocation and private agent research may use heuristics or learned models. They cannot replace a required native proof or supply an omitted premise. Authentication binds operational authority and advertisements, not mathematical truth or political attribution.

This amendment repairs concrete gaps in the v2 reciprocal loop: incomplete producer proof views, expensive or incompatible proof composition, unreachable unfamiliar providers, uninformative broad indexing, and foundation evolution. A bounded safe refusal remains required, but refusal alone is not the intended useful product.

## Three distinct successful outcomes

`LocallyReusable(r)` requires exact independent native checking, complete protected local evidence, current permitted foundation/policy, and no applicable blocking incident. This is the same v2 correctness boundary.

`PubliclyContributed(r)` additionally requires an actual authorized public index/service entry for that exact replay. It does not imply discovery elsewhere or indefinite retention.

`ReusedByAnother(r,s)` requires a separate agent's checked application/construction s using the exact supplied interface/evidence. Merely returning a provider advertisement or fetching a file does not satisfy it.

These predicates separate mathematical validity from dissemination and usefulness. A valid but unobserved theorem is still valid; popular advertisements are not proofs. The software reports its actual local scopes and observations rather than a fictitious globally instantaneous status.

## Lean foundation and HEIGHT1

The qualification candidate remains Lean `v4.34.0`, commit `293d5d0c0c3f3dded4688b3ccd6a33939ac5102b`, with matching mathlib `5ed2965256430c3649e86755f9576b54eca72435`. Historical v4.22 evidence remains archival input, not a deployment candidate. New bootstrap bytes, reserved-name inventory, adapter and binary image still require actual qualification; this document fabricates no resulting digest.

Select **HEIGHT1** for reconstructed ordinary safe transparent definitions, both in the qualified bootstrap and contributed declarations:

\[
h(d)=1+\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{h(c):c\text{ is a regular transparent constant syntactically referenced in }d\text{'s value}\}\bigr).
\]

Dependencies are resolved first. The scan follows the finite value DAG's constant occurrences without delta/beta expansion. Types alone do not add a body-reference height. Theorem, opaque, inductive and recursor constants are not reclassified as regular transparent definitions. Any native-generated regular constant referenced by a value must have the exact height supplied by its qualified reconstruction; unknown/unbound metadata yields nondecision, not an invented default. Source producer hints are never accepted as authority.

Compute heights with checked arithmetic and require representability as UInt32 before insertion. Overflow is a supported-range failure, never wrapping or truncation. Reconstruct the declaration with `.regular h(d)` and its exact original selected type/body. This changes an unfolding heuristic, not the permitted axiom set, but the reconstruction contract must still be explicit and qualified.

**Registry binding:** NativeRules keeps type/schema `0x0003/1` and the v2 payload shape. Set `ruleSchemaCode=3`, defined as Native2 with HEIGHT1, keeping feature codes `[0,1,2,3,4,5]` and the same exact permitted axiom paths/types. The changed NativeRulesID changes FoundationID. Native2 object schemas remain unchanged, but their exact FoundationID and dependent identities are new. Rule code 2 continues to mean the frozen v2 reconstruction; it is not reinterpreted in place. No old rule-2 checking certificate is relabeled rule-3.

The source-backed motivation is concrete: equal zero hints can unfold both sides of a simple chain comparison down to its base, while derived strict heights unfold only the newer definition. This gives an avoidable quadratic conversion-step family over fresh insertions, not a claim that all Lean checking becomes linear or that performance has been measured.

## Complete producer evidence

The selected producer is an owner-controlled full-data collector over exact matched build artifacts. It explicitly chooses global `OLeanLevel.private`, not merely `setExporting false`, one `import all`, or a public `ConstantInfo` view. It reads complete actual theorem and opaque bodies, including `allowOpaque := true` where the chosen accessor requires it, or direct body-bearing record inspection.

The collection boundary is a completed declaration/module snapshot, with the actual selected kinds, universe parameters, types, bodies, groups and safety flags. Preliminary async information, error recovery, missing private artifacts and projected axiom-shaped public records do not become proof evidence. An owner-controlled source rebuild or per-module completed capture may supply missing complete artifacts; this conceptual route does not authorize running it now.

The collector traverses full types and bodies, rewrites exact native references, and exports inert descriptors. Peer source, `.olean` files, plugins, compiler code, environment extensions and tactic instructions are never receiver acceptance inputs. A local producer can run such tools only under its own separate authority; their output still passes the independent native path.

Actual closure axioms are checked against the exact allowed foundation inventory. In the pinned source, native evaluation can generate fresh axiom declarations, so a blacklist of historical `reduceBool`/`reduceNat` names is insufficient. A genuinely prohibited witness is distinguished from a public projection whose real kind/body has not yet been retrieved. Safe logical definitions with compiler `extern` annotations are not rejected solely for those annotations: export their safe logical model, not the executable replacement.

The new String bootstrap is an actual logical dependency closure through `String.ofList` and its safe dependencies, not a renamed old constructor. It must be derived and qualified as such.

## Exact proof composition

Keep the v2 rule: every selected Replay has one exact ProofID per reached JudgmentID, including its proof root. Missing choices, extra choices and selected-support cycles are not allowed. A theorem installed with witness A is not an environment checked under witness B merely because their statements match.

The existing alias-expansion fallback is valid only at its actual cost. The composition note gives a family where literal substitution creates exponentially many distinct universe-instantiated nodes despite raw DAG sharing. It does not claim all equivalent proofs are large.

Select a bounded **proof-capsule producer transformation** where its exact eligibility conditions hold. It preserves source polymorphic proof sharing by replacing theorem implementation references with checked proof-valued OpaqueDefinition records and exporting checked root wrappers. Capsules contain and retain independently checked bodies; they are not axioms.

The unchanged-interface route requires no back-reference from retained semantic backgrounds/types into the transformed theorem slice, plus disjointness of the complete fixed background witness-map domain from the exported root JudgmentIDs. Source-name disjointness alone is insufficient. All generated terms, exact witnesses and actual footprints are rechecked. No capsule enters the original root statement identity under this route.

Within that guard and the **same exact FoundationID**, source-size sharing and the original JudgmentID can be retained while different proof capsules coexist. Moving from rule 2 to HEIGHT1/rule 3 or otherwise changing foundations does not preserve that ID. Proof-dependent statement types or nominal declarations can violate the guard. The producer then chooses a separately justified checked transformation/alternate witness or returns an exact compatibility/resource outcome. The design does not claim universal cheap library merging.

The minimum client uses an explicit native application environment with exact generated names and arguments. Rich ordinary Lean authoring with notation, elaborator extensions, instances and simplifiers remains a separately locally qualified producer/client convenience. Accepting native mathematics does not silently install a conventional mathlib frontend or peer code.

## ROUTE1: actual cross-community discovery

The current baseline must include a working unfamiliar-provider path; one-hop hints and a fixed 256-contact query directory alone admit a fully honest connected network that never discovers a useful remote contribution.

ROUTE1 selects iterative XOR-distance rendezvous lookup for bounded signed provider advertisements under exact foundation, judgment and STRUCT1 keys. Routing holders store operational metadata, not every advertised theorem. A client follows scoped provider leads through ordinary FIND/FIND_WINDOW and exact independent native acquisition.

The separate routing table has at most 4096 total admitted contact slots and 8 MiB aggregate metadata. It does not enlarge the mathematical PeerDirectory or grant extra native query authority. Client-only nodes need not retain advertisements or serve arbitrary routing requests; reachable routing service nodes explicitly allocate that role. Bootstrap requires a willing reachable contact, not a central root server or trusted theorem authority.

Hot keys use exact provider-hash byte-prefix cells at depths 0 through 32. A cell holds at most 4096 ads and 256 immediate child markers under node-wide limits. A deeper publication is observed complete only when its admitted leaf copies and ancestor-marker path are installed within the selected publication contract. Expiring leases, partial placement and fresh re-publication remain visible; four receipts do not prove independent failure domains or perpetual discoverability.

Lookups are initiator-driven, bounded and paid. Serving a route request cannot recursively start remote lookups, fetch an anchor proof, or execute an agent instruction. A result is closest-seen scope, not proof of a complete membership roster. Snapshot rows describe their named cut, not a newly checked theorem or synchronized global current state. Explicit lower boundaries and retained bounded traversal state prevent repeated expired snapshots from forcing every scan back to BEGIN.

### Fine-key coverage is explicit

The foundation fallback catalog is useful but insufficient for efficient targeted discovery. If all providers expose the same coarse keys, an unfamiliar exact query can require linear provider probes. Accordingly, a node selects and funds a finite exact-index scope, maintains per-target publication/renewal state, and reports incomplete coverage. The initial ordinary profile chooses up to 4096 exact judgment targets, with larger scopes up to 65536 only under their actually supplied resources. This is a local role capacity, not a cap on mathematical validity or the global corpus.

The exact-routing extension batches bounded membership changes and renews exact receiver-local provider lease groups. It cannot remove initial index construction, actual membership storage or the cost of distinct holders. For one settled scope's chosen placements alone, N keys with r selected replicas over M holders can use as many as min(M,rN) active holder/provider groups. Actual allocated state also includes partial placements, empty groups, replacements and retired-but-unreclaimed groups; it is not bounded by that chosen-placement formula and must fit the separately counted total allocation. A single global O(1) renewal is not claimed.

Group batches commit all their bounded changes or none under exact version/root checks. Renewal validates the producer's current eligibility for the entire exact member set, and extends only an existing admitted holder lease. Group closure/expiry gates current row visibility immediately, while delayed reclamation uses exact old-owner/row-generation fences and cannot delete replacement rows. Retired group records remain charged until actual reclamation; reopening at the same holder does not make those records disappear. Snapshot results retain their explicit historical-cut meaning.

Full exact coverage is claimed only for the named finite scope and actual observed publication condition defined by that note. Outside it, valid public mathematics can be pending, selectively structurally routed, or found through ordinary provider collections without a uniform targeted-discovery promise. No advertisement says the entire network has complete knowledge. Structural relevance remains an explicitly evaluated retrieval heuristic.

## Foundation evolution: preserve bytes, establish new evidence

Use parallel explicitly adopted foundations and incremental local selected-root migration. There is no mandatory global latest-version event or universal whole-corpus rewrite. An affected suspended basis nevertheless cannot continue supplying trusted current reliance to save migration cost.

MIGRATE1 is a locally admitted finite operation: obtain exact source evidence, perform an explicitly mapped syntactic re-expression, independently check the complete target closure under an already qualified/adopted target, and carry out authorized publication/custody effects. It is not a remote opcode or a software installer. Source may be untrusted archival input; its old acceptance does not transfer.

The initial correspondence grade is **REEXPRESSED_AND_RECHECKED**, not semantic equivalence. Successfully checking a translated theorem does not prove that an old primitive or definition has the same meaning. Equal names or equal function types do not justify seed mappings. Stronger preservation needs a separately specified checked interpretation/bridge.

Translation cache keys bind exact source objects/witness context, source and target profiles, policy/tool identity, and the exact transformation context—including capsule/background boundaries and target reference/witness choices. Cache hits return candidate syntax, not current target acceptance. No output's own unknown ID is needed to define its input context.

Migration admits temporary coexistence of source and target evidence. Target checking, public publication and new custody admission are distinct transitions. Source custody is not silently released; same-judgment recovery across changed FoundationIDs is not assumed. Crashes, failed publication and partial scopes have explicit outcomes.

Software distribution/installation remains separate local authority. Peer mathematics cannot install a checker, expand axioms or clear a security breaker. Multiple mirrors or signers are operational evidence, not a theorem of source soundness.

## Representative end-to-end executions

### A newly shared theorem reaches an unfamiliar agent

1. A privately develops a proof in its owner-controlled Lean workspace. The full-data collector obtains actual bodies and maps the selected finite closure to the active HEIGHT1 foundation.
2. A independently checks the Native2 result, retains complete evidence and separately admits public service/indexing.
3. A's exact/structural publication policy funds scoped provider placements. Status remains partial while required routed memberships or ancestor links are missing.
4. B, initially without A in its mathematical directory, makes an exact existing-content or structural lookup through its independently bootstrapped routing contacts. Returned candidates remain untrusted leads.
5. B funds provider FIND and exact acquisition. It derives the actual selected closure, checks it, records permitted assumptions, and establishes current protected local reuse.
6. B constructs an actual application or extension. Any capsule transformation obeys its full eligibility/map guard; unchanged evidence is reused only under compatible receiver-owned certificates.
7. B independently checks and optionally contributes its new result. Neither A's identity nor B's private motivation affects native acceptance.

Successful completion is conditional on the named evidence being supported, budgets and service being adequate, relevant routes/leases surviving, and actual checks succeeding. This trace is not an executed demonstration or an unconditional promise under arbitrary floods/churn.

### Different valid proof choices meet

Two exact roots use different permitted proofs of the same statement. A compatible map union succeeds only if choices agree. Otherwise the local client selects a fresh explicitly justified transformation or eligible capsule route, rechecks new declarations and derives the actual footprint. A fixed background containing a conflicting root JudgmentID fails the guard. No old certificate is transferred by proof irrelevance alone.

### A checker repair arrives while old evidence remains

Local incident policy suspends affected current reliance while preservation can continue. A qualified replacement basis is adopted separately. The owner selects finite migration roots, reserves temporary storage and checks translated target evidence. Some roots may succeed while others lack a seed correspondence or evidence. Only successful target objects are reusable; old source obligations and target publication state remain separately visible.

## What makes this an operability specification

The eventual downloadable deliverable comprises the node's local authority/store/checker adapter, full-evidence producer tooling, explicit native client workspace, ordinary public indexes, bounded peer/offline transfer, unfamiliar-provider routing, exact-index maintenance, and optional owned custody. No blockchain consensus, remote-proof SNARK or centralized online-trained model is required by the selected baseline. Such techniques can be reconsidered against actual bottlenecks, with separate correctness contracts.

The present deliverable is the selected layered specification, pinned source evidence, counterexamples, repaired audit findings, resource/state contracts and checkpoint trace. It is not application code.

The high practical bar remains: realistic complete-evidence export, useful checked application, affordable independent branch joins, unfamiliar-provider discovery, observable fine-key coverage, and durable fault behavior under declared resources. Broad mathlib compatibility, practical route/capsule coverage, actual checker images/bootstrap, and measured costs are not established by this paper design. A successful request or an explicit error for every case is not by itself proof of reciprocal mathematical growth.

No guarantee of exponential novelty, universal complete search or survival for all future time is claimed. These limits coexist with concrete selected mechanisms and falsifiable future qualification obligations. The project goal remains active until its actual agreed operability bar is met.
