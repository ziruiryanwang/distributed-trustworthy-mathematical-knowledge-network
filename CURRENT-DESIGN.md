# Current design: decentralized Lean mathematical commons

Updated 2026-10-28. This is the current architecture and navigation page.
Historical decisions remain in Git history, research/NOW.md and the linked
checkpoints; they are not alternative current installations.

## Product and scope

The current software is an ordinary Lean 4 library commons. Independent people
and agents choose their own investigations, obtain useful work from others,
check selected contributions locally, develop concepts and consequences, and
return reusable mathematics. No global research agenda, human-intention field,
central mathematical authority or total blockchain ledger is required.

The current [local distribution](software/v5/ordinary_usable_distribution/ADOPTION.md)
provides one adopted extraction and archive.

The guide, matching operator, offline preview and direct build closure accompany
that unchanged package. Use its current root guide, not historical overlay

This is progress toward the full v5 goal, not a declaration of worldwide readiness.

## Mathematical acceptance and reuse

Portable inert evidence binds exact definitions, declarations, dependencies,
foundation and interpretation. Independent recipient checking admits a selected
extension only against its actually warranted compatible imports. Names alone,
provider signatures, popularity, successful search and saved report flags do not
establish mathematical truth. Multiple independently developed contexts coexist;
merging requires the actual compatibility/checking rules.

Ordinary approved Lean projects supply rich authoring. Receipt of inert evidence
avoids executing producer source. Executable local projects require the existing
explicit local approval. Resource limits do not constitute a hostile-code sandbox.

Persistent checked contexts and compatible installed libraries amortize repeated
verification. The default cold route can require a bounded dependency closure;
it does not promise ancestry-independent verification for every arbitrary new
result. Certificates are optional supported research paths, not a mandatory cost
on every contribution. The [architecture revision](research/cycles/v5-2026-10-22-architecture-reaudit/ARCHITECTURE-REAUDIT.md)
explains that practical choice and its assurance boundary.

## Discovery and interpretation

Exact IDs locate objects. Formal symbol/features queries locate candidate
mathematics through selected replaceable providers. Selected posting-list scans
and indexed probes can avoid unrelated proof data; selectivity and routing
coverage remain premises, and broad queries can still have corpus-sized work.
A finite query reports its coverage/budgets, not universal absence or relevance.
See the [source-based discovery audit](software/v5/ordinary_dedicated_host/QUERY-PAYOFF-AUDIT.md).

Before acquisition, the [offline formal viewer](software/v5/ordinary_preview_view/ADOPTION.md)
shows a candidate type with exact symbol/universe/binder identities. It either
renders the complete type and legends within fixed limits or reports an
incomplete display with no partial theorem. This is advisory syntax, not proof
acceptance. Approved ordinary source is often the best reading route when present.

## Concept and theory growth

The growth-bearing unit can be an abstraction, model, countermodel, bridge or
parametric method, carried by ordinary Lean declarations. Investigators choose
and revise concepts; the network checks exact submitted work and makes selected
results reusable. A count of new declarations is not a count of valuable ideas.

The [concept-capability argument](research/cycles/v5-2026-10-24-growth-selection/CONCEPT-CAPABILITY-THEOREM.md)
justifies safe removal of exactly refuted local conjectures and states when
prefix savings exceed all added discovery/adoption/service costs. The baseline
has equally capable ordinary libraries, tools and caches. This is a conditional
resource argument, not a guarantee of invention or exponential research growth.

Actual [independent recipient continuation](software/v5/ordinary_finite_map_refinement/FRESH-RECIPIENT-REVIEW.md)
and [observation theory return](software/v5/ordinary_observation_growth/OUTCOME.md)
exercise checked concepts, further consequences and return through the software.
They establish operational mathematical continuation, not causal acceleration
or literature novelty. No forced synthetic search episode is required to keep
adding successful-looking results.

## Custody and operation

Dedicated inert hosts store evidence/indexes without a mathematical owner.
Recipients independently check acquired work. Existing finite service leases,
explicit budgets and host generations expose real resource commitments; they
are not promises of perpetual availability. Replication on one PC is not
independent failure tolerance. Exact object replies retain complete-object and
aggregate-budget bounds even when a client maximum exceeds remaining capacity.

[Current host capability](software/v5/ordinary_dedicated_host/ADOPTION.md) and
[reply accounting](software/v5/ordinary_reply_capacity/CONTRACT.md) record the
implemented boundaries. The [sustained-use audit](software/v5/ordinary_usable_distribution/CONTINUOUS-USE-DECISION.md)
supports existing batched owner updates, incremental publication to the retained
index (`create:false`), and finite service cycles. Dedicated hosts serve stable
selected generations; they are not evolving unbounded aggregators. Normal host
restart checks retained metadata, not every proof. A new updater is deferred:
partial updates across its separate databases need a real recovery design.
No previous service allowance is automatically renewed.

## Completion criteria still open

The final destination remains a usable deployable decentralized commons with
scalable reliable access and justifiable useful growth. The current local
checks cannot supply independent operators, security review, sustained public
custody, broad platform support, release authorization, license or maintenance
ownership. These remain separate from the soundness of an individual accepted
mathematical extension. Follow [research/NOW.md](research/NOW.md) for selected
work, [GOAL.md](GOAL.md) for requirements and [WORK.md](research/registry/WORK.md)
for nonduplicative agent ownership.
