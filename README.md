## Historical initial framing

The remainder preserves the early CP-001A/v0 framing and its links. It is historical context, not the current selected protocol or a claim that its proposed deployment/testbed exists. In particular, the older fixed-cell testbed and coordination/evidence machinery below are not all prerequisites of the current minimum mathematical commons.

This repository starts the concept and specification phase for **DTMKN**: a global, decentralized mathematical research network in which diverse human and AI agents may investigate independently, publish typed artifacts, discover and reuse earlier work, or voluntarily coordinate in shared research programs.

The P0 outcome is **useful mathematical results produced or reused efficiently and accepted reliably** under heterogeneous interests and capabilities, honest mistakes, bounded malicious admitted load, churn, overload, and partitions. Coordination is an important enabling mechanism, but it is secondary: a routing or scheduling protocol is valuable only if it improves end-to-end mathematical yield, reliability, or cost. DTMKN has no protocol-level root goal and no global utility function.

> Research may be autonomous and fallible; mathematical acceptance must be context-bound, evidence-bearing, and locally checkable; every receiver controls its own scarce resources.

The default loop is autonomous. An agent creates a local work item or investigates freely, works with its own tools, and may publish a bounded untrusted `PublicationHint` for a typed content-addressed artifact. Other cells discover hints through exact keys, structural indexes, and overlapping interest subscriptions. A receiver fetches, checks, indexes, retains, or replicates a candidate only after its own policy creates a grant, atomically allocates an exact root/child holder-bound ticket, reserves a per-candidate budget, and admits exactly one metered execution claim. Permission capabilities may authorize work but carry no work vector; a finite control-record allowance bounds their bookkeeping. Per-component authoritative validation spend, conservative gap charges, durable local retention, released balance, and optional promotion/outbox intents are committed at one local authority boundary; actual external effects require separate funded executions. Finalized grants/tickets cannot be resurrected, exact child spend rolls into ancestors, crash-safe writer-time epochs prevent reboot extension, and nonreclaimable issue allowances bound zero-work creation. Proofs, counterexamples, partial results, and useful failures remain candidates until local sandboxes check exact contracts. A receiver relies only through its own `RelianceQuery` and a finite signed evidence tree; no producer work item is required. Every support-relevant state change, dirty-seed set, and next epoch publish at one linearization point or remain fail-closed in progress, so newly checked opposing evidence cannot leave a stale acceptance labeled current.

A valid proof has no automatic right to another receiver's validation, storage, indexing, replication, priority, or attention. Independently operated cells always re-admit imported candidates; matching policy commitments never create trust by synchronization. Optional `CoordinationScope` objects may share goals, budgets, reviewers, routing hints, or stopping rules, but they are overlays rather than prerequisites and need not contain a distinguished root goal.

In this project:

- **global** means protocol-level interoperability, not one database, ontology, total order, canonical view, goal, or utility;
- **decentralized** means that research choice, discovery, scheduling, storage, integration, and checking do not require one global coordinator;
- **knowledge** includes reusable claims, definitions, proofs, counterexamples, partial results, failed methods, abstractions, dependencies, and unresolved obligations—not a Boolean truth database;
- **autonomous** means that a valid execution may contain zero shared programs: each participant can choose local goals and learn from artifacts produced earlier for unrelated purposes;
- **AI-assisted** means AI is an untrusted producer and consumer of objects and receives no special epistemic privilege.

## Historical first-artifact proposal

The first deliverable is **DTMKN Reliable Mathematical Research and Reuse Testbed v0**. It has a Lean-first local research/validation core, a typed reusable knowledge layer, and separable passive-federation and active-coordination layers. The primary deployment uses exactly five authenticated independent cells, atomic exact-root/attenuated-child issuance and spend roll-up, `RESERVED -> RUNNING(executionID, principal, meterID) -> FINALIZED` per-candidate execution, per-component accounting, local settlement/outbox boundaries, crash-safe time, irreversible terminal ledgers, nonreclaimable descendant-issue/control-record allowances, permission-only work capabilities, grounded signed AND/OR evidence with query-relative path-preserving reliance, atomic reliance-state/dirty-seed/epoch publication, interest-scoped discovery, receiver-owned service classes with protected validation capacity, 8–16 real or scripted workers, local Lean replay, and an event simulator through \(10^5\) logical agents with \(10^6\) as a stretch run. Queueing, leases, work stealing, and remote orchestration remain measured mechanisms rather than project outcomes.

The mandatory autonomous profile contains **zero `CoordinationScope` objects**. Independently released local task streams generate artifacts; later tasks at other cells must discover, re-admit, validate, and reuse them through overlapping subscriptions. A secondary coordinated profile may instantiate shared programs. Frozen target sets and sealed held-out tasks are preregistered experimental instruments only; they are not visible protocol control objects and do not define network utility.

Evaluation reports base task-solving capability, invalid-acceptance and semantic-mismatch behavior, local reuse, passive cross-cell reuse, active coordination, optional-program effects, and decentralization overhead separately. It uses task deadlines, sealed future held-out inputs, complete seed replicates containing every profile needed by each estimand, within-seed paired contrasts for multi-arm claims, one-profile statistics for isolated and queue gates, whole independent workload instances for population inference, equal arm-wide resource ceilings, and explicit yield-superiority, resource-Pareto/noninferiority, and reliability/resilience claim types. A negative coordination result is informative and does not invalidate the local research/validation artifact.

Operational identity is P0 wherever cell membership, capabilities, semantic-review authority, revocation, or failure-domain measurement needs it. Real-world authorship, credit, priority, prestige, and ownership remain secondary. Native Lean certificates are P0. SNARKs, public blockchains, permissionless membership, a public DHT, global ANN discovery, learned scheduling, token incentives, and global governance are deferred until a measured requirement and explicit security model justify them.
