## P0 acceptance state

A receiver may stably rely on a signed judgment only through an exact finite signed tree satisfying its `RelianceQuery`; a producer work item is optional. \(U_P^{\mathrm{strict}}\) is defined normatively in [Cooperation Core section 5](../spec/COOPERATION-CORE-v0.md):

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Stable}_P(q;A,O)\iff\exists T:
{}&\operatorname{root}(T)=\operatorname{requiredSignedJudgment}(q)\\
&\land\operatorname{FiniteActiveTree}_P(T)
\land\operatorname{nodes}(T)\subseteq U_P^{\mathrm{strict}}\\
&\land\operatorname{TypedCIDsAndClosuresValid}_P(T)\\
&\land\operatorname{CheckContract}_P(\operatorname{Contract}(q),T)=\texttt{ACCEPT}\\
&\land\operatorname{ExactContext}(q,T)
\land\operatorname{ClosureAvailable}(T,O).
\end{aligned}
\]

If \(q\) names semantic intent, its exact contract also requires the named fidelity evidence; a formal proof closes only the formal child. A work item compiles to such a query when applicable. Scheduler score, agent count, popularity, political attribution, foreign admission, and publication history are absent from this predicate. Operational credentials may authorize bounded roles, while grants/tickets authorize resources; neither replaces the evidence checks.
