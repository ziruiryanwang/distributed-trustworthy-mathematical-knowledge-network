# CP-001D working goal

Date: 2026-09-19

Develop and adversarially audit a deeply thought-through, coherent, and practically operable design for our decentralized mathematical knowledge commons—not merely a collection of partial ideas. Support trustworthy contributions and effective accumulation and reuse of mathematics, using formal methods, engineering judgment, heuristics, LLMs, or other suitable techniques as appropriate. We may tentatively make it Lean4-based while shifting towards better or hybrid options when better options emerge. Require harsh and convincing justification and critical examination, not formal proof that every component is good. But you still should do formal proofs when possible. Keep rigorous Markdown notes and remain at the concept/specification stage until implementation is explicitly requested. Partial result doesn't count. The ultimate goal is still that humankinds can use this to gather their mathematics/formal knowledge forever autonomously with agents operating locally. Do not return as long as there is anything uncertain or there is any potential loophole. We need an fully operable and fully justified system.

## Interpretation of the bar

Formal evidence must establish what an admitted mathematical artifact claims under its exact assumptions. An optimization need not prove universal usefulness: a defensible heuristic can be part of the design when its role, limitations, and failure containment are clear. For example, local observed reuse can guide indexing and retention without becoming a proof of mathematical validity or a guarantee of future importance.

The goal is an actionable, critically examined design—not theoretical perfection, fully specified implementation minutiae, or a promise of exponential growth. Partial architecture sketches and lists of desirable properties are insufficient.

This is the user's latest edited goal, with trailing encoded whitespace omitted. It supersedes earlier goal wording. Concrete subsystem drafts remain design proposals to evaluate against this goal, not mandatory commitments merely because they were drafted.

Established scope remains unchanged: independent investigators operate outside the corpus; human intention and registries of unsolved mathematical tasks are not system components.

The latest direct foundation choice is Lean 4. CP-001D therefore specifies a concrete Lean 4 native profile; alternative or hybrid foundations remain possible through explicitly versioned later profiles, not implicit substitution during checking.

The user has strengthened the adversarial-review bar. Review must pursue concrete loopholes and formal arguments, not declare perfection from lack of findings. No design review can certify absence of all unknown flaws in every implementation or environment. Such limitations must remain explicit; a hypothetical total-certainty claim is not evidence of completion.

Indefinite autonomous accumulation is the architectural objective: no mandatory central service, imposed finite research campaign, or centrally assigned investigation is required. Continued availability still depends on supplied resources, surviving complete copies, and maintained checking software. Finite machines cannot guarantee retaining every possible generated artifact forever.
