2. The finite selector completes its prescribed scan and atomically claims at most one currently feasible nomination. Age and marginal-custody-cost rounds alternate. Exact already-known custody costs plus an **8-MiB reservation** supply the pre-extraction ranking/fit inputs; no free exact index-size prediction is assumed. 3. Actual E EXACT_REPLAY custody is activated before preparing the public index. One paid STRUCT2 extraction must fit its reserved unit. A guarded single-writer transaction activates full-closure public permission and complete postings together. Preparation failure can leave custody-only state, not public success.
4. Native exact/STRUCT2 search and native reads/NET2 exports provide a finite source opportunity. Receiver acquisition, checking and application are separately admitted and paid. An index hit or committed source publication is not a successful independent use.
5. New-service eligibility ends at the actual horizon or earlier guard/resource failure. After lawful drain, the delegated owner explicitly issues E's versioned release for this singleton. Other protections survive. Failed or uncertain release remains protected and counted; physical reclamation is separate.

The illustrative envelope has 32 candidate rows, four rounds/starts per funded interval, one preparation in flight, and four total unsettled lifecycle slots. Its 16-GiB custody union includes the foundation; 256-MiB metadata, 2-GiB staging, work, traffic, storage I/O and actual whole-node stock are separate simultaneous constraints. ## Shared discovery and withdrawal are explicit

FIND is provider-wide, not root-addressed. Before offering a fresh query, the local policy selects a fitting fixed sponsor among at most four active exposures and reserves the whole operation, snapshot, reply and settlement. Empty results cost resources too. The paid existing public-cut/authority summary binds every currently active EGRESS dependency conservatively, even nonmatches; an unavailable summary yields a nondecision, not a free corpus scan.

Let H be the minimum horizon among those dependencies. The sponsor is fixed independently of returned rows; new calls/claims require time below H and valid exact guards. A continuation keeps its old snapshot, payer and dependencies and requires a fresh paid call. It cannot migrate to another payer, filter old rows or silently rebase. A new paid query may use a new current cut. Physical shared allocations are charged once; dependent ownership/control obligations remain counted.

Thus A ending at 10 cuts off an old mixed A/B snapshot even if B ends at 100 and pays for it. This deliberately conservative rule can interrupt a query seeking only B. It closes an authority/accounting ambiguity, not a recall or availability optimization. Independently funded public grants are not thereby revoked.

Ordinary cutoff blocks new execution and unclaimed delivery, including fresh calls on old data leases. Already claimed work may finish only under its surviving authority. Actual disclosure withdrawal additionally invalidates affected scope versions and dependent permissions, cancels unclaimed disclosure and requests bounded cancellation of claimed sends. Already disclosed bytes cannot be recalled. H+600 seconds is an upper bound on otherwise valid authority, never a new permission or a guarantee that recovery records disappear then.

## Conditional invariants and their limits

At the stated exact transactions and under inherited checker, storage and local-authority assumptions:

- Public activation implies checked exact evidence, full-closure disclosure authorization, actual custody and committed complete postings. It does not imply remote acquisition or future service.
- A policy-funded service claim requires its prebound payer, resources, exact current scope/guards and applicable deadline. A result row cannot retroactively choose its payer or extend authority.
- Closing this public grant does not remove another owner's protection. Releasing this custody reference requires the separate authorized E transition; release is not physical deletion.
- A nomination's claim is once-only. Failure, eviction, restart and interval rollover cannot remint the spent authority or discard outstanding obligations.

These are conditional paper arguments from the selected transitions, not machine-checked proofs of a new implementation. They preserve the inherited native acceptance boundary; heuristic selection quality is not an additional truth premise.
