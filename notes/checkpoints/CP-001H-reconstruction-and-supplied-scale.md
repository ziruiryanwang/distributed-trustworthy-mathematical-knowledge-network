# CP-001H: reliable reconstruction and supplied scale

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: integrated concept checkpoint with independent paper/source audits and an [integrity manifest](CP-001H-SHA256-MANIFEST.md). Not a software release, executed interoperability result or completion of the project goal. Frozen G and earlier files remain unchanged.

## Purpose and entry point

The reciprocal loop requires more than valid isolated proofs: an independent investigator must be able to reconstruct useful dependencies, apply them, contribute new evidence and make those contributions discoverable with actually supplied resources.

Start with [Operational corpus v5](../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V5.md), its four detailed mechanisms and the [audit disposition](../audits/CP-001H-AUDIT-DISPOSITION.md). The mutable [current-design pointer](../../CURRENT-DESIGN.md) identifies the latest integrated checkpoint. V5 preserves the unchanged v4/v3/v2 contracts, explicitly refining only reconstruction/bootstrap and owner-local feature coverage/resource planning.

Lean 4 remains the initial basis. There is no mandatory central server, global theorem ledger, common research agenda, human-intention assessment or public unsolved-task registry. Local agents choose their own investigations. Heuristics may guide selection and search; native mathematical acceptance requires actual complete checked evidence under an exact adopted foundation.

No implementation, executable model, Lean replay, build, artifact extraction, exploit or benchmark was performed. Evidence here is source inspection, paper derivation, arithmetic, document-integrity checks and independent review. Those evidence grades are not interchangeable.

All four mechanisms are concept/specification choices with scoped conditional claims. They introduce no new mathematical axiom, theorem-authority vote, peer execution privilege or wire opcode.

## A concrete computation obstruction and repair

The pinned native checker unfolds theorem bodies but does not ordinarily unfold genuine opaque declarations. An accessibility proof can serve as the major premise of a Nat-valued recursion. If a transparent source proof is replaced by an opaque-backed representative of the same proposition, the original fixed reflexivity proof can lose its required native conversion route.

This does not falsify the proposition or proof irrelevance. It demonstrates that a structurally acyclic representation plan is not an operational preservation theorem for every original body. A bounded candidate pool can also contain incompatible source-occurrence preferences.

One checked repair chooses a computation-compatible representative and reconstructs/rechecks the affected environment. Another keeps the chosen representative p, retains an alternative q, explicitly checks `p=q` by proof irrelevance and applies ordinary congruence to obtain the needed data equality. That yields a new proof instance at the original statement, retaining unchanged semantic descriptors where applicable. Its extra support and policy requirements remain real. The general procedure is not claimed complete.

Independent source review confirms the relevant native/frontend control paths and the paper construction. The example has not been compiled or replayed, and no actual broad mathlib failure is reported.

## A concrete scaling obligation

The supplied fixture has 1,048,576 selected targets, two qualified placements per target, 1,024 index/custody slots, 20,000 routing holders and 100,000 investigator clients. Sixteen features per target are stipulated, eight shared and eight target-specific. These incidence and cost premises must be realized or rejected in later qualification; they are not inferred for arbitrary mathematics.

Initial selected stock is 33,554,432 postings, 16,785,408 provider/token ads and 67,141,632 chosen four-copy route rows. A stipulated 2-GiB common core per slot plus at most 256 KiB incremental evidence per target gives up to 2.5 TiB retained native evidence, before working sets and metadata. Physical shared storage is charged once only where actually shared; independent copies still occupy disks.

If all 100,000 clients need that core, cold onboarding alone moves about 195.31 TiB. At the example's total slot supply of 8 TiB/day, this is at least about 24.4 days even if every byte is dedicated to onboarding. Similarly, 2,048 target checks exhaust a slot's 28,800-CPU-second day at a mean 14.0625 seconds per target before any core checking, maintenance or queries. These calculations expose conditions, not throughput results.

The coverage repair keeps provider-level token deduplication while preserving exact row ownership. A token shared by many local targets needs one provider advertisement per selected cell/holder, not one ad per theorem. Consolidation addresses renewal multiplication, but its amortized work is not a daily deadline guarantee. Actual peak load, churn, native closure compatibility, informative features and service willingness remain necessary.

## Review and continuation

Independent audits cover export/group recovery, proof computation, foundation/bootstrap and resource/coverage composition, followed by integration review. The disposition records revisions, findings, repairs and evidence limits. The checkpoint manifest binds the final selected bytes; document hashes are not mathematical or deployment assurance.

Substantive unresolved work is named, not declared solved: actual complete String/Syntax/numeric seed closure and successful staging, generated-inventory reconstruction over real library slices, practical frontend use, the frequency/cost of proof-computation repairs, useful structural recall, cold/warm native memory/checking, and actual sustainable custody/index service. Some require implementation or artifact-extraction authorization; source-level work can still improve selected mechanisms before then.

One architectural question deserves the next concept audit: can an explicitly untrusted search broker improve index economics without retaining/checking every advertised mathematical closure, while preserving precise acquisition paths and independent client acceptance? That would be a distinct service contract, not a silent weakening of current public-native eligibility.

The goal remains active. This checkpoint advances an operable design but does not claim a perfect system, guaranteed exponential usefulness, universal finite search or preservation forever without continuing resources.
