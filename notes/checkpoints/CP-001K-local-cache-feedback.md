## The reciprocal feedback actually supplied

The new arrow is **new receiver-checked applications → bounded local observations → discretionary retention decisions**. It is not application → mathematical value, application → publication, or application → permanent preservation.

One row represents one exact JudgmentID and its current indivisible P4 bundle, at most four exact proof/replay alternatives. Only once-settled local checked applications earn credit; repeated reports, imports, remote claims and downloads do not. One application credits at most four distinct enrolled judgments. Three daily bins saturate at three events each; `u = 4*c0 + 2*c1 + c2` is between zero and 21. Durable serial, overflow and enrollment fences prevent delivery or row churn from replaying old credits. Genuine cheap repeated uses can saturate this signal and remain a defeating case.

One low-history exploration slot and three reuse slots select at most four families per admitted round. Every slot requires whole-bundle fit; marginal dependency cost is recomputed after each proposed winner. Reuse slots can choose score-zero families too. Zero history receives a one-day probation term; positive recorded history receives seven days. These are engineering choices, not measured best constants.

There are at most 64 rows, 64 pending observations, 16 active/reserved/unsettled family-term allocations and one round in flight. An authorized daily interval permits at most 64 nomination attempts and four rounds. Per-round CPU, time, workspace, fit-evaluation and aggregate inspection limits bind the same admitted work; smaller parent limits still prevail. Observer, control, finalization, reconciliation and physical stock are not free.

First-term eligibility requires an explicit paid one-shot nomination token. A retained row, expired handle, metadata update or use event cannot arm it. CLAIM consumes included first-term and due-renewal opportunities, including nonwinners; retry needs fresh explicit eligible nomination, not merely another round. Renewal has one decision in the old term's final hour and must commit before expiry, with its new fixed interval starting at that expiry.

Only complete planning can enter COMMIT. Interrupted or aggregate-budget-exhausted planning discards all proposals and spends/fences its claims; it creates no term. Once COMMIT has begun, a valid committed prefix may remain, but each actual term separately rechecks its exact claim, native/clock/version guards, interval capacity and physical-transition capacity. Uncertainty never rearms a claim or refunds spending.
