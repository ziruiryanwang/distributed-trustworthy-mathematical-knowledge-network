## Main result

A node may provide useful candidate search without retaining and checking every indexed proof. This optional role is distinct from an actual native evidence provider. Its output is bounded claimed metadata and concrete exact-replay source leads. Every downstream mathematical use still requires complete independently checked evidence and actual local application under the receiver's adopted foundation and policies.

That separation can avoid discretionary replicated native stock and checking; it cannot remove required custody, source serving, cold acquisition or receiving-agent checking. Safe false leads still cost resources. Neither a larger catalog nor more participating identities proves useful mathematical growth.

## What the audits changed

Independent reviews repaired three protocol seams and three local retry seams. In particular, two prepared mutations may not reuse a stale precommit comparison; a withdrawn lead can still be restored by a distinct expected-absent action after ABA unless the source quiesces its own requests; and retry completion replay may not count as another actual failure.

The resource cross-check exposed an important stock-time mismatch: eight snapshots held for 600 seconds support only 1152 new starts/day sustained. The repaired illustrative 10000/day case selects at most 60-second snapshots and counts at most 66 seconds of total slot residence at 8.64-second reservation spacing, including orphan replies and cleanup. Its operation count also funds both native sources' self-submissions. These are explicit conditional supply premises, not favorable averages masquerading as a service guarantee.

The final integration review found no remaining blocking contradiction in this selected scope. Its exact reviewed cuts and the editorial selection delta are recorded in the disposition. H and earlier files remain immutable.

These ledgers organize this project's engineering investigation only. They are not features requiring the eventual network's mathematical agents to register work or expose their intentions. Frozen source paths were preserved rather than moved, and no duplicate “latest” specification was created.

## Next investigation and remaining bar

A separate reciprocal-operability review supplies a conditional three-investigator paper path: publish associativity over A; discover/acquire it; use checked injective structure preservation to derive associativity over B; independently reuse that result for a further checked construction. The path does not require a shared goal. It is not an executed example or a proof of resource advantage.

The next bounded conceptual question is entering such a path when the useful target interface and theorem are both initially unfamiliar. Investigate owner-local query-view expansion through candidate maps, with one aggregate work budget and unchanged proof/application checks. A query hint is not an equivalence proof; constant/nonreflecting maps and expensive cold closures are required defeating cases. This new investigation is outside the I selected manifest.

Actual bootstrap, broad-library reconstruction, isolation/recovery behavior, native memory/throughput and causal useful-growth evidence remain unperformed. The concept allows practical heuristics without requiring optimality proofs, but does not label these absent measurements as achievements. No claim of complete operability or guaranteed exponential accumulation is made by freezing this checkpoint.
