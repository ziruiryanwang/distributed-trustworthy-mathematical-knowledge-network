## What is—and is not—bounded

Keep five observations separate: an attempted refresh; a completed scoped lookup; actual placement observations; a reader obtaining an advertisement; and independently checked mathematical reuse. A failed attempt cannot reset the age of completed routing or eligible placement. `CLOSEST_SEEN` does not certify a globally nearest holder set.

For a key admitted before changes quiesce at t0, assume:

1. Its exact public support, ownership and resources remain eligible.
2. The supplied calendar starts a fresh post-t0 lookup within Δ.
3. That lookup actually reaches the required reader-relevant frontier within L and its admitted limits.
4. Useful destination holders actually admit the required copies and marker paths within P, with real eligibility and service at the relevant subsequent reader cuts.

Then the stipulated placement path is established by t0 + Δ + L + P. The proof is the addition of the three attained bounds, not an inference of routing convergence from dispatch. A reader obtaining the ad needs its separately supplied traversal bound; complete evidence and checked application need their own contracts. Without remote progress, admission and overlap, the controller supplies only bounded attempt opportunities and honest failure reporting.

F's normally completed lookup requires at least sixteen successful contacts plus its frontier/settlement conditions. H's four-RPC favorable examples can be lawfully partial or explicitly shorter-exhausted attempts followed by paid operations at actual observed holders. They cannot be described as normal nearest-sixteen completion. Cached contacts do not supply unperformed fresh observations.

## Countercases and composition limits

An isolated incumbent contact graph can keep returning old holders indefinitely; newly reachable holders can refuse storage; delays can be unbounded; scope growth or unreclaimed tails can consume transition capacity. All participants can be honest in these cases. The finite policy cannot promise recovery under them.

Deeper sharding needs independently funded ancestor paths and marker maintenance. A renewed leaf group does not renew a marker. Same-holder scope migration still has F's DROP-before-PUT gap. Historical snapshot pages can remain valid historical reports after ad expiry, subject to actual disclosure authority; they do not prove current placement, acquisition or native usability.

This policy changes discovery timeliness under explicit premises, not mathematical acceptance. It supplies neither worldwide knowledge synchronization nor complete retrieval, perpetual preservation or exponential useful growth.
