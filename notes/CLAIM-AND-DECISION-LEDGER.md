# Claim and decision ledger

## 1. Claim classes

- `DEFINITION`: fixed by this project.
- `DERIVED`: follows from definitions and stated assumptions.
- `EXTERNAL`: supported by identified external work or standard.
- `EMPIRICAL`: measured by a reproducible experiment.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: testable but not yet established.
- `SPECULATION`: plausible long-term possibility without current evidence sufficient for planning.
