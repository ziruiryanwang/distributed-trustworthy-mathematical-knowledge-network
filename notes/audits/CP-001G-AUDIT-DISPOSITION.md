## Claims not established

- Native checking under one exact adopted basis is not a proof that the basis is consistent or the software implementation bug-free.
- Acyclic structural grounding is not native acceptance, optimal representative selection or resource feasibility.
- Unchanged semantic IDs within F4 do not imply unchanged IDs or transferred certificates across F3-to-F4 migration.
- Proof irrelevance is not evidence-footprint irrelevance, arbitrary data equality or constant-cost conversion.
- Finite feature coverage is not full semantic indexing, universal candidate recall or a global completeness certificate.
- Owner-local frontend conveniences are not automatic compatibility with every installed mathlib macro/tactic/plugin.
- A checked B-to-A map does not transport an arbitrary A predicate back to B.
- More instances, encodings, aliases or new hashes do not automatically represent new mathematical insights.
- Bounded queues and honest failure labels alone do not establish efficient autonomous useful accumulation.

These boundaries do not excuse known contradictions. The specific discovered contradictions were repaired or the stronger claim was removed. Remaining source/qualification questions retain their actual names and cannot be closed by document formatting or consensus among reviewers.
