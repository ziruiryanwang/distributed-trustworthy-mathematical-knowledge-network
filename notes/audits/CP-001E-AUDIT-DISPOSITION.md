# CP-001E: integrated audit disposition

Date: 2026-09-20. Work: concept, mathematical arguments, official-source inspection and independent subagent review only.

## Claim being reviewed

The [v2 concept](../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V2.md) should give independent Lean-based investigators a coherent reliable reciprocal path with plausible scale behavior. It should not confuse a safe refusal-only prototype with efficient useful accumulation. It also must not promise complete global discovery, provably optimal heuristics, effortless infinite storage, or unconditional checker soundness.

This disposition records concrete counterexamples and repairs. A closed concept finding means the reviewed specification states a coherent chosen mechanism; it does not mean an implementation has passed the corresponding test. The exact reviewed intermediate hashes in individual audits remain historical review cuts. The checkpoint manifest binds the final frozen document set.

## Not closed by conceptual review

The following are real release obligations. They are not silently marked passed because every named paper finding received a disposition.

- Exact bootstrap/rule/reserved-name bytes have not been instantiated and checked under the new candidate. Actual FoundationID and executable image identities therefore are not invented.
- No proposed exporter/decoder/native adapter has replayed a mathlib slice, validated a complete axiom footprint, or exercised native generated recursors.
- No native cache/environment implementation, storage pin/GC transaction, incident journal, or generation-seal recovery path has been executed or mechanically verified.
- No protocol codec, partial-transfer boundary, scope revocation, advisory activation or hostile index case has been tested.
- No measured discovery/application coverage, end-to-end cold/warm speed, repair throughput or funded long-term adoption is established.
- STRUCT2's suggested feature allocation is not a fully instantiated interoperable extractor contract. The selected first path uses exact/STRUCT1 retrieval plus bounded client matching and FIND_WINDOW; later heuristics must define their exact public contract before advertisement.
- Future unknown implementation or foundation defects remain possible. Current source review is targeted, not exhaustive; independence of checker failures is not assumed.

None of these gates demands a proof that popularity or learned ranking is globally optimal. They demand evidence for the particular reliability, compatibility, usability and cost claims the software will make. Performing their executable parts requires subsequent implementation authorization, which has not been given.

## Checkpoint interpretation

CP-001E is a substantive integrated concept revision: it repairs known trust-basis and scale defects, selects missing custody/client mechanisms, and fixes concrete cross-layer grammar gaps. It does not complete the ultimate autonomous-accumulation goal or certify a deployable product. The goal stays active.

Frozen CP-001D and earlier snapshots retain their bytes and historical meaning. The mutable top-level current-design pointer identifies this revision without rewriting the old index or making historical acceptance claims appear current.
