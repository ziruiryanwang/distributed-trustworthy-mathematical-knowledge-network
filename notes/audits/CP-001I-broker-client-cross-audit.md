## Reviewed cut and initial disposition

It correctly preserves an implication about each actual acceptance, not equality of accepted sets or a useful-liveness guarantee across executions.

Two concept precisions were requested before selection: the coverage of an acquisition observation must be explicit, and autonomy/no mandatory goal registration must not be presented as query confidentiality. Neither is a demonstrated route to accepting a false theorem under the note's unchanged acceptance assumptions. Both matter to a reliable agent workflow and truthful service claims. ## Preserved mathematical acceptance boundary

For the ordinary acceptance event, the selected exact foundation and rule interpretation remain receiver-owned. An arbitrary foreign transcript can influence which candidate is investigated, but may not select a new foundation, weaken an axiom policy, install checking code or manufacture native evidence. Cryptographic authentication does not remove these requirements.

The note correctly separates a metadata feature claim from a feature established about the receiving node's checked mathematics. A broker that has not performed native reconstruction cannot assert that syntactic descriptor copying qualified a constructor type or normalized view. Conversely, a client need not prove every candidate's metadata true before cheaply ranking or rejecting it as a lead. The actual useful application still requires the exact checked instantiation or transport.

The safety argument is conditional on the full ordinary checker and local-state assumptions. It cannot be promoted into a proof of parser safety, absence of denial of service, selection quality or continued availability. The note explicitly rejects those stronger readings.

## C1: observation coverage versus evidence obtainability

The initial table grounds `EvidenceObtainable(s,R,scope,observation)` in an unspecified “concrete bounded acquisition observation.” That is too weak to infer that all selected evidence for R was available: a successful descriptor, root, manifest, type or one-range read can coexist with a permanently absent dependency or an exhausted source scope.

State the actual observed coverage, at minimum the exact source/scope, requested identities/ranges, received and integrity-checked portion, failed or unattempted remainder, and observation time/cut. A partial observation should have a correspondingly partial/source-response name, not a blanket full-evidence-availability assertion. Full selected evidence acquired locally is a separate state, still distinct from native validity and current reusable protection.

No past observation proves future availability, even if the entire closure was acquired on that occasion. Combining exact chunks from several permitted sources can establish local acquisition after all integrity/closure checks; it does not establish that any single source independently had the whole closure. Different replay alternatives remain different acquisition and support obligations.

This precision prevents false success accounting in the reciprocal loop: a cheap responsive metadata endpoint is not already a durable mathematical contribution. It need not introduce a central custody registry or political attribution rule.

## C2: optional research coordination is not confidential search

The initial reciprocal-success paragraph says that local research choices “remain private and autonomous.” Autonomy and absence of a required public task registry are supported by the design. Confidentiality against contacted services is not supplied merely by decentralization.

A transmitted exact target, feature-token set, pattern, authenticated client identity/address, query timing or subsequent evidence request can reveal information about a local investigation. Even a query containing only opaque content identifiers can expose equality and access-pattern correlations. Brokers may collude or share logs unless an independently specified mechanism prevents it.

Replace the unqualified privacy claim with explicit autonomy/no mandatory public-goal-registration language. Query disclosure should be owner-admitted, minimized to the selected service grammar and described as visible to the contacted services and relevant transport observers under the actual transport assumptions. Private local evaluation cases are not a public task registry, but their network queries may disclose information when an evaluation is run.

No anonymity, PIR, encrypted search, traffic-analysis resistance or cross-broker unlinkability should be claimed without an explicit adopted mechanism and its costs. Such mechanisms may be future options; they are not necessary to preserve the paper mathematical-acceptance implication.

## Acquisition authority and bounded resource obligations

The root proposal preserves the baseline separation between a lead and a read capability. A broker cannot mint a third party's export lease, turn its own metadata manifest into a read scope on another node's CAS, or treat a failed public read as permission to enumerate private objects. Contacts remain untrusted network data; normal address, connection and disclosure admission apply.

The inherited G [consumer bindings](../analyses/CP-001G-profile-and-consumer-bindings.md) already state that a structural hint is not generic CAS permission, and that inline structural descriptors and native read scopes are distinct. [F routing](../analyses/CP-001F-dissemination-and-routing.md) likewise does not let ads install callbacks or compel private-endpoint connections. A broker proposal must preserve these exact boundaries rather than reuse a native-provider role name for a candidate-only service.

Budget admission is aggregate across attempts, candidates, failures and redirects. One bounded call is not permission for an unbounded chain of bounded calls. Stage budgets may differ, but a metadata result may not authorize native acquisition merely by requesting more work. Resource exhaustion is nondecision, not a mathematical negative result or an automatically funded retry.

Candidate records, external descriptions and remote error text, if displayed to an LLM-based agent, remain untrusted task data rather than instructions that can acquire local tool or policy authority. A typed minimal grammar can reduce this surface. The companion mechanism should not accidentally reintroduce scripts, free-form instructions or arbitrary URL callbacks through a convenience field.

## Evaluation boundary

The comparative requirement is appropriate: retaining all prior qualified indexes and adding free brokers is not a resource saving. Savings from replacing checking/custody roles must count the retained proof replicas, serving throughput and locality that were displaced, as well as acquisition work shifted to clients.

A broad useful-query pool with hidden candidate IDs is stronger evidence than a known-ID fetch demonstration. Nevertheless it remains a declared workload, not a universal relevance or theorem-equivalence oracle. All costly failures, partial acquisitions and checked-but-irrelevant results belong in the denominator. A broker listing and a token hit are not a downstream checked application or useful new contribution.

## Concrete broker mechanism review

Read [BROKER1](../analyses/CP-001I-untrusted-search-brokers.md) in full at initial SHA-256 `93357B7AFCC7A87C02CD7CD4024E3F6004EE609C910F0B7E2A6B16C5FFEDC059`. Then read the added module map, aligned exact-read observation predicate and repaired mutation passages at final inspected SHA-256 `2A404FAE9F79FE7ED40D1430CAF8AB0B0738CE35F53F340A4BA490C2ECB83C1F`.

No mathematical-acceptance or read-authority contradiction was found in the minimal direct-contact proposal. It keeps D's exact claimed F/E/target/R bindings, separate same-R native source leads, actual source authentication and unchanged receiver reconstruction. Its token-only service does not acquire the native baseline's pattern selectivity by retaining a pattern digest. The optional broker-routing profile remains explicitly incomplete and unselected; this review does not qualify that profile or any actual service capacity.

### C3: final-lead removal

The initial row grammar requires one to four leads, while an accepted removal of the sole selected lead could otherwise leave a current zero-lead row. The final inspected cut now requires the same charged local transaction to unlist/retire that row and its current postings, or explicitly decline the removal. This closes C3 without pretending already disclosed copies vanish. Historical immutable snapshots may retain their older rows only under their separately live disclosure and retention authority.

### C4: submission success must bind the actual lead

The initial `already present` response was ambiguous when d existed only with other sources or an older endpoint payload. That could report successful self publication while the requested lead was not selected, particularly on a fifth-lead refusal. The final cut binds both admitted and already-present success to exact d and the requested canonical self-lead payload at the stated cut. C4 is closed. Existence of the descriptor alone is not a successful lead update or custody receipt.

### Snapshot and authority trace

The snapshot binds immutable row versions, including lead lists, not only descriptor identities. A later lead replacement therefore cannot change the meaning of an old cursor. A source's withdrawal also does not retroactively make the descriptor proposition false; the source's actual present read authority is checked at acquisition.

The shortest-posting choice, exact conjunctive predicate and canonical d ordering are fixed at the snapshot. A matching row advances the boundary only after complete inclusion in the committed output. Page fullness and interrupted testing leave that row unprocessed. END covers only the chosen finite posting above the original lower bound, not all broker metadata, absent descriptors, other brokers or mathematics. Malicious omission remains possible because authenticated cursors authenticate the broker's statement, not its completeness.

The 16-KiB row and 16-KiB nonrow allowance fit the stated 32-KiB minimum result capacity when accounted as the complete encoded result components; larger row counts remain subject to actual aggregate bytes. Per-page CPU, inspections and memory do not imply that every request makes progress. The conditional scan property properly requires enough per-step progress resources and enough funded calls before expiry.

Metadata authority is rechecked at delivery claim. A broker-owner revocation can therefore cancel an unclaimed historical reply rather than leaking it because computation started earlier. Ordinary source-lead churn does not rewrite the old snapshot or confer native source authority. Lost replies and repeated submissions use the inherited exact invocation identity and charged state; a reply or cursor is not new funding.

### Route-role trace

The proposed broker ad is not ProviderAd2, its token key is not native K4, and its honest anchor obligation concerns an authorized metadata row rather than a retained checked proof. The proposed broker contact promises the broker service only. A broker ad in a native key/record slot must fail the role/profile check rather than relax native public eligibility.

The current proposal uses ungrouped leases and does not claim H's group-renewal economies. Namespace/hash, exact invocation/result allocation, lease/revision/recovery mechanics and sustained route supply are still stated selection gates. A known-contact example can be useful without closing unfamiliar-broker routing, but cannot be advertised as evidence that the latter works network-wide. No numeric capability, opcode or schema has been allocated by this audit.

## Four independent module-interface checks

Read the mutable [research module map](../../RESEARCH-MODULES.md) at SHA-256 `B5D0BC3553CA4B88D92CD252810568F1B002D8651025859050BC48D4D8EC1A78`. Its I-META/I-SEARCH/I-LEAD/I-SAFE/I-ROUTE/I-COST questions are consistent with the proposal's evidence grades. The following checks isolate the four receiving interfaces without reopening the settled H checker/reconstruction mechanisms.

### Metadata claims to candidate search

**Input contract.** A finite owner-admitted set of bounded codec-valid D payloads and lead lists, each with an exact candidate identity and disclosure scope. Codec validity does not establish native validity, actual feature extraction, correct F/E semantics, target/replay correspondence or continuing custody.

**Output contract.** An honest broker's completed page gives the exact matches and processed interval for its explicit predicate over one retained authorized immutable metadata cut. A receiver of arbitrary broker messages can rely only on validated message/record bindings, not the broker's asserted completeness. Search ranking and local suppression do not acquire proof authority.

**Minimal countercase.** An attacker supplies a syntactically valid D containing the requested token but unrelated mathematics, or omits the useful D entirely. Search can match the first or miss the second while the native acceptance predicate remains sound. Consequently neither a hit nor END establishes mathematical applicability or nonexistence.

**Concept exit.** The current token predicate, ordered immutable scan, disclosure recheck and non-skipping boundary close this interface at its conditional paper grade. Actual retrieval usefulness and hidden-candidate recall remain I-COST/evaluation questions. C3 and C4 additionally ensure that mutation outcomes preserve the current row grammar and publication claims.

### Candidate result to exact source acquisition

**Input contract.** The client has a claimed descriptor D with exact R and at most four bounded `PUBLIC_NATIVE` leads whose R equals that descriptor's R. A lead is data naming a possible source, not source endorsement, a lease, a generic CAS capability or a delegation to another broker.

**Output contract.** The client obtains exact source-authenticated service observations and scoped unaccepted bytes, charged to its admitted investigation. Observations name exact CIDs/ranges and intervals. Source negotiation and actual public scope or freshly issued holder-bound export authorize the reads; no broker object supplies that permission.

**Minimal countercases.** A source serves only a root/header but lacks a dependency; another returns a different valid replay of the same theorem; a lead points to a private endpoint; a forwarded export head belongs to a different holder. The first is partial acquisition, the second is not an exact response for R, and the latter two do not authorize the proposed fetch. None may silently become complete acquisition or a successful original-candidate use.

**Concept exit.** BROKER1's same-R grammar and six-step receiving path close these authority transitions at the stated grade. Full closure availability, service willingness, resident acquisition resources and source diversity remain substantive conditions, not facts established by the four-lead bound. Combining exact authorized pieces across sources is allowed only with the ordinary integrity and complete-closure checks.

### Acquired bytes to unchanged mathematical acceptance

**Input contract.** Bounded staged bytes, exact expected foundation/replay/target, and receiver-owned adopted policy/context. Valid content hashes establish byte identity, not well-typedness, complete logical support, canonical source correspondence or actual current-use eligibility.

**Output contract.** Only the inherited complete native path can establish a receiver acceptance event, and a useful application additionally checks its actual arguments, premises or transport. Ordinary qualified certificate reuse retains its exact guard conditions; a broker verdict is never such a certificate.

**Minimal countercases.** A perfectly valid proof has a different target, a wrong foundation is well formed, a constructor descriptor names no actual member at its selector, or a cached proof realization no longer matches the selected dependencies. Acquiring those bytes cannot establish the requested original judgment. Wrong-target evidence may be separately considered as another candidate, but not relabeled as this success.

**Concept exit.** The exact expected-root check and unchanged native/current-use transition preserve conditional mathematical safety for any broker transcript. This is not noninterference with research outcomes: the broker can affect which valid result is found, which budget is spent and whether any useful acceptance occurs. It also cannot confer local tool authority through text or contacts.

### Failure and cache observations to future retries

**Input contract.** A failure or success observation with the exact stage, relevant data identity, source/scope, checker/realization/policy context, resource cause and observation interval. The required fields depend on the observation class; one universal negative bit attached to a JudgmentID is insufficient.

**Permitted output.** Bounded local scheduling, suppression or candidate reuse within the original observation's meaning. Every actual retry needs current local admission and current remote scope; it does not recover expired authority or reset cumulative expenditure. A prior positive candidate or read observation is not current native acceptance or future service.

| Observation | Valid cached conclusion | Invalid strengthening |
|---|---|---|
| Returned bytes fail an expected CID/range check | This response did not supply the requested verified bytes | The CID has no valid bytes anywhere, or every source is corrupt |
| Hash-correct exact bytes fail their selected codec | These bytes fail that exact interpretation | Every proof of the target is invalid under every profile |
| Exact native candidate/realization fails checking | That recorded candidate/checking context did not establish the judgment | All alternative instances, realizations or repaired witnesses fail |
| Source refuses or becomes unreachable | This scoped attempt did not obtain its requested evidence | The theorem is false, or another source cannot serve it |
| Local policy/disclosure refusal | This local action is not admitted under that policy | The underlying mathematical evidence is invalid |
| CPU/byte/time exhaustion | This attempt is a nondecision at its spent budget | More resources cannot help, or the target is disproved |
| Broker reaches END | Its asserted scan covers the stated local snapshot interval | The useful theorem does not exist or will never be listed |

**Minimal countercase.** A temporary source refusal is stored as a permanent target-wide invalidity flag, preventing a later fresh source lead or valid proof alternative from being tried. That policy may remain kernel-safe while destroying the intended reciprocal reuse loop. A policy that resets all budgets for every retry has the opposite defect: it can turn a finite false lead into unbounded work.

**Concept exit and remaining choice.** The root and broker notes correctly constrain negative meanings and require aggregate bounded work. They do not yet select a complete quantitative retry/backoff/fair-revisitation policy. Such a policy is a small remaining scheduling module if future operability claims rely on eventual retry after transient failures. Its sufficient premises must include actual renewed owner funding, disclosure permission and service opportunity; no unconditional retry liveness follows from correct caching alone. This is a named unselected policy question, not an identified violation of the current conditional safety claim.

## Final bounded disposition for this review

C1–C4 are closed at the exact repaired cuts recorded above. The minimal direct-contact broker proposal and root trust contract compose coherently at the inspected paper level. The module interfaces preserve exact identities, scoped observations, admitted resources and the unchanged native acceptance boundary; no new blocking inconsistency was identified.

This conclusion does not select BROKER1 or close the proposed routing wire/lifecycle profile, recurring service allocations, quantitative retry policy, actual cost advantage or useful retrieval measurements. Those remain separate bounded research/selection tasks. H remains unchanged, and no implementation has begun.
