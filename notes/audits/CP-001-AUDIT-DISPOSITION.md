## 6. Residual risks and CP-002 obligations

The following are not contradictions in CP-001; they are explicit work required before an implementation claim:

1. freeze deterministic encodings, state-machine schemas, reason codes, conformance vectors, and protocol versioning;
2. select a ledger/database/VFS/filesystem profile, one-writer fencing rule, recovery protocol, and nonoverlapping cross-home escrow representation;
3. model finite exact root/child issuance and spend roll-up, reserve/claim/meter/cancel/local-finalization/outbox, crash-safe time epochs, irreversible terminal state, capability action/fanout bounds, atomic reliance publication, admission, overrides, leases, and partitions in TLA+/TLC or an equivalent state-machine tool;
4. prove the declarative signed fixed-point/tree lemmas in Lean or equivalent and differentially test the implementation;
5. pin the Lean/Mathlib/toolchain closure and implement isolated parsers, builders, and checkers with the frozen validation taxonomy;
6. first demonstrate the local no-network capability floor, then implement the five-cell, 8–16-worker zero-scope workload and seal all harness-side online tasks, budgets, held-out reuse, blinded treatments, calibration, and analysis artifacts;
7. publish fault histories, raw metrics, failed runs, assumptions, and confidence intervals rather than only aggregate success counts;
8. keep Byzantine intra-cell admission, permissionless membership, public DHT/ANN, SNARK aggregation, blockchain anchoring, incentives, and political attribution outside the minimum slice unless a new checkpoint states their exact requirement and threat model.
