## Compatibility and remaining limits

Configuration/store schema advances from 3 to 4, while formal metadata schema
stays 3 and native stage plans stay 2. Existing schema-3 nodes refuse under the
new code; there is no silent migration/backfill. Start a separate fresh node.
Trust remains conditional on the selected Lean foundation/checker and trusted
owner-local runtime/filesystem, not proof of security against a hostile owner.

The prior [serialize-once candidate](../2026-09-22-serialize-once-candidate/RESULTS.md)
and [original local candidate](../2026-09-21-local-v1-candidate/RESULTS.md) retain
their own qualified scopes. They supply earlier peer/custody/recovery and
independent-consumer evidence on those named cuts, not reruns of every historical
test against this one. The new native workflow uses no network.

Known limitations remain: loopback-only transport, a 32-entry remote catalog,
finite context/storage/resource grants, bounded recovery rather than arbitrary
crash recovery, and no broad mathlib/global semantic-search qualification.
Learning a key from this tiny catalog is not evidence of unknown-result search
at scale. Sustained useful accumulation and full workflow affordability remain
open. No independent-host or completed public-v1 claim is made; license and
external publication are still undecided.
