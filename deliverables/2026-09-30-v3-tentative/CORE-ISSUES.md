# Core issues, not a peripheral feature backlog

Tentative v3 issue register, September 30, 2026. Runtime dispositions
belong in the support report. The governing objective remains efficient,
trustworthy reciprocal accumulation in a very large decentralized network.

## Acceptance and production are different bottlenecks

The certified receiver removes a mandatory dependence on ancestral proof
history: it checks the selected interface, full finite journal and final receipt
under its adopted policy. That is an architectural locality property, conditional
on checker correspondence and certificate soundness. It is not free proving.

The current producer still pays substantial proof-generation and local-controller
cost. The actual public controller screen measures 8.5 CPU seconds for initialization
and 1.953125/2.15625 CPU seconds per crypto rebinding. Its optimistic 476.5625 CPU
full-operation projection excludes live scans. Every 50 ms polling pass currently
scans the growing local operation. This is a real central efficiency risk, not
an issue solved by a compact receiver certificate.

Actual public production now provides a more relevant measurement: the second
slice used 224.078125 public-controller CPU seconds inside 3252.859375 inclusive
Job seconds, for nine new stages. That is about 6.89% of its enclosing Job CPU;
it is not an isolated scanner cost. The older experiment's tiny outer-supervisor
fraction does not measure this inner public controller and cannot justify
deprioritizing this newly observed risk. The local producer-state census is
separate from—and does not reintroduce—the receiver's transitive-proof-history
input. All 122 stages now actually completed in twelve disjoint invocations.
Preparation plus those invocations used 42,781.21875 known nonoverlapping CPU
seconds. No invocation was repaired or replayed. Small final cold-check time
does not cancel this producer cost.

The completed operation is immutable. Its evidence can inform a successor,
but cannot be repaired by changing bound software, importing
old proof outputs or resetting its allowance. A structural successor should
separate cheap every-poll process/deadline checks from a justified finite-frequency
storage census, and retain strict pre/post-stage adoption and accounting. It
requires its own invariant, transient-file/refusal tests and actual producer
measurement; this document implements or qualifies no such successor.

## Huge-network search needs more than bounded lookup

Current queries avoid mandatory unrelated-theorem-body scans and enforce a
shared finite RPC/metadata/scan/deadline budget. Actual shape filtering has both
a positive differing-shape case and a same-shape saturation miss. The miss is
an omitted exact full structural type, not merely a different theorem name;
it demonstrates syntactic candidate crowding, not measured loss of downstream
mathematical capability. Actual routed
publication can place an advertisement and reveal an initially unknown holder.
These are useful components, not a production scaling theorem.

Unsplit partition0, local popular-key limits and a constructed closer-hop topology
do not establish useful reachability as the corpus and peer count become huge.
The next central search design must couple query refinement and bounded key
partitioning/replication with a maintenance protocol. It must specify what
publishers place, which views consumers consult, how split/lease transitions
preserve conditional intersection, and how churn/spam/hotspots are charged.
Strictly decreasing XOR distance alone is insufficient. No all-peer or whole-
corpus fallback is acceptable as the ordinary successful path.

Safety must remain independent: an incomplete hint or an eclipsed route can
cause a miss, but must never authorize an unchecked theorem. Universal cheap
complete semantic search and globally fresh status under arbitrary partitions
are not realistic requirements. State conditional availability/recall and
explicit exhaustion, rather than pretending every node knows all mathematics.

## Useful growth requires a causal and economic discriminator

For a matched workload, let P be incremental certification/setup cost, M(R)
incremental maintenance/failure cost for R downstream uses, and d_i the saving
on downstream use i relative to a legitimate locally checked baseline. A net
advantage requires:

```text
sum(d_i for i=1..R) > P + M(R).
```

This is an accounting condition, not observed growth. Warm local caches and
short direct proofs are legitimate controls. One cannot infer d_i from inner
cryptographic milliseconds or force the baseline to replay an invented long
history. All setup, failed attempts, searches, transfers, checking, certification
and replication costs must be visible. Where d_i varies, a single universal
break-even reuse count is unjustified.

Useful accumulation means accessible reusable capabilities enabling independent
later work, not artifact counts or new names for consequences already known.
Exact syntactic deduplication is useful but not semantic novelty. Usage-frequency
and learned ranking may guide local effort without becoming proof authority.
Even valid additions can make a fixed bounded search policy worse through
crowding: the same-shape miss already exhibits that risk. An ideal agent able
to ignore additions and preserve its old view has a nondecreasing *set of
available strategies*, not a guaranteed improvement in actual completion cost.
Practical growth therefore needs retrieval/retention policy and full-cost
evidence together; append-only theorem counts do not prove monotone usefulness.
The current evidence does not establish net useful-growth speedup or exponential
growth. An actual reciprocal loop is an integration prerequisite, not this
economic discriminator. Use a discriminating matched independent-agent workload;
do not multiply expensive
certificate fixtures merely to fill a growth report.

## Production assurance is an essential, separate workstream

Selected native/export/checker examples and refusal tests do not prove general
Lean correspondence. Execution receipts attest a pinned program, not the
correctness of its specification. The fixed-support Prop/same-image restriction
must stay visible. New definitions, computation, inductives and broad mathlib
need explicitly extended semantic inputs and correspondence arguments.

Likewise, recursive cryptographic security and the adopted verifier/runtime
need construction-specific review before adversarial public production claims.
Operational TLS identity does not replace mathematical acceptance. Political
attribution, mandatory research agendas, a global ledger and natural-language
intention matching are not prerequisites for these central improvements.

## Next consequential modules

These are prioritized follow-on decisions, not implementations included in v3.
The [detailed core-gap checkpoint](../../research/cycles/v3-2026-09-29/V3-FINAL-CORE-GAPS.md)
gives the proposed finite tests, cost scopes and falsifiers.

| Priority | Smallest useful next module | A result that would defeat its claim |
|---|---|---|
| Useful search | Materialize an optional exact-type-diverse candidate stream at offer time; then specify visibility across one hot-key partition transition. | Hidden duplicate scans, poisoned first representatives hiding valid supply, or no reachable publisher/consumer view intersection under the declared assumptions. |
| Producer economics | Separate fast process/deadline polling from justified finite-frequency storage census; separately screen the dominant guest parser round trip. | Missed declared resource/binding violations, no controller saving, or migration/recertification cost consuming the trace saving. |
| Causal useful growth | Compare the same downstream endpoint with legitimate local support, exact certified interfaces, and non-oracle bounded discovery. | No full-cost advantage, no actual parent use, or an artificially handicapped baseline. |
| Semantic assurance | Extend one demanded reusable definition feature with explicit meaning/conversion commitments and a faithful checking relation. | The same advertised interface means different things in allowed contexts, or ordinary use again requires full ancestral-proof replay. |

For the optional diverse stream, changing enumeration policy does not by itself
require changing global discovery identity. If key feature bytes and adopted
image/foundation/profile identity remain unchanged, keep their namespace and
keys; version the store policy and query/reply/remote/cursor mode contracts.
Unsupported peers must reject the mode. The first proposed seam is direct-query
only, not a silent extension of existing routed selectors. A namespace change
is needed only for a genuinely changed feature identity. This clarifies the
earlier design proposal; no diversity source or runtime is adopted by this guide.
