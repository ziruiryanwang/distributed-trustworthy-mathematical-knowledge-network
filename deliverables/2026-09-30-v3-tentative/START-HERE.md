## What this tentative version is not

This is a locally qualified, experimental **fixed-support Prop / same-image**
profile, not a representation of all mathematics. New arbitrary definitions,
inductives and general mathlib are outside its supported certificate relation.
General checker-to-Lean correspondence and construction-specific adaptive
recursive cryptographic security have not been formally established here.
Production, transport and verification costs remain real even when ancestor
proofs are unnecessary at receivers.

There is no demonstrated net useful-growth speedup or exponential growth law.
The network has bounded operations, not proved logarithmic lookup, universal
recall, automatic hot-key sharding, adversarial public membership or globally
fresh status. Partitions and stale hints can impair discovery without changing
certificate validity. No sender identity, popularity or human intention enters
mathematical acceptance.
