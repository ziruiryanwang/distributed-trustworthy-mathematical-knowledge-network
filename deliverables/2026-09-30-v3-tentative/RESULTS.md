# Tentative v3 support report

September 30, 2026. This is the qualified local handoff, not completion of the
broader mathematical-commons goal. Neither huge-network efficient discovery
nor net useful-growth speedup is established. The implementation does close
one restricted public contribution-and-reciprocal-return cycle.

## Exact delivery and environment

[START-HERE](START-HERE.md) identifies the frozen base and optional contributor
archives, manifests and separate peer launcher. The adopted file-only package
audit covers 608 base and 12,396 contributor members. Actual public workflows
then exercised the shipped relocations; archive identity alone is not runtime
qualification.

Supported experimental execution is Windows x64, ordinary fixed NTFS paths,
pinned Python/Lean/checker runtimes, explicitly adopted profiles, cooperative
local ownership and finite supervision. Tests use separate processes on one
physical machine with loopback pinned TLS. Independent-host and public-network
operation, arbitrary hostile owner-source isolation and production licensing
are unqualified. License remains undecided; no external publication occurred.

## What remains central

Receivers no longer have mandatory transitive-proof-history inputs in this
profile. They still need their adopted runtime, selected capsule, full finite
journal and final receipt. Arbitrary new definitions, inductives, demanded
evaluation and general mathlib are unsupported. General Lean/checker
correspondence and adaptive recursive production security remain unproved.

Search is budgeted and can be incomplete. Exact indexed locality and a small
unknown-holder path do not establish logarithmic routing, hot-key splitting,
maintained placement under churn, Sybil resistance, semantic recall or globally
fresh status. The observed same-shape miss is syntactic crowding, not a measured
failure of downstream mathematical capability.

A valid reciprocal loop does not prove net useful growth. Legitimate local
support, direct short proofs and warm native caches must be allowed in a fair
comparison. Extra certification/setup, failures, transfer, replication and
maintenance must all enter the account. The optional E1 growth screen was
omitted after its unchanged latest-start cutoff; it was not an attempted test.

[CORE-ISSUES](CORE-ISSUES.md) prioritizes actionable next modules. Political
attribution, a mandatory global task list, a global all-results ledger and
human-intention matching are not substitute milestones.
