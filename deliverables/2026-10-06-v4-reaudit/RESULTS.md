Working record, 2026-10-06. **Full v4 is not achieved.** The new evidence materially
advances both central goals, but does not close the recurring-certification and
usable library-growth gaps. A package, a preparation pass and an inequality
with unestablished cost premises do not jointly establish the requested outcome.
This document does not relabel a qualified development checkpoint as v4 delivery.

source-free theorem application, a real native local checking route retaining
genuine frontier verification, thinner recurrent checking dependencies, direct
definition transport/compilation interfaces and the corrected growth model.
The associated original evidence remains unchanged in experiments and research.

## Requirement-level discipline

The new package is a real implementation advance, not merely a paper proposal.
Nevertheless, do not replace the user's growth requirement by artifact counts,
or assume that a theorem about conditional savings has established all of its
deployment premises. The remaining central questions concern reusable library
interfaces, proof-production cost and a credible sustained service/cost envelope.
They are not political attribution, a universal ledger, a mandatory task list,
human-intention interpretation, or a demand for autonomous novel discovery.

A fixed foundation is not itself a mathematical defect: an expressive fixed
foundation can support indefinitely many developments. The relevant engineering
question is whether reusable definitions and laws can grow with manageable
representation, checking and distribution costs. The existing F-closed compiler
does support checked composition; its limitations must be established from its
actual sharing/import semantics, not assumed from the word "fixed". Likewise,
arbitrary Lean/mathlib support is not silently introduced as a new release gate.

The [independent requirement judgment](../../experiments/compact-sealer-01/REQUIREMENT-LEVEL-JUDGMENT.md)
supports this disposition. The [next central decision](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-06-resumed/NEXT-CENTRAL-DECISION.md)
selects authenticated named library extensions, with explicit cost and validity
obligations. Its proposed relation is not part of the shipped package.
