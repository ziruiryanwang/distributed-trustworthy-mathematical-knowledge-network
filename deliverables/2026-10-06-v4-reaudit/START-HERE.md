## What the software does

Independent agents share formal mathematical resources without a central server.
An agent can search for candidate lemmas or definitions, acquire exact bytes,
check the mathematics under its adopted policy, construct another resource and
offer it to others. It chooses its own investigation. Neither human intention,
a global problem list, attribution nor popularity determines formal acceptance.

The three important layers are:

| Layer | Responsibility | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical warrant | Verify genuine certified premises and check newly used proof/definition data | Downloaded, packed and locally stored do not mean checked |
| Decentralized access | Maintain routing, advertise holdings and return bounded selective candidates | A search result is a hint, not truth or proof of global completeness |
| Useful accumulation | Reuse checked laws and methods at accountable cost; maintain holdings and fund new certified cuts | Useful capability is not the number of theorem names |

A certified cut lets a receiver avoid the proof history behind it. The receiver
still pays for its requested interfaces, definition bodies, new proof and actual
certificate verification. The new native route avoids generating a virtual
machine execution trace for ordinary local checking. It does not manufacture a
transferable certificate or change the adopted mathematical relation.

## What justifies growth support?

The network offers reusable warranted methods, not a universal discovery oracle.
The [growth model](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-06-resumed/GROWTH-UNIFORMITY.md)
fixes causal agent policy, initial endowment, service and full costs. The
[method/cost argument](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-06-resumed/NET-BENEFIT-OPERATING-REGION.md)
exhibits a checked squaring method requiring r rather than 2^r-1 multiplications
for its explicitly named comparator. A complete reuse route has advantage when
its total extra cost is smaller than those saved operations at the declared
cost rates. These are conditional engineering guarantees, not measured global
research acceleration or an assumption that popularity establishes truth.

## Deployment conditions that still matter

The supported installation is Windows x64 and a restricted Lean-derived
mathematical profile, not arbitrary Lean/mathlib. A node may start alone or on loopback. Joining a wider network requires direct
routable IPv4 and an owner-selected reachable bootstrap contact; no built-in central
bootstrap, NAT relay or universal partition recovery is supplied. Providers fund
retention, renewal and query service. Bounded discovery can be incomplete.
Transparent bodies and unsealed fragments remain capacity-limited. See the
[scaling contract](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-06-resumed/SCALING-CONTRACT.md).
