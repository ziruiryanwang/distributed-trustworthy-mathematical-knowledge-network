## What the scaling result says

The [implemented locality audit](../../research/cycles/v3-2026-09-27/IMPLEMENTED-LOCALITY-AUDIT.md)
finds no mandatory input equal to the unrelated global corpus or the transitive
ancestor proof history in the selected receiver/producer algorithms. Receiver
cost depends on its adopted installation, requested interface and certificate.
Contributor cost depends on its new work, selected semantic support and
immediate premise certificates. The new child's 7,639-byte input contains a
body-free L premise, not L's proof.

This does not establish constant cost for arbitrarily large statements,
definitions or new proofs. It does not establish global discovery complexity,
hostile-filesystem isolation, independent hosting, or a large-network load
benchmark. A node cannot read every result at unbounded production rate;
selective access, retained availability and independently checked exact
objects replace mandatory total synchronization.

## Remaining central issues, in priority order

1. **Complete recursive reuse.** Finish the actual child, discharge its one
   assumption, and freshly verify the resulting exact certificate. A saved
   trace, conditional Lean proof or intermediate checkpoint is not enough.
2. **Make contribution a usable product path.** Connect verified parent bundle,
   exact interface import, ordinary source, new checking/proving and returned
   bundle through an installed workflow. The research adapter is not yet that
   independently usable contributor or an upgraded native node.
3. **Keep the trust boundary defensible.** General Nanoda-to-Lean fidelity and
   compatible sealed-interface realization remain unestablished. The actual
   verifier accepts the SDK's whole default recursion-program root, broader
   than the honest producer's schedule. Production assurance must address that
   accepted set and adaptive multi-claim recursion, not infer security from
   passing examples or invent a numerical error bound.
4. **Cover enough mathematics.** New definitions/inductives, authenticated
   selective computational support and proof-sensitive reduction are not
   implemented in this theorem-only fixed-foundation profile. The foundation
   has 43 records / 66 names; it is not all of mathematics hidden in a baseline.
5. **Make total cost worthwhile.** Cheap recipient checking has been observed,
   but certification is expensive. Compare matched full native and certified
   intake/contribution costs, including warm caches and cold trust domains,
   before claiming an amortization win. Optimize new-proof work where evidence
   identifies the bottleneck; do not weaken acceptance.
6. **Demonstrate useful accumulation.** The earlier resource-sensitive and
   catalytic-hypergraph models remain hypotheses for evaluation. No positive
   matched useful-growth result follows from object counts, the congruence
   example or the mere existence of recursive certificates.
