# V4: reciprocal, bounded proof reuse and justified capability growth

Final local qualification report, 2026-10-06 UTC. The software archive, fresh
extraction, five relocated public commands and actual returned-fragment check
passed their scoped audits. This is a usable restricted-profile local v4,
not completion of the two global-scale goals. No runtime remains live.

## What materially changes

The October 2 checkpoint could authenticate its received parent but failed the
foundation bridge before making downstream work. The current successor restores
the exact protected foundation witnesses without changing the foundation or
checking image, and actually constructs new results from network-acquired work.

More importantly, it no longer requires buying a new cryptographic certificate
for every intermediate theorem. An agent can publish a **bounded proof fragment**:
the complete new proof material above selected genuine certified cut points.
Another agent substitutes those proofs into its next contribution and checks the
whole transformed result. No temporary assumption representing an imported
unsealed result may remain. Explicit hypotheses such as associativity remain
part of the published theorem. The proof transformer is not mathematical
authority; the unchanged pinned checker is.

This creates a practical tradeoff: ordinary contributions can avoid immediate
expensive proof production; their recipients check a bounded delta. A genuine
certificate can later replace that delta for wider succinct reuse. Certificate
production is still costly. Local checking is not a transferable receipt, and
the two routes have different explicit assurance assumptions.

## Demonstrated mathematical loop

1. A genuine certified seed supplies a body-free formal interface and required
   definitions under the adopted profile.
2. A contributor makes `V4Growth.iterated_factor_map` as a fully checked unsealed
   fragment over that certified frontier.
3. Real pinned TLS moves that candidate A to B; A shuts down; B serves exact
   custody to a fresh C. Mathematical truth is not inferred from custody.
4. An independent next source uses C's acquired fragment to prove the generic
   law `V4Growth.associative_square_blocks`. A full statement/proof-structure
   audit confirms that the actual received proof was materialized, not merely
   mentioned or replaced with a success flag.
5. That new law returns through real TLS to B's fresh custody. The relocated
   public package checks those actual returned bytes against the genuine
   certified frontier and the complete composed proof. The final receiving gate
   passes; no packaged example or producer-side success flag substitutes for it.

The separate public local-shard sequence initializes a shard, describes the
returned fragment, offers it as custody, and finds it by the freshly described
exact type key. Its stored eight files match the returned B custody exactly.
The final mathematical invocation consumes the original returned B directory,
not the new shard copy; the identical-byte comparison is separate evidence.
Neither custody nor local indexing is mathematical acceptance.

The two-instance composition test separately exercises different universe
instantiations of the same incoming proof. Source-free `prepare-seal` separately
preserves an exact received theorem and captures one actual checking snapshot,
without needing its author's source or repeating standalone execution. It
reaches READY, not a new certificate. No new cryptographic proof campaign was
needed to establish the new composition/network seams.

## What useful growth means here

The working philosophy is resource-bounded warranted capability. A fixed
foundation's unrestricted deductive closure does not enlarge whenever a proof
file arrives. What can improve is the collection of justified actions finite
agents can perform with available computation, memory, communication and evidence.
Validity is independent of human intention and popularity. Usefulness depends
on local circumstances and can be estimated fallibly; no universal score or
shared investigation list is imposed.

The new squaring law supplies one precise, limited witness of this interpretation.
For any associative operation and element a, a specified left-associated program
using `2^(m+n)-1` multiplications has the same result as two shared-state squaring
blocks using exactly `m+n` multiplications. The generic equality is actually
checked by the software; the named-algorithm call counts follow by induction.

This is a theoretical algorithm-family advantage, not an empirical extrapolation.
Exponential-versus-linear total work additionally assumes fixed-size data,
bounded positive multiplication/control costs, and reuse of already evaluated
intermediates. The law does not itself ship a fast evaluator or make kernel
reduction logarithmic. It is a standard algorithmic idea, not a claim of a new
mathematical discovery or an advantage over every agent's existing library.

Net benefit requires cumulative savings from actual applications to exceed all
production, discovery, acquisition, checking, storage and application overhead.
Demand and those application costs are not fabricated. Neither theorem counts,
nominal exponents nor these small exchanges establish exponential knowledge growth.

Two further conditional policies are justified, not silently implemented as an
autonomous research planner:

- With a fixed successful permanent investment B > 0 and known nonnegative
  per-use premiums, buy before accumulated rents plus the next premium reach B.
  This costs at most twice the clairvoyant two-route optimum, strictly less when
  that optimum is positive; if B=0, immediate purchase is trivially optimal.
  Arbitrary future demand is allowed. Unknown
  prices, expiry, failures, resource coupling and unequal endpoint requirements
  invalidate the simple model unless separately handled.
- A prepaid optional discovery/application allowance H followed by an unchanged
  direct route of cost T gives an additive H+T bound, plus charged overhead,
  under genuine resource isolation. This is not same-budget nonregression and
  does not control an external LLM's deliberation merely by prompting it.

The packaged layered guide includes the law, derivation, boundaries and cost
model. The full research checkpoint additionally treats complementary bundles,
rare-result exploration, public-good underinvestment and workload dependence.

## Scale and trust contract

An operation depends on its selected semantic data, bounded new proof packet,
selected certified interfaces and demanded computation—not the total archive
or the ancestral proof history below the cuts. Current limits include four
local fragment inputs, 16 MiB aggregate selected-fragment bytes, a 4 MiB final
packet and eight certified frontier interfaces. Each output capsule has at most
four type-needed definitions; proof internals have separate bounds.

These caps are not a polynomial-time theorem for dependent type checking.
Actual compute/memory limits remain enforced. At a cap, an agent must obtain a
shorter proof, fund another certified cut, choose another route or stop. It may
not silently download an unbounded unsealed ancestry.

The peer indexes selected metadata and queries bounded postings; it never grants
mathematical acceptance. Local-cut completeness is not global completeness.
Known contacts and selected shards are implemented; maintained global routing,
hot-key/churn maintenance and universal semantic recall are not. No consensus
ledger, universal fresh network state or central server is required or claimed.

The trust base includes the adopted restricted relation/foundation and exact
image, local runtime/OS where execution is used, and cryptographic assumptions
where receipts are used. Sound relative checking does not establish foundation
consistency or correspondence with every feature of Lean. The fixed F43 and
F-closed definition lane do not support arbitrary contributed-definition graphs,
all inductives or transparent mathlib use.

## What remains central after this release

1. Reduce repeated operation-local profile/serialization/context work while
   preserving immutable input bindings and final checking. Model the removable
   work before benchmarking or adding a cache.
2. Extend reusable semantic vocabulary through a precisely bounded acyclic
   definition graph. Its demanded semantic closure is different from proof
   ancestry; changing the checker profile requires new assurance work.
3. Use concrete capability applications to guide both efforts: prove costs for
   a specified before/after endpoint, including adoption and premise obligations,
   rather than counting examples or assuming all standard lemmas are novel.
4. Add maintained decentralized placement, routing, custody and recovery with
   stated churn, replication, admission and capacity assumptions. Bounded local
   lookup alone does not make a globally workable network.

Public licensing remains undecided, and third-party redistribution obligations
need review. This is local delivery to the project owner, not external publication
or live deployment. Those legal/operational limits do not replace the central
technical work above. V4 is a material step toward the full goal, not completion
of a universal or “perfect” mathematical commons.
