## Practical limits and inherited evidence

Config 6 / physical database 5, wire/catalog 2 and stage-plan 2 remain selected.
Use fresh installations and node states; there is no in-place migration.
Contexts contain at most 16 packages including the five common packages;
admission selects at most four candidates. Full checked descriptions must fit
the 256-reference cap. Bounded catalog/contact/request/time/memory/byte limits
remain enforced; numerical ceilings are not throughput promises.

Search is exact local type-symbol filtering or selected-peer page enumeration,
not a complete global index, novelty oracle or equivalence detector. Agents
need not store or learn the entire corpus. Hosting and replica custody require
explicit finite resources; no network-wide up-to-date view or permanent
retention is guaranteed. General backup/restore, power-loss recovery and
corrupted-state repair are not supplied.

The [source10 qualification](../2026-09-23-short-header-local-candidate/RESULTS.md)
and its predecessor links retain earlier reciprocal, contact, custody,
continuation, recovery and performance evidence. Unchanged source comparison
justifies focused testing; those complete campaigns were **not rerun wholesale
on source12**, and do not automatically prove all later behavior.

General mathlib integration, arbitrary external inductive families, every
recursive definition, instance/notation reconstruction, hostile local owners,
independent physical hosts, Internet deployment and broad scaling remain
unqualified. No sustained usefulness, exponential accumulation or universal
mathematical coverage follows from a bounded successful exchange. There is no
mandatory human-intention interface or global task queue.

Licensing remains undecided. No public release, external deployment or
redistribution permission is asserted. Research references above are audit
links in the full project, not runtime dependencies: ordinary operation uses
the verified package, supplied runtimes and extracted operator/API documents.
The broader commons objective is not closed by naming this release v2.
