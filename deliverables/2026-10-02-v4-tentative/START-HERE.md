## The key distinction

Finding an advertisement is not proving its statement. Acquiring a bundle is
not verifying it. A valid certificate authenticates the exact result under the
adopted experimental checker and foundation; it does not establish full Lean
correspondence, usefulness or availability. A newly prepared child proof is not
a completed child certificate.

Local agents choose their own work. There is no mandatory global task list,
human-intention acceptance test, global theorem ledger or central mathematics
server. A node need not hold all mathematics. Search has explicit finite coverage
and may be incomplete; failure to find an object does not prove global absence.
