# V4 results, costs and remaining central gaps

Tentative milestone, 2026-10-02. The shipped reader is independently qualified.
Acquired-parent preparation failed at its foundation-protection bridge; the
contributor-reuse release is held. This is not full-project completion.

## What advances beyond the previous certified lane

V4 can carry safe new transparent definitions together with exact proposition
interfaces. An agent need not obtain a separate definition certificate merely
to use such vocabulary: the definition's type and body are checked as semantic
inputs. Proposition premises still require genuine certificates. Definition
identity includes meaning, not just a display name or type.

The profile is deliberately restricted: definitions must depend only on its
fixed foundation, with bounded context and one/two-member certificate collections.
It does not yet admit arbitrary Lean libraries or indefinitely chained contributed
vocabulary. The separate Lean/native and Nanoda/zkVM checking paths have not been
proved equivalent for full Lean. Cryptography certifies the pinned checking
program's execution; it does not remove that semantic assurance obligation.

Typed discovery retains these distinctions. Same-type definitions with different
bodies are not silently collapsed. Index advertisements and transport successes
remain unverified metadata. New policy/image namespaces do not reinterpret old
receipts or node state.

## What checking locality achieves

The completed seed's exchange bundle totals **230,200 bytes**: manifest 823,
capsule 5,575, journal 192 and receipt 223,610. The receiving role contains Python,
the verifier and adopted semantic policy, without Lean, the guest image or proof
ancestry. It authenticates the selected result from this bundle, not from the
seed's production checkpoints.

Recursive certificate composition is designed to avoid mandatory replay of
ancestral proof steps. It does not erase the costs of selected interfaces,
definition bodies, demanded computation, certificate production or network
availability. This rank-zero test alone is not a new depth-scaling benchmark.

## Useful-growth criterion and present verdict

The adopted theory is growth of **resource-bounded, independently checkable
capability**. A proved lemma does not enlarge a fixed foundation's unrestricted
deductive closure; it may make later correct work feasible or cheaper. Intention
does not enter formal acceptance. There is no mandatory global usefulness score.

For equal successful downstream endpoints and a declared additive resource r,
let A be direct cost, C reuse cost, J incremental shared investment and M
maintenance/failure costs not already included. Net saving over horizon H is:

`G_r(H) = sum_i (A_i,r - C_i,r) - J_r - M_H,r`.

Positive savings require positive G, not merely citations, smaller source files
or more receipts. CPU, agent effort, bytes, latency and memory need separate
accounting. A forced-citation example proves a composition mechanism, not that
an unconstrained agent would benefit from choosing that lemma. The present
direct/reuse authoring exercise is not a blinded causal agent trial.

Even if reuse preparation were free, savings of at most the direct 35.890625 CPU
seconds would require at least 2,591 identical-use opportunities to repay just
the seed's measured 92,976.578125 CPU seconds. This is a lower-bound diagnostic
for preparation-only savings, not a forecast, a count of novel theorems or a
bound on unmeasured proof-production savings.

The reused source's actual native output capsule is byte-identical to the direct
source's capsule: 8,790 bytes, SHA-256
`6a894fda3d5d78e2c9d97b1c4af572ca1ff1594da990f0c6e69325c6c76305be`.
This binds the same endpoint type and embedded definition bodies. Native reports
also witness actual use of the selected parent. These are substantive conditional
native results, not a completed guest/certificate path.

**Net useful-growth advantage has not been established.** Reuse preparation
failed before a guest snapshot. Its segment count and future stage count are
unknown, not zero. It cannot be compared as an equal-quality completion against
direct READY with a 114-segment snapshot and zero parents (zero completed proof
stages). Both full endpoint certificates remain absent;
no preparation speedup or proof-cost saving is inferred from this failure.
See the [philosophical model](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-02/PHILOSOPHY-OF-GROWTH.md)
and [independent growth audit](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-02/GROWTH-CLAIM-AUDIT.md).

## Negative evidence is part of the result

The typed-row optimization failed its prospective efficiency gate: the retained
AB workload rose from 66 to 75 proof segments despite fewer guest instructions.
It is not reported as a speedup. Its parser is present in a separately justified
semantic successor, without borrowing an efficiency claim.

The first typed transfer failed a harness process-accounting ceiling. Its
allocation and 6.46875 known CPU seconds remain retained. The separately reviewed
successor distinguishes six direct commands from cumulative OS Job processes;
successful transfer02 does not rewrite transfer01. The unused reader's outer
concurrency allowance was separately given prospective margin, not represented
as a demonstrated product defect.

The downstream failure is a separate product integration issue, not another
process-ceiling failure or timeout. The bridge refused a reserved foundation
declaration whose exported structural digest did not match the adopted registry.
The safety check remains intact. Used package/source/state and failed costs are
preserved; no automatic retry, silent state promotion or budget renewal occurred.

The identified declaration is `congrArg`: a shared expression in the imported
parent supplied a different binder display name before the foundation declaration
was exported. Lean's expression interning permits alpha-equivalent sharing,
whereas this protocol's exact structural digest includes the display name. The
correct repair must preserve the adopted foundation's exact commitment and
reject genuine semantic drift, not merely exempt this declaration from checking.
The [saved-data diagnosis](../../experiments/certified-growth-01/REUSE-PREPARE-COST-TRIAGE.md)
and [independent diagnosis review](../../experiments/certified-growth-01/REUSE-BRIDGE-DIAGNOSIS-REVIEW.md)
distinguish this representation
defect from proof invalidity; no repair is included in these frozen archives.

## Remaining central release gaps

1. **Repair the actual contributor blocker:** make foundation serialization and
   the exact-interface bridge agree without weakening the adopted foundation or
   accepting unverified replacement declarations. A fresh reviewed successor
   must pass changed-boundary controls and this acquired-parent seam before it
   can be recommended. A source-level diagnosis alone is not a repaired release.
2. **Producer affordability and useful leverage:** identify dominant proved work,
   reduce it without weakening acceptance, and demonstrate optional-use, matched
   endpoint gains after full production/discovery/maintenance costs. Do not fund
   another large certificate campaign solely to obtain a favorable count.
3. **Huge-network maintenance:** the maintained bounded directory covers a
   cooperative split/locator/lease slice, not arbitrary repeated repartitioning,
   bootstrap and membership churn. Bounded search cannot promise complete global
   discovery or instant universal status under partitions.
4. **Semantic generality and assurance:** extend safely beyond foundation-closed
   definitions and small contexts; strengthen checker/Lean correspondence and
   changed-image/migration policy. Never substitute popularity for proof safety.
5. **Deployment qualification:** packaged contributor/peer runtime relocation,
   independently operated hosts and operational hardening remain open. Licensing
   is undecided; this handoff is not external publication.

The broader goal remains active. V4 closes concrete central integration steps,
not the claim that global, economically accelerating mathematics is solved.
