## What this successor adds

After initialization, the owner can add an immutable supplier alias with
`manage.py add-peer --config ABS_CONFIG --request ABS_ENVELOPE`. Use the exact
schema in the API; `status` supplies the configuration digest and authorized
contact list. Ordinary agents then use the same `peer-find --peer ALIAS` route.
Sixteen initial-plus-added contacts are supported; old aliases cannot be
rebound/deleted. Adding a contact neither dials it nor accepts its mathematics.

The real same-host continuation completed third-contact addition, exact replay,
conflict refusal, old-handle retrieval and fresh admission of a different audited
theorem while preserving earlier checked state. Its exact audit disposition is
in the linked qualification record. Two harness failures are retained, not
reclassified; the successful initialization was continued without a reset.

The preceding operator05 cut supplies the preserved packaged authoring/hosting
and reciprocal-contribution evidence. Those unchanged-core operations were not
all rerun on this successor. This new run is specifically contact/storage/admin
integration, using static public theorem suppliers rather than new publications.
