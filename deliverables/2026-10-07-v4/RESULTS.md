## What is justified about useful growth

The model is proof-relative, resource-sensitive mathematical structuralism:
formal structures and checked relationships are the mathematical content;
useful accumulation enlarges warranted capabilities available under resources.
Human intention and popularity do not decide mathematical validity. There is
no mandatory global evaluation distribution or list of unsolved tasks.

For the ten exact retained constructions, all actual lowerings succeed and give:

| Representation of those endpoints | Payload bytes |
|---|---:|
| Shared recipes once | 19,963 |
| Recipes plus one largest capsule | 27,609 |
| Every independent flattened prefix | 53,480 |
| Recipes plus every flattened cache | 73,443 |

Therefore `27,609 <= 32,768 < 53,480`. This proves an implementable local
retention-capacity region, conditional on retaining recipes, limiting retained
capsule copies and separately funding nonpayload resources. It is a 62.67%
recipe-only payload reduction against independently retained prefix capsules,
not a whole-disk or whole-cost reduction. The analyzer retained every output for
audit; cache eviction was not executed or automated. Every retained canonical
capsule copy counts, including copies in evidence/native artifact directories.

The acquired semigroup law also supplies a warranted algorithm transformation.
For L=m+n, two blocks of squaring compute the same positive power as a fixed-base
loop: L rather than `2^L-1` multiplications in the specified operational model.
This is a mathematical cost theorem, not a measured evaluator speedup or new
mathematical discovery. End-to-end savings additionally require avoided costs
to exceed acquisition/checking/proving/service/other overhead. No unmeasured
quantity is assigned a convenient value to claim net research acceleration.

## What is justified about scale

Let M denote global retained objects and H the proof ancestry behind selected
certified frontiers. The selected path needs neither an M-object census nor
H's proof steps. It still pays for requested interfaces, transparent semantic
bodies, actual frontier receipts and unsealed proof work. Bounded index/routing
queries can return incomplete results; common-type candidate examination can
still be linear in the relevant candidates. No universal cheap complete search
or globally fresh status is promised.

The adopted overlay has maintained advertisements and finite local routing,
metadata and query budgets. Favorable routing/liveness depends on coverage,
reachability, load, churn and funded refresh. A finite local-state bound is not
a proof of Internet-scale service quality. The exact service conditions are in
the [scaling contract](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-06-resumed/SCALING-CONTRACT.md).

Current lowering accepts at most 64 modules, 16 MiB raw selected input and 4 MiB
output, subject to graph/depth/work bounds. Acquisition can have larger bounds
and still fail later lowering. Large global size means many independent finite
selections, not unbounded work hidden behind one request.

The compact source-free certification route remains implemented. Its selected
runtime dependency premise was discharged by the
[source/build/binary dependency audit](../../research/cycles/v4-2026-10-07/SEALER-DEPENDENCY-AUDIT.md).
No duplicate proving campaign was required or run. Cryptographic soundness is
relative to the adopted relation/image and assumptions; relation correctness
against all Lean behavior and foundational consistency are not proved by a
receipt. This release neither changes those assumptions nor supplies arbitrary
Lean/mathlib support.
