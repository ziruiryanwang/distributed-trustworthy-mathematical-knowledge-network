# Finite due-window calendars: same-offer TENURE1 comparison

Date: 2026-09-20. Root-authored conditional concept investigation. UNSELECTED; no code, executable model, Lean run or benchmark. [TENURE1](lead-tenure-policy.md),35,648 bytes / SHA-256 `79DA034872C011A8387AA8080BCEF79BF8992973898C6AE96C7CEB0DC7B381CE`, and its two audits are preserved unchanged. This note compares permitted local calendar policies; it does not amend I or retroactively add a wire-visible lease.

## Question and comparison boundary

Uniform polling spends work between due windows even when no current installation can yet expire. Does a finite calendar that activates only known due windows provide the same bounded-residence offer and the same stipulated useful discovery path with fewer scan executions? A positive answer is not total-resource superiority, better health classification, fair admission, native soundness or sustained useful mathematical growth.

Both alternatives keep the same M slots, r-slot cyclic scan, initial cursor, immutable installation deadlines, private incarnation fencing, qualified clock, owner authority, admission cutoff A, maintenance horizon H and actual resource accounts. They preserve I's op21/22/23 meanings and native/source/reader responsibilities. Source and reader attempts are fixed before comparing outcomes. Old snapshots and historical contacts remain real; current removal is not their erasure. The externally offered guarantee is TENURE1's conditional bound `e + C*h + w`, where `C=ceil(M/r)`. Sparse scheduling is allowed to decline a new installation if it cannot reserve its full lifecycle; it does not promise identical admission outcomes on every possible workload.

The original99-round hypothetical trace reserved a full uniform calendar; it also explicitly allowed stopping after authorized complete drain. A fair comparison must give that option to both sides. We therefore distinguish reservations from actual scans and do not advertise99 versus4 as the actual-work saving when the uniform policy can stop earlier.

## Fixed finite calendar, no future-arrival oracle

At episode admission, name a qualified clock grid with positive h and finite indices `k=1,...,S`, appointments `t_k=k*h`, and `t_S+w <= H`. Nonzero activation origins can be translated in the same qualified clock. All grid arithmetic is exact bounded local arithmetic with checked overflow. Require `w<=h` for this serial recipe; if the supplied scan bound is longer, this recipe cannot promise every adjacent appointment with one scan at a time. Choose a positive tau and commit windows so installation/adoption preparation precedes every needed appointment. The whole grid and maximum budget are fixed at episode admission; later activation of cells does not invent new appointments, an extra execution grant or an endlessly renewed episode.

An appointment is a possible service opportunity, not an already pending scan job. A finite array records each cell's inactive/reserved/claimed/completed/failed state and charged references to its grants/outcomes. Uniform U reserves every cell at setup. Window W reserves a union of due windows as installations become actually admissible. These are prospectively admitted alternatives, not permission to retrofit an active uniform episode, relabel its admitted round count J, or cancel its prior obligations. Each fixes an immutable finite maximum round count inside the actual grant; W's mutable activation set can only consume that cap. W uses the same authorized scheduled caller as TENURE1. It may keep a sorted linked set of reserved unclaimed cell indices: next/previous links are stored in the finite array, and bounded linear search through at most S cells finds splice positions. This is an explicit paid calendar representation, not a theorem-deadline heap or a free timer service. At most one actual scan job is claimed at a time; the array entries do not consume one pending-execution slot each.

The dispatcher arms only the next reserved appointment and, after its bounded completion, the next. Skipped grid cells cause no scan, no cursor advance and no phantom inspection. An earlier newly activated appointment requires an authenticated charged timer update; it must precede the old next appointment without breaking any admitted job. Timer/dispatch latency is included in the supplied service premise. If actual starts must be later than grid times, admission must use a correspondingly enlarged first-start/gap bound or a specifically reserved slack calendar; it cannot count intended grid times as observed starts. The concrete comparisons below stipulate actual starts equal their grid times.

The W cell ledger is never allowed to grow beyond its admitted S entries or actual byte limit. Logs, old outcomes, interrupted preparations and timer generations remain separately charged. All occupied/free map, row, slot and incarnation state still follows TENURE1. This calendar does not index proofs, rate usefulness or classify source health.

## Paid online admission

For an exact known due time e, define `b(e)=ceil(e/h)` and the required C-cell window

\[
W(e)=\{b(e),b(e)+1,\ldots,b(e)+C-1\}.
\]

Require `1<=b(e)` and `b(e)+C-1<=S`. Its first start lies in `[e,e+h]`, and its C consecutive starts have gap h. Every reserved job must supply its actual r-slot guarded decisions within w, including any admitted due removals, map/index/version changes and settlement. Existing cheap empty-scan reservations cannot be reused as full removal capacity without upgrading their actual resource reservations. A conservative option reserves worst admitted r-slot work and transient state for every activated cell, independently of which incarnation eventually occupies each slot. This remains a symbolic service bound until representation and primitive costs are qualified.

Actual commit time is not known merely from request arrival. Before admitting an installation, choose a short funded preparation/commit window `[l,u]`, with `u< t_b` for the earliest appointment it may need. Reserve the union for all possible due times `a+tau` with `a in [l,u]`:

\[
b_- = \lceil(l+\tau)/h\rceil,\quad b_+ = \lceil(u+\tau)/h\rceil,\quad V(l,u)=[b_-,b_++C-1]\cap\mathbb Z.
\]

There are `C+b_+-b_-` cells, not necessarily C. Every potential `W(a+tau)` is a subset. Reject an out-of-grid or unserviceable plan, or shorten the commit window before work is promised. Preparation reads current cell/grant/timer generations and reserves every missing cell plus actual additional removal/stock obligations. Coalescing an existing cell saves only a second scan job; it does not permit sharing the same finite CPU, bytes or retained version capacity twice. The plan must preserve all existing installation and unrelated obligations at every time.

At the serialized successful row/slot insertion, recheck actual `a in [l,u]`, `a<=A`, `a+tau+C*h+w<=H`, current authority/clock, unchanged plan generations and sufficient actual reservations. Atomically attach the fresh incarnation's original a/e to that funded calendar coverage. An expired/stale preparation yields a charged bounded retry or decline, never an installation backed by a stale time bucket. Plans whose preparation is incomplete when their earliest appointment becomes unserviceable cannot admit the installation. Because the preparation window precedes its required starts, no already completed/failed cell can supply future coverage.

Local reservation transactions and remote mutation grants must actually permit/pay the planning work. It can be bounded internal installation-admission work if the inherited operation's actual limits suffice, or separately admitted owner preparation under standing policy; this is not permission to exceed a remote grant. Distinct planning jobs consume the candidate's shared execution allowance as well as their other resources. There is no new remote scheduling operation and no forced foreign-lead removal inside op21.

For initial adoption, exact enumeration supplies the common activation a0 and cohort deadline a0+tau; prepare before activation and recheck the complete selected cut as TENURE1 requires. A shared C-cell plan can cover the entire same-deadline cohort, but all M inventory/map/slot work and its worst removal stock remain paid. This avoids needlessly planning identical C cells M times; it is not permission to skip adoption enumeration. More general online arrivals pay their own plans without knowledge of future cohorts. No-op observations, endpoint updates and invocation replay do not create plans or renew deadlines.

After successful admission, retain all its reserved appointments even after early withdrawal/removal; no per-installation reference-count cancellation is assumed. Failed preparation can conservatively retain any actually committed provisional appointments too, with their grants and eventual execution/settlement charged. Abort need not leak unbounded resources: fixed S/stock/grants cap such attempts, further optional work declines, and finite drain settles them. A failed attempt may be costly or exhaust admission capacity without adding a theorem; charge that fact rather than erasing it. Only after new-installation admission is closed, no preparation can still commit and every managed installation is actually gone may either policy stop unnecessary future scans under TENURE1's authorized complete-drain rule. Releasing the unclaimed reservations then is explicit funded bookkeeping, not erasure of completed/uncertain outcomes.

## Coverage argument and what it does not prove

Fix an admitted installation still current at its due e. Its committed plan contains W(e); future insertions add appointments but never remove this coverage. Suppose authority/clock/resource premises persist and these C reserved scans actually start at their grid times and finish their guarded decisions within w. The first start is at most e+h. The last finishes at most `e+C*h+w`. The original fixed-slot traversal proof therefore removes the incarnation by that bound. Additional successful scans cannot skip a still-current due installation: cursor advances only with the corresponding completed guarded decision. Interrupted work does not advance it. Earlier actual removal already satisfies the conclusion.

Reservations alone prove no progress. Failure, clock uncertainty, sealing, revocation, missed starts and partial jobs are charged incidents under TENURE1; they can invalidate this service premise. A new arrival may be declined without invalidating already admitted service, provided its failed planning/settlement was itself bounded and reserved. Continuous healthy availability and fifth-source fairness are still not guaranteed.

The theorem is **bound equivalence**, not trace equivalence. Omitting pre-due scans changes cursor phase. For example, M8/r4/c0=4, e=8: uniform starts4 and8 inspect4..7 then0..3, whereas a sparse window8/12 inspects4..7 then0..3. A slot0 incumbent can disappear four seconds later under W; a new source attempting at11 can see a vacancy under U but not W. Both satisfy the same e+10 bound for h4/w2. Thus equal externally stated residence bounds do not justify cancelling all source/reader costs or claiming workload-wide availability dominance.

## Planning, storage and genuine break-even

Let K count all bounded preparation attempts, including stale/failed ones; let n_j=`C+b_+(j)-b_-(j)` be attempted cells for attempt j. A direct finite-array implementation can inspect those n_j cells, compare their current grants, and use at most an additional S-cell traversal to splice its interval into the paid reserved set. A deliberately loose admission-work envelope is `O(S + sum_j(S+n_j))` cell operations, with actual key/grant/byte/CPU/transaction work charged separately. Initial array setup and any provisional-cell retirement are not free. Grouped exact adoption can use one cohort plan, not M plans. No claim is made that this algorithm is fast enough for I's smallest per-call grant or a maximum-table burst.

Let J_W be distinct activated cells actually claimed before authorized drain, including conservatively retained failed-preparation cells; let P_W count distinct planning/dispatcher/drain jobs not already inside a counted claim. The shared execution condition is `Q+U+J_W+P_W+other <= actualRemainingGrant`, with CPU/I/O/traffic and all-times memory/durable stock tested separately. U obeys the analogous condition including its setup/dispatcher/drain. Calendar cells and resource reservations are not a pool of20000 ready-to-run pending jobs; actual claim concurrency/pending/retained-control limits still bind. A full r-removal service reservation is not proof that the real row versions fit the resident limit.

For the matched small trace, suppose all genuinely common source/native/public work has been identified, not simply presumed equal. Define S_saved as the actual resource vector of U's50 omitted scan jobs, including their eventual retention/finalization. Define D as W's additional planning/calendar/dispatch/stock costs minus U's corresponding costs, plus every remaining difference in the four nonomitted jobs and their aftercare. W's total resource advantage on each cumulative axis requires `D <= S_saved` on that axis. Peak stock and deadline service require their own all-times inequalities; negative cumulative work does not imply lower peak memory. These are decision conditions, not supplied measurements. Source/reader owners obtain no automatic reusable grants from broker savings.

The counted difference is meaningful but limited: it removes unnecessary scan claims in a concrete useful path without changing its source/reader behavior. No actual numerical all-owner resource fit or measured total efficiency is established. Qualification of these costs is already routed through [the readiness module](../qualification-readiness/README.md); this note does not commission another generic benchmark plan.

## Large-table sparse case and recurrence defeat

For a separate declared arithmetic case, retain the same maximum M262144, r32 and candidate shared20000 execution-count ceiling used in the periodic exclusion. Choose h=1/4 second and supplied w=1/8 second, so C8192 and `C*h+w=2048.125 < 3600`. This is a supplied same-offer one-hour certificate for **both** calendars in this case, not a measured32-slot service time or an unexplained carryover of the small case's clock. Let H86400 and grid indices1..345599; the last appointment86399.75 finishes by86399.875. Fix adoption at0 and tau10000, with all adopted leads due10000, and close further installation admission before that due window. Any full adoption/removal costs and existing old state remain real.

W can cover that one known cohort with8192 activated scans at10000,10000.25,...,12047.75, last finish12047.875. Those scans perform262144 slot inspections and can inspect every fixed slot once because M is divisible by r. The scan count alone is below20000; at most11808 executions remain for every other counted task, not a proof they suffice. Uniform U, even allowed the same earliest complete drain, must start40000 jobs by10000 and48191 through12047.75, already exceeding20000. There is no need to deny U early drain or rely only on full-day polling to expose this particular count obstruction. U's fixed phase may remove some individual leads earlier; no exact query-by-query equivalence at this large case is claimed.

This is only a **scan-count repair**. Reserving8192 rounds of worst-case actual removal work and retained state, dispatching them at four per second, the finite calendar's345599 entries, complete adoption, preparation and settlement can fail other resource axes. A smaller calendar episode or another paid representation may be preferable; neither is required to establish the narrow counterexample to a uniform-polling necessity claim.

The same recipe can fail with very sparse but recurrent installations. Keep fixed M/r/h/w/tau and enough horizon/admission time; offer three otherwise admissible known installations with a0=0, a1=10000, a2=20000, hence due10000/20000/30000. At most two live source leads suffice; exact service could be much cheaper than scanning the large fixed table. Their C-cell windows are disjoint, so this W recipe requires3*8192=24576 scans before other jobs. It must decline some new admission under the unchanged20000 grant. That is not a claim that the network must refuse those theorems, that a cyclic scanner cannot do anything smarter, or that every calendar with a one-hour offer needs three whole sweeps. It excludes this conservative per-installation full-sweep certificate. Multiple simultaneous deadlines share one sweep but can instead fail removal/version burst capacity.

More generally, distinct exact due windows require `|union_i W(e_i)|` reserved jobs, at most C per known exact-time plan before overlaps, plus guard-window widening and failed-preparation reservations. Phase dispersion or dense arrivals can fill much of the grid; savings over uniform scheduling can vanish. An online policy cannot promise acceptance of every future sequence using only the observed prefix and finite stock. Declining safely preserves old obligations but is not sufficient evidence of useful sustained growth.

## Disposition and stopping rule

At the source-draft stage: retain due-window activation as a candidate implementation strategy for TENURE1's already permitted local calendars, with no new wire operation or raised grant. The small fixed workload has a same-path scan-count reduction; the large isolated cohort removes one periodic count obstruction; recurrence and planning/burst costs remain explicit defeats. Independent whole-source reviews must check this exact scope before integration.

Do not select TENURE1 or its tenure constant from these counts. Do not immediately design a heap: the current question is answered if the scoped count comparison survives review. Reopen a different calendar/index only for a specified workload and consequential residual obstacle, with the same admission/service/accounting comparisons. Useful default tuning may be heuristic, and no universal theorem of optimal usefulness is required. Conversely, supplied service times and repeated paper reviews cannot establish actual Lean interoperability, trustworthy implemented checking, all-owner capacity or beneficial long-run mathematical growth. All six native/eight reciprocal runtime gates remain NOT RUN.
