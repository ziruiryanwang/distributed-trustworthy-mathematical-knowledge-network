The narrower question investigated here was **which newly checked derived roots enter that explicitly authorized public cut, with what paid protection and exposure opportunity**. The [reciprocal evidence plan](../qualification-readiness/reciprocal-evidence-plan.md) requires funded re-contribution and later independent use but is not a live egress selector.

Minimal all-honest case: an agent checks P, uses it to derive C, and credits P's cache signal. C remains private under short staging/cache authority and later expires without an admitted publication request. Another agent could use C but cannot find/acquire it. Every selected safety contract may hold. This is not a violated sustained-growth guarantee; it identifies an optional software decision that can increase opportunities for reciprocal contribution.

## Required countercases and exit

- A genuine checked new root is selected, its whole actual replay closure is authorized for disclosure and protected, its native service/index is activated, and a separately funded receiver discovers, acquires, checks and uses it before the declared opportunity ends.
- The only exposure slot is occupied. Selecting a new root cannot release the old owner's pin, erase an anchor or pretend a smaller eventual set fits its larger physical transition.
- A public theorem depends on undisclosable exact proof/support bytes. Statement-level permission alone cannot publish the complete replay.
- An export or snapshot admitted near the service boundary remains charged through its real completion, revocation or reconciliation. Exposure end is not immediate reclamation or recall of disclosed bytes.
- Exact-ID duplicates, distinct cheap logical variants and delayed useful results require different observations. Neither a new ID nor high self-use establishes mathematical novelty, independent demand or benefit.

Concept exit requires a determinate finite nomination/selection rule, actual authority/resource bindings through exposure and retirement, explicit incomplete/uncertain outcomes, a nonvacuous use trace, an ordinary defeat and independent review of the same source cut. Heuristic quality may remain empirical; correctness cannot rely on that quality being good.
