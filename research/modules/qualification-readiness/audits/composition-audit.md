## Additional composition checks

### Positive controls and outcome denominators

The joined positive native milestone requires complete capture/bootstrap, accepted intended roots with the stated correspondence, and an actual predeclared local application plus receiving replay of its derived result. A collection of correct refusals cannot satisfy it. NQ-GROUP/REPAIR are coverage facets where exercised or claimed, not fictitious stand-alone stages which each charge all common work again.

RQ-LOCAL requires nonzero useful local capability before a network-efficacy claim. Its failed floor cannot be repaired by deleting difficult workload families after outcomes: the later study freezes its pool, floor, strata and decision rules in advance, while every assigned opportunity remains in the main denominator. Unsupported helpers, denied disclosure, unavailable sources, failed applications and ordinary bounded stops are scored nonsuccesses. The held integration card and both plans agree that such noncompletion can still satisfy a safety/refusal expectation.

An incomplete or corrupted observation can be inconclusive about the intended mechanism or causal estimate. That does not erase attempted work or costs, impute success, or turn a theorem into a false statement. The reciprocal plan requires a frozen conservative missing-data treatment or an inconclusive inference. Capability/native/access-fidelity failures withdraw their corresponding stronger claim rather than silently selecting a favorable subset. Changing a budget or intake creates a separately reported attempt.

### Actual arguments and optional coverage

Root flagged an earlier draft's unused-argument wording before this held-cut audit. The reviewed RQ-ENDPOINT now explicitly rejects an **unassigned effective binder or incomplete application**, while permitting a fully supplied argument that the checked term does not computationally use. This matches the selected J/VIEWS1 boundary; operational use of every argument is not a native-validity requirement. No earlier draft was certified by this review.

Folded/generic-helper misses remain declared finite-policy limitations and denominator losses, not a new safety defect or a requirement that the first pilot solve universal discovery. Optional broker/VIEWS1 comparisons need their own on/off scope; they do not become prerequisites for a direct native-reuse claim. Exact formal evaluation obligations remain observer instruments, never public network tasks or human-intention acceptance tests.

### Control-plane and resource evidence

Native success does not certify codec/runtime isolation, durable authority, clock/restart behavior or network service. The card keeps those orthogonal and binds existing V2/NET2/custody/I/retry obligations rather than inventing another recovery protocol. Unknown authority cannot be positive qualification; lost replies do not refund resource spending; allowed value-CAS ABA is not tested against an unselected fence; snapshot retirement is not reply-spool reclamation.

A small honest-service pilot can make a matching conditional claim. It cannot inherit hostile-network or crash-recovery assurance from the native tests. Both plans retain actual cold checks, reconstruction working sets, failed branches, carry-in work, source service, publication, custody and stock-time/peak constraints. Numeric ceilings and illustrative daily arithmetic are not resource supply or a measured service rate.

### Causal scope and historical machinery

The reciprocal plan carries forward the historical evidence discipline, not historical blackboards, work-item APIs, active coordination, topology or numerical thresholds. It freezes solver/model policies and exogenous inputs within each pair without forcing endogenous descendants to match. Blind filtering covers alternate leads, caches and leaking dependent closures; uncharacterized bypasses leave the causal claim inconclusive. Independent recomputation is not automatically contamination.

Future-task content stays inaccessible until both corpus cuts and their read/discovery interfaces are sealed. Evaluation outputs do not leak into another opportunity or arm. Shared queues, budgets, caches, participants and descendants keep the connected workload in one interference unit; theorem-edge resampling cannot manufacture independent samples. Named finite decisions separate useful-yield, resource/resilience, null/harm and inconclusive outcomes. A negative utility effect is not native unsoundness, and a positive finite effect is not exponential or indefinite growth.
