# Native qualification readiness: exact evidence handoff

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: proposed evidence contract for independent review. **Every observed-result and qualification-status field is currently NOT RUN.** No implementation, collection/extraction, build, native execution, executable model, measurement or benchmark is performed or authorized here. Only this new research note is authored; frozen H/I/J sources remain unchanged.

## Overlap check and narrow delta

[BOOT1](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-foundation-bootstrap-contract.md#selected-boot1-derivation-algorithm) already defines full seed collection, literal-aware ordering, checked replay, exact generated inventory and the noncircular commitment order. [EXTRACT1](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-complete-export-and-group-recovery.md) already defines complete source cuts, semantic/instance maps, group recovery, cache boundaries and distinct export/replay/correspondence outcomes. [REDUCE1](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-proof-computation-and-reconstruction.md#required-future-conformance-cases) already specifies proof-sensitive conversion and checked-repair conformance cases. Their algorithms and case lists are not replaced or repeated here.

The concrete handoff gap is how an actual qualification report would join those stages without treating an exporter flag, an independently valid but different target, or correct refusal of every input as usable operation. This note adds only a documentary acceptance matrix and cross-stage evidence bindings. It supplies no new native predicate, codec, wire record, foundation rule or qualification authority. Existing required cases remain required; a bounded successful slice is not qualification of all inherited release obligations.

Scope: AS-03/04/05 and their AS-01/06/07/09/11 dependencies in [ASSUMPTIONS](../../registry/ASSUMPTIONS.md); [IF-BOOT, IF-RECONSTRUCT and IF-USE](../../registry/INTERFACES.md). Native acceptance remains conditional on the trusted basis. A successful finite run cannot prove kernel consistency, correct implementation on all inputs or practical usefulness across mathematics.

## Verdicts that do not reward vacuous refusal

Record the native/source operation's actual result separately from whether the **expected fixture observation** was obtained. A negative fixture whose intended safe refusal occurs can pass its conformance check while producing no usable result. It cannot enter a positive-success count.

For a declared finite positive slice, the joined handoff requires: NQ-CUT and NQ-BOOT completion; accepted exact intended roots with all correspondence predicates explicitly required for that slice established; and at least one predeclared NQ-USE application followed by receiving replay of its derived output. NQ-GROUP/REPAIR observations bind the cases actually exercised and remain mandatory for claims about their coverage. This finite milestone does not qualify every Lean declaration, every frontend operation or the whole network.

If a correctly identified positive fixture is attempted and hits its admitted memory/time/attempt bound, it is an **honest failed completion under that envelope**, with mathematical nondecision and actual cost. It remains in the denominator. Native rejection or a declared correspondence mismatch likewise fails the relevant positive objective without refuting its proposition. Do not rescue a claimed success by silently narrowing the scope, adding assumptions, changing the goal or forgetting failed plans.

An observation can be **inconclusive about its intended mechanism** when required setup/evidence is absent or corrupted, an integrity failure makes the result untrustworthy, or interruption prevents distinguishing the hypothesized mismatch from another cause. Record that uncertainty and mark the claim not qualified. It does not erase an attempted workload from end-to-end accounting; NOT RUN before authorization is different from an attempted noncompletion. A changed intake/budget is a separately reported attempt, not retroactive success at the earlier cut.

## Missing supply, consumers and stopping rules

Presently missing are the actual matched full-private/captured source corpus; complete seed inventory and successful literal-aware order; an implemented/qualified inert codec/adapter/image; exact native output artifacts; and admitted hardware, memory/storage, CPU/time and collection/checking budgets. Existing illustrative bounds do not establish that even one cold foundation or library slice fits. These absences are not failures of tests that ran.

The reciprocal plan consumes exact usable roots and new derived results, not a bare “Lean works” label. It must separately establish discovery exposure, authorized possibly multi-source acquisition, publication, custody and downstream applications. Resource evaluation consumes actual bytes and work: library/source size is not native working-set size, and present bytes are not a free native check. Long-term survival, useful yield and growth require their own evidence.

Runtime isolation, OS/hardware integrity, local authority/storage/clock recovery and network-service conformance are **orthogonal gates**. The input report records the actual environment and its assurance limits; successful NQ fixtures under that build do not qualify those other mechanisms. Conversely, a correct recovery/refusal demonstration supplies no missing positive native application.

Stop the affected qualification claim for missing/unsafe bindings, a full-seed obstruction, unavailable required bodies, unsupported scope or actual budget exhaustion. Preserve scoped prior outputs only under their ordinary protection/current-use guards. Reopen concept design only for a concrete contradiction or necessary change to the selected pin, stage rule, grammar, native interpretation, generated correspondence or acceptance boundary; document a new amendment instead of editing frozen sources. Do not reopen it merely because execution is still unauthorized, a broader library remains untested or a finite heuristic missed a repair.

The completion of this evidence plan is a reviewed handoff specification. The completion of native qualification would require the actual separately authorized evidence above; no further editorial agreement can stand in for it.
