## Missing binding, not a new network regime

[CP-001C growth analysis](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001C-growth-analysis.md) already distinguishes causal future benefit from proof/citation volume, and the [historical testbed](../../COOPERATION-TESTBED.md) specifies access blinding, sealed future tasks, complete-replicate inference and full costs. The missing item is a versioned binding from those evaluation obligations to **current V6 native/candidate operations plus optional J/VIEWS1**, with executable evidence prerequisites and finite claim decisions.

This plan supplies that binding at document level. It neither selects the historical five-cell topology, `PublicationHint`, `CoordinationScope`, work-item/reliance-query APIs, signed-support machinery, simulator nor active-coordination ladder as the current network. Their exact numerical defaults remain historical testbed choices. A later study must select and review its own fully instantiated record before execution; unfilled choices below mean **not ready to run**. No public mathematical-task registry, common research goal, human-intention acceptance test or mandatory evaluation service is introduced.

The network accepts exact mathematics under its selected foundation and current-use predicates. An optional external observer may score completion of preregistered **exact formal obligations**, not whether a proof matches a producer's intention. A changed formal statement/assumption set cannot count as the original endpoint, even when its proof is valid. Observer weights, hidden labels and ground-truth answers never become native acceptance or live scheduling authority.

## Treatment contract

A later study names the contrasts it claims; it need not run every mechanism comparison. Within each pair, freeze the same agent/model/tool policies, initial permitted corpus, foundation/checker, participant resource assignments, exact opportunity releases, fault distribution and coupled random-tape rule. Couple exogenous inputs, not outcomes after access changes. No arm gets a weaker local solver, free helper, extra hardware/custodian, free source service or wider total ceiling. Actual spending may differ and is an outcome.

| Arm / contrast | Exactly changed access or policy | Permitted interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `I` isolated capability; `L` local retained workspace | Same native-capable local solver; `I` lacks persistent result memory, `L` retains its own admitted work. Neither receives later cross-owner results. | Capability floor and optional local-memory effect; useful local work must be nonvacuous before a network-efficacy claim. |
| `N` passive native sharing versus `L` | Other owners' eligible results become discoverable through selected native STRUCT2 contacts, independently acquired through actual scopes and applied through BRIDGE1. No remote research assignment. | Total passive-sharing effect, including discovery, custody and checking costs. |
| `B` versus `N` | Replace the specified metadata contact role with selected capability-9 direct broker service; actual evidence custody/service remains fixed and separately funded. J is off in both. | Broker-role effect. Use a matched token-only corpus/contact/ordering comparison; separately compare the strongest applicable funded native pattern-filtering baseline. If corpus, ordering, custody or resources also change, label the bundle, not a pure broker effect. |
| `V(X)` versus `X`, for fixed `X=N` or `B` | Enable the exact J/VIEWS1 policy; all view/map/helper work consumes the same parent allocation as the control's allowed local search. No answer identifiers are added. | Incremental unfamiliar-interface policy effect, not the effect of extra resources. Do not silently mix native and broker variants. |
| `X-blind` versus the named sharing arm `X` | Before outcomes, choose eligible publication cohorts/consumer boundaries. Filter their access at the consumer's metadata/read interface, including alternate leads, prefilled caches and dependent closures that would leak the withheld information. Production, validation, retention and publication attempts continue and are charged. | Total effect of access, including changed downstream traffic/work. Downstream production may legitimately diverge. Own independent recomputation is not leakage. An optional content-only effect needs a separately defined paid shadow-traffic intervention. |
| `X-static`, `X-continuing`, `X-continuing-blind` | Same admitted prehistory. Static exposes only that sealed cross-owner snapshot; continuing permits later outputs through ordinary publication/re-admission; blinded continuing blocks the preregistered later cohorts at selected consumers. | Marginal ongoing-circulation effect. No forced matching of endogenous descendants; no claim of individual additive credit. |

Blind eligibility uses committed operational publication/cohort rules, not a post-hoc list of successful theorem IDs or political authorship. Freeze exact filter placement, dependency treatment, refusal/absence behavior and treatment-fidelity checks. Filtering is observer-side experimental control, not a protocol opcode or a remote instruction to change policy. It cannot grant scope, erase checks or smuggle hidden labels into a client. If bypasses cannot be excluded or characterized, the causal contrast is inconclusive rather than evidence of no benefit.

For unfamiliar-interface claims, initial investigator inputs contain only the selected local F/E/context, B/operation/problem, owner-approved contacts and resources. The target A, map/helpers and useful theorem identifiers or equivalent answer-bearing aliases are absent. Corpus builders' answer keys remain inaccessible; training/existing-memory exposure is recorded and balanced. Do not preselect only positive pages or force target placement after observing runs. Declare rank/order, representation and availability strata in advance, including ordinary folded/generic helpers, decoys, missing reflection and large cold closures. A known-ID acquisition control is useful diagnostics, not unknown-statement discovery evidence.

## Observation unit, secrecy and interference

An opportunity is a receiver-private exact formal obligation with fixed release, deadline, scoring-cut rule and allocation. Define online `A` and future-task `H` as weighted fractions of successfully completed opportunities in their respective preregistered pools, with nonnegative weights and strictly positive fixed denominators. Every assigned opportunity remains, including unsupported syntax, no useful candidate, denied disclosure, unavailable evidence, resource stops and failed applications. Report unweighted counts and prespecified family/representation/cold-warm strata alongside weights. Do not condition the main denominator on successful discovery or survival.

Future exact obligations are committed before outcomes but withheld from producers, workers and tuning until all compared autonomous corpus cuts **and their discoverable/read interfaces** are sealed. Read-only evaluation uses common evaluator policies and allocations; its generated artifacts cannot enter another evaluation opportunity or arm. The sealer does not create a central runtime corpus: exact per-owner cuts and actual interface availability are enough. A consumer gets its own formal question and budget at release, never hidden weights, causal labels or answer maps. Measurement-only relabeling must leave participant-visible actions unchanged; the named access intervention intentionally changes accessible information and is not covered by that invariance.

Default causal/inference unit is a complete paired world replicate: all independent investigators, shared descendants, caches, native sources, brokers, queues, budgets, faults and later evaluations needed by the contrast. Worlds have isolated mutable state and no cross-arm training/memory/service-budget spillover. An entire communication/dependency-connected workload is one interference block. A smaller block is allowed only with a preregistered demonstration that no resource, information, participant or measurement interference crosses it. Do not randomize/resample individual theorem edges or tasks as though independent.

For a fixed-suite claim, resample whole complete stochastic replicates with paired arm differences. For population inference, independently generated workload instances are outer clusters; repeated seeds of one connected workload do not create new populations. Freeze replicate/instance counts, confidence method, multiplicity family, practical-effect/noninferiority margins, finite horizon, stopping/missing-data rules and random-tape coupling before confirmatory work, using excluded pilot evidence where necessary. No optional extension until significance. There is no numerical sample or margin selection in this document and hence no ready-to-run statistical protocol yet.

## All-cost comparison and finite disposition

Use equal componentwise whole-arm ceilings, with explicit per-owner allocations and scheduling/service assumptions. Include agent/model tokens and accelerator/CPU work; native reconstruction/checking; actual resident and peak memory; retained/staging/control stock and byte-time through the horizon; metadata/index/custody maintenance; publication, source serving, network/setup/traffic, failed probes/retries and uncertainty settlement; repair and operator effort; elapsed opportunity and episode time. Warm local reuse and missing network bytes do not remove native qualification obligations. Charge common prehistory transparently, incremental construction/publication once, and finite carry-in work credited to later completions. Count a physically shared object once locally but actual independent copies separately. No hidden price vector combines these dimensions into mathematical value.

Report offered, admitted, attempted, completed, refused, shed and censored work, with actual failure stage; retain all costs and exposure/retention/deletion intervals. Technical data loss is not silently imputed as success or removed: apply the frozen conservative bound/missingness rule, or declare the affected inference inconclusive. Ordinary bounded nondecision is a scored nonsuccess, not missing data or theorem falsity. Publication/custody costs are not recovered merely because an artifact is later deleted. New sources, pages, views, keys or generations do not replenish a parent allocation. A larger resource trajectory is a separate bundled treatment.

|---|---|
| Qualification failure | Any observed invalid acceptance, wrong formal endpoint counted as fulfilled, current-use bypass, hidden-label/answer contamination, scope violation or resource-conservation breach fails the corresponding overall qualification claim, regardless of yield. Diagnose the exact scope; zero observed failures would not prove zero error. |
| Supported useful-yield claim | Relevant gates pass. | Evidence against the specified gain | Valid finite evidence has an adjusted upper bound at or below the prespecified minimum worthwhile gain, or demonstrates harm under the declared rule. Report the affected workload/mechanism and distinction between no worthwhile gain and negative effect. Utility loss is not a soundness failure. |
| Inconclusive / not qualified | Intervals cross decision boundaries, sample/exposure is inadequate, capability/native gates lack evidence, treatment cannot be isolated, or conservative data bounds cannot decide. No significant result is not a proof of equality. Unperformed gates remain NOT RUN, not failed tests. |
