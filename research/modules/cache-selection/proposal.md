# CACHESEL1: bounded owner-local discretionary warm-cache selection

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: authored optional-policy proposal; independent signal and resource/lifecycle reviews pending. No implementation, native execution, extraction, executable model, experiment or benchmark. Constants below select one finite paper policy, not optimal values or measured Lean capacity.

## Scope and inherited authority

[V6](../../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V6.md) remains the network/native baseline. This policy fills [ORG1's nomination-to-term-decision seam](../../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V1-ORGANIZATION.md#retention-and-index-maintenance-policy), using current PI1/schema-3 `(ProofInstanceID, InstanceReplayID)` evidence bindings. It selects across exact judgments; [P4](../../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V1-ORGANIZATION.md#deterministic-automatic-selection-p4) still selects their proof alternatives. No wire change, global value score, public task, mathematical authority or human-intention field is added.

Only owner-enrolled, already locally checked proof families are considered. Nonproof roots can occur as dependencies, not separately ranked candidates here. Independently acquired results need no special producer identity or agreement on research purpose. A remote source key does not prove independent authorship or demand. Hints, query counts, downloads and foreign use claims earn no use credit and initiate no work.

The owner supplies an immutable policy/account binding: allowed native policy, comparable cost profile, finite cache payload/metadata/root limits, whole-node and staging/transition limits, inspection/CPU/I/O/control allocations, and standing interval authority. All sit inside existing accounting. No limit supplies hardware. Cache payload counts the **union of complete closures, including the qualified foundation**; a quota smaller than that foundation can admit nothing. This conservative cache subquota is not a claim that shared physical bytes are duplicated. Whole-node accounting counts actual physical sharing separately.

Durable custody, seed pins, other cache owners, active uses/exports, index snapshots and H publication anchors keep their own protections and costs. CACHESEL1 neither releases them nor changes public eligibility. It authorizes no new publication, route placement, native import/check or maintenance service. Such work requires ordinary separate admission, counted by the owner's aggregate account. The policy can safely return no winner.

## Bounded state and invocation

One fenced owner writer maintains:

- At most **64 family rows**, one per exact JudgmentID: enrollment serial, observation lower boundary, three use bins, current P4 version/bundle of at most four exact evidence pairs, current qualification/closure references, measured-cost reference, a first-term nomination generation/state, any CACHESEL1 term/reservation and last reason.
- At most **64 pending observation records**, a durable global consumed-completion cursor and overflow boundary, plus one exploration position. These are not per-publisher tables.
- At most **16 active or reserved family-term allocations**, counting outstanding future renewals separately, and one decision in flight. Settling/uncertain records remain counted. Required fences cannot be evicted for space.

One owner-funded 24-hour interval admits at most **64 nomination attempts and four decision rounds**. A round processes at most 64 observation records, considers 64 rows and proposes at most four families. All rounds share the supplied interval account; each additionally stops at 2 CPU-seconds, 10 qualified local seconds, 16 MiB selector working state, 256 candidate-fit evaluations and 65,536 aggregate closure/reference inspections. Before round admission reserve bounded finalization, outcome/fence retention and recovery capacity from the same parent account. Planning/commit may use only the remaining admitted work/deadline allowance. Persistent metadata has its own supplied bound inside whole-node memory/storage. Smaller parent limits prevail; incomplete inspection never means zero cost.

These are maximum opportunities, not timers. Only an explicit owner call or separately admitted standing local caller invokes a round. Signals and expiry create no callback or grant. Interval boundaries follow a fixed qualified local origin; unused opportunities do not accumulate. New keys, policy versions or reboots do not reset expenditures. Later funding needs existing standing authority and demonstrable interval continuity, or fresh explicit admission.

Nomination is an owner-local paid request naming a completed, currently applicable P4 record and exact locally present evidence. No P4 recomputation or recheck is hidden here. If that prerequisite needs work, return `NEEDS_P4_OR_NATIVE_WORK`. An existing JudgmentID row is updated in place; repeated nomination changes neither enrollment age, observation boundary nor deadlines. A new row occupies a free slot, otherwise replaces the oldest enrolled row having no live/reserved/unsettled CACHESEL1 term, nonterminal nomination or decision reference; tie by JudgmentID. With none available, decline. Row removal releases only expendable selector metadata, not mathematical protection or required replay fences. A new row starts with zero recorded credits and an observation lower boundary equal to the owner's latest committed application serial.

A successful explicit nomination can arm exactly one first-term token `(rowEnrollmentSerial, nominationGeneration, exactP4BundleVersion)` only with no live/reserved/unsettled CACHESEL1 term or prior claim. Its states are `ARMED`, `CLAIMED(roundID)`, `TERMINAL(outcome)` and `FENCED_UNKNOWN`. Replaying the same request returns its recorded result; a distinct request while ARMED does not create another token or revise its bundle. After terminal settlement, another explicit paid nomination may advance the checked generation and rearm, preserving the row's age, observation cutoff/history and all cumulative spending. Requests during a live term cannot arm first-term eligibility or reset its renewal decision. Metadata refresh is not rearming. Row presence or absence of a handle after expiry is never sufficient authorization.

## Actual-use signal, duplication and aging

A qualifying observation is one **new, successfully committed owner-local checked application**, under the allowed native policy, with complete current evidence. Its receiver-verified application record identifies exact reused proof/replay inputs. Credit at most four distinct enrolled JudgmentIDs among those verified inputs, in canonical JudgmentID order, once per family for that application. More inputs are deliberately omitted. Do not traverse an unbounded descendant graph. An import/check by itself, failed application or claimed dependency is not a use event. Syntactic checked premise participation is not logical necessity: an application can contain a redundant premise.

The local finalization ledger assigns a strictly increasing durable completion serial to each genuinely new application settlement. Duplicate delivery/finalization of the same application retains its original serial. The optional observation is appended **only at that once-only local commit**, with its clock generation, completion tick and bounded verified family/evidence bindings, under reserved observer work. Callbacks cannot reinsert it. If observer resources are unavailable, omit the signal and record bounded loss telemetry; do not rerun the application.

The queue keeps the latest 64 records. Overwriting unconsumed records advances a durable `droppedThrough` boundary. Processing is increasing-serial order: atomically apply one record's eligible credits and advance the consumed cursor; records at or below the consumed/dropped boundary are never credited later. A missing range is skipped as lost signal, not reconstructed from requests. Credit only rows enrolled before that event's serial, still having its exact JudgmentID/native interpretation. Thus eviction and re-entry cannot replay pending old credits. Bundle changes within that judgment do not multiply credits; costs remain bound to the actual new bundle. Queue/cursor mutation and settlement are charged atomic local state operations.

The bounded observation retains the actual used proof/replay binding while it exists. Its judgment-level credit is only a heuristic: use of an old witness does not establish that a cheaper same-judgment P4 replacement supports that caller's realization, conversion or footprint. Selecting a replacement never claims preservation of earlier exact-replay hits or applications. Every later application needs its actual evidence/compatibility checks; discarded diagnostic history cannot be reconstructed from the score.

Use three fixed 24-hour bins in one qualified clock generation. Each family/bin saturates at **three** distinct eligible applications. At decision time, let `c0,c1,c2` be recorded counts in the current and two preceding bins; older bins contribute zero. Rank signal is `u = 4*c0 + 2*c1 + c2`, between 0 and 21. Rotate/age lazily during paid observation/decision work, using completion time rather than receipt time. Zero means no retained credited history, not no demand. Truly separate cheap applications may saturate every bin; they consume real work but do not establish unique users, independent demand or causal benefit. Duplicate deliveries contribute nothing additional.

All serial/bin/deadline arithmetic is checked before increment/addition. Exhaustion stops the affected policy; no wraparound. Cursor/dropped boundaries survive candidate eviction and clean service rollover. After loss of retention-clock continuity, discard old-generation bins/queued signals without credit and conservatively expire old warm terms under NET1; preserve durable consumption and spending fences. If cursor/ledger continuity itself is unestablished, stop and reconcile under inherited recovery—never restart from zero. A newly authorized observer generation begins after the reconciled current completion serial, not by replaying history.

## Exact candidate choice

A row can participate only through an ARMED first-term token or its sole renewal decision in the **last hour before its current expiry**. Non-due live terms cannot be topped up. All exact evidence must still be checked, compatible and completely present in usable physical generations under an existing funded staging/cache/protection handle. Missing bytes, lost checking guards or an already deletion-claimed generation give a scoped nondecision; selection does not fetch or resurrect them.

A family is one indivisible current P4 bundle, not four competing score entries. If P4 changes while a CACHESEL1 term/reservation protects its older bundle, defer that family until those CACHESEL1 obligations settle; neither selection nor a better witness permits early release. Other protections survive afterward. This avoids accumulating extra automatic alternatives over P4's four-root limit. A family that cannot fit its whole selected bundle is skipped; choosing a smaller alternative subset is not this policy.

The round phases are `OBSERVE -> CLAIM -> PLAN -> COMMIT -> FINALIZE`. After bounded observation processing, atomically freeze the row/cost/P4/resource snapshot and claim its ARMED first-term tokens and unclaimed due-term decisions for this round, before testing eligibility. A prior claim excludes that token/decision. Complete each claimed outcome as committed or terminally declined/stopped, including nonwinners and failed prerequisites; uncertainty stays fenced to this round. Never restore ARMED on failure. An interruption before the atomic CLAIM changes no unclaimed eligibility; after it, another round cannot reuse those claims. Cost rank is the **largest measured cold replay CPU among the bundle's roots**, under the fixed comparable profile; missing or mismatched measurements rank after measured bundles. This is a heuristic burden indicator, not total actual checking cost or predicted savings. Actual work is accounted independently.

Make four passes, committing nothing while planning:

1. **Exploration:** one slot considers only `u=0` actionable families, circularly after the persistent enrollment-serial position. Inspect at most all 64 rows, advancing the position for every inspected row, including failures. Select the first completely feasible family. An empty/infeasible lane leaves this slot unused; it is not transferred to popularity selection.
2. **Reuse:** for each of three slots, reevaluate all remaining actionable families against the already proposed winners. Among completely feasible candidates, order by decreasing `u`, measured before unmeasured cost, increasing measured CPU, increasing exact marginal cache-union payload, increasing incremental metadata, enrollment serial, then JudgmentID. Select the first; stop if none fits.

Use bounded deterministic insertion comparisons over at most 64 rows; all fit passes share the inspection/CPU allowance. Never reuse an earlier marginal value after adding a winner. Advance the exploration position durably even when its selection fails; new nominations do not reset it. This offers a conditional low-history opportunity within the retained window, not fairness or survival for every topic or adversarial arrival stream. Admission churn, cost and lost observations can defeat useful exploration.

An explicitly incomplete/unavailable candidate inventory yields a completed candidate-local `UNKNOWN_FIT` skip while allowance remains. A proved capacity failure is likewise a completed skip. In contrast, aggregate work/deadline exhaustion or interruption before the prescribed passes finish yields `PLAN_INCOMPLETE`: discard **all** inert proposals, commit no terms, and settle claimed tokens/renewal decisions as spent. PLAN completes only after exploration selects its first feasible row or exhausts its scan, and each reuse pass finishes its comparisons or a completed no-feasible-candidate pass ends selection. Only `PLAN_COMPLETE` enters COMMIT. Reserved finalization records the stop; if hard failure prevents it, recovery only reconciles/fences that round, never resumes its term commits.

## Fit, terms and commitment are different operations

For every proposed family compute exact closure unions and per-root/reference/index/control costs. There are **two required fits**:

- **Term fit:** at every start/end breakpoint of existing and proposed active/reserved cache terms, their complete payload union, metadata, roots and funded term-related obligations fit the cache subquota. Existing nonselected terms still count until their actual ends; future renewal reservations count before they start and reserve capacity over their full interval. Unknown or longer obligations are conservatively included. Zero marginal payload still adds handles/metadata/work.
- **Physical transition fit:** whole-node currently protected, staged, reserved, retiring and GC-pending generations, all other-role allocations, selector state and proposed transition coexist within real aggregate stock/work limits. An eventual smaller retained set does not fund its larger transition. Physical deletion/reclamation is not assumed at a term boundary.

Metadata discovery or retention does not automatically entitle a candidate to public indexing. Existing H anchors and borrowed coverage dependencies remain in the physical/protection accounting regardless of the selected set. Any index retention/removal affects its own exact scope through inherited admitted operations; this policy cannot silently advertise stale eligibility.

At planning, `u=0` proposes a **one-day probation** term; positive `u` proposes a **seven-day reusable-cache** term. For a first term, start is its actual successful commit tick. A renewal must commit before the old expiry; its fixed interval is `[oldExpiry, oldExpiry + chosenDuration)`, in the unchanged retention-clock generation. Record the old term's one renewal decision even if declined or failed. Uses, callbacks, clean service rollover and late replies never move deadlines. Each new term can face its own later once-only renewal decision, funded anew; this is not an infinite promise. After an expiry, later admission is a fresh explicit nomination/term under current resources, not delayed renewal or a free retry.

After PLAN_COMPLETE, commit proposed winners sequentially in selected order. Before each, atomically revalidate the exact row/enrollment/nomination-or-renewal claim bound to this round, P4/cost/protection versions, current native/clock guards, start/end feasibility and **both** complete fits including earlier commits. Reserve all term and transition allocations before installing that exact cache handle and recording the immutable terminal nomination/decision outcome. A stale plan, late renewal, interruption or unavailable resource stops remaining commits with explicit partial status; no automatic replan. First-term projected intervals are recomputed from actual commit time, not an earlier planning timestamp. Successful earlier terms remain valid under their own guards; uncertain commits remain fenced until reconciled, and uncommitted claims are not rearmed.

Selected membership is not eviction authority. Nonselection merely denies new CACHESEL1 authority. Existing terms run to their original expiry; this policy selects no early eviction. At expiry the inherited paid, version-checked transition removes only that term's cache handle, updates availability/index state and settles its allocations. Active exports/uses, durable owners, snapshots and anchors can keep every byte alive. Releasing a handle does not report its full closure as reclaimed.

NET1/NET2 generation-aware GC remains mandatory. A deletion-claimed generation is not free reusable dedup because its bytes still appear on disk: use an inherited permissible preclaim cancellation or a newly staged, hashed and protected generation through separately admitted work. Count overlap until actual reclamation. An old eight-unit allocation plus a disjoint new six-unit candidate needs fourteen transition units; a ten-unit available limit forbids that transition despite a six-unit final set. The selector does not delete the incumbent first.

## Outcomes, failure and recovery

Return a bounded record naming policy/account, snapshot, cursor/coverage loss, considered rows, proposal/commit order, scores/cost provenance, union/resource deltas, actual term IDs/deadlines and paid stops. Distinguish `COMMITTED`, `NO_FEASIBLE_CANDIDATE`, `NOT_ARMED`, `NOT_DUE`, `P4_CHANGED`, `NEEDS_P4_OR_NATIVE_WORK`, candidate `UNKNOWN_FIT`, round `PLAN_INCOMPLETE`, `DECLINED_RESOURCE`, `STALE_PLAN`, `LATE_RENEWAL`, `PARTIAL_COMMIT` and `FENCED_UNKNOWN`. None means mathematical falsity or global uselessness.

Observation consumption, interval counters, renewal decisions and commit outcomes use the existing durable one-writer/replay discipline. An interrupted decision consumes its start/work allowance. An uncertain commit is reconciled by its original identity while reservations remain counted; it cannot create another term. If settlement/expiry/GC resources cannot be reserved, admit no new term. Required recovery/retiring state is retained even when optional candidate history is dropped. No restart, table eviction or new policy hash refills capacity or recredits events. Clean service rollover preserves warm deadlines; actual clock loss does not preserve warm validity or refund physical obligations.

**Conditional invariants.** Under the inherited durable writer/ledger premises, atomic serial/drop/enrollment fences credit each event-family pair at most once. Saturating bins imply `0 <= u <= 21`. Four proposals per round and four funded rounds cannot bypass the independent term-slot, interval-union and physical-transition fits. Complete planning is not authority: each actual commit still enforces native, owner and resource guards, while expiry removes only its permitted cache handle. Thus heuristic order changes retention choices, not mathematical acceptance or other owners' protection. None of these invariants establishes usefulness.

## Two numeric set-selection cases

These are invented accounting units, not measurements. Each family has one eligible P4 proof, equal measured CPU, and no current cache term. All have already paid complete checking; their present eligible staging handles remain valid through commitment. Foundation F occupies **two payload units** under its own pin. Residual closures are `A=S+a`, `B=S+b`, with `|S|=7`, `|a|=|b|=1`; C has eight disjoint units. Thus A/B each add eight beyond F, together nine; all three plus F occupy nineteen.

Supply explicitly: cache payload quota **11 including F**, candidate staging quota **17 incremental units beyond F**, whole-node payload **19**, and a separate **one-unit aggregate metadata/control/transition allocation** assumed sufficient for these exact three rows, inventories, handles and outcomes; total storage supply is twenty. Root/term slots and finite inspection/CPU/I/O grants also cover this declared tiny fixture. No other allocations exist. These premises are essential; a real foundation or metadata footprint need not fit. Staging and cache handles share the same qualified generations, not duplicate physical bytes.

| Case | Recorded scores; deterministic choices | Exact result and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Successful shared-set selection | `u(A)=3,u(B)=2,u(C)=1`; no zero-history candidate. Reuse selects A, then recomputes B's marginal payload as one while C remains eight. | Cache `{A,B}` costs `2+7+1+1=11`; C would raise it to 19 and is skipped. During transition all nineteen payload units plus one control unit still fit. C's staging is not deleted by this choice; its original authority must settle normally. |
| Frequency-inflation defeat | Same bytes/costs, but nine genuinely distinct cheap C applications over the three bins give `u(C)=21`; A/B remain 3/2. No duplicate delivery contributed. | C wins first, costing ten including F. Either A or B would raise the cache to eighteen, so both lose. `{A,B}` was feasible and may serve two later exact opportunities while C's repeated use adds little future value. The heuristic can therefore lose useful opportunities; no causal/optimality claim rescues it. |

The first case exercises set-dependent union accounting, not a theorem that greedy selection finds the best set. The second respects every safety bound while defeating frequency as a usefulness proxy. Zero-history exploration can also miss delayed valuable content outside its admitted window. Evaluation must retain those losses, costs and exposure histories under the existing [growth](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001C-growth-analysis.md#how-to-test-compounding-causally) and [current reciprocal-evidence](../qualification-readiness/reciprocal-evidence-plan.md) boundaries. No performance claim, durable-preservation guarantee or automatic release follows from selecting this policy.

## Timed renewal and the full-slot refusal

Separate invented fixture: F occupies two payload units; T adds three; fourteen other one-root families add one each. T's current term is `[day 0, day 7)`; seven others have `[day 5, day 12)` and seven have `[day 6, day 13)`, admitted within those days' ordinary round/slot budgets. Cache/whole-node payload supply is nineteen. Existing control state occupies two units; supply three, with an explicitly reserved 0.2-unit renewal/finalization peak allowance. All native/P4/physical generations remain unchanged, term work fits its finite grant, and no other allocation exists.

At `day 7 - 30 minutes`, T is the only due family, has positive recorded score and an unspent renewal decision. Fifteen term allocations exist. The round claims that decision, finishes PLAN, and commits a seven-day renewal before expiry: its exact interval is `[day 7, day 14)`. The future reservation raises counted terms to sixteen and control stock to at most 2.2, within three. Payload union is nineteen until day 12, twelve until day 13, then five for continuing terms; unreclaimed bytes still fit the nineteen-unit physical ceiling. At day 7 the already committed future interval begins under current guards; removal/settlement of the old handle can reduce counted term allocations only when actually completed. No access moved either endpoint.

For refusal, add a fifteenth other family with `[day 6, day 13)` and explicitly supply twenty payload units plus the same three control units, so sixteen existing terms are themselves valid. Renewing T would require a seventeenth term allocation even though its extra payload is zero. The 16-slot cap rejects it; its sole decision is spent, its old term remains until day 7, and no periodic round rearms first-term eligibility afterward. Only a fresh explicit nomination after all relevant CACHESEL1 obligations settle can authorize another first-term attempt. The cap promises bounded stock, not uninterrupted renewal of sixteen occupants without reservation headroom.
