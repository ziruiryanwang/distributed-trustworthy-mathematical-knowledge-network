## Current research outcome

Five independently reviewed layers now exist: stable groups, a fair four-option comparison, a separate structural catalog, a joint service envelope and a bounded-domain alternative. None selects SG or the catalog. RETAIN already avoids remote movement during pure local layout compaction; actual holder incidence determines group savings; paid adoption and native/maintenance work remain decisive.

For the catalog's stipulated 480-entry / 61,440-member single sweep, sorting, duplicate checks and exact anchor lookup have a 619,199 key-comparison allowance instead of the original 29,491,200 linear-search allowance. This is not an actual-speed ratio or full-resource fit. Native/support dependency visits, actual maps, token checks, posting validation and all bytes/guards remain paid. Old modes can use the same complete catalog with their own predicates. The hypothetical 371,496 scoped-array arithmetic is explicitly outside this recipe; no scoped projection is supplied.

The joint envelope now derives explicit all-owner work/stock/deadline tests. Specified dispersed adoption needs 245,760 calls before other work, above a 196,608-call J-B grant. Dense adoption still needs actual transient holder headroom. A full J480 sweep retains 61,440 member occurrences. The exact 60/15/5 timing case fails under 70-second C changes before expiry at 600 even with a 640-second work allowance. Mandatory completed full rebuilds with the initial e0 rows and every new distinct selected locator row remaining current require at least `n*e0+n*(n+1)/2` current-row visits. These are scoped conditions/counts, not measured performance or a realized J480 corpus; Q484 remains untouched.

The bounded-domain comparison supplies a conditional O(NK) inventory-visit branch only for complete actual inputs and O(N) initial/other/failed attempts. Small selected domains can still require growing exact native closures, extra common-key provider rows and too many unfamiliar-provider probes. Lawful windows cannot defer authoritative invalidation or replace original arrival deadlines. These are different failure axes, not a universal argument for incremental catalogs. Existing selected I already offers candidate-only indexing for known contacts; it does not automatically reproduce G native-pattern filtering or unknown-broker discovery. Next compare those existing roles on complete reciprocal service in the [broker-reuse cycle](../../cycles/2026-09-20-broker-reuse.md). Actual bytes, native/resource/service premises remain unqualified. See [NOW](../../NOW.md) and the [completed domain cycle](../../cycles/2026-09-20-bounded-domains.md). ## Limits that must survive integration

The positive service argument is only named-holder survivor-row/lease continuity under admitted finite service. It is not uninterrupted source coverage, discovery, native availability or sustained growth. Unknown outcomes remain charged; destructive cleanup need not repair the removed theorem's support; current guard invalidation cannot be suppressed by a full repair queue.

The full-scan fallback is finite but potentially expensive. With common inventory size e, summing independently paid group scans includes `G*e` entry traversal and `e*sum(m_g)` anchor-search comparisons, before dependency and byte costs. Zero remote mutations during pure compaction do not establish total efficiency. Adoption from old groups still pays actual owner transfer and possible service interruption.
