## One admitted structural cut

A catalog build names the complete finite immutable semantic inventory C for the provider's current FIND service at fixed F/E/binding, the exact current physical FIND layout R0 implementing that domain, and one retained authoritative guard vector V. R0 denotes a layout, distinct from the total member-occurrence count R used below. This v1 recipe is global only within that **named provider-local current service domain**: it neither requires nor purports to know all network results or all mathematics, and creates no central server or network-wide current-state oracle.

V includes the exact C selection generation, current R0 generation, installed descriptor/extractor/schema identities, the applicable authority/provider support fences, and complete structural publication/disclosure/protection guard versions needed to validate this catalog. Counter continuity and invalidation completeness are inherited obligations, not inferred from unchanged bytes. Required per-anchor current native guards are additionally inspected at each use. This branch conservatively invalidates on a coarse support-fence change even if the particular catalog's structural bytes would remain identical.

A cohort consists of finitely many named support-scan attempts whose structural inputs are **exactly this same cut**, with bounded map/member/dependency inputs and supplied service slots. The cohort list is a local resource schedule, not a network research-task queue. Different F/E, schemas, current C/R0 cuts or incompatible guard vectors require separate builds. C cannot be silently widened or narrowed to obtain a favorable cost. An immutable old owner scope or namespace does not establish a smaller current FIND domain or a current-layout projection. This recipe admits no separate per-scope catalog construction. SG and old groups may use the same complete current catalog while retaining their different per-use predicates. Separately justified old scoped algorithms outside this recipe remain legitimate competitors; this restriction does not require them to scan a global inventory.

The named inventory has a complete finite manifest and exact record enumeration, including selected entries, known excluded/ineligible current entries, tombstones and retained historical records actually traversed. Every enumerated record has an explicit current-or-historical disposition. A historical repetition never becomes a second current witness. Missing inventory pages, incoherent ownership of a record, ambiguous current dispositions or an unaccounted traversal tail make the input incomplete, not an empty set. No assertion about all knowledge in the network is needed.

The complete structural input includes the actual token-to-StructuralDescriptor FIND relation, the relevant descriptor/extractor/predicate/ordering rules, and the immutable physical layout traversal or bounded lookup paths proving that relation. Counting selected tokens or hashing a layout is insufficient. Preparation must establish **both inclusion directions**: every expected selected posting is realized and every realized current posting belongs to the declared relation. Repeated physical occurrences must satisfy the existing exact logical-set and snapshot semantics; they do not manufacture duplicate result rows. The full actual physical traversal, sorting if needed and comparison of expected versus actual posting sets are charged. This contract supplies no free `contains` operation on the layout.

## Exact locator and functional anchor chain

For a retained ProviderAd2/K4 member, define its locator as the complete canonical tuple

`k = (providerNodeID, FoundationID, ExtractorID, anchorPublicDescriptorCID, anchorReplayID)`.

These are the exact signed ProviderAd2 fields and K4 F/E, not a target/token pair, theorem name, shortened digest, claimed equivalent replay or embedding. Provider is the authenticated local producer named by the catalog. The token and actual cell/ad revision are still checked per member; they are not omitted from the support predicate by omitting them from the locator. Multiple legitimate tokens may use one exact anchor.

A catalog row stores k and the complete checked entry tuple

`(targetRef, StructuralDescriptorID, PublicIndexDescriptorID, InstanceReplayID)`,

its exact current structural-selection disposition, its checked at-most-32-token list, and bounded references to the retained canonical descriptor/replay-link records and actual selected-posting witness. The row is **not a native assessment** or cached truth bit.

The G chain checks make this locator functional at the named coherent input:

1. The ad's anchorPublicDescriptorCID names the exact integrated PublicIndexDescriptor3 `0x8111/3`; its structural hint selects exactly the required StructuralDescriptor `0x8124/1`.
2. The structural record fixes the exact F/E, targetRef and anchorReplayID. The public descriptor, ad and structural record agree on that replay, which has InstanceReplay type/schema `0x0015/3`.
3. The replay root and target obey the exact allowed judgment/declaration/opaque/selected-constructor relationship. A convenient same-target proof or another constructor selector cannot be substituted.
4. The complete bytes, typed IDs and relationships are validated under the inherited content-integrity assumptions. Hash collision or corrupt/conflicting bytes are failures of those assumptions or input validation, not permissions to choose a convenient witness.

Consequently, a coherent current row with k determines the full entry tuple. If two supposed current rows have the same k but different chains/dispositions, preparation returns UNKNOWN_INVENTORY. Even identical duplicate current entry rows violate this deliberately unique current-inventory representation and must be resolved before rebuilding; the builder does not choose the first row. Explicit historical/tombstone copies are still visited and charged but do not enter the current-row table. A tombstone that establishes that no current row exists is not itself a positive witness.

This rule intentionally shares one structural row across repeated **exact** anchors. It does not merge two locators sharing target, token, statement, proof body, native truth value or algebraic equivalence. Equality here is byte-exact typed identity under the named installed binding.

## Preparation and finite representation

Catalog states are `BUILDING`, `READY`, `INVALID`, `RETIRING` and `RECLAIMED`, with a never-reused local generation and counted lifecycle record. These are local dispositions, not new wire messages. BUILDING has no authorization value. There is at most the admitted finite number of live/current/building/invalid/unreclaimed catalog generations, under shared node caps; new generations do not each obtain a fresh budget.

The complete paid procedure is:

1. Reserve work, peak storage, pins, a finite build lifetime and aftercare. Capture C/R0/V under the authoritative writer, then pin the immutable inputs. Record all input identities and exact traversal limits before reading. A pin protects bytes; it does not freeze current support or delay incidents.
2. Enumerate the full named inventory and exact current/historical dispositions. Validate typed entry links, complete extraction metadata and token lists, and the exact declared FIND relation against the actual physical R0 relation. Known excluded/ineligible entries are traversed but not promoted. Unrelated dirty native assessments need not be rechecked; no group has yet received a support conclusion.
3. Emit one finite locator row per coherent current entry, including explicitly nonselected current entries needed to reproduce the reference classification. Sort fixed bounded locator slots by the exact canonical tuple. Check adjacent keys and reject ambiguous or duplicate current locators. No LLM ranking or semantic similarity participates in lookup.
4. Verify complete row/payload counts, checked offsets, every expected input consumed, all successful structural checks and total resources actually spent. Under the writer compare C/R0/V and relevant current guards against the captured cut. Only an exact match permits one atomic transition to READY. Any changed guard yields STALE_INPUT; partial output remains charged scratch until lawful reclamation.

READY means only that this exact structural index is complete and coherently validated. It does not mean every row is public/native-eligible, every group is supported, or every future query can still use this generation. A complete empty current inventory yields a READY catalog with zero rows. A missing catalog, an absent input page or a failed build is not that empty catalog.

One concrete bounded local representation makes the lookup cost meaningful: an admitted **resident fixed-width slot array** in exact sorted-key order, plus a retained immutable payload arena. Each slot has bounded key length/bytes, padding and a checked payload offset/length; record maxima come from the installed codecs and declared finite input ceilings. References bind exact catalog and payload generations as well as offsets. All offset arithmetic is checked and nonwrapping. Sorting uses two admitted slot buffers. Payloads may remain in their pinned immutable arena; a slot is not a promise that its target bytes remain available forever. Reused array offsets or equal byte hashes cannot revive a released generation.

This is an explicit conditional RAM-backed choice, not a free random-access abstraction. The entire live slot array and any payload bytes needed to meet the lookup schedule must fit their named residency grant. Array addressing, key-byte reads, comparisons, payload reads, cache misses allowed by the supplied primitive bounds, and page/pin bookkeeping are paid. If the planned residency is not supplied, this branch returns RESOURCE_UNAVAILABLE or requires a separately admitted storage-specific algorithm; it does not inherit O(log n) disk I/O from the word “sorted.” No implicit remote fetch or native execution is hidden in lookup.

## Per-group use and unchanged support conditions

For each support-scan attempt, the producer first binds the **actual coherent** group version/root/map and transaction history after any required old-effect settlement. It validates and enumerates all actual member leaves, including extra or partial placements when those belong to the group. A desired membership set, member count or catalog's target population cannot substitute for this map.

For each actual signed ad in canonical member order:

1. Check its exact type, signature/identity, K4/cell/F/E/revision and current owner/state bindings under the existing mode. Decode k from the actual ad, binary-search the READY slot array, then compare the complete located key for equality. No approximate match is accepted.
2. Validate the exact full entry chain and signed-ad linkage using the retained structural row. Check the ad's token in the checked at-most-32-token list, current selected disposition and exact actual FIND posting for that descriptor/token under C/R0. Preparation may supply the immutable structural witness, but the per-member binding and final current guards remain explicit paid work.
3. Apply the **original mode-specific eligibility predicate**. SG requires its A/owner/support binding. Old FULL_FEATURE retains its exact immutable FeatureScope, F/E, declared token and eligible signed anchor within that scope, complete scope obligations and relevant guards. A global catalog hit does not establish scope membership. All exact scope lookup or complete scope preparation work is separately admitted. UNGROUPED keeps its current exact anchor/owner guards without pretending it has an all-members group-renewal predicate. Existing FULL_EXACT/K1 is outside this K4-only refinement.
4. Inspect the actual anchor's complete current native-acceptance, protection, disclosure, custody and other support dependencies under the inherited native assessment reuse rules. A coherent existing native assessment may be reused only when that contract already permits it. Count every dependency-record visit and byte read for every member occurrence, even if another group inspected the same anchor. No new cross-group native-assessment cache theorem is asserted.
5. Produce the member's supported/definitely-unsupported/unknown disposition, with the same distinctions as the reference scan. Build the exact group summary or complete classification partition. At its publication boundary atomically compare catalog generation and all C/R0/V, group/map/owner and actual support guards. Only a current complete all-supported summary permits the existing RENEW gate. No side table silently changes the exact group root/version to which that summary applies.

A key absent from a **complete current READY catalog** means that exact anchor is absent from this current structural publication inventory; it is unsupported for this publication, not mathematically false. An explicitly nonselected/withdrawn row is likewise unsupported. Missing descriptor bytes, incoherent native history, stale support dependencies, malformed catalog or unestablished input completeness yield UNKNOWN/nondecision as applicable, never a negative mathematical judgment. Work exhaustion also yields no partial all-supported summary. An unrelated row with UNKNOWN_SUPPORT does not contaminate an otherwise complete group's exact anchors.

The reference incident procedure still has **two actual group scans**: classification of the reconciled postsettlement map, then complete authorization over the actual resulting map after cleanup. They may share this catalog only if every catalog dependency remains valid throughout both uses. DROP changes a group map, which each scan binds separately; if its local consequences also change C/R0/V, the second scan requires a newly paid build. UNKNOWN_SUPPORT still requires a separately admitted branch. Cleanup cannot replace the second map/support scan with “all intended invalid rows were dropped.”

Every existing asymmetric authorization rule remains: DROP/CLOSE do not require residual native support, PUT requires introduced/replaced anchor support, and RENEW requires support for every actual remaining member. The catalog never releases an unresolved effect lane, settles an outcome, extends a lease, supplies a current source coverage bit or changes the original-dispatch lease-clock discipline.

## Conditional success equivalence

Fix one admitted stable complete provider-local current FIND cut, complete coherent input bytes in the catalog-compatible unique-current representation, and current support dependencies; assume the inherited content-integrity, local writer, dependency-invalidation and native-assessment contracts hold. Give both procedures enough resources to complete their specified work. Let Reference be the held SG paid full scan over that same complete inventory, or the same structural fallback with the exact old mode predicate. Let Catalog be the preparation and use above. The catalog's stricter current-row uniqueness admission is an explicit premise; inputs outside it may be refused even if a separately supplied reference procedure could disambiguate them. This equivalence does not instantiate a smaller per-scope input or establish a current-layout projection for another algorithm.

For every actual group member a, define its support predicate as the conjunction of its actual map/ad/owner binding, exact designated anchor chain, selected/token/FIND relation, mode predicate, and current complete native/protection/disclosure/support conditions. Catalog preparation establishes the same finite structural relation Reference enumerates; exact unique lookup finds exactly the row Reference's exhaustive anchor search finds, or establishes its absence. The per-member algorithm leaves all remaining predicate conjuncts and native dependency inspections unchanged. Therefore both algorithms return the same complete member classification at this stable cut and authorize the same all-supported group state.

The argument is extensional over a finite exact relation, not a claim that the catalog certifies mathematical truth. Its steps are: complete input preservation; locator functionality; exact lookup equality/absence; unchanged residual predicates; current final-guard comparison. Duplicates/unknown inventory are excluded by the coherent-input premise and produce explicit nondecision outside it. If resources differ, one algorithm may exhaust its allowance while the other finishes: **success equivalence is not equality of cost, errors, timing or admitted operating region**. No equivalence is promised across a changed cut or missed authoritative invalidation.

## Invalidation, compaction and bounded lifetime

Any change to C, current R0 or the catalog's relevant V/guard dependencies invalidates READY authorization immediately through the authoritative guard comparison. Invalidation must precede reliance on the changed fact. Guard overflow/uncertain restart fences current use; it does not wrap or restore an old READY status. BUILDING records captured before the change can finish only as stale scratch, never as current summaries.

The minimal layout rule is deliberately strict: **even a proved representation-preserving pure compaction R0 to R1 invalidates this catalog for new current support authorization**. Build a fresh catalog against R1. This avoids an unproved chain of free RepEq rebindings or pinning a no-longer-current FIND relation as if it were current. Existing immutable FIND snapshots and already authorized operations retain their inherited historical semantics, not renewed support authority from the old catalog. Compaction still need not cause remote SG mutations; its local rebuild cost is real.

For this conservative branch, newly produced catalog-dependent summaries record their catalog/R0 dependency and lose that authorization value when it invalidates; subsequent reuse needs fresh admitted preparation/use. This is an additional **local policy and cost of this recipe**, not a claim that the original SG protocol makes R0 part of every summary. A preexisting independently justified SG summary unaffected by exact representation-preserving compaction keeps its original contract; this note does not retroactively invalidate it. Comparisons must charge the recipe's extra dirty intervals/rebuilds rather than silently weaken service or modify the predecessor's safety semantics.

Old R0 generations, old catalog slots/payload arenas, active readers/builders, action histories and incident summaries stay charged until their exact reference owners release them and actual local reclamation occurs. A current pointer swap is not physical deletion. Catalog retirement does not release independent OWNER_RELEASE_REQUIRED native custody. Any dependency on a retained old generation must be listed; two catalogs referring to one pinned arena must have correct reference ownership, not two fictional copies or zero copies.

A full build/repair queue cannot suppress authoritative invalidation. Advance the bounded coarse fence and expose INVALID/DEGRADED; refuse additional service promises or builds when capacity is full. Use already bounded catalog/generation records and a bounded dirty disposition, not an unbounded new task per incident. BUILDING has a finite admitted lifetime and a funded stop/reclamation policy. If readers prevent release, their real pins remain charged and new work may be refused. No unbounded retry loop follows from “rebuild on change.”

## Explicit structural counts, reads and work

For one successful build and its finite cohort define:

- e: all traversed inventory records, including excluded, ineligible, historical and tombstone records; n: coherent current rows inserted, with `0 <= n <= e`.
- i and p: actual token/posting visits and physical index-node visits during complete preparation; b0: all preparation input bytes read, including repeated reads and set-comparison/sorting work not already charged elsewhere.
- G: number of actual group-scan attempts; `R = sum_j m_j`: member occurrences across those attempts; `D_R = sum_j d_j`: actual per-member support dependency visits, counted with repetition.
- `L(n) = ceil(log2(max(1,n)))` and `H(n) = ceil(log2(n+1))`; Kmax: admitted maximum encoded locator-key bytes; w: admitted fixed slot width; Bpayload: actual retained payload-arena bytes.

A conservative stable two-buffer merge sort makes at most `n*L(n)` key comparisons. Its sorting-pass slot-movement reads plus writes are bounded by `2*n*L(n)`; repeated key-byte access for those comparisons, initial slot emission/copying, input decoding, duplicate-key scan and final validation are additional explicit preparation work. Adjacent duplicate detection takes at most `max(0,n-1)` key comparisons. Sorting the expected/actual posting relation, if required by the chosen layout traversal, has its **own** actual record/byte/comparison bound inside preparation; the n-row sort does not pay for it.

One exact binary search uses at most H(n) ordering comparisons for a lower-bound search plus one final equality comparison when a candidate exists. Thus anchor location uses at most

`R*(H(n)+1)` key comparisons,

with zero ordering comparisons for n=0. Conservatively charge each key comparison up to `2*Kmax` key bytes inspected, plus its slot/addressing reads and actual retained payload reads. Repeated member payload, token and dependency reads remain in the total b_member, even under resident storage. The maximum 32R token comparisons remain. Source guard, map, signature, summary and mode-specific scope work are separate, not absorbed into a key-comparison count.

The optional resident working-stock bound must cover at least two sort-slot buffers `2*n*w`, retained Bpayload, complete input/payload pins, layout traversal/set-comparison buffers, group maps, active summary output and the exact other live generations. Count disjoint physical allocations once on each owner/resource axis; multiple pins on one arena retain one arena's bytes plus their real separate reference metadata, not fictional duplicate arenas. READY generally retains one slot array; the second is not released until actual completion/reclamation. Sorting may not assume the input, output, scratch and all peers' maps each own the same spare RAM.

One sufficient **cost-shape**, with separately supplied primitive vector bounds, is

`Qcatalog = Qprepare(e,i,p,b0,n) + sum_j [Qmap_j + Qlocate_j + QmemberBindings_j + Qscope_j + Qdependency(d_j) + QsummaryGuards_j] + Qaftercare`.

Qprepare includes slot sorting/duplicates, complete structural and posting checks and current-guard commit. Qlocate includes actual slot/key/payload byte reads; QmemberBindings includes signature/chain/token work not already proven immutable in preparation. Qdependency remains D_R actual visits plus complete bytes/work; sharing structure does not divide it by replica count. The baseline's repeated common preparation and linear search are replaced, not retained and also charged a second time. Immutable structural checks legitimately shared by preparation are identified explicitly; everything else remains paid per use. All Q terms are real supplied cost bounds or qualification obligations, not measured facts here.

## Joint stock and cohort deadline admission

Admission uses the actual entire live catalog/layout/group/history state and disjoint residual work reserves. Before any build, reserve its complete finite work and aftercare jointly with incumbent native/custody/routing duties and all dependent group scans. Work already spent remains spent after invalidation; a successor build is new work. Reserve failed/abandoned attempts when the offered incident load includes them. Every resource axis and owner is checked independently.

At every prefix require the inherited form

`spent + disjoint unspent incumbent/build/cohort/aftercare reserves <= current grant`,

and actual charged stock plus admitted positive peak increments below each cap. The categories are a partition of reservations, not overlapping copies. Forecast release credits are zero. A later actually completed local release may define a new measured base without erasing spend or deducting that release again. Foreign lease expiry, desired catalog eviction or future reader completion is not present physical capacity.

A shared build is a predecessor of **every** scan depending on it. In one sufficient fully serial calendar, if Tprep is its complete supplied duration and Tj includes group j's actual settlement/map/member/dependency/cleanup/renewal work as applicable, then each serviced group's conservative deadline margin must exceed

`Tprep + sum_{j in its actual admitted service prefix} Tj + explicit incumbent/guard slack`.

The precise calendar may interleave settlement and construction, but must count their actual precedence and resource conflicts. Tprep is spent once, not G times; nevertheless every later group's deadline sees that predecessor delay. Do not divide preparation by G and subtract the quotient from each deadline. Use the held SG original-occurrence safe margins and supplied primitive services without inventing a new clock or assuming replay refreshes a deadline. A group-count or CPU-total saving cannot prove all-prefix timing.

For incident repair, a shared catalog can replace repeated **structural preparation within each scan**, never either scan's actual map/dependency/summary work. If a required C/R0/V transition invalidates the first build, the second preparation lies on the affected repair deadlines and requires its own stock/work reservation. No finite guarantee holds under unlimited relevant churn without bounded cumulative rebuild work or a supplied stable completion window.

## Defeating and boundary traces

**Anchor alias.** Two rows use the same target and token but different InstanceReplayIDs or public descriptors. Only one replay has a current complete assessment. A target/token dictionary would falsely authorize the other's signed ad. Distinct k values keep them separate; the missing/stale exact anchor remains unsupported or UNKNOWN. A conflicting same-k row instead prevents READY construction. No “another proof exists” substitution is made by the catalog.

**Unchanged theorem, changed support.** All descriptor/proof bytes and catalog sort order stay identical, but public-disclosure permission is withdrawn or a native dependency becomes dirty. The authoritative support fence invalidates the catalog; per-member dependencies and final guards also fail current support. Reusing the old READY bit would violate the contract. Missing incident propagation is a failure of an explicit integration premise, not something sorting repairs.

**Empty versus missing.** A completely enumerated C has zero current entries. Preparation succeeds with n=0; an actual nonempty group has no designated current anchor and cannot renew. If a C manifest page is unreadable instead, preparation yields UNKNOWN_INVENTORY; it cannot use the same empty-table conclusion. A genuinely empty group still owes exact map/history, mode, lease and resource checks.

**Failed search is not absence.** A partial build, unreadable payload/page, exhausted binary-search budget or released catalog generation cannot return a completed NOT_FOUND classification. It yields UNKNOWN/WORK_EXHAUSTED/STALE_INPUT as applicable. Treating one of these failures as absence could fabricate the incident theorem's exhaustive Keep/Remove partition; this recipe expressly forbids it.

**Owner scope is not a current FIND projection.** Let current R0 realize `(k,dx)` and `(k,dy)` for distinct eligible x/y descriptors in different old owner scopes. A proposed x-only catalog declares only `(k,dx)`, so this recipe's reverse-inclusion check fails on `(k,dy)` even though x's old scope is valid. Substituting a retained x-only run is not a repair: after current selection withdraws x, that run may still contain `(k,dx)` while current FIND does not. It then proves only historical presence. The admitted complete current catalog contains the full current relation; old groups still apply their exact scope predicates. Smaller current projections require the separately deferred mechanism and paid checks.

**Wrong resulting map.** Classification found a unsupported and b supported. An old pending PUT changes a's exact ad before settlement, or cleanup removes a but also leaves unknown c. Catalog membership cannot identify the actual resulting group by intent. Both scans use the reconciled exact map/history, and the second cannot authorize c from b's cached row. Catalog reuse does not repair missing outcome lineage or erase UNKNOWN_SUPPORT.

**Churn defeats amortization.** After each paid complete build, a relevant selection or R0 commit occurs before its dependent summaries publish. Every build becomes INVALID, every comparison and retained buffer stays spent/charged, and no support sweep completes. A frozen finite cohort with sufficient stable guards can benefit; infinite or excessive invalidation has no progress theorem. Batching commits changes visibility/delay and must be compared as a different service policy, not assumed free.

**Retention defeats final-size fit.** Two resident slot arrays fit in isolation, but old catalog generations and R0 readers already consume the remaining RAM/pin cap. The next build's overlap is inadmissible even though its eventual READY size would fit after hoped-for cleanup. Reject or defer it while preserving actual old pins; do not evict a still-owned dependency or let a queue-full condition suppress invalidation.

**Queue deadline defeats aggregate savings.** In declared symbolic duration units, two supported groups have safe margins 5 and 9. A shared build takes 4 and each group's remaining serial work takes 2, with no other work. Prefix completion times are 6 and 8: the first service promise fails even though total work 8 looks small. A separately justified alternative whose already available structure completes the first group in 2 may meet that deadline; the deferred scoped-catalog calculation does not supply that alternative. These are supplied timing examples, not measured seconds, and do not claim the reference full scan actually has that cost.

**Small cohort or large native work.** With one group, few early-hit anchors or dominant D_R/native byte reads, the paid sort, resident overlap and conservative invalidation may cost more than the original scan. Reducing a structural upper bound is not a general improvement in actual resources. Existing coherent summaries may already avoid any new scan; building this catalog then would be optional extra work, not a saving.

At one complete stable provider-local current FIND cut, paid exact structural preparation can replace repeated inventory validation and linear anchor search while preserving the original current per-member support conditions. This is a conditional finite algorithm argument with transparent costs and failure states, not evidence of runtime fit or a new mathematical validity mechanism. Smaller scoped catalog inputs and their 371,496 locator-count illustration remain outside this recipe and provide no admitted resource saving.
