### Minimal counterexample

Let the provider's current logical FIND relation, implemented by R0, be

`P = {(kx, dx), (ky, dy)}`,

where dx and dy are distinct exact StructuralDescriptor IDs for eligible entries x and y in different immutable old owner scopes. Choose the proposed catalog for scope S={x}. Its complete scoped inventory C_S declares

`P_S = {(kx, dx)}`.

Literal application of the stated two-way equality fails: `(ky,dy)` is a realized current posting outside P_S. This remains true when all bytes, guards, native assessments and scope ownership are correct. The conservative algorithm should refuse such input; the problem is that the displayed scoped cost case has not been shown to satisfy its admission premises.

Substituting an old immutable run R_S for R0 is insufficient. For example, R_S may still contain `(kx,dx)` while a later current overlay excludes x. Validating C_S against that old run establishes a historical structural fact, not x's membership in the provider's current FIND relation. Merely calling R_S a scoped index does not supply current overlay, tombstone, snapshot or selection semantics.

The SG/global branch can instead choose C for all current provider entries and validate all of P. That branch is not defeated by this counterexample. A broader global catalog serving old groups can likewise be safe if every old group still checks its exact scope predicate; it simply does not justify the claimed smaller scoped inventories by itself.

## Structural safety and exact lookup

### Functional signed-anchor locator

The locator `(provider,F,E,anchorPublicDescriptorCID,anchorReplayID)` is adequate under the source's coherent-content assumptions because PublicIndexDescriptor3 commits to one optional structural descriptor, and a usable K4 anchor requires that exact hint. StructuralDescriptor fixes targetRef, F/E and the replay. The admitted public descriptor/replay-root relationship then fixes the exact permitted judgment, declaration, opaque or constructor view. A row with the same locator but a different complete chain is not an alternative witness; it is rejected as incoherent.

The source keeps token membership, cell, ad revision, authenticated provider, signature and actual row ownership in the per-member predicate. Omitting the token from the locator therefore shares an anchor lookup, not the assertion that every token is supported. Two different replays proving the same judgment retain different locators. A same-target or same-token fallback is expressly prohibited.

### Current, excluded and historical records

The catalog does not silently deduplicate conflicting current entries. Even identical duplicate current rows are outside this deliberately strict admitted representation. Excluded/ineligible current entries may be represented with their negative structural disposition; historical/tombstone copies are enumerated and charged but are not positive current witnesses. This can make the recipe refuse inputs that another justified scanner could handle. The success-equivalence theorem explicitly conditions on unique coherent current representation and does not claim equal admission regions or equal errors outside it.

The explicit current/historical disposition and complete manifest are necessary inputs. A builder cannot infer current selection merely from the newest-looking object hash or first row encountered. Their completeness and authoritative ownership remain local integration obligations, not conclusions of sorting.

### Presence, absence and nondecision

Exact binary lookup is used only in a complete current READY generation. Successful absence can establish absence of the exact anchor from that structural publication inventory, not falsity of its theorem. A missing page, partial build, exhausted lookup, released generation or malformed payload is UNKNOWN/WORK_EXHAUSTED/STALE_INPUT as applicable. These distinctions prevent a failed optimization from manufacturing the exhaustive Keep/Remove partition needed for positive incident repair.

A complete empty inventory and a failed build remain distinct. An empty group still requires its actual coherent map/history, mode and operational authority. Catalog emptiness cannot settle an unknown remote action or reconstruct a missing group.

## Arithmetic and cost audit

For n rows, the source's two-buffer merge-sort allowance `n*ceil(log2(max(1,n)))` is conservative. A pass reads and writes at most n slots, so `2*n*L(n)` bounds those pass movements; initial emission/copying, duplicate detection, key-byte comparison reads, payloads and final validation are separately charged. Posting-relation sorting has its own bound rather than borrowing the locator-sort budget.

For a standard exact lower-bound search, `H(n)=ceil(log2(n+1))` bounds ordering comparisons; one final equality comparison when a candidate exists gives the conservative H(n)+1 allowance. At n=0 there are no ordering comparisons and no candidate equality; the general displayed allowance remains an overestimate, not an understated cost.
