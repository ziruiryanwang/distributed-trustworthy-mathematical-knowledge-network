## JS-RES-001: retained arrivals are not necessarily new current catalog rows

**Disposition: CLOSED on the repaired 31,100-byte cut.** The reviewer raised this issue after the full initial held read; root independently confirmed the domain gap. This is one shared issue, not two independently discovered findings. The following paragraphs preserve the initial finding; the closure paragraph names the actual repair.

The source states that if e0 current entries remain selected, n distinct arrivals are retained, and a fresh whole-current catalog is completed after each commit, the mandatory preparation visits are at least

`n*e0 + n*(n+1)/2`.

The sum requires catalog build j to contain at least `e0+j` distinct current rows. Retaining distinct arrivals alone does not imply that property. A local agent may supply two distinct valid new proof replays that are retained under native custody while the provider continues selecting an existing public anchor. Alternatively, distinct submission events can refer to an already selected exact locator. Retention of the new material or arrival history does not require a new selected current locator row in C. Paid C commits or fresh builds do not manufacture that missing row.

Thus a legal interpretation of the stated premises can leave each completed catalog with only e0 selected current rows. Its required current-row enumeration is `n*e0`, not necessarily the displayed sum. Historical traversal might add other work, but it cannot repair a lower bound specifically derived from additional current rows without an explicit corresponding premise.

The minimal repair is to require that each counted arrival adds at least one distinct selected current catalog row, distinct from the initial e0 rows and all earlier counted additions, and that every such row remains current through every later named build. State no pruning, replacement, coalescing or deduplication of those counted rows. Carry the same premise into the fixed-batch formula. For arbitrary finite admitted n this gives at least `e0+j` current-row visits in build j and proves the stated sum. It does not assert that one bounded provider admits arbitrarily large n.

The formula remains inapplicable where the offer permits skipping superseded cuts, arrivals do not enlarge selected C, old rows are removed, or a separately specified incremental mechanism avoids complete enumeration. No such extension is designed by this audit.

An associated editorial clarification is to state `k > 0` immediately before the displayed numeric `mu_CPU` expression and `0.46875/k`. The preceding general threshold already limits division to `V > 0`, so this is not a separate substantive finding. For k=0 there are no sweep member occurrences and no per-member division; use the undivided reservation inequality.
