# Core interoperability: portable mathematics versus compatible-project islands

Date: 2026-09-20. Root-authored concept comparison, pending independent complete-source review. No implementation, Lean run, extraction, executable model or benchmark. This investigates the central reciprocal product; it does not select a new native profile or narrow the full diverse-network objective.

## Decision to be tested

Retain the selected PI1-based exchange architecture as the reference. A name-preserving project cut is a plausible owner-local convenience or a separately specified narrower exchange alternative, but it is not currently an equivalent operable substitute. Separately, make existing **general-root and finite-collection selection** visible in the conceptual product: definitions and interfaces must not be treated as mere accidental attachments to popular theorem roots. That conclusion does not require a new fragment protocol, global library authority or compulsory retention policy.

These are two different axes. The exchange representation determines what can be checked and composed. A local selection policy determines what receives preservation and discovery resources. A coherent library fragment can use PI1; a name-preserving archive can contain unusable, irrelevant or undiscoverable material. Neither format determines mathematical value.

The full goal remains independent mathematical investigation feeding a large decentralized reusable corpus. A successful same-project example is not a substitute for combining independently developed mathematics, and a bounded paper argument is not deployed operability. The purpose here is to decide what central work is justified next, without recursively designing optional mechanisms.

## Non-oracular discovery and equal starting information

The selected P cases use a known willing native service with an authorized finite public snapshot. Knowing a peer is allowed; knowing the useful answer's ID in advance is not. Other services and broader routing may exist, but this example needs neither a broker nor a global catalogue. Both compared alternatives receive the same mathematical basis, local formal expressions and known peer set.

For safe definitions of type `Nat -> Nat`, a consumer that already knows Nat can issue STRUCT2 FUNCTION_OUTPUT with available input Nat and desired output Nat. Let n be the informative HeadAtom of the exact admitted Nat reference. The tag-4 payload is `[4,n,n]`, hashed by the selected ExtractorID rule. No reference to the unknown function occurs in it. Use absent PatternOption `[0]` so this bounded type-head candidate lane does not assume higher-order unification. Native checking and local body/type inspection discriminate returned candidates.

For a finite witness, the complete selected posting contains only the relevant functions and a declared bounded number of decoys, at most eight total rows. Supply sufficient real page/byte/scan/CPU and source/receiver budgets; request enough actual progress to inspect the complete chosen snapshot posting. That supplies a **conditional finite path**, not a guarantee that eight rows always fit one particular CPU or byte budget. Candidate descriptors do not themselves certify the fetched meaning. A busy prefix, missing contact, omitted target, incompatible profile or expired snapshot can defeat discovery on a larger workload; there is no global absence or recall conclusion.

Once a function's exact checked interface is locally known, it may occur in a subsequent query for a theorem about that function. For example, a local expression `f(f n)` permits a REWRITE_OPERAND query with the admitted f operand-head token, and a formal equality involving f and g permits the exact Eq operand-head pair lane. The useful theorem ID is still learned from the page, then its complete selected evidence is acquired and checked. All query objects and profiles use actual admitted references; these paragraphs give token construction rules, not invented hash bytes.

N cannot simply receive those same P rows as if their F4 bindings had N semantics. It has two honest comparison outcomes: supply its own independently specified finite formal index/reader with equivalent initial information, **not provided here**; or use P at the public boundary, becoming L for this comparison. An external 'oracle gives N the useful capsule' is not a matched end-to-end result. This missing alternative binding is central, not a reason to draft another optional index now.

## Case one: compatible extension

Over B, producer A supplies a safe transparent definition f of type `Nat -> Nat` with body `fun n => Nat.succ n`, under its nonreserved source name `Project.shift`. It also supplies a checked theorem

\[
\ell : \forall n,\ f(f(n))=\operatorname{succ}(\operatorname{succ}(n)).
\]

The displayed proof can be the reflexivity term after unfolding the actual transparent definition. This is a paper native-typing derivation under the common exact Nat/Eq meanings, not a claim to have obtained its serialized elaborated Lean body. Full basis and actual body acquisition remain prerequisites.

Independent B first discovers/acquires f through the function-type lane, then searches using `f(f n)` and discovers ell without its ID. B can apply ell at `f n` and contribute

\[
\ell_3 : \forall n,\ f(f(f(n)))=\operatorname{succ}(\operatorname{succ}(f(n))).
\]

Its evidence uses the exact imported ell rather than replacing an acceptance record by the claim 'we could prove this locally'. A third client can discover ell3 from a local repeated-f expression, check it, and instantiate it at its own native argument. Ell3 may be easy to prove without the network; this case tests genuine evidence use and interoperability, not usefulness or superiority over local recomputation.

Under P, f receives its exact DefinitionID; ell and ell3 have exact JudgmentIDs, ProofInstances and replays. For this deliberately simple family, f's definition and the theorem types contain no semantic theorem reference whose representative could alter f's computation. The witness for ell3 refers to the exact imported proof instance for ell. Native reconstruction, correspondence, footprints and all closure checks still occur. CLIENT1 may provide the local name, but the explicit native application is sufficient; no peer tactic or class metadata is needed.

Under an independently specified N checker, preserving this one compatible source binding can avoid source-to-canonical naming and semantic-representative substitution within that environment. It still needs complete types/bodies, exact basis binding, actual independent checking, current protection and successor evidence. Local owner tools that build ell3 remain untrusted producers. This is a conditional **mathematical ingestion/use path** for N, not an established network reciprocal path, because N's actual interchange and non-oracular discovery binding remain unspecified. L can use current services, but must export through P.

No structural argument here establishes that N has lower total cost. In this simple P case there is no proof-sensitive representative conflict to repair in the first place. The [REDUCE1 countercase](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-proof-computation-and-reconstruction.md) shows that such extra work can arise elsewhere; avoiding that particular transformation does not establish blanket preservation of all Lean generated interfaces or source frontend behavior.

## Case two: conflicting names, one mathematical application

Take two independent producer cuts over the same B. A has the f above. D has a safe transparent function g of type `Nat -> Nat`, body `fun n => Nat.succ (Nat.succ n)`, also named `Project.shift` in D's source environment. The names are nonreserved. Both complete source developments can be individually well-typed; neither is authorized to reinterpret the other's exact declaration.

An independent receiver wants to use both actual functions in one construction and establish

\[
m : \forall n,\ f(g(n))=g(f(n)).
\]

After unfolding these two exact bodies, both sides reduce to three successive `Nat.succ` applications to n. A reflexivity proof therefore supplies a finite conditional checked path. The requirement is not merely 'find any two equal Nat expressions': both imported meanings and their source-to-received correspondences must be retained in the new artifact's statement and support.

**Name-conflict lemma.** A native environment assigns at most one declaration meaning to each fully qualified name. A strict name-preserving embedding of A and D into one environment would have to map their common spelling `Project.shift` both to the one-successor body and to the two-successor body. Those records differ and, for example, their values at zero have distinct constructor forms. Thus no such identity-on-names joint embedding exists. This is a finite representation impossibility for N's strict joint-name requirement, not a mathematical inconsistency or a theorem that the functions cannot be combined.

P instead assigns different DefinitionIDs to the distinct body descriptors under the same admitted F4, assuming its hash-binding and exact decoding rules. The receiver reconstructs different qualified native names and may present them as f and g through an explicit CLIENT1 map. There is no source-name collision in the content-reference graph. The elementary proof above then has a conditional recheck/export path. P must not infer that every larger branch composition succeeds: incompatible representative maps, nominal groups, foundations, limits and actual checking can still obstruct other joins.

N can safely refuse, retain isolated environments, choose only one meaning, or introduce a checked renaming/inlining/transport construction. The first three do **not** supply this required combined application. Silently replacing the functions by equal-looking literals or lambda expressions loses the required correspondence unless that substitution is independently checked and recorded. A consistently renamed-and-rechecked ordinary acyclic fragment could solve this particular example, but it is an additional checked transformation—not untouched name preservation and not free authoring convenience. It must rewrite all actual references and carry exact evidence through successor export; broader generated-name/group cases add further obligations.

This case rules out using strict compatible-project islands as a same-service replacement for diverse independent composition. It does not forbid an optional compatible lane. The user has not authorized redefining the full objective around that lane's refusals.

## Case three: a third independent user of the combined successor

Assume the combined m is actually checked under P and receives separately funded custody, native service and index exposure. A third agent C independently has the admitted f/g interfaces and chooses the local target `f(f(g n))=g(f(f n))`; it does not start with m's identity. That target's Eq operand heads are f/g, so its own formal syntax directly supplies the STRUCT2 tag-2 operand-head-pair query. Use absent PatternOption `[0]`: a full pattern for this more deeply nested target could filter out m despite the shared head-pair token. In the stipulated complete finite posting, C discovers m, independently acquires/checks its selected proof and constructs

\[
u : \forall n,\ f(f(g(n)))=g(f(f(n))).
\]

The explicit derivation is transitivity of `congrArg f (m n)` with `m (f n)`. Their intermediate expression is `f(g(f n))`. Both premises refer to the imported m; neither the producer's verdict nor a download counter replaces them. C independently checks the resulting actual term and exports a new proof instance and exact support. A further peer's later use would again require its own acquisition, current checks, resources and visibility; no automatic perpetual recurrence follows.

The relevant closure contains m's actual type/body support and the exact f/g declarations. An unrelated safe declaration z retained by A or D need not be included if it is outside that complete support and basis. In P, selective rooted evidence is already part of the native contract; all actual shared dependencies count once in physical union accounting, not once per root. Old promises for z cannot be cancelled merely because C does not need it.

N's whole-project packaging might acquire/check z unnecessarily. A selective rooted name-preserving subpackage could avoid z, but must then capture the exact transitive type/body/group/literal dependencies, preserve the exact declarations and bind all references unambiguously. Selectivity is possible in principle; it is not unique to canonical IDs. Conversely, a module boundary or deduplicated archive alone does not prove selective independent checking or exact source correspondence. This case therefore identifies **obligations moved**, not an intrinsic proof that every N package grows with its entire lineage. N still owes the checked joint representation and discovery binding exposed in case two.

Current protection applies throughout discovery, acquisition, checking and use. If the original source disappears but complete required evidence is protected at another willing provider, C can use that provider under fresh actual authority. If every complete copy vanishes, historical validity records and index descriptors cannot supply missing proof data. The library format does not eliminate custody or finite resource requirements.

## A concrete definition-first accumulation opportunity

Fix an exact shared native product type `P = Nat × Nat` and the admitted addition definition. Producer A independently constructs only the safe standalone function

\[
s:P\to P,\qquad s(x,y)=(x+y,y).
\]

Its exact native DefinitionID/replay has been locally checked and is temporarily protected. Stipulate that no selected proof root's full closure already contains s and A publishes no theorem about it. This is important: definitions already required by retained proofs are not absent, and this comparison may not charge them a second time or pretend to discover a capability gap there.

The proof-family-only automatic nomination paths in [K](../cache-selection/proposal.md) and [M](../egress-selection/proposal.md) cannot independently nominate s on this stipulated input. That is their explicit scope, not mathematical rejection. A separately produced/reflexively checked wrapper theorem `forall p, s p = s p` could put s in a proof-root closure, but changes A's produced output and incurs its own checking, records and selection. Proof-only automation is therefore not an absolute transport impossibility.

Existing operations already provide a direct path. [ORG1](../../../spec/OPERATIONAL-CORPUS-V1-ORGANIZATION.md) distinguishes nonproof replay roots and permits general object/declaration lookup, transfer, dependency extraction and root-union accounting. STRUCT2 explicitly indexes safe DefinitionID targets by their checked types. An owner can choose s as an explicit root or in a finite [custodied collection](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001E-custody-contract.md), fund its complete closure/aftercare and separately authorize public serving and indexing. There is no necessity to manufacture a theorem wrapper, new trusted curator or new fragment wire object.

For discovery, an independent agent B already working with exact P may query FUNCTION_OUTPUT with available P and desired P. With p the informative HeadAtom for the exact product group reference, token payload `[4,p,p]` is emitted by the function's single-domain/terminal-output type. Nat parameters remain in the actual checked type; the token only knows the type head and is deliberately coarse. Use absent pattern and a fully examined bounded candidate posting; no s ID is in the initial query. The actual fetched type/body distinguishes s from swaps, identities and other maps. Assume P is represented directly in that type rather than through an opaque alias hidden from the bounded extractor. If the head is hidden or the posting cannot be examined within budget, the example's recall premise fails explicitly.

B can reuse the checked s interface in a local construction and derive

\[
s(s(x,y))=((x+y)+y,y).
\]

Under the exact transparent pair/function definitions this follows by native reduction and reflexivity; explicit projection/constructor correspondence and full body checks remain required. A more substantial finite construction is also explicit. Define a local safe iterate by `I_s(0,p)=p` and `I_s(n+1,p)=s(I_s(n,p))` using the admitted Nat recursion rule. B may construct

\[
\forall n,x,y,\quad I_s(n,(x,y))=(x+n\cdot y,y).
\]

Supply independently checked arithmetic evidence for zero multiplication/addition, addition associativity and `(n+1)*y=n*y+y`, either already in B's exact local support or separately acquired/derived under actual budgets. These lemmas are not silently assumed to be primitive seed axioms. The zero case rewrites `x+0*y` to x. In the successor case, unfold the iterate, apply the induction hypothesis under s, and rewrite `(x+n*y)+y` to `x+(n+1)*y` by the stated equations. Pair congruence gives the result. The exported native witness must contain the actual recursor, s, arithmetic instances and all other support; this paper induction is not an executed Lean term or permission to trust the outline. B can contribute the checked iterate definition and theorem through the same general/proof-root paths. Neither this construction nor the simpler two-step theorem proves speedup, novelty or inevitable future value; local rederivation may be cheaper.

The singleton fragment `{s}` is just general-root selection. Adding useful interface lemmas can form a coherent finite collection if its selected realizations genuinely coexist and all necessary support is admitted; membership alone does not prove coherence. A broad package `{s,z_1,...,z_k}` with k unrelated expensive roots can cost strictly more retained stock than `{s}` while providing the same stipulated service to B. Full-proof-closure selection can already share definitions/bytes. Thus the opportunity supports an ordinary **definitions-and-collections affordance**, not universal fragment superiority, mandatory indivisible bundles or a new consensus-maintained library.

## Product consequence using existing contracts

The conceptual first product should expose the same root-neutral sequence to an independent local investigator:

1. Choose existing exact checked proof, definition, group or supported opaque roots, singly or in a finite inventory. This is a local selection, not a public unsolved task or a human-intention acceptance field.
2. Resolve current admissibility and the actual complete support union. If roots need incompatible realizations, explicitly reconstruct/check a coherent chosen view or decline that combined use; individual validity is not joint compatibility.
3. Separately admit preservation and service/disclosure within real owner resources. Report which roots are only locally checked, currently protected, publicly served or indexed. These facets have actual prerequisites, not logical independence: a qualified current native-index target must be locally checked, public and retained, and scoped native reads require their authorized exact checked closure. Local checking or protection alone does not authorize public service/indexing, and an index entry or service authorization does not guarantee successful remote acquisition or future service. Historical snapshots and untrusted discovery claims do not by themselves establish current native eligibility.
4. Expose eligible formal type/structure candidates through existing native services; another owner acquires and rechecks before use. A collection does not force unrelated-root download.
5. Export successors through the same independent native boundary and choose their own funded retention/exposure. Updating a collection's chosen inventory never silently changes old exact meanings, rebinds an imported name or releases promised old evidence.

This is a composition/product requirement on existing operations, not a new schema or replacement of K/M. K and M remain valid optional proof-oriented policies. Explicit owner selection, a bounded heuristic or locally authorized agent may nominate nonproof roots. Popularity, measured reuse frequency, estimated import cost or exploration can guide that choice without proving optimality. They cannot assert native correctness, create resources, compel replication or make all original dependencies globally available forever.

A global canonical library, human identity/credit scheme, LLM natural-language gate and shared mathematics task queue are unnecessary. Exact operational authentication, local resource authority and secure independent checking are still necessary where their actual contracts require them. A network can accept diverse collections without promising automatic equivalence between independently invented definitions.

## Costs, failure cases and decision threshold

For each real owner and comparison horizon, separate complete evidence acquisition, basis/closure checking, correspondence/reconstruction, candidate search including decoys, actual application, successor checking/export, preservation/service, and old/failed work. Symbolically, let these resource vectors be A,V,C,D,U,X,R. Then `T=A+V+C+D+U+X+R`; no common term cancels until it is actually the same in both alternatives. Peak stock and timely service require separate all-times conditions. A newly named collection/profile supplies no grant.

N may remove P-specific naming/representative transformation from C inside one compatible environment. It does not remove V, exact support acquisition or independently checked output. If it then feeds P, the transformation remains at X. If it adds joins or a new service profile, their work is additional. REDUCE1 establishes a particular possible reconstruction burden; no measured positive difference in T is known. Similarly, selecting s directly avoids constructing a wrapper by specification, but no network-wide utility or throughput improvement follows from a saved object count.

Decisive defeating cases are part of the result:

- Name-preserving conflict refuses a genuinely required two-definition application; narrowing the task hides the failure rather than solving it.
- Public signature-only import omits bodies; a profile digest or producer identity cannot launder them into axioms.
- One accepted collection's roots use incompatible representative environments; 'all individually checked' does not authorize a combined term.
- Function-head search finds too many candidates or misses an opaque wrapper; a learned ranker cannot certify applicability or retrieve a target never exposed.
- A peer has checked s but never funds disclosure/indexing/custody, or every copy expires before B needs it; mathematical eligibility does not supply availability.
- A fragment bundles unrelated roots or changes an interface revision; bytes and checking grow, and the old consumer still needs its exact prior meanings.
- The same checked result can be rediscovered locally more cheaply than importing it; successful reciprocal plumbing is not proof of beneficial accumulated capability.

## Proposed disposition

Retain P as the selected reference; do not introduce N as a replacement native/wire format on the basis of unexecuted convenience claims. L remains an owner-local arrangement under existing source/correspondence constraints, not a new elimination of exchange work. The name-conflict and profile/discovery distinctions are sufficient to reject **same-service equivalence** of the strict N proposal; they do not prove that no future alternative can outperform P.

Use existing general-root/finite-collection selection as the normal conceptual affordance for definitions and interfaces as well as proofs. Do not select a new fragment object or automatic ranking/retention policy. The explicit s case establishes why proof-only nomination should not be mistaken for the entire product, while existing primitives demonstrate that the basic opportunity needs no additional protocol.

The decisive remaining central discriminant is actual feasibility of the selected full native path—complete BOOT1 basis, source-complete export, receiving reconstruction, usable local application and independently replayable successor—under real finite resources. The [readiness module](../qualification-readiness/README.md) already owns that evidence contract. This comparison neither requests execution nor claims its absent results. A concrete new contradiction could warrant further concept repair; absent such evidence, another optional maintenance refinement cannot substitute for native qualification.

All six native/eight reciprocal runtime gates remain NOT RUN. Useful diverse-library recall, sustained capacity and causal mathematical benefit remain unproved and unmeasured. The full goal is active, not replaced by the success of these small cases.
