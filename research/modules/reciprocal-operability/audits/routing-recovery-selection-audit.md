## Selection is the missing operating choice, not a new protocol

F already distinguishes direct renewal of known groups from a separate fair routing-refresh cursor. The proposed addition chooses a finite admitted obligation set, calendar, maximum fresh-start gap and joint resource allocation for that existing cursor. The fixed set S consists of provider/key/cell-path publication obligations, not theorem IDs, every target at the provider or the complete mathematical corpus. The main fixture preserves the initial H slot's 2,048 targets and 16,392 keys. Twenty daily new targets remain separately counted inputs; their existence neither enlarges S silently nor inherits its eight-day recovery claim. Omissions and degraded obligations remain reported.

No new opcode, remote acceptance predicate, compulsory research task, intention test, central directory, global ledger or globally synchronized clock is introduced. The policy acts only on already admitted F/H publication duties. M selects known-contact native exposure and explicitly declines outputs already in automatic F/H routed cuts. ## Independent success and failure traces

### Honest-join displacement remains a real counterexample

Take the old ordered nearest sixteen h1 through h16. Let h1 through h12 decline storage under their actual capacities, while h13 through h16 each admit a copy. Introduce four closer honest nodes. A new reader's relevant sixteen can now be those four newcomers together with h1 through h12. All queried holders can respond honestly without an ad, while all four old retaining holders renew successfully.

This execution changes discovery, not mathematical validity or evidence custody. It is consistent with F's four-copy target and permitted holder refusal. A fallback path might still find the provider; the counterexample only refutes guaranteed discovery through the stated current key/holder path. Neither the source nor checkpoint overstates that conclusion.

### The supplied recovery trace has the necessary additional premises

For an obligation admitted before t0, a fresh post-t0 occurrence starts at some s no later than t0 + Delta. It actually completes the required scoped lookup and reaches a reader-relevant frontier by s + L. Four useful destinations actually admit the required guarded copies, with adequate lease headroom and any required ancestor path, by s + L + P. Thus the stipulated placement exists by t0 + Delta + L + P.

This is a valid composition of attained service bounds, not an inference that the local controller can force those outcomes. The reader still needs actual overlap, current eligibility at the relevant holder-authoritative snapshot/read cuts and its own finite funded traversal. Adding its bound R gives a reader-lead bound only with those extra premises. Acquisition, complete native checking and useful application remain separate later steps.

The guarantee survives guarded movement of physical holder/group/row references for the same admitted provider/publication-scope/key/path obligation. Treating that intended movement as an invalidating scope-owner change would defeat the policy's own conclusion. The final source explicitly avoids that error. Actual changes to the admitted owner, membership, path or disclosure/native eligibility still require affected-scope revalidation and readmission, with old custody, uncertain work and cleanup obligations carried forward.

### The same scheduler can fail honestly forever

A live incumbent contact graph can keep returning an old neighborhood; useful newly joined holders can remain undiscovered, decline storage or respond without a finite bound. Every calendar opportunity can then be spent without a new useful placement. Even a normally completed CLOSEST_SEEN lookup need not intersect the reader's current retaining holders.

The source and checkpoint preserve this counterexecution. They do not derive global routing convergence from quiescence, physical connectivity, dispatch fairness or a local success status. Capacity exhaustion, lost continuity, changed eligibility and unreclaimed tails are additional explicit ways to defeat the bound without weakening mathematical acceptance.

The fixed key slots provide one main occurrence plus, when paused, one maintenance lookup: at most two live route occurrences despite serialized individual calls. The retained state remains charged.
