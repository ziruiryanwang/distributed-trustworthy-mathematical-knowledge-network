## Independently reconstructed work, not a batching assumption

H's simple logarithmic carry argument explicitly excludes splitting. The new source therefore correctly charges normalization separately before applying the insertion-only equal-size carry bound. An already-published twenty-target input cannot simply be relabeled a sixteen-target run, and repeated whole-run doubling cannot remove its factor of five.

The supplied case uses **two existing** twenty-target inputs among N's eight, not forty new contributions outside N's accounting. Each input owns 160 unique keys at four copies; its eight common keys remain borrowed from the unaffected main scope. The following direct arithmetic reproduces the source:

| Successful physical transition | Owned keys moved | Moved placement copies |
|---|---:|---:|
| A20 to A16 and A4 | 20 × 8 = 160 | 640 |
| B20 to B16 and B4 | 20 × 8 = 160 | 640 |
| A4 + B4 to C8 | 8 × 8 = 64 | 256 |
| A16 + B16 to C32 | 32 × 8 = 256 | 1,024 |
| **Total** | **640 key moves across the four transitions** | **2,560** |

The last row is a count of transition work, not 640 newly introduced provider tokens. The same unique token moves again when its owner participates in a carry.

Two actual single-row mutations per moved copy give **5,120 DROP/PUT calls**. Allowing one OPEN and one lawful empty-parent CLOSE per moved copy gives the conservative **10,240-call** upper allowance. Sharing groups can reduce the OPEN/CLOSE count. It cannot make unrelated holders participate in one atomic batch. The source assumes no cross-holder batching and does not divide this count by the 64-mutation batch limit.

The bound needs the named favorable initial state: the two parents contain their exact selected rows and no extra empty, partial or uncertain groups needing additional cleanup. The source explicitly states this premise. General legal inputs can contain that extra state; their full plan must charge it separately. A preexisting empty group need not correspond to any row in the calculation above.

No fresh DHT lookup is needed solely to change the scope owner at the same known holders. This does not establish that those holders remain reader-relevant. The source preserves separate routing recovery and invalidates affected N ownership cuts. If a new holder, marker path, anchor change, retry or extra eligibility scan is actually needed, this favorable work count is no longer sufficient by itself.

## Preparation, state ownership and failure challenges

The complete plan must exist and pass fresh version/authority admission before row mutation. It includes exact child unions, actual old/new ownership, carry collisions, peaks, recurring renewal and cleanup. Preflight is itself a paid stage: its 16-MiB planning ceiling is not a proof that the maximal 4,096-target/131,072-incidence/264,000-membership plan fits in memory. A larger actual plan is declined; it cannot silently truncate the array or omit an expensive target. Reusing an already completed feature extraction needs its exact unchanged bindings and does not make union construction or full disjointness checks free.

One logical partition and all physical representations are kept distinct. The active parent and provisional children are not counted as newly discovered mathematical targets, but every actual scope record, map generation, history and possible committed row remains charged. A colliding dyadic child is ready for a carry, not a second completed incumbent. The source uses the same one active H handoff allowance for a split or a carry; children do not create new handoff slots. Serializing row operations also limits actual pending mutations, not merely the labels attached to them.

The following countercases were checked against the held source:

- **Common tokens in both children:** all child emitted unions retain them, but only the child containing the selected eligible anchor owns a moved key. Other children borrow. Keys already owned by the incumbent main scope are not moved or counted as new ads. An external borrower of a parent-owned key resolves the paid stable owner-ledger entry/version; an unversioned copied-reference representation needs its own complete update plan or refusal.
- **DROP commits and its response is lost:** possible committed work and its exact before/after state remain charged. The next owner cannot be inserted on an assumption of absence. Reconciliation uses retained outcomes/current observations, never reuse of spent call authority.
- **DROP succeeds and PUT fails:** the missing copy is reported. The old scope record or remaining replicas do not recreate it. Any later repair is a separately paid transition, with no assertion of uninterrupted availability.
- **A parent group contains other keys:** moving one key does not authorize CLOSE. The source closes only a confirmed empty group; remaining rows keep their exact scope/anchor checks and actual renewal duties.
- **Logical completion occurs before physical reclamation:** old parents, map versions, snapshots and generations remain charged, and GC is fenced to the retired ownership/generation. An old cleanup cannot delete a replacement row or free retained snapshot bytes early.
- **A reboot, unknown outcome or timer expiry interrupts the phase:** no new grant or false completed carry appears. The inherited conservative group-recovery rules remain, actual unknown allocations survive, and further work requires remaining authority or readmission. Old receipts alone are not live group state.
- **Continued input or large carry exhausts a dimension:** the finite plan excludes later arrivals; they remain separately admitted pending work. A carry can be refused or deferred despite ample mathematical target-count room. Safe refusal is not relabeled successful growth.

These rules preserve obligation accounting; they do not force remote participants to remain available, provide group capacity or honor a future service interval.

## Calendar, renewal coexistence and tails

The positive phase requires actual service, not just a CPU ceiling. Its 10,240 serialized calls take at most 1,024 elapsed seconds under the stipulated 0.1-second bound. Additional local work fits within the separately stated 512-second progress bound, with further phase overhead included in the 2,048-second supplied phase. The earlier N work point plus this conservative phase is **71,008.4 + 2,048 = 73,056.4**, leaving **13,343.6 seconds** of the day before charging remaining maintenance pauses. This deliberately counts the carved calls conservatively again in that elapsed placement; it does not create more call authority.

N's two renewal windows, main key slots and actual check-before-new-publication order remain. The phase yields for its existing paid maintenance opportunities and keeps the retained paused lookup inside the aggregate four-executor envelope. One normalization occurrence plus one maintenance lookup is not one executor per child or per destination. Only one planned mutation is in flight, below the 32-batch ceiling.

Temporary active empty/intermediate groups are a real additional renewal risk even when the selected side-row count remains 5,120. The held source now explicitly requires every successful DROP/PUT and the confirmed CLOSE of emptied parent groups to finish before the next fixed renewal window in its favorable trace. Closure removes current group eligibility; physical tails are still retained. Hence the settled side-group renewal bound may be reused there without counting the retired generations as active leases.

Outside that favorable trace, failure, lost delivery or restart can leave additional ACTIVE groups. Their ongoing renewal and exact headroom must be reserved/readmitted, or coverage visibly degrades. The source explicitly forbids inheriting the favorable 5,120-pass count in that state. This was an early resource-review caution incorporated before the held cut, not a later empirical finding.

Source and holder headroom must actually cover the transition and its delays; a historical duration observation is not a synchronized foreign expiry. Remote holders separately need the actual map/row/snapshot admission, authenticated operation service and reclamation capacity. Source-side arithmetic does not supply those remote resources.

The new tails are not reclaimed merely because hours remain before the next day. Existing N tail obligations are preserved; additional tails need actual funded generation-fenced retirement. If settlement fails, the next normalization is readmitted against surviving stock or deferred. The source does not repeatedly apply a one-cycle stock bound to accumulated unreclaimed history.
