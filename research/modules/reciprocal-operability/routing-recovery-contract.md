# Bounded routing recovery for a finite publication scope

Status: proposed optional owner-local policy; independent review pending. Concept only: no implementation, executable model, native replay or measured performance. This note changes no wire format, native acceptance rule or selected baseline. It sharpens the existing fair routing-refresh cursor in [F exact coverage](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001F-exact-routing-coverage.md), using [F ROUTE1](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001F-dissemination-and-routing.md) and [H resource supply](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-resource-supply-and-discovery.md).

## Purpose and finite obligation

Renewing an advertisement at yesterday's holders can preserve bytes without preserving today's discovery path. The additional policy question is whether a willing owner can fund a finite maximum gap between fresh placement-refresh starts, while continuing its actual custody, serving and contribution duties. No global research goal, server, roster or knowledge snapshot is introduced.

Let S be an exact finite admitted set of provider/key/cell-path obligations at one local ownership/publication cut. S contains publication keys, not merely mathematical target IDs: H's 2,048-target example has 16,392 such keys. Record its actual owners, advertisements, anchors, disclosure versions, current groups and required ancestor paths through existing F/H bookkeeping. Every omitted intended obligation remains explicitly outside this guarantee, pending or uncovered; choosing S must not relabel a larger corpus fully covered.

The owner assigns existing refresh jobs a recurring supplied calendar, a maximum fresh-start gap Δ and finite budgets. A cursor alone proves neither. The existing urgency/initial-placement rules still apply; the owner must admit jointly satisfiable shares or report overload. This policy does not create a fresh allocation per key, reset spent work at a cursor wrap, or make any remote party serve.

## Five distinct observations

The following distinctions are logical reporting meanings, not new protocol records or a large new state machine.

| Observation | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Attempted refresh | A named occurrence started; its calls, failed work and elapsed time are charged. | Completed routing, renewed eligibility or placement. |
| Completed scoped lookup | F's actual normal termination predicate holds, or an explicitly smaller exhausted frontier is reported. | A globally nearest set or convergence with readers. |
| Observed placement | Exact existing group/row operations admitted the observed copies under current guards, with their actual lease observations; required markers are separately present. | Future availability, continuous remote willingness or current mathematical acceptance. |
| Reader obtains provider lead | A particular funded traversal actually intersects retaining holders and returns the advertisement. | A useful theorem, complete native evidence or checked application. |
| Checked mathematical reuse | Existing acquisition, foundation and application contracts accept the actual complete evidence and use. | That the search was economical, future preservation or useful exponential growth. |

Keep the last attempted start separate from the last completed lookup and last eligible placement observations. Partial work must not reset a claimed completed-refresh age. Coverage is per obligation and per cut; the largest relevant age or a missing observation cannot be concealed by a recent successful unrelated key.

F normally needs at least sixteen successfully contacted nodes, settled closer candidates and its other termination conditions, within at most 64 calls/120 seconds. Four concurrent calls are not four total calls. H's favorable four-RPC examples can lawfully describe partial observations, or a genuinely exhausted shorter frontier, followed by separately funded actual reads/placements at observed holders. Given H's explicit retaining-holder overlap premise, these can succeed. They cannot certify normal sixteen-contact completion. This policy's strong completion age uses normal completion; a separately selected shorter-exhausted case must name that smaller scope and still supply all required copies. Cached contacts are not twelve free fresh successes.

## Supplied service and lifecycle discipline

Admission reserves actual stock, peak workspace, bytes, CPU, executor occupancy and calendar opportunities together. For each obligation, a fresh occurrence begins at least once per Δ while admission remains valid. A failed occurrence still consumes its slot and budget; ordinary paid reconciliation/retry work may follow only inside its separately admitted reserve. Exceeding a per-occurrence deadline or any shared dimension yields partial/degraded status, not automatic replenishment or an assertion of recovery.

Routing-refresh and old-group renewal have separate jobs. During movement, retain old eligible copies until the admitted replacement condition is actually observed wherever distinct-holder capacity permits. Four proposed destinations are not four admitted rows. An already active matching group may be reused; OPEN does not extend its lease. PUT/DROP check current roots/versions and remaining leases. CLOSE is used only when retiring that actual group is lawful, never merely because one key moved out of a nonempty group. A same-holder change of scope/row owner requires F's old DROP before the new PUT; this policy invents no atomic make-before-break there. It needs surviving other copies or must expose the intervening gap.

Unknown outcomes retain their possible rows, group generations, snapshots and allocations until the existing exact outcome/root or bounded reconciliation resolves them. Old cleanup is fenced to its own generation. Deadline expiry or a clean invocation seal cannot erase this stock. Deep placement additionally needs independently funded lookup, admission, renewal and repair of every required ancestor marker: group renewal never renews those markers. The numerical fixture below deliberately uses depth zero.

Changes to S's selected keys/scope membership, provider/publication-scope ownership, required cell path or disclosure/native eligibility invalidate the old-cut recovery claim for affected obligations. Here the scope-stability ownership premise concerns that admitted provider/publication-scope owner, not immutable holder/group/row placement references. Guarded replacement of those physical placement references for the same admitted provider/key/cell-path obligation is precisely the recovery transition; it does not itself invalidate the claim. Update actual old/new group and row owners through the existing guarded ledger/operations, without treating an intended destination as an observed placement. A change to the admitted scope owner, cell path or eligibility still requires revalidation and a newly admitted cut/share before claiming its bound. Carry forward actual old/new duties and all spent/uncertain work; invalidating the theorem does not discharge custody or deletion obligations. Independent mathematical activity and new public scopes can continue within separate, jointly charged admission. Scope stability is not a prohibition on new mathematics.

A restart conservatively terminalizes old jobs and resumes accounted cursors only through existing recovery fencing. Where uninterrupted clock/grant continuity or group membership cannot be established, neither old age nor a historical receipt establishes a live guarantee; fresh admission and actual reconciliation are required. Recovery does not mint a new daily grant. A clean seal with valid continuing authority is different from uncertain rollback. The timing proposition below excludes an unaccounted scheduling outage; after one, its premises must be established again, without pretending the lost interval was covered.

## Conditional recovery proposition

Fix t0 after the relevant changes have quiesced, and an obligation k already admitted in S before t0. Assume throughout the required recovery/traversal interval:

1. The actual owner retains eligible public native support and valid authority for k. The finite scope/ownership guards used by the claim remain applicable; old and new duties fit their real allocations.
2. The supplied local calendar starts a **fresh** k lookup after t0 within Δ. A lookup already underway before t0 is insufficient for this premise. Scheduling and all competing duties actually receive their admitted service.
3. That occurrence reaches its required reader-relevant holder frontier and completes F's scoped lookup within L and its call limits. Independently, the publisher's observed frontier and the intended reader's actual query path overlap retaining destinations. `CLOSEST_SEEN`, physical connectivity and quiescence alone do not establish this condition.
4. Actual willing destination holders admit the required eligible copies and, where relevant, complete marker path within P after lookup, with funded transition stock and adequate lease headroom. For the reader implication, actual ad/group/marker eligibility and service/disclosure authority hold at each relevant holder-authoritative ROUTE_LIST snapshot/read cut. Old-copy renewal does not imply these new admissions or foreign eligibility.

Then k has the stipulated observed useful placement path by t0 + Δ + L + P. Proof: choose the first promised fresh start s; then s ≤ t0 + Δ, lookup finishes by s + L, and the separately supplied placement finishes by s + L + P. At depth zero, an actual retained copy on the reader's queried holder path supplies the ad; deeper paths use F's finite ancestor induction. Obtaining the ad by an additional bound R requires the reader's own complete, fair, funded traversal within R. Native acquisition and checked use require their own premises and costs.

An admitted historical page may arrive after ordinary row/lease expiry, subject to inherited reply-disclosure checks. It still reports only presence at its stated snapshot cut; there is no new live-at-delivery requirement. Neither that history nor receipt age establishes current source availability. Later native acquisition and use retain their own current guards, and actual disclosure withdrawal is not interchangeable with ordinary lease expiry.

This is not circular evidence of an achieved placement: the controller can enforce admission, dispatch limits, exact accounting and honest failure reporting; it cannot enforce the environment's bounded responses, newly useful contact reachability, willingness or future retention. Those external service assumptions must be stated or qualified separately. A dispatch schedule proves bounded **attempt opportunities** without them, not bounded recovery. Even a successful placement observation is not a certificate of future assumptions.

## Full-H-size supplied eight-day case

All costs below are **stipulated actual bounds for a paper fixture, not measured costs**. Keep H's initial 2,048 targets, eight common/eight target-specific tokens and U = 16,392 depth-zero keys at one slot. Initial chosen rows are 4U = 65,568. Each relevant cell and holder actually has room; average network occupancy is not admission evidence. The fixture presupposes qualified native support; it does not execute or resolve native qualification.

An additional, substantial premise bounds the lifetime union of holders addressable by this managed scope during the eight-day case by 20,000. This does not follow from H's instantaneous population of 20,000. Changing active subsets, including four joining nodes, are allowed within that union; it is an analytic premise, not a central roster. One active group per scope/holder therefore gives at most 20,000 managed active groups, including empty groups needing renewal. Larger unions or other scopes require additional supply. Renew every such group twice daily, with actual eligibility summaries and 48-hour extensions; initially provide at least fourteen hours of genuine lease headroom, including the selected timing margins.

For this numerical witness only, all seconds/hours/days refer to one mathematical elapsed-time parameter. Stipulate continuous unit-rate owner/holder monotonic clocks with arbitrary offsets, uninterrupted scheduling and the stated response bounds; every actual new group receives a 48-hour grant on that scale. Offset synchronization and remotely comparable expiry timestamps are unnecessary. Actual holder headroom/eligibility, including observation-to-commit delays, must satisfy these premises; a received duration alone does not prove them. Clock discontinuity, incompatible drift or unavailable service invalidates the witness. The protocol acquires no global clock or timing authority.

Assign exactly 2,049 fixed keys to each of eight days. A key job allows 64 routing calls plus at most four OPENs, four single-row PUT batches, four old-row DROP batches and four CLOSEs of actually empty old groups: **16 total** group operations, not sixteen per destination. Stable ownership and distinct replacement holders avoid same-row migration in this fixture. All four useful destinations actually admit on this attempt; refusal, ambiguous outcomes or additional operations consume the separate reserve and can defeat the declared timing bound. Successful lookup alone is insufficient.

| Daily call class | Reserved calls |
|---|---:|
| 2,049 full refresh/replacement jobs × 80 | 163,920 |
| Two managed-scope renewal passes | 40,000 |
| Bucket/contact maintenance | 4,096 |
| Other scopes, new contributions and source/query/service roles | 32,000 |
| Retry, reconciliation, cleanup and tail reserve | 22,128 |
| **Total shared H grant** | **262,144** |

The maintenance share includes 144 bucket lookups and 24 own-position lookups/day, here each actually completing within sixteen calls, plus up to 1,408 probes/other maintenance calls. If these instead require their full 64-call horizons, this share fails: no average or unused mathematical target capacity supplies the missing work.

The other-role share is not zero contribution. In this finite case twenty new targets/day actually complete native acquisition/checking/protection/export and feature/index preparation before their simultaneous batch admission and route placement. Each has eight new unique keys and the eight already-owned shared keys. H's existing token-owner rule avoids duplicate common-key ads; the old anchor remains eligible. Across eight days there are at most nine scopes, 2,208 targets, 17,672 owned tokens and 5,120 additional chosen rows/groups in the worst one-new-group-per-row case. New unique-key lookups actually complete in sixteen calls, so daily new placement costs at most `160 × (16 + 4 + 4) = 3,840` calls; their own twice-daily renewals cost at most 10,240/day at the final size. The remaining 17,920 calls in this class cover actual ordinary query/service and other control work. All native checking, acquisition, export and index maintenance are separately included below.

These eight twenty-target input scopes remain in H's counted pending-input/consolidation queue during the finite witness; they are not eight completed same-size tier runs. Their constructed feature scopes and actual row owners can serve contributions while consolidation is deferred. There is one completed 2,048-target run, at most eight pending inputs, and no active consolidation handoff. Pending status does not waive renewal, coverage guards, byte-time or eventual cleanup costs. No merge is claimed or charged as completed. Before claiming a later settled size-tiered layout or extending this contribution cadence beyond the witness, separately admit and pay the actual existing H merge/handoff/retirement path, or refuse further inputs. This finite deferral does not establish sustained H growth or consolidation throughput.

### A realizable conditional calendar, not an average deadline

For this example every actual control operation, including admitted local processing, completes within 0.1 elapsed seconds; each entire managed key job, with routing, mutations and its local work, fits twenty seconds. These are strong service premises. Serialize actual route/group calls, but count up to **two live route occurrences/executors**: one key job and one paused maintenance lookup. Both retain their bounded F lookup state inside the declared metadata/workspace. There is at most one pending mutation batch. This fits four aggregate executors/32 batches without a four-by-four concurrency multiplication.

At the same times every day reserve 3,000-second renewal windows beginning at seconds 0 and 43,200. Each pass covers at most 20,000 main and 5,120 side groups: 25,120 calls need at most 2,512 seconds, with side calls charged to their 32,000-call class. Remaining slack accommodates maintenance. Their maximum inter-commit gap is less than thirteen hours on the stipulated elapsed scale. After the first window place 1,950 key slots of 20.4 seconds, ending at second 42,780. After the second place 99 such slots, ending at 48,219.6. This still gives exactly 2,049 keys/day.

Each twenty-second job leaves 0.4 seconds for maintenance. A sixteen-call maintenance lookup uses four such gaps, finishing within 82 seconds of a due opportunity, below F's 120-second deadline; retain its state throughout. Bucket opportunities occur every ten minutes, while the hourly own-position opportunity is offset by two minutes from that hour's bucket opportunity. Complete one before starting the other. The remaining probe allowance, gaps and renewal-window slack cover the full 409.6 seconds/day of stipulated maintenance. Outside key slots, paid local/native work yields at those due opportunities. There are never more than the two live route occurrences counted above, and all call waiting/settlement is included.

After the key slots, perform the first 1,000 seconds of the 16,000-second native-work allocation: here all twenty new targets actually complete their acquisition/check/protect/export/index preparation within fifty elapsed seconds each. Only then perform their at most 3,840 route/group placement calls. Across the remaining day, the other/reserve class has at most `32,000 - 10,240 + 22,128 = 43,888` nonrenewal calls, including those placement calls, requiring 4,388.8 seconds. Finish the remaining 15,000 seconds of native/query work and 2,400 seconds of local maintenance. These finish by second 71,008.4 before inserting any still-due maintenance pauses, leaving over four hours of slack. New side groups obtain their initial 48-hour lease when created, then join both subsequent renewal passes.

All local phases genuinely receive these elapsed service bounds; a CPU ceiling alone would not supply progress. Actual server-side grants and reclamation work are additional remote premises. The calendar is an existence construction for these costs, not a claimed deployed latency.

The fixed key order repeats every eight days. Thus Δ = eight days to a fresh post-t0 start, and L + P ≤ twenty seconds for these actual successful jobs. The proposition gives useful placement by t0 + eight days + twenty seconds, subject to every named premise, including reader overlap and continued eligible support. A reader's own work is extra. To cover this horizon across a cycle boundary, the continuing owner must actually grant the corresponding next cycle; the eight-day budget is not a promise of future funding.

Retired main-scope tails remain charged throughout the cycle. For repetition, explicitly supply funded fenced reclamation and expiry of remaining old snapshots before the next admitted cycle, while retaining current selected rows; in this case stipulate old snapshot lifetimes at most one hour and actual tail settlement within two hours of each final mutation. The last day's early placement finish leaves time for that settlement. If settlement or reconciliation does not occur, carry the charged tails forward and readmit against actual remaining capacity or stop the next cycle. Do not repeatedly apply a one-cycle stock bound to unreclaimed history. Continued new target admission likewise needs new scope/renewal/stock admission; this finite example does not make growing side scopes free forever.

For comparison, lookup-only full-64-call work costs `16,392 × 64 = 1,049,088`. After 40,000 renewals, the residual daily grant is 222,144: approximately 4.72 funding-days at that sustained rate, before any other duties. This is a long-run necessary average under those stipulated costs, or a smooth-rate calculation—not a universal elapsed lower or upper bound for arbitrarily phased daily buckets. The explicit calendar, not that division, supports the conditional eight-day statement.

## Defeating executions and limits

An honest displacement: old closest holders h1–h12 decline rows because their allocations are full; h13–h16 admit four copies. Four closer nodes join. A newcomer correctly queries its new closest sixteen: the new four and h1–h12, none retaining the ad. Old renewals and native preservation succeed, but this exact-key path misses. The successful fixture repairs this only when its fresh route actually reaches useful destinations and four new rows are admitted. This says nothing about every possible fallback search.

Without those extra premises the same execution can fail forever: the source's live incumbent contact buckets can keep revealing only its old neighborhood, or messages to useful new holders can be delayed without bound, or new holders can refuse storage. Every scheduled occurrence may finish as PARTIAL or return a legitimate old `CLOSEST_SEEN`; no finite Δ forces discovery. Finite capacity consumed by new scopes or unreclaimed tails can also stop replacement while old leases remain valid. Insufficient granted service defeats the timing theorem even if all participants are honest.

The policy supplies a precise finite recovery target and falsifiable service/stock conditions. It proves neither globally nearest lookup nor survival under arbitrary churn, Byzantine censorship resistance, complete theorem discovery, native feasibility or beneficial exponential growth. Those are separate questions; mathematical trust still rests on actual independently checked evidence, not routing freshness or identity.
