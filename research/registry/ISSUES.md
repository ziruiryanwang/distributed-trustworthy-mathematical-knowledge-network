These first two limits fail a sufficient envelope, not every actual trace. Moreover, with arrivals between renewal passes, the maximal first/second stocks sum to `G_(d−1)+G_d=5120d−2560`, not twice final stock. Do not call day 30 a proved actual call exhaustion. The next question is a finite-horizon local admission/normalization policy producing another auditable cut from surviving day-24 state. Normalization reduces pending logical count, but renewal savings additionally need actual holder/group coalescence. Cross-scope ownership transfers, tails, custody and ongoing service never disappear. No new global task queue or capacity-market opcode is inferred.
