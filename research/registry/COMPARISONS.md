## Required comparison record

Each substantive comparison records only what is needed to decide:

1. **Common outcome and starting cut.** Name what remains equal, including actual obligations and availability. A temporary loss of current coverage cannot silently become equivalent to uninterrupted coverage because remote rows survive.
2. **Mechanism-specific work and stock.** Separate recurring work, arrivals, reorganization, adoption, failed/uncertain effects, retained history and useful service. Give costs per real owner and resource axis, not a freely fungible network sum.
3. **Regimes and defeating cases.** Include a favorable and unfavorable regime; record assumptions that change the direction. Distinguish failed sufficient envelopes from necessary lower bounds.
4. **Decision threshold.** State the resource/deadline conditions under which an option is preferable or inadmissible, or explain why evidence is still insufficient. No required winner.
5. **Evidence boundary.** Label source-derived facts, conditional derivations, illustrative choices, heuristics and unmeasured costs. An independent audit checks the comparison, not runtime performance.

The current placement comparison must include dense holder overlap and dispersed holders; actual grouping capacities and encoded batch widths; both `G*e` traversal and `e*sum(m_g)` fallback anchor searches; old-mode transfer and interruption; all extra/empty/uncertain/retired state; and continuing renewal even with no new mathematics. Common work cancels only after showing that it is genuinely equal.
