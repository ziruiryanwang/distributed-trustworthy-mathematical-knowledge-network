## What counts as progress

The objective is reliable reciprocal accumulation by independent agents. A new document, positive tiny fixture or closed refusal path is not by itself progress toward efficient sustained operation. A useful investigation must remove a consequential uncertainty, expose a defeating case, compare a genuine alternative, or close a specified mechanism gap. Formal invariants and practical heuristics have different jobs; neither should be forced to impersonate the other.

## Root's research-admission gate

Before commissioning a mechanism or further audit, root must state: **which central product capability it changes; what evidence shows a current obstacle; which architectural decision its answer can change; and why a simpler existing path does not already meet the chosen scope.** Name the smallest sufficient investigation and its stop condition. A claim confined to an optional unselected feature normally waits until that feature earns a role in the core path. This is a decision rule, not a numerical score that can be gamed by subdividing work.

Prioritize trustworthy import→actual Lean use→independently replayable successor; then usable discovery/compatibility and continued valuable retention under real finite supply. Reprioritize when an actual counterexample makes a lower-layer issue block that path. ## Resource questions that every growth claim must answer

For each real resource owner, separate:

- **Retained stock:** current evidence, indexes, ownership maps, old snapshots/generations, custody pins and uncertain outcomes.
- **Incumbent recurring work:** renewal, custody repair, discovery maintenance and already-promised service.
- **Arrival work:** inspection, native checking, evidence protection, extraction/indexing and initial placement.
- **Reorganization work:** local compaction, authority changes, actual remote mutations and eventual cleanup.
- **Useful service:** discovery followed by acquisition and checked application, not only successful publication.

No term is supplied merely by a new identity or a changed day number. All-times stock, burst/deadline feasibility and elapsed responsiveness are separate from average work. A numerical envelope must state which terms are measurements, fixture choices, external service premises or derived bounds.

The target of B-AMPLIFY is specifically unnecessary reorganization work. Even an ideal zero-remote-mutation compaction leaves the other four categories. Stable placement therefore deserves investigation without being advertised as a complete scaling solution.

## Handoff discipline

One active source owner per path; source contributors are not the independent auditors of their own design. Root gives reviewers the same complete held cut and combines overlapping findings into one repair request. Architectural alternatives and baseline continuations remain separate until a deliberate integration decision. See the [handoff checklist](../templates/review-handoff.md).

Close an entry only at its stated scope. Keep a wider question open when a small conditional case closes. A paper proof can discharge a conditional invariant; it cannot manufacture service, native artifacts or useful workload evidence. Conversely, lack of a benchmark is not itself proof of a concept contradiction.
