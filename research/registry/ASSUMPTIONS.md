## Reading the conditions

Each row identifies a consequential premise, not a claim that the premise has been established. **Local** means a node can check or enforce the stated obligation through its own boundary, conditional on correct implementation and underlying execution/storage integrity; it does not mean the implementation is already correct. **External** means actual resource, participant or environmental supply that local records cannot manufacture. **Qualification** identifies an unperformed correctness/conformance obligation. **Empirical** identifies a usefulness hypothesis rather than a safety axiom.

The next-evidence column names evidence that would most directly test or qualify the premise. Runtime examples there are future gates only: no implementation, execution, extraction or benchmark is authorized or performed by this ledger. A passing finite test would support only its tested scope, not prove the general assumption.

## Consequence discipline and change review

Acceptance safety depends on the binding, adopted basis and faithful local checking/state boundary; it does not require every publisher or broker to be honest. Acquisition, exact finite retrieval and lasting useful accumulation have additional supply and coverage premises. Losing one of those premises must withdraw only the affected claim, unless a shared safety boundary is also violated.

For a changed premise, name the row and exact changed source cut, follow its dependent [interface](INTERFACES.md) and [claim](CLAIMS.md) records, and request the smallest relevant countercase/review. Do not treat a larger key count as independent storage/checker diversity, an explicit nondecision as falsity, or a new paper audit as runtime qualification. This ledger itself introduces no new protocol, mathematical authority, compulsory research objective or automatic work allocation.
