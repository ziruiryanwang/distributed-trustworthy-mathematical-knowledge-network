## Decision

Do not repeat the passed memo controls, rebuild this guest, replay the failed
screen, or automatically buy a higher cap. The optimization preserves the
accepted relation and reduces repeated syntax work, but did not close the
affordability gate. Another source intervention needs an argument addressing
the remaining representation work, with its real integration costs; a larger
cap requires a separate complete-path cost decision. Neither is selected here.

Advance the ordinary product seam concurrently: one finite query/acquisition
and authoring workspace, not fixture-specific campaign logic. The ongoing
contract review has already found real blockers (hard-coded experimental
location adoption/output paths, exact-type/profile differences between proof
lanes, and one-shot budget handoffs). Resolve those in the contract before
implementation. Do not invent a mixed-network I-only mode or saved trusted
handles to get an apparent pass.

The growth comparison must beat both optimized local development and ordinary
shared-library reuse under stated workload/resources. A certificate is not that
comparison. See GROWTH-CRITICAL-PATH.md for the existing theory and concrete
next product selection. The full v5 objective stays active and unachieved.
