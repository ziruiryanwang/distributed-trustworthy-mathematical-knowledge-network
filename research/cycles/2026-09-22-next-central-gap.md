# Next central gap: affordable, reliable hosting of a paged view

2026-09-22. Read-only source/evidence assessment of the selected paged peer candidate. No Python, Lean, SQL, network service, product test or preserved evidence was run or changed. This note selects a next local discriminator; it does not qualify a new product cut.

## Decision

Treat **repeated finite service of a nontrivial paged view within the existing owner grant** as the next central operability gate outside the separate checked-package-insertion investigation. The narrow source candidate is to authenticate the complete frozen view once per service session, pass that already authenticated in-process view into the transport listener, and retain the per-response page/bundle byte checks. First measure a frozen multi-page workload against the selected cut. Adopt the change only if the fixed safety and complete-session gates below pass under unchanged limits.

This issue matters to reciprocal use because a receiver can only acquire what a supplier actually keeps serving. Paged discovery lets a receiver traverse more advertisements, but every finite hosting slot must start a fresh worker and make its listener ready. If full-view startup work consumes the slot's wall/CPU budget, the advertised later pages or bundles are operationally unavailable even though their catalog is well formed. This is a concrete repeated-cost path, **not an observed large-view failure or a demonstrated speedup**.

## Evidence and priority boundary

- The [goal](../../GOAL.md) requires reliable, efficient reciprocal discovery, checking, reuse and contribution at measured cost, and expressly separates availability from acceptance. The [current design](../../CURRENT-DESIGN.md) calls out full-view startup validation and continued useful operation as unresolved after the paged candidate.
- The [qualified candidate](../../deliverables/2026-09-22-paged-peer-candidate/RESULTS.md) passed fifteen protocol contracts and one genuine later-page Lean receiving/contribution chain. The 65-entry protocol views were inert fixtures; the first native supplier advertised 32 inert decoys and one genuine historical package. There is no ordinary exposure or repeated hosting result for 33 or more genuinely published entries. Its 256-entry/64-MiB view bounds are ceilings, not throughput evidence.
- The [native result](../../experiments/peer-discovery-v2/NATIVE-RESULTS.md) explicitly leaves incremental view construction and cheap service restarts open. Its 233.12-second complete workflow is a different workload from earlier runs and cannot establish a speed comparison.
- In the selected source, [service_worker.py](../../product-local/peer-discovery-source-01/py/service_worker.py) calls wire.load_view before validate_selection checks each selected row against publication or replica-custody authority. It then invokes service.serve. [peer_service.py](../../product-local/peer-discovery-source-01/py/peer_service.py) calls wire.load_view again before binding/listening. [peer_wire.py](../../product-local/peer-discovery-source-01/py/peer_wire.py) makes each load parse every pinned page and read/hash every retained bundle. The [peer contract](../../product-local/peer-discovery-source-01/PEER.md) explicitly acknowledges this duplicate whole-view authentication and the unchanged CPU/time/bundle grants.
- The [hosting contract](../../product-local/peer-discovery-source-01/HOSTING.md) starts fresh finite sessions with durable up-front reservations, no automatic grant extension, and readiness only after view loading. The retained [integrated hosting outcome](../../software/qualification/integrated-release-01/OUTCOME.md) served one ordinary contribution in two short sessions; it does not measure multi-page startup.

The previous [v1 gap review](2026-09-22-v1-delivery-gap.md) selected bounded discovery ahead of a direct-LAN rewrite; paging has now addressed its immediate 32-entry visibility obstruction. It also treated two fixed contact slots as a finite first-release boundary absent a workload showing that they prevent useful operation. The one-pass hosting seam is therefore a smaller, source-demonstrated next discriminator than contact routing or a new network protocol. Remote exact-symbol filtering remains useful retrieval work, but a filter cannot make a selected view available when its supplier cannot start serving it.

## Minimum source change

Make the owner worker's authenticated load the one load used for that session. Keep ledger-only publication/replica-custody validation over **every** selected entry before readiness. Add a private in-process transport entry point that accepts the fully loaded view; retain the existing standalone serve entry point as a validating wrapper. Do not serialize a trusted-view token, accept a caller-supplied cache, persist a validation result across sessions, or weaken source/configuration pins.

The loaded object contains exact catalog bytes, page paths and pins, and bundle paths and pins. Continue reading and hashing the selected page or bundle at each GET before sending it. A changed file after startup must still refuse or close that transfer; historical readiness does not promise present availability. Preserve the same HTTP routes, wire-v2 snapshot/page binding, immutable view bytes, loopback policy, finite session charges, request replay and receiver-local package/admission checks. The checker and Lean must remain absent from the service worker.

Risks are an accidental bypass of owner publication/custody authority, a changed-file race after the single load, and treating an authenticated in-memory catalog as permission to send changed backing bytes. Keep selection validation before listener creation and explicitly test last-page/last-bundle corruption both before startup and after readiness. A result that improves startup but changes the mathematical acceptance or hosting resource boundary is a failure.

## Falsifiable acceptance gate

Freeze the exact source cuts, view fixtures, grants, workload, CPU/wall/byte accounting and independent reviewer before execution. Use separate fresh installations and retained run directories; preserve the selected archive and all used states. No new Lean work is needed for the native-free differential phase.

1. On 1-, 33-, 65- and 256-entry pinned views, including byte-limited pages and a later-page target, compare old and candidate catalog/page/bundle responses and terminal hosting records. Instrument complete-view loads and bytes read. The candidate must perform exactly one full-view load per session; both cuts must return identical valid bytes and the candidate must keep all authority checks. Report complete preparation, readiness, session and transfer CPU/wall/retained-byte costs, not only the transport function timer.
2. Under the unchanged finite grant, run at least two consecutive service sessions for the same 65-entry view; fetch root, every page and the last bundle across the sessions. Each slot must reach readiness and close with a reconciled receipt, quiescent process tree and no counter refund or silent renewal. Freeze a prospective per-slot readiness deadline inside the existing grant before running. A missed deadline, failure/uncertain receipt, or absent last-page transfer fails the gate. The 256-entry case is a bound/control, not a promise that its complete transfer fits the 128-connection default; explicitly issue another authorized session if needed.
3. Corrupt a last page, last bundle and publication/custody binding before a new session: each must refuse before readiness. Change a selected page or bundle after readiness: its GET must refuse/close, with no substituted bytes. Exercise old snapshot 404, private-route refusal and exact historical request replay. No checker/native stage may load or run during service.
4. In a separate fresh real-Lean follow-through on the changed service path, publish and serve an ordinary contribution, let a second node discover/acquire it, then freshly admit and substantively use it. Independent operational and canonical review must bind the result. This tests the changed hosting seam; it does not turn synthetic 65-entry fixtures into 65 mathematical publications.

If the one-pass cut passes correctness but still cannot meet the frozen multi-session readiness envelope, inspect measured view-construction/startup phases before changing limits or proposing incremental views. If it passes, report the measured scope only: finite same-host reliable service, not independent-host availability, sustained useful growth, large-network recall or public release. Checked-package-insertion cost, the unavailable second host and licensing remain separate decisions.
