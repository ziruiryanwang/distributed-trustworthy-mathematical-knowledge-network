## Decision

**No further growth-specific mathematical primitive is presently justified.**
The most consequential remaining growth obligation is a practical one: deliver
the complete supported native-first route by which another investigator obtains,
continues and returns an independently authored theory. Resolve concrete public
handoff defects before optimizing the optional grounded-cut backend.

This is not a claim that the existing components already constitute final v5.
Nor does it replace the user's concept-development requirement with a theorem
store. The product includes participating investigators and their ordinary Lean
projects; the trusted acceptance core need not invent the concepts itself.

## The precise sufficiency claim

Consider a finite locally chosen investigation sequence. At step j an investigator
has an approved compatible ordinary Lean realization E_j and chooses additional
definitions, structures, models, conditional laws or checked bridges Delta_j.
Assume the exact supported declarations and their selected semantic/proof closure
fit the existing operation bounds; required dependencies remain obtainable; source
execution and acceptance follow their separate existing policies.

If ordinary Lean checks the extension and the independent receiving path checks
its exact export closure, the recipient gains warranted declarations it can use
under that receiving policy. If a compatible authoring realization is also retained
and obtained, a subsequent investigator can propose another extension without
requiring a network-approved concept name, predeclared question or fixed vocabulary.
Induction over successful supported steps supplies this *continuation property*.
It does not supply the next idea, remove bounds or make all Lean developments
supported. Branches with incompatible meanings remain distinct until explicitly
reconciled; symbol similarity is not a semantic merge rule.

The relevant inspected source facts are concrete:

- `ordinary_lean/CheckedExtension.lean::allowedDeclaration` permits safe
  definitions, theorems, safe opaque declarations and complete inductive groups.
  `checkBatch` checks grounded references, actual `addDeclCore` with checking
  enabled, generated records, freshness and the resulting name census.
- `ordinary_native_package/owner.py` exposes `produce-baseline`, independent
  `intake-baseline`, supported `contribute` and `export-module`. The primary
  ordinary-project route does not require research to happen inside a cut store.
- `ordinary_research_distribution/README.md` supplies the discover/inspect/
  approved-project/produce/intake/publish crosswalk. Its PUBLIC-PATH-AUDIT finds
  source-expressible public inputs, not a turnkey or final-release result.
- `ordinary_custody_return/custody_return.py::write_bundle` now exposes retained
  complete units under explicit `selected-units` or `closed-basis` policy using
  the existing manifest format. It grants no mathematical or source authority.

Ordinary Git/Lake already supplies most of the creative capability. The commons
adds interoperable discovery and exact independently checkable interchange and
custody under stated constraints. If those adapters are reusable inside another
ordinary library workflow, that is a successful contribution, not a failure to
possess an exclusive form of mathematics.

## Why this cannot become an unconditional growth theorem

Take agents that repeatedly propose aliases of existing definitions, or never
publish any useful work. They can satisfy every soundness and transport rule
without improving anyone's affordable research capabilities. Conversely, a new
definition can be worthwhile before it has any downstream use. Thus kernel
acceptance and theorem counts cannot decide scientific usefulness or ensure
indefinite conceptual progress. This counterexample is a logical limit, not a
reason to weaken correctness or force a centralized usefulness authority.

The prior resource-bounded capability model remains appropriate: assess whether
particular new abstractions and connections make actual further work possible or
cheaper under disclosed resources. The missing prevalence and cost magnitudes
are not supplied by another abstract inequality. Distinguish three claims:

1. The system permits genuine independently chosen conceptual continuation:
   supported in a bounded retained example and in the inspected ordinary route.
2. The complete shipped workflow makes such continuation practically accessible:
   requires public integration, coherent inputs and release assurance.
3. It causes faster useful research in realistic use: requires a defensible
   operating region or actual uptake/cost evidence; not established by (1) or (2).

The user requires an effective research commons, not universal success on every
investigation. State these claims accurately without relabeling a component pass
as delivery or using the impossibility of (unconditional) progress as an excuse
to leave the actual product unfinished.

## One bounded next action and its falsifier

**Finish the selected public native-first theory-continuation handoff, using the
existing code and retained theory; inspect first, repair only a named defect.**
Root's separate public-path investigator should own that concrete source audit.
No repeated A/B invention, broad benchmark or third checker is selected here.

An especially concrete boundary to resolve is source-to-export correspondence.
`concept_episode/AUTHORING.md` explicitly says that the ordinary retained package
builds B as `TheoryB02`, while the historical accepted episode used logical module
`TheoryB`. Equal source bytes therefore do not establish identical generated
names or export identities. This is disclosed, not a newly established bug.
If the public handoff claims continuation of an exact offered export, preserve
its mapping or determine the actual correspondence through the existing producer;
otherwise describe the rebuilt theory as a separately identified realization.
Do not quietly bind the old accepted ID to a merely similar rebuild.

Falsifier: an independently selected compatible recipient cannot perform the
documented continuation/return without a private root-authored identity, omitted
source input or unsupported manual state intervention. If no such defect exists,
stop this growth-specific audit and proceed with ordinary release integration
and assurance; do not manufacture another component to keep research busy.

The strong comparator has the same Lean/Git/Lake tools, source access, compatible
libraries, discoveries, caches, mirrors and equivalent acceptance requirements.
Credit existing source sharing and its integrations fully. Charge our discovery,
failed leads, source correspondence, independent checking, custody and maintenance.
The immediate claim is a usable interoperable public tool, not speedup over an
ideal alternative that already includes every one of its implemented facilities.

## Fixed-basis acceleration is a separate investment

The actual cut cost exposes repeated fixed-basis construction; the reviewed
singleton snapshot can plausibly reduce it. That is a credible conditional
optimization, not a prerequisite for concept invention or the default authoring
route. Defer its implementation behind a concrete higher-payoff public handoff
defect. If that route is already coherent and lifetime cost is again the central
obstruction, use the existing FIXED-BASIS-PAYOFF/REVIEW decision rather than a new
growth theory. Select it only with a useful repeated-use region against native
batching/small contexts and a smallest decision-changing discriminator.

No successful creative episode, this note or a faster fixed basis closes the
remaining public-deployment, scalable discovery, assurance and availability
obligations. This recommendation preserves them and the unchanged full-v5 goal.
