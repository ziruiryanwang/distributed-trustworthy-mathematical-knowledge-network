The required executable loop is: an independent investigator chooses a region
of mathematics, proposes an abstraction not specified by the harness, develops
and challenges its theory, offers checked outputs, and a second investigator
chooses and proves a further extension. The second investigator must not merely
fill a supplied theorem template. A completed loop establishes a real creative
workflow at its stated scope; it does not establish perpetual research success,
world-first novelty, or a universal productivity advantage.

The minimum new software is likely **a local investigator workflow and coherent
handoff**, not another mathematical acceptance mechanism. First use the current

## Three cooperating layers, without a central researcher

| Layer | Responsibility | What it must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Local investigator | Choose formal territory; propose definitions, conjectures and proof strategies; compare models; revise and publish | Its prediction, taste or failed search is mathematical authority |
| Lean project and checking boundary | Represent exact definitions, laws, assumptions and proofs; independently warrant accepted extensions | A well-typed definition is automatically inhabited, useful or new |
| Decentralized commons | Discover, acquire, preserve, recheck, compose and return selected work across replaceable participants | It assigns everyone a research agenda or guarantees scientific progress |

The full distributed system includes its participating investigators; its trusted
core need not be creative. A usable v5 should supply at least one documented,
executable local-investigator workflow, replaceable by human, symbolic or learned
investigators. Delegating creativity to unspecified future clients indefinitely
would leave this product requirement unfulfilled. Conversely, making one model
or prompt an indispensable global server would contradict decentralization.

An investigator may privately keep conjectures and unresolved obligations. They
are not accepted premises or a compulsory public task catalogue. A completed
conditional theorem is different: it has a proof of its exact quantified or
conditional statement, even when its hypotheses have no known instance.

## Representing concepts and theories without changing foundations

An operational concept consists of ordinary definitions or structures, their
formal operations and laws, and selected checked relationships. A theory is a
coherent compatible module family, not necessarily a new axiom system.

Represent an abstract theory by parameters and an ordinary Lean structure or
predicate `Laws data`. Then develop theorems of the form

    theorem consequence (data : Signature) (h : Laws data) : Q data := ...

This does not add `Laws data` as a global axiom. It quantifies over a model of the
laws. Defining an empty class is sound; claiming an actual instance requires an
inhabitant, and claiming two concrete nondegenerate realizations requires those
separate proofs. A proof of such a model supplies relative consistency evidence
under the existing foundation, not an unconditional consistency proof for Lean.

Useful outputs include definitions, new inductive datatypes, invariant notions,
representation relations, abstract operations/laws, concrete models,
countermodels, classification results and checked translations between theories.
The same ordinary named declarations can carry them; special wire object kinds
are not logically necessary. Quotient constructions remain subject to the exact
supported existing foundation and declaration path, not a new quotient axiom.

For theory transfer, a provider supplies actual data maps and preservation proofs
for the particular relation being claimed. Full arbitrary formula translation
would require a stronger soundness theorem and is not provided by matching names.
Start with ordinary typed instance/application theorems, not a generic morphism
executor. Independent branches can remain incompatible until a checked connection
and jointly usable environment exist; a concept name never resolves that issue.

## Formal assessment versus scientific judgment

Keep these four separate records or explicitly qualified claims:

1. **Validity:** exact declarations and consequences check under the allowed
   foundation; conjectures are never inserted as warranted facts.
2. **Content:** for this proposed abstraction, which models, distinctions,
   counterexamples, generalizations or connections have actually been proved?
3. **Practical leverage:** which further derivations, representations or
   procedures become usable, at what full acquisition/adaptation/research cost?
4. **Novelty:** new to which disclosed starting corpus or investigation? A
   literature/world-first claim needs separate prior-art investigation.

There is no need to make (2)-(4) universal admission predicates. They are local
research-selection and evaluation judgments. Many valuable concepts have one
canonical model, are deliberately empty under some parameters, or begin without
known applications. Requiring two nonisomorphic models from every contributor
would wrongly turn one evaluation heuristic into an ontology.

For a selected episode, however, concrete models and failed variants can defeat
specific bad explanations of apparent success. Test whether the candidate is
only an alias, a conjunction of copied statements, an empty hypothesis disguised
as a generalization, or a definition that encodes its desired answer. Supplied
equivalence/instance proofs can establish these relationships in particular
cases; no total semantic novelty decision procedure is presumed.

Counterexamples are positive mathematical contributions when formally warranted.
A timed-out search is not a counterexample or an impossibility result. Failing
to prove one candidate may rationally change a local investigator's next choice,
without changing the status of any separately proved side result.

## Minimal creative loop and candidate policies

Use ordinary Lean files, compiler/LSP feedback and the existing native package
route. The following are local policy stages, not new network protocol messages:

1. Inspect a bounded set of exact formal artifacts and actual proof patterns.
2. Propose several possible definitions/generalizations/representations. Examples
   are factoring repeated hypotheses, separating an invariant from a concrete
   implementation, or introducing a structure that makes two constructions
   instances. Neither the definition nor its endpoint is supplied by the harness.
3. Develop consequences and challenge the assumptions. Try small models,
   counterexamples to tempting weakenings, and checked maps to known structures.
4. Compare at least the viable proposal and its strongest simple alternative.
   Keep a bounded account of rejected variants rather than counting successes
   alone. Compression, applicability and proof/search cost are fallible local
   selectors, never acceptance authority.
5. Preserve the chosen coherent module, its exact foundation/imports and named
   proved interface. Use normal `produce-baseline`/`intake-baseline`, or supported
   contribution/completion, when inert independently checked sharing is needed.
6. Another investigator acquires the declared work and develops an unprescribed
   extension, model, counterexample or bridge, then returns checked outputs.

Theory formation is not automatically a strictly increasing chain of definitions.
Competing definitions, rejected hypotheses and revised interfaces can be useful
work. Immutable old versions remain available, but obsolete choices need not
receive permanent indexing/retention. No global canonical concept election is
required. A resource-limited diversity allowance can prevent one popularity
ranker from suppressing every unfamiliar abstraction; prior growth notes already
give its conditional limits, so no new policy theorem is needed here.

## Actual source support and constraints

The first thin-base integration is theorem-only **by selected workload**, not
because the core only permits theorem declarations. Direct source inspection:

- `ordinary_lean/CheckedExtension.lean:59-70` permits safe definitions, theorems,
  safe opaque declarations and inductive groups. Lines 130-134 perform actual
  `addDeclCore` checking; complete generated inventories are checked afterwards.
- `ordinary_lean/OriginalDecl.lean:223-257,295-376` encodes/decodes these four
  descriptor forms, including complete inductive type/constructor groups. It
  excludes arbitrary axioms and unsafe/partial declarations. Safely lowered
  ordinary definitions are distinct from an unsupported unsafe mutual group.
- `ordinary_thin_delta/ReferenceModule.lean:431-460` decodes those same units and
  calls the same allowed-declaration rule. Generated output kinds are 1..6;
  there is no theorem-only filter. Actual new checking occurs in both imported
  private and exported environments. Its public request cap is 32 units.
- `ordinary_module_anchor/OrdinaryModule04.lean` permits new kinds 1..6, while
  its special already-installed collision case is singleton-theorem-only. Do
  not generalize that collision exception to arbitrary existing definitions.

These are source facts, not runtime qualification for a new concept episode.
Complete semantic closure, supported group size, exact compatible base, public
computation and resource limits still determine whether a proposed theory fits.

A substantial remaining limitation is **frontend usability of new concepts**.
GROWTH-REUSE-FIDELITY already establishes that checked kernel structures and
projections do not automatically restore class registration, instance search,
structure syntax, notation or simp metadata. Same-base reuse preserves existing
recipient metadata; it does not automatically install a foreign new hierarchy's
metadata. Ordinary approved package authoring remains available. Explicit
constructors/projections and locally selected instances may suffice for a chosen
small theory; full generic metadata replay is not implicitly authorized.

Thus inspect this actual boundary during creative use. If a worthwhile theory
cannot be developed independently except by elaborate manual reconstruction,
that is evidence for a different packaging/frontend route. Do not silently
restrict concept proposals to fit a theorem-only demonstration and then report
that concept formation is supported generally.

## One decisive assessment and its decision outcomes

Select a **bounded open-ended theory handoff**, not another prewritten theorem.
Give investigator A a disclosed small, mathematically diverse compatible seed
corpus and a finite budget. Use existing ordinary Lean libraries; do not rebuild
a large dependency stack or manufacture a hard proof. Do not prescribe a concept
name, final definition, desired theorem or consumer proof. A chooses a coherent
abstraction and develops enough theory to make a serious handoff.

Seal A's declared interface, exact proofs, examples and cost/failure record.
Investigator B gets only that handoff and the shared base, not A's scratchpad,
private plan or a prewritten continuation. B chooses a further direction. Both
agents may use ordinary reasoning tools equally; no crippled local comparator.
Keep the investigator prompts/starting corpus as evaluation evidence, not core
metadata. Source-generated artifacts still need actual independent admission.

Before launching, root should set finite resource bounds and these outcomes:

- **Continue this architecture:** agent-originated abstraction plus checked
  substantive consequences survives a challenge and enables an independently
  chosen checked extension through the real software at acceptable total cost.
- **Revise the investigator policy, not the kernel:** only aliases/vacuous
  proxies or already supplied abstractions result; search fails; or B finds no
  substantive direction. Preserve useful side results and charge failures.
- **Revise the software boundary:** useful work exists, but compatible new
  definitions/groups, practical frontend use, discovery or return are blocked.
  Implement that named obstruction rather than increasing proof volume.
- **Reject an acceleration claim:** the ordinary local/sharing route achieves
  the same capability with no greater cost. Interoperability can still be useful;
  a successful creative episode is not itself a causal research-speedup study.

Do not demand new-to-literature success as a deterministic software test. Do
report whether the resulting notion is recognizable existing mathematics,
episode-relative invention, a new combination, or a presently unassessed novelty
claim. Real unprescribed construction and extension can be demonstrated without
claiming that no mathematician has previously thought of the same concept.

This decision uses source/theory to avoid another infrastructure detour. The
remaining informative uncertainty is what independent investigators can actually
construct and extend with the supported product. A small genuine investigation
now has higher marginal payoff than another generic growth lemma or packaging
campaign. Root must separately select its exact scope after the at-hand boundary.

## Established design precedents

Root inspected the primary [HR thesis abstract](https://era.ed.ac.uk/items/44d34b4f-90c9-4a99-a10c-3f4271c32214):
concept production, conjectures, and OTTER/MACE proving/countermodels form an
explicit theory-formation loop. Root also inspected the [QuickSpec overview](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.03721v1),
sections 1-2: typed term enumeration and testing suggest equations and prune
redundancy; testing is explicitly not proof, and semantic type distinctions can
improve relevance. These justify reusing mature investigation patterns, not an
installed Lean integration or our novelty claim. Fixed-signature equation search
alone is insufficient for the requested ability to invent new abstractions.
