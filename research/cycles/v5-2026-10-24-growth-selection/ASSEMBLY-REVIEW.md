## Harness and shared allowance

The harness selects only three disjoint steps:

1. Wrong source-map digest: requires nonzero exit, the exact
   `NATIVE_ASSEMBLY_INPUT_PIN:<source-root>/.../source-map.json` message, and no
   negative-output directory. In the recipe this check precedes destination
   creation; a different failure cannot satisfy the stated message condition.
2. One actual assembly into a fresh package directory, using the reviewed digest.
3. Actual public profile loading from that package, pinning the loader bytes and
   newly generated PROFILE; requires the exact 111-role map and the two allowed
