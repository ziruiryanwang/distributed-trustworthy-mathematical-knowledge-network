# Growth release thesis: usable transfer without an accumulating reuse tax

2026-10-24. Bounded theory/source decision by `growth_release_thesis`.
No product changes, imports, tests, builds, downloads, installations or Git work.
This note does not change the full v5 goal or authorize a successor experiment.

## Verdict

The existing theory is sufficient to justify **a plausible useful product**, not
to assert observed research acceleration. No additional philosophy of mathematics,
composition theorem, catalytic graph, rent-or-buy rule or LP illustration is
needed to establish the principle. The outstanding issue is whether the actual
product realizes affordable, usable transfer over a growing research history.

The commons need not defeat an ideal Git-plus-Lean stack that already contains
our checker and adapters. Supplying those adapters as reliable reusable software
is itself a contribution. Ordinary libraries supply most of the mathematics and
authoring tools; our incremental responsibility is the interoperable discovery,
selected evidence, independent acceptance, local use and continued custody path.
Do not require a new transport monopoly to justify that public good.

The next architectural question with greatest expected payoff is **the locality
of repeated use as a recipient's warranted library grows**. Existing full-chain
restoration is a concrete reason that safe accumulation may become increasingly
expensive even without repeated kernel proof checking. Fixing distribution alone,
or adding another method, does not resolve that question.

## Falsifiable product thesis

For compatible independently produced Lean developments, the software should make
selected reusable mathematics available to a recipient who does not approve the
producer's executable environment, let that recipient apply it in actual local
work, and let resulting useful extensions return to replaceable holders.

The thesis has four separately falsifiable premises:

| Premise | Required consequence | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|
| Execution approval is separated from mathematical reuse | Recipient uses inert checked evidence with its already approved tools | Recipient must execute the foreign producer package to obtain or use every result |
| The accepted interface is usable | A method is applied to genuine changed arguments/premises and the resulting theorem checks | Only a detached constant is admitted; needed representations or authoring facilities cannot be recovered affordably |
| Retention produces reusable capability | Subsequent supported uses and returned extensions remain obtainable and checkable | Retained metadata merely points at a vanished sole provider or requires its private authoring state |
| Accumulation has an affordable lifetime envelope | Charged discovery, cold adoption, warm restoration, adaptation, failures and service fit the intended supported research histories | Every small new use must revisit an ever-growing irrelevant local history, erasing the avoided work |

These are product mechanisms, not promises that arbitrary research succeeds.
The first three have scoped retained evidence in SUPPORTED-METHOD-PATH, not broad
ecosystem or hostile-input qualification. The fourth needs an explicit operating
region and a design response to the source-level scaling issue below. A whole
release also retains assurance, availability, security and deployment obligations.

The philosophical claim stays resource-bounded warranted capability, independent
of human intention at acceptance. Local investigation choices and judgments about
what is worth retaining are not a protocol-wide importance oracle. Parametric
methods, bridges and witness checkers are useful units already expressible in
ordinary Lean; a special global method language is unnecessary.

## What ordinary sharing must receive credit for

Reuse the comparator-simulation lemma in GROWTH-COMPARATOR-CHALLENGE. Give the
ordinary route the same discoveries, bases, caches, proof tools, usable adapters,
mirrors and publication timing when those are actually available. Do not force
it to rebuild everything, withhold a cheap tactic, or invent a central release
delay. Conversely, transporting files through Git does not implement an
independent inert importer for free: the real integration work belongs somewhere.

Conditional capability and full-cost advantage are different claims. The current
no-producer-code case removes a particular required approval/execution action.
It does not establish that code review otherwise takes many hours, that users
will adopt this policy, or that remaining costs are smaller. Those prevalence
and cost questions are empirical, not gaps repairable by another abstract lemma.

The fair conclusion is not "our network has unique mathematical power." It is
"this interoperable tool can make independently funded mathematics usable under
constraints that the recipient's previous supported workflow did not satisfy."
If ordinary tooling incorporates it, that is successful reuse of the product.

## Source-supported obstruction: warm reuse is not necessarily local work

Root highlighted the current selected restoration boundary; direct inspection
confirms it. `ordinary_lean/LocalSnapshot.lean:168-242` checks and imports the
complete protected one-parent chain. `local_catalog.py:224` explicitly warns that
a fresh worker still deserializes the chain. This is not a newly discovered bug.

**Adopt, do not rederive, ORDINARY-PRACTICAL-COST.md:126-174.** It already establishes:

- `portable_catalog.context` rebuilds selected lineage and binding information;
  reservation makes additional full-context metadata work.
- Fresh-owner integrity checking reads selected artifact bytes, and the workflow
  worker rebuilds allowed names from the parent environment.
- One bounded-size addition per operation can incur quadratic cumulative metadata
  visits in the number of additions, within configured bounds.
- Snapshots themselves contain deltas. Quadratic cumulative visits do not imply
  quadratic stored payloads or that Lean eagerly copies every ancestral proof.
- Native import memory, page touching and deserialization cost are separate;
  the explicit metadata loops already establish the issue without guessing them.

Thus "previous proofs are not rechecked" is true but insufficient as a growth
cost argument. Independence from the size of the worldwide network also does not
imply independence from a recipient's accumulated branch. A fixed hard limit
prevents runaway consumption by refusing work; it is not a sustainable-growth
argument unless the user has a workable way to stay inside useful local contexts.

## One decision to resolve before more growth engineering

Choose an explicit **bounded useful-context strategy** for the ordinary workflow,
and justify its lifetime costs and mathematical boundary from existing source.
Compare the already identified options; do not automatically build any of them:

- Existing batching and deliberately bounded specialty contexts are the cheapest
  first option. They must include the real cost of initial adoption, repeated
  bases and cross-context use; a local-agent instruction cannot erase those costs.
- Root-bound immutable metadata caching can remove repeated metadata work for
  the *same* warranted context. It does not by itself solve a new larger root on
  every contribution or fresh native environment restoration.
- A resident worker may amortize loading but changes the process-completion and
  promotion boundary, failure recovery, invalidation and resource accounting.
- Demand-scoped dependency views can omit irrelevant local history, but require
  exact rules for definitions, generated groups, conversion and imported warrants.
  Arbitrary pruning or accepting a peer's success flag is not such a rule.

The existing practical-cost note already frames these alternatives. The new
priority is to decide their product consequence now that method interchange and
portable composition no longer justify deferring it. Start with a source-level
bound/counterexample for the intended operation sequence. Only a remaining
constant or changed integration boundary should trigger a small execution.

This issue is distinct from **first-use compatibility**. The current materializer
imports Init or generated registered anchors, not arbitrary original projects.
NATIVE-FIRST-WORKFLOW-SELECTION explicitly warns against co-importing duplicate
original definitions; intake admits more than materialization can realize.
GROWTH-REUSE-FIDELITY explains missing class/notation/tactic metadata. Therefore
an ordinary-project example does not establish painless thin extensions of every
existing rich package. Retain that as a separate coverage limit; do not reopen the
parked original-reference work without a concrete supported-use decision.

**Priority between these two issues:** lifetime locality comes first. The approved
ordinary-package route already preserves rich authoring, with independent endpoint
validation; general thin rich-base interoperability is not the sole available
path. A bounded checked session is therefore a reasonable next feasibility
candidate, not an adopted daemon: first specify how each accepted extension has
an exact event warrant without relying on process exit, and how failure, budget
expiry and support withdrawal affect it. A metadata cache alone cannot establish
that amortization. Reopening the old reference branch would instead add another
compatibility investment before settling recurrent cost on the working route.

The earlier consumer-driven compromise remains explicit: bounded initial learning
and persistent use can be the ordinary mode; a requested ancestry-free cold mode
must still obtain its genuine warrant or refuse. This note does not silently
claim that native replay satisfies the stronger mode or manufacture its approval.

## Retain, remove from the default, and stop

Retain ordinary package authoring, selected inert interchange, exact independent
checking, explicit local budgets, existing search tools and replaceable custody.
Retain useful named methods, application bridges and optional structured witnesses.

Keep compulsory expensive certificates, universal method schemas, generic frontend
metadata replay, speculative global usefulness scores and exhaustive publication
outside the default path. Preserve earlier code/evidence; "outside the default"
does not mean delete it. Do not invest in them merely to enlarge the growth story.

Stop producing additional demonstrations of easy method composition. The missing
scientific variable is prevalence of worthwhile transfer, and the missing
engineering variable identified here is affordable repeated use. A contrived
local endpoint cannot estimate the former. Existing traces/source can resolve
much of the latter before another runtime.

This selects a **decision**, not an automatic cache/daemon/DAG implementation,
dependency installation, new benchmark, or completed-v5 label. It preserves the
high release bar while moving effort to an actual accumulated-cost bottleneck.
