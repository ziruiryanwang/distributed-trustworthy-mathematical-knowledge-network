## Decision standard

Separate three questions: does the route yield a usable checked capability;
does it improve resources for a stated history/policy; do people choose it in
practice? The first is constructive, the second needs an honest comparator and
cost premises, and the third cannot be decided by another local theorem fixture.
Lack of universal novelty/speed guarantees is not a reason to reject a useful
adapter. Equally, a successful adapter cannot stand in for the global release.

If a real existing workflow has an unresolved interface boundary, specify the
smallest source/theory question whose answer changes implementation. If all
needed operations already exist, do not invent a growth component. Name the
remaining uncertainty and choose work addressing it, rather than polishing an
unrelated mechanism. Source reasoning should decide what it can; execution is
reserved for an uncertain changed boundary with a finite decision criterion.
