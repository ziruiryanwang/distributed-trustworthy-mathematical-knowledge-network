# Investment challenge: grounded cuts in a concept-developing commons

2026-10-24. Independent growth_payoff_gate, source/theory only. Read the current
goal, creative-growth decision, retained-authoring decision, lifetime decision,
grounded-cut contract and its independent review, actual theory dependency trace,
and closed formal-search integration decision. No execution, protected-state
access, source implementation or Git mutation. Root decides adoption/selection.

## Decision

**Prefer a bounded grounded-cut source slice over a new cross-theory bridge-search
service now, but do not authorize a full backend or claim growth from the current
byte reduction.** The useful target is sequential independent admission during
continued theory development, not one isolated theorem or one final batch.

The source slice must settle indexed membership/freshness, exact local summary
authority, and one-theorem checking/commit/reopen together. A worker that checks
under caller-supplied assumptions alone is not the proposed capability. Stop
before native execution if those paths still traverse all proof history or if
they cannot preserve a distinct, genuine local acceptance origin. Reuse existing
supervision and codecs. Do not expand declaration kinds or invent a network lane.

This is a targeted scale repair of the existing growth workflow, not the whole
growth architecture. Its priority is conditional on the lifetime case below.
The 36-unit trace is a semantic feasibility witness only; its modest concrete
closure supplies no latency advantage over current native admission.

## What developing concepts and theories requires

An investigator must be able to introduce a mathematical interface, exhibit or
study models, derive laws, change hypotheses, and prove connections to another
interface. Other investigators must be able to obtain and continue that work.
This is richer than instantiating a fixed catalogue of questions or storing
isolated propositions. Ordinary Lean structures, definitions, inductives and
parameterized theorems already provide the expressive route; the retained A/B
episode and standard source package demonstrate a bounded instance of it.

The network need not choose research directions or contain a trusted concept
generator. Local agents can perform abstraction, analogy, conjecture and proof
search independently; their outputs must still reach the ordinary checking,
discovery and authoring-retention paths. Advisory tools can suggest poor concepts
without corrupting mathematical acceptance. Acceptance cannot establish worldwide
novelty, future importance or that a concept is not merely an inconvenient alias.

A theorem-only cut accelerator neither admits a new structure nor replaces this
authoring route. Its prospective contribution is to make further laws over an
already developed theory affordable as that theory and its proof ancestry grow.
Blocking new definitions in the entire product would contradict the goal;
restricting one optional local accelerator does not.

## A real lifetime discriminator, not an inflated benchmark

Consider rounds in which independently acting investigators publish a checked
extension, another investigator observes it and chooses the next investigation,
and each round requires a durable independent admission before the next decision.
Do not assume this timing for every project: a single investigator can often
author a large ordinary module and submit it once, where batching is preferable.

Let n rounds use the same bounded semantic vocabulary while exact theorem-proof
ancestry and the accepted catalogue grow. Suppose selected interfaces and concrete
semantic closures stay bounded, but required old witnesses grow with the rounds.
These are substantive workload assumptions, not consequences of correctness.

Existing catalogue context reconstruction and snapshot-plan traversal revisit
the parent history. If each round incurs at least a*i units of such work, total
restoration/metadata is at least a*n*(n-1)/2. This is NOT a claim that native
checking re-proves every old theorem. It is the existing explicit traversal cost.

A genuine indexed-cut implementation could instead charge initial migration A,
per-round indexed lookups/updates O(log N), bounded semantic reconstruction S,
new proof checking, export/retention and failed attempts. Thus it can remove this
particular quadratic term under the bounded-interface premise. Large growing
types or computational definitions can defeat the premise. No theorem says that
useful research always has compact interfaces or bounded proof-checking costs.

One final batch does not satisfy the stipulated intermediate-admission timing.
Small native roots do not erase genuinely required proof ancestry: charge its
re-admission or restoration when moving roots. Conversely, where small contexts
or batching already provide the required service cheaply, prefer them. A capable
Git+Lean+search baseline gets its source mirrors, ordinary incremental builds,
good tactics and caches; compare equivalent independent-checking and publication
obligations, not our checks against a deliberately impoverished authoring system.

This creates a nonempty conditional efficiency argument, not measured benefit.
Do not count proof-interface bytes saved as CPU saved: rebuilding concrete groups
can exceed cheap native import costs. Source inspection must first eliminate
hidden whole-root scans; only a later narrowly selected runtime can resolve an
uncertain implementation boundary or constants that would change adoption.

## Why the specific alternative is lower payoff now

A cross-theory bridge-search service is closer to creative discovery in name, but
the closed formal-search decision identified no new source-level obstruction
beyond a compatible approved corpus/provider. Existing native-pattern search can
already search independently named theories in principle. There is no selected
worthwhile independent corpus whose search failure a new matcher would repair.
Building a generator, matcher or provider now would test a speculative need.

Retain that alternative as soon as an actual useful corpus and failed discovery
case exist. Exact-symbol search remains incomplete across independently named
concepts; more exact-index replicas cannot repair it. Grounded cuts also cannot
repair that limitation. Do not relabel the cut work as semantic discovery.

## Minimum decision-changing evidence and stop rule

The current finite semantic trace already supports further source work; do not
repeat it or generate a fresh concept demonstration. The next source artifact
must show a complete local recursive path: derive summaries from an eligible
native root once; resolve exact demanded signatures and new-name absence through
an indexed root; check one theorem; commit a distinct event; reopen and use it
without replaying its ancestry. Check withdrawal, cross-branch and partial-commit
cases in the same design. No peer assertion may stand in for a local warrant.

If this requires full history inspection per event, collapses into scratch-axiom
trust, or changes ordinary return claims, stop that implementation. If it succeeds,
one finite changed-boundary run can be justified later, with full costs and an
honest native comparator. No general scaling, creativity or certificate campaign
follows automatically. A source-feasible accelerator is not a release.

Uptake and incentives remain separate: cheap sharing does not ensure anyone finds
or extends a contribution. The software's defensible growth mechanism is retained,
discoverable, independently warranted and extendable mathematical capability.
New abstractions and connections are produced by independent investigators; the
commons should reduce avoidable continuation costs without claiming to guarantee
their ingenuity or replace ordinary mathematical judgment.
