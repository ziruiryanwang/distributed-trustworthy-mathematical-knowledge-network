development. No missing implemented mathematical/public command edge was found

as release content/integration evidence, not as an excuse to add a downloader,
concept generator, search engine, correspondence oracle or blanket certificate.

The first three transitions need not discover the recipient's eventual connection
automatically. A new structure and its proved laws are legitimate contributions
even when no previous search anticipated them. Existing exact indexes can expose
published bridge declarations; ordinary advisory formal-pattern tools can suggest
unwritten cross-vocabulary connections. Neither becomes an authority for truth.

Public `PRODUCER.md` specifies project paths/modules, structured target names,
approval and bounds; owner dispatch at `run_producer` supplies the selected

inputs. `peer_api.publish` rechecks name/export/unit against that native context.
Nothing here requires a caller to construct a protected root or patch SQLite.
Current README/API crosswalk is expert-facing, not evidence of turnkey usability.
