## Architectural correction: two discovery routes, one acceptance rule

Exact known-symbol search answers a useful question: which indexed declarations
explicitly mention this mathematical interface? It does not adequately answer:
which independently named theories suggest a useful analogy or abstraction?
More replicas of the same exact index cannot repair that information boundary.

Local investigators may therefore use two replaceable routes:

- Exact discovery,preview,inspection and bounded authoring offers for already
  expressed connections and precise reuse.
- Ordinary formal-pattern or other advisory search for unfamiliar representations,
  followed by investigation in an approved Lean project.

The second route need not be implemented inside our trusted node. Existing Loogle
has repeated-metavariable/conclusion patterns and configurable corpus providers.
Its parsing,heuristic matching,provider coverage and cost are separate limitations.
The source discriminator found an expressible candidate route,not a successful
remote query or a worldwide recall guarantee. Reuse it before inventing machinery.

propose definitions,conjectures,models and bridges. Exact checked outputs enter
the commons through the existing independent admission path. There is no global
task list,no human-intention acceptance field,and no universal importance score.

## Why connections can create theories rather than additional aliases

The retained Repair interface is an idempotent map r:X->X. Follows(r,s) means
that s preserves every fixed point of r. Its proved schedule theory supplies
stable compositions when later operations preserve earlier constraints.
Independently,Lean Init contains Nat.mod_mod: (x mod n) mod n = x mod n.
Thus r_n(x)=x mod n supplies an arithmetic instance. Here mod0 is identity;
for n>0 its fixed states are exactly x<n. This is not a symbol renaming.

The following generalization explains this connection without relying on modulo
syntax. Let (X,<=) be any preorder. Call r a lower-fixed normalizer when:

1. r(r(x))=r(x) for every x;
2. r(x)<=x for every x;
3. Fix(r) is downward closed: y<=x and r(x)=x imply r(y)=y.

This is a proposed mathematical interface,not a new network object kind.
It can be expressed by ordinary Lean parameters,definitions and proof fields.

**Preservation lemma.** If r,s are lower-fixed normalizers,then Follows(r,s).
For fixed x of r,deflationarity gives s(x)<=x;downward closure makes s(x) fixed
by r. More generally this uses only downward closure of Fix(r) and deflationarity
of s;idempotence is not required for that particular implication.

**Closure lemma.** Write r*s=s composed with r (execute r,then s). The identity
map is a lower-fixed normalizer,and so is r*s. Deflationarity follows from
s(r(x))<=r(x)<=x. Since r(x) is fixed by r,the preservation lemma gives
r(s(r(x)))=s(r(x));idempotence of s then proves idempotence of the composite.
Its fixed set equals Fix(r) intersect Fix(s): its outputs are fixed by both,and
an x fixed by both is unchanged. Intersections of downward-closed sets are
downward closed. This proof needs no antisymmetry;an inequality chain alone would
not establish equality in an arbitrary preorder.

**Algebraic consequence.** Function equality gives associative composition,identity,
r*r=r,and (r*s)*r=r*s. Thus these maps form a left regular band with identity
under this execution-order convention. The last law follows directly from
r(s(r(x)))=s(r(x)). In any finite sequence,a later repetition of the same map
can be deleted:apply the law to r and the composite of the intervening maps,
which belongs to the class by closure. Iterate deletions while preserving the
order of first occurrences. This is not permission to reorder operations or
identify different maps merely because their fixed sets agree.
An executable deduplicator would additionally need decidable syntactic identity
or supplied equality evidence;the mathematical argument assumes no equality
decision procedure for arbitrary functions. No such deduplicator is selected.

**Concrete noncommuting model.** All Nat remainder maps r_n belong:mod_mod gives
idempotence,mod_le gives deflationarity,and their fixed sets are initial segments
or all Nat when n=0. At x=5,r_4*r_3 gives1 whereas r_3*r_4 gives2. Consequently
the schedule laws are substantive but do not imply commutation. The modulo family
is not asserted closed under composition;the larger normalizer class is.

## What this justifies,and what it does not

A reusable concept can turn one local correspondence proof into access to an
entire parametric theory:here arbitrary finite schedules,not just one numerical
example. An investigator may recognize the common invariant,propose the general
interface,prove its laws,then expose both concrete models and connections. That
is an actual mechanism for theory development,distinct from counting declarations.

The network's role is to make independently developed interfaces and connections
accessible and independently checkable. It need not contain the creative policy
inside its trusted core. Checked bridge declarations subsequently become exact
search links;advisory exploration can therefore improve future precise discovery.
This feedback is conditional on content,successful investigation and retention,
not a theorem of sustained or exponential mathematical progress.

The comparator keeps ordinary Git,Lean,search,good tactics,caches and the option
to derive its own general theorem. Do not compare repeated bespoke schedule proofs
against a shared general theorem while forbidding that baseline optimization.
The local Init example is already available to both routes:it justifies no network
advantage. Full-cost advantage requires access or integration savings in an actual
workflow to exceed discovery,failed search,adaptation,checking and provider costs.
No abstract restatement of that inequality supplies its missing magnitudes.

The defensible release aim remains:useful obtainable software for independently
proposing,developing,checking,sharing and continuing theories,with affordable
resource envelopes and honest scope. Passing advisory tests or finding this
conceptual bridge does not waive scalable discovery,assurance,availability,
hostile-input isolation or public-deployment obligations.

## Stop rules for additional growth engineering

No extra matcher,concept generator,ranking authority,certificate campaign or
repeated creative episode is selected. Reopen a mechanism only for a named real
obstruction:available relevant corpus cannot be queried usefully;an actual project
cannot be continued through public operations;or full-cost analysis identifies
an avoidable dominant cost. Source reasoning can establish such an obstruction;
experiments are reserved for consequential unresolved implementation questions.
