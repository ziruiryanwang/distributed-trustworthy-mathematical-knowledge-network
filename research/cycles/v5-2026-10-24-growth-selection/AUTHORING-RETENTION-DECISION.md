## Architecture decision

Independent custody of checked declarations is not necessarily custody of an
extendable ordinary Lean project. Our native-first creative workflow depends on
the latter for frontend facilities. A source locator is not retention. Therefore
a claim of durable practical theory continuation must identify both:

1. Selected exact mathematical declarations/evidence and their supported warrant.
2. An obtainable supported authoring realization, including its non-reconstructible
   sources, dependency closure, compatible toolchain and build instructions.

These need not be one object or provider. An already supported inert realization
can supply the second role when sufficient; ordinary Git/Lake project retention
is the general practical route. Availability, source execution approval and exact
mathematical correspondence remain three distinct obligations. Source approval
is recipient-local; it is not a test of mathematical truth or human intention.
No new global registry, acceptance predicate, source-host protocol or certificate
is selected. A valid theorem-only contribution remains valid without a project.

For a chosen research operation, let R be the finite set of required retrievable
inputs after accounting for documented reconstruction. Retaining every member of
R and a functioning compatible build environment removes loss of those inputs as
an obstruction to that operation. It does not prove successful research, affordable
reconstruction, independent failure tolerance or indefinite availability. Exact
dependency closure matters: Git commits omit submodule contents, LFS blobs and
external/generated dependencies unless separately retained. A toolchain name is
not possession of that toolchain. Existing Git/Lake users receive full credit for
this same mechanism; no incremental speed theorem is asserted over those users.
