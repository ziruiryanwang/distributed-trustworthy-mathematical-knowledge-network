## Why unchanged deductive closure is the wrong objection

Restrict this paragraph to supported conservative definitional extensions of a
fixed coherent foundation E. Eliminating new definitions translates old-language
consequences back to E; this alone creates no new old-language truths. Elimination
can nevertheless expand representations, and knowing that a proof exists gives
no algorithm-independent bound on finding it. Named interfaces, known witnesses
and bridges change what a finite investigator can actually obtain and rule out.
Neither definition count nor merely shorter displayed syntax measures that effect.

**Justified conceptual discrimination** distinguishes law packages, refutes exact
generalizations or excludes proposed representations. It can guide better concept
creation without implying new logical truths or universal scientific value.

## Proposition 1: checked separating models safely shrink a local search space

Let E be a consistent, warranted checking environment. Fix any finite local
family H of exact closed conjectures q_j. This is an investigator's optional
analysis device, not a network task list or mathematical admission requirement.
For a typical concept comparison let X encode candidate structures and set

    q_j := forall x : X, A_j x -> B_j x.

An ordinary record m : X with checked a : A_j m and b : Not (B_j m) yields

    r_j : Not q_j := fun h => b (h m a).

It simultaneously demonstrates an actual instance of A_j and a failure of B_j.
X need not be finite and the predicates need not be decidable: the actual terms
must check. A timeout, failed tactic, unverified model or sampled test is not r_j.
Let F(W) contain exactly the indices with available checked refutations from W.

**Claim.** Removing F(W) from H cannot remove a conjecture provable in E.
Adding checked refutations monotonically shrinks the remaining candidate set;
it shrinks strictly when an additional previously unrefuted index is eliminated.
These eliminations remain valid in a consistent compatible conservative extension of E.

**Proof.** A proof of q_j together with r_j produces False, contradicting
consistency. Adding records cannot remove old refutation terms. Weakening embeds
the terms in the compatible extension. These three observations give the claims.
They prove exact non-implication, not universal progress or importance.

Equal-looking labels do not identify conjectures. Different universes, meanings,
axioms, added hypotheses or representation maps require their own checked terms.
A model of A and not B does not refute a theorem about a narrower class A'.
Nor does it exclude a useful translation to another carrier. A claim-specific
transport can reuse a countermodel; syntactic similarity cannot supply it.
The size of F counts eliminated local branches, not distinct mathematical ideas;
aliases must not be advertised as additional conceptual growth.

## Proposition 2: a sharp, limited finite-budget consequence

Consider a disclosed local investigation pass with candidate trials 1,...,n.
Success means a checked proof of the trial's exact conjecture in E or its
compatible conservative extension, not an arbitrary useful side output.
The strong ordinary baseline may use Git/Lake, libraries, search, countermodel
tools, tactics and caches. Assume only that its retained trials are independently
replayable: removing another trial does not change their inputs, random seeds,
tools, useful side results or costs. Let c_i be each baseline trial's actual
charged resource cost, including its own testing and checking. Use one explicit
resource measure; multidimensional budgets require the following inequalities
componentwise, and wall-time needs a compatible sequential/no-interference model.

A sharing route independently validates exact refutations for a subset F and
skips those trials. Let h_j be all additional charged costs through completion
of retained trial j: discovery (including misses), transfer, compatible adoption,
validation, bookkeeping and the attributed publication/retention/service costs.
Common costs remain in both routes; obtaining a refutation is never free.

For every retained successful trial j, its completion costs satisfy exactly

    T_share(j) - T_base(j) = h_j - sum_{i <= j, i in F} c_i,

when h_j includes all route differences and the replayability premise holds.
Consequently the sharing route preserves every baseline success by each given
budget boundary if h_j does not exceed that removed prefix cost at those successes.

**Proof.** Sum the unchanged retained trial costs in their original order and
add h_j. The omitted trials cannot yield sound proofs of their refuted claims by
Proposition 1. Subtract the baseline prefix sum. The stated prefix condition,
not merely a positive lifetime total, gives the budget-boundary conclusion.

**Necessary cautions/counterexamples.** If the first useful trial costs one unit
and an up-front lookup costs two, a one-unit budget loses that success even if
many later false trials would be avoided. If a failed trial discovers a lemma
needed later, removing it violates replayability. A creative adaptive investigator
can change its concepts and schedule after seeing a countermodel; its history no
longer has this coupling. Such a change may be valuable, but this proposition
does not prove it beneficial. No probability of invention or proof-length lower
bound is smuggled in. Cheap local refutation can make external lookup pointless.

This safe-pruning/prefix theorem is not the prior generic investment inequality:
aggregate savings alone do not guarantee greater resource-bounded capability.
