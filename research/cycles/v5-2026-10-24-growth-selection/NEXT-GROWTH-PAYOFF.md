## Formal distinction that changes the design

For a selected theory T and recipient profile P, distinguish:

- V(T): selected mathematical declarations have actual warrants under P.
- D(T): selected declarations and exact dependency evidence remain retrievable.
- A(T,P): a compatible ordinary authoring realization is obtainable and its
  execution has separately been approved by the recipient; alternatively an
  already supported inert realization supplies the required authoring facilities.

In this architecture neither V(T) nor D(T) implies A(T,P). A counterexample is
a checked new structure whose inert declarations remain on replicas, while the
only ordinary project containing its class/instance/notation metadata disappears.
Prior GROWTH-REUSE-FIDELITY establishes that current general inert realization
does not recover all such facilities. The counterexample does not assert that
the mathematics is unprovable or cannot be manually reconstructed; reconstruction
is additional real research/integration work, not preserved authoring capability.

Consequently independent mathematical custody is insufficient to justify the
selected claim "another investigator can economically continue this theory after
its author disappears." One must retain a supported realization or pay/document
the reconstruction path. No new logical rule is needed; this is an availability
dependency of the actual native-first workflow, not a philosophical novelty test.

Exact correspondence also remains separate. A retained project at a Git commit
is not a mathematical warrant. The existing produce-baseline target identities
and independent admission path establish the relevant exported relationship;
source execution still needs its own approval. Do not promote an offer or build
success into either of those authorities.

## Why this is growth work, and its strongest counterargument

A new concept is useful partly because others can revise its definitions,
construct models, weaken hypotheses and connect it to independently developed
theories. Those operations need a usable authoring realization, not only a
statement preview. Losing that realization can convert cheap continuation back
into manual reconstruction even though all retained theorems remain true.
Preserving it removes that specific avoidable loss; it does not guarantee any
recipient chooses it, discovers an important extension or saves resources overall.

Optimized Git+Lean+formal search already supports source mirrors and continuation.
Give it full credit. Our incremental claim should be interoperable association
between those ordinary projects and selected independently warranted mathematical
exports, not a uniquely creative network or a speed theorem over equivalent tools.
Existing tools are the preferred implementation of this retention layer.

The counterargument is that contributors can arrange all this themselves. That
is correct; source hosting need not enter the trusted core. But then the release
must supply a complete supported operator workflow or explicitly depend on an
existing one. "Somebody could write a controller and mirror the missing project"
is an architectural possibility, not completed practical availability evidence.

No new abstract growth inequality, importance oracle, concept generator, matcher,
benchmark, certificate campaign or automatic global source replication follows.
Independent investigators remain responsible for research choices. Selective
retention costs and source licensing remain real; mathematics acceptance remains
independent of human intention, popularity and the source author's identity.
