# Creative growth: stop expanding seams; make the supported loop obtainable

2026-10-24. Independent source/product challenge. No execution, service, download,
owner-state access or Git mutation. Recommendation to root, not a runtime selection.
The full deployable v5 goal remains active and unfinished.

## Decision

**After the selected authoring-offer boundary, add no new growth mechanism. Make
one fresh clean-source distribution successor include the already completed
investigator operations.** This is the next executable product change recommended
here. Do not create another concept generator, theory registry, novelty metric,
offer format, generic importer, research benchmark or workflow framework.

The reason is concrete: an ordinary researcher can already invent abstractions
with Lean, and our retained A/B episode confirms independent creative continuation.
The commons adds candidate access, exact inert inspection, optional independent
checking and replaceable publication. These are plausible useful services. But
the maintained source-build composition does not yet ship the latest operations;
the current join depends on a succession of historical experiment packages and
centrally prepared configurations. Adding another advisory seam would not fix it.

This is not a recommendation to stop research or claim growth solved. It is a
decision not to turn every unresolved scientific question into another component.

## What the user should actually be able to do

An independent investigator starts with a working approved Lean project and
locally chosen questions, not a globally assigned task. The investigator:

1. Uses familiar libraries and ordinary formal/source search, optionally asking
   selected commons providers for connections to known formal interfaces.
2. Inspects the returned statement and unfamiliar concepts before expensive use.
3. Chooses an obtainable ordinary project or a supported inert reuse route.
   Retrieval/execution approval is a real local decision, not missing automation.
4. Changes definitions, explores models, proves bridges, rejects failed variants
   and develops an independently chosen theory using the ordinary authoring tools.
5. Offers selected checked declarations and sufficient ordinary project information
   so another investigator can continue, rather than only count the endpoints.

The network is a research commons, not a theorem generator hidden inside its
trusted node. Independent human, learned or symbolic investigators are components
of the complete system. Providing their tools/API contract can be enough; a new
central model service would add neither mathematical authority nor a guarantee
of original ideas. Local investigator policies can change independently.

## Four different claims

| Claim | Present warrant | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| Concepts/theories can be developed and continued | A independently chose asymmetric repairs; B developed closure-order bridges and countermodels; ordinary compilation and inert endpoint checks succeeded | World-first novelty or creativity caused by this network |
| Selected unfamiliar connections can be inspected | A-symbol find, exact preview and one-unit `OrderData` inspection completed | Relevant large-corpus recall, blind discovery, or accepted truth from inspection |
| Someone can obtain a practical project | Native project authoring works; advisory offers provide a missing locator channel | Synthetic `example.invalid` offers are not obtainable projects, correspondence or source availability |
| The software lowers full research cost | Existing conditional capability/cost arguments identify possible benefit | Its magnitude, prevalence, sustained acceleration, or a speed advantage over good ordinary sharing |

The first two are not substitutes for the third. The first three together do not
prove the fourth. Conversely, the absence of a universal speedup theorem does not
invalidate an affordable interoperable implementation of the first three.

## The strongest surviving growth mechanism, with its counterfactual

The useful causal chain is **independently developed structure becomes accessible
before another investigator must reconstruct it; the investigator then spends
resources on a different extension or connection**. Definitions, models and
representation bridges matter because they change what further problems can be
expressed or attacked economically, not just how many proofs are stored.

Ordinary Git+Lean+search can realize the same chain. Give that baseline the same
available mathematics, normal branching, mirrors, caches, good tactics and local
ingenuity. Our incremental contribution can be a maintained shared protocol and
optional inert assurance path; it need not be a uniquely powerful invention.
Do not attribute the A/B mathematics to our transport or charge the comparator
for an invented centralized merge delay.

There is no theorem that more communicated concepts always help. A larger set of
interfaces can increase incompatible dependencies, adaptation work, low-value
search results and researcher distraction. A ranker/popularity count may improve
selection, but cannot justify the selected concept's importance or mathematical
truth. Preserve an ordinary local route that does not wait for network exploration.

The relevant conceptual gain is often a change of representation or a bridge
between frameworks. It cannot be inferred from corpus size or exact hash novelty.
The current exact-symbol index can find explicitly expressed connections sharing
known symbols; it cannot supply a connection that nobody has expressed, nor make
arbitrarily equivalent representations discoverable. An investigator may create
that bridge and publish it as an ordinary theorem. This is a limit to model, not
an immediate reason to build an ontology or semantic-equivalence oracle.

## Source-grounded deployment obstruction

The source build map currently selects:

- `peer.py` and `peer_api.py` from `ordinary_preview_package` (map lines 431-432).
- `profile.py` from `ordinary_native_package` (line 434).
- Both transport modules from `software/v5/transport` (lines 449-450).
- No `support/transport/authoring_offers.py` payload.

Thus building that source composition does not reconstruct the completed
inspector plus the selected advisory-offer successor. The file-only successor
is useful evidence, but not a source release someone can independently build.
Do not repair this by referencing an ignored historical experiment package from
the new build map. Use reviewed repository sources and the existing build recipe.

The minimal successor changes the six logical Python roles selected for offers,
including the inspector facade already contained there, and corresponding exact
map/census/documentation. Preserve the native source/toolchain and all used bytes.
Source review should establish which compilation inputs remain identical before
deciding whether any native execution has information value. Do not rebuild Lean
merely to retest the same two-read inspection or the same A/B proofs.

## A finite criterion, not another open-ended campaign

The proposed source successor must satisfy these source-level conditions:

1. A checkout plus the documented supported runtimes supplies every build input;
   no experiment directory, private accepted state or retained historical binary.
2. The exact package map includes `find`, `preview`, `inspect`, `authoring-offers`,
   ordinary production and independent intake with their actual reviewed bindings.
3. One public investigator workflow names the inputs/outputs at every transition:
   discovery result -> selected interface; offer -> independently approved project;
   produced target rows -> exact comparison; admitted targets -> publication.
   Each required authority and each deliberately manual choice remains explicit.
4. Existing saved evidence is reused for unchanged operations. Only a changed
   source-map/assembly/loader boundary earns a focused control, if source analysis
   leaves that boundary uncertain. This is not an invitation to rerun A/B.

A public API requiring configuration is not automatically unusable. Its values
must be obtainable through public outputs or explicit user choices. Do not build
a convenience wrapper merely because root previously wrote configurations by
hand. If the transition requires information unavailable through those means,
identify that missing information first; a wrapper cannot invent it.

Actual hosted source availability and security approval remain unresolved. A
local synthetic metadata test cannot close them, and no new external repository,
license, hosting service or execution permission is presumed by this recommendation.

## When to reopen a new mechanism

Reopen only on a named reproducible obstruction: useful independently supplied
content cannot be found through available ordinary/formal search within its
selected scope; a chosen project cannot expose an obtainable required input;
or a supported checked return requires private-state manipulation. The evidence
may be source/data reasoning; an expensive new creative episode is not necessary.
Then choose the smallest change eliminating that particular obstruction.

Do not reopen a generator because new theories are not guaranteed, an index because
global completeness is impossible, or a certificate because ordinary checking has
nonzero cost. These conclusions do not identify a positive-payoff implementation.
Large-scale selective discovery, accumulated local costs, hostile-input isolation
and independent availability remain release obligations; this finite integration
does not waive them or justify a final-v5 label.
