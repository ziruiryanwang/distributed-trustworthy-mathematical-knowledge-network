```text
ha : a = a', hb : b = b', hc : c = c'
-------------------------------------------------------------
f (g a b) (h b c) = f (g a' b') (h b' c').
```

Its explicit proof applies the genuine BASE `CommonsV5.binary_congr` three
times: once for g, once for h, and once for f. The middle equality is shared.
The universe instances are `(u,u,v)`, `(u,u,w)`, `(v,w,z)`. This is a reusable
parametric application-context transformation, not a numerical equality,
renaming or a difficult new mathematical discovery. Its established value here
is testing certification of a real new typed composition from a genuine parent.

`d : D` and `hd : f(g a' b')(h b' c') = d`, then concludes

by `Eq.trans (U f g h ha hb hc) hd`, with all implicit type/term arguments
preserved by the existing telescope. This really uses U's theorem interface;
