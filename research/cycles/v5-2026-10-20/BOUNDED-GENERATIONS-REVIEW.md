## Concrete review findings corrected before execution

1. **Returned-but-unattached service leak.** The original composer called
   `PlacementService.open`, then `_attach`; attachment failure left
   `handle.service=None`, so generic handle cleanup skipped the acquired service
   stores/lock. The corrected composer retains that exact provisional local
   service and independently attempts its close and the locator close. It
   preserves the primary exception with at most three static cleanup notes and
   records/holds UNKNOWN rather than converting unvalidated attachment into
   positive CLOSED authority.
2. **Retirement disabled automatic shutdown.** Setting `_closing=True` before
   waking the exact service monitor suppressed its `finally` branch, which
   schedules the tracked close only if not already closing. The corrected
   manager revokes `grant.closed` and sets `_wake`, leaving `_closing` to the
   existing close task. New work is refused by the existing grant guard, while
   both standalone retirement intent and normal composer closure preserve the
   finite shutdown path.
3. **Late startup could erase retirement intent.** The underlying service
   startup can complete before its shielded caller resumes. A concurrent owner
   retirement in that interval writes RETIRING; the original `_started` then
   accepted RESERVED and overwrote the phase with core OPEN/SEALED_SERVING.
   The corrected transition requires PROVISIONING/OPEN/SEALING/SEALED_SERVING
   before any positive update, then reruns `service._active()`. RETIRING and a
   revoked/expired service cannot become LIVE through that continuation. The
   existing failure/close path preserves the retirement intent and charge.

The root's separate prior partial-open finding is also correctly handled:
`_release` attempts both database and owner-lock closure even if the first
fails, poisons uncertainty and preserves the opening exception. None of these
source corrections edits the already used installed service or old evidence.
