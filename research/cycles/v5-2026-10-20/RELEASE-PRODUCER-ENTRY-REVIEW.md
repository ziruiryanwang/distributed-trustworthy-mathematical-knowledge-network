but is not certification. Request construction does not preflight twice. The
entry never chooses or increases an allowance, stage budget or deadline. Saved
reports remain observations, not resource or mathematical authority.

The run success checks are stronger than mere existence of `RESULT.json`.
Its byte count and digest must equal `encoded(actual)`; status must be exactly
`PASS_SELECTED_PREFIX_ONLY` or `PASS_FINISHED_RECEIPT_ARTIFACT`, with the exact
owner profile, `closure_accounting_known is True` and
`final_binding_validated is True`. Those flags arise from the unchanged real
scheduler, which requires clean process closure and bound stage artifacts.
It is not necessary to reimplement that scheduler in the entry.

The report records attempted dispatch and actual returned counters. Before
calling run, host/proof extent becomes unknown (`None`), so a raised call is
not falsely described as zero work. A successful prefix is not a finished
certificate, and even a finished producer artifact is not receiver acceptance.
