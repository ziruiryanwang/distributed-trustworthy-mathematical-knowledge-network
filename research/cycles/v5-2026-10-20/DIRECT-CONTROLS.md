## Nine changed-boundary cases

1. Successful bind performs no I/O, uses the original grant/budget, prepays the
   full search allowance once, copies caller configuration, uses the exact
   guarded search/structural acquisition, and issues no authority.
2. Wrong client type, invalid labels, duplicate endpoints, policy-disallowed
   loopback and public plaintext inputs refuse before a dial or prepayment.
3. Missing/disagreeing adopted helper pins refuse before search attachment.
   This tests pin requirements, not genuine issuer construction/source checking.
4. Rebinding or an already-used query slot cannot renew the allowance.
5. Search requests/reply bytes/providers must fit their original suballocation.
6. One captured-origin consistency control disconnects acquisition from its
   search and requires contact-guard refusal before dialing.
7. Expired contacts refuse both search and acquisition without renewal; search
   abandonment and acquisition unknown-outcome charges remain conservative.
8. A structurally valid record not returned by the bound search cannot resolve.
9. One actual IPv4 loopback offer -> authenticated candidate page -> exact
   structural group resolution; select the nonempty partition from returned
   metadata, not from a supplied result ID. Confirm retained custody, original
   ledger charges, zero verification, normal client closure and drained server.
