## Concrete complete-proof decision

Recommend a prospective **128-segment ceiling, one I assumption**, same po18
geometry and unchanged target. This is an explicit engineering-investment cap,
not a theorem about affordability or an inferred demand forecast. Root may adopt
or reject this budget *before* executing the changed guest; do not tune it after
seeing the result. At 128 segments it removes at least 117 of the old 502 host
calls (23.31%), with no numerical runtime saving claimed before observation.

For s segments and m assumptions the existing graph is

`s segment + s lift + (s-1) join + m resolve + 1 finish = 3s+m calls`.

With the existing maximum stage CPU/wall allowances, its sums are

`worker_CPU_cap = 1700s + 600m + 50`,

`stage_wall_cap = 540s + 600m - 30`.

For s=128,m=1 these are 385 calls, **218250 worker CPU seconds** and
**69690 summed stage-wall seconds**. They are conditional authorization bounds,
not predicted completion times. Recommend one production allowance:

- 220000 worker CPU seconds; 7200 controller CPU seconds;
- 75600 cumulative active-attempt wall seconds;
- 1152 MiB new output; 4000 entries;
- unchanged per-kind stage CPU/wall/output limits; one proof worker at a time;
- one prefix attempt and at most one continuation, identical allowance/profile/
  session/budget, all prior charges and successful checkpoints carried forward.

The existing `_envelope` checks the complete missing graph even when selecting
only a prefix. At the maximum 385 calls, its conservative output charge is

### Decision and continuation rules

1. **Fresh build/binding failure:** preserve it; no proof and no automatic rebuild.
   A specific source defect may justify a new separately reviewed action.
2. **One complete matched execution:** require same selected mathematics and
   genuine premise, completed capsule and actual segment histogram. If s>128,
   the proposed complete-proof allocation is not selected. Preserve the result;
   do not replace the theorem, increase limits or rerun unchanged input.
3. **If s<=128 and bindings/review pass:** root may fund the complete allowance.
   Begin with five real graph calls: segment0, lift0, segment1, lift1, join1.
   For the unusual s=1 result use its actual smaller graph instead. The ordinary
   five-call prefix has caps 3150 worker CPU seconds and 930 stage-wall seconds.
4. **Prefix failure or unknown closure/accounting:** stop this allowance, preserve
   all evidence. No repeated stage, widened cap or fresh allowance concealing it.
   Its concrete error, not RAM reconnaissance, determines any next source fix.
5. **Prefix success:** independently audit actual checkpoints and accounting,
   then continue only missing graph nodes under the same complete allowance and
   exact source/session pins. Existing `prior_attempts`/`missing_plan` enforce
   retained successes and unchanged budget. Prefix timing is not an upper bound
   for later segments, assumption resolution, finalization or total duration.
6. **Production success:** require actual I assumption discharge, complete
   segment composition, resolved succinct receipt, exact final image/header
   verification and independent receipt/membership acceptance. The current host
   already verifies both full composite integrity and final succinct claim.
7. **Renewal use:** use FIRST via its real A-prime opening for unchanged SECOND;
   require exact certified-parent binding and checking of only the new suffix.
   A separately finite SECOND production is necessary before claiming observed
   same-A-prime renewable certification; first assess its actual measured graph
   against the same full-cost decision discipline. Do not relabel native SECOND.

## Fixed-size stages: what has and has not been solved

Typed decoding reduces work presented to the zkVM, not the workspace of an
individual full-size proof stage. The already-built allocation correction is
not a new optimization. The current sources provide no small additional change
that provably removes fixed po18 proving/recursion cost. The five-call prefix
is an early falsification gate containing useful final-graph work, not a new RAM
experiment and not a guarantee that every remaining stage succeeds. Later
resolve/finish remain necessary and unqualified until they actually complete.

There is **no evidence here of an insurmountable PC blocker**. The sensible action
is a once-only bounded useful prefix after a meaningful whole-graph reduction,
not indefinite resource surveys. Keep proof production serial on this PC while
independent lightweight source/theory work proceeds in parallel.

## Genuinely different route considered

A restricted proof-carrying composition relation could verify only authenticated
theorem instantiation, explicitly typed substitution and equality composition,
with a proved embedding of each rule into the Lean-based policy. Its interface
would bind exact parent certificate/type/universe identities, substitution terms
and types, the resulting proposition and the target module/J; reject unsupported
reduction rather than silently assuming it. Such a relation can avoid general
kernel reconstruction for its restricted language and is materially different
from parsing optimization or proof caching.

It is **not the higher-payoff next implementation**. FIRST proves an induction
law; merely applying previously certified theorems cannot establish it unless
an adequate method is already certified. SECOND is a promising composition use,
but its polymorphic applications and definitional equality still require a sound
typed substitution/conversion implementation and receiver policy integration.
The currently selected producer does not implement this relation. No source-backed
short implementation is available that preserves FIRST while eliminating these
obligations. A Merkle opening or native success cannot supply the missing theorem.

Similarly, fixed-foundation parser cloning has no reuse advantage for this one-
delta target and direct-I FIRST has no observed cheap path; neither demonstrates
same-A renewal. Therefore prefer the already implemented typed refinement with
the complete endpoint above. If the matched threshold fails, the next producer
research should target an exact restricted composition/checker refinement, not
another capsule/layout sweep; its adoption must precede any proof experiment.
