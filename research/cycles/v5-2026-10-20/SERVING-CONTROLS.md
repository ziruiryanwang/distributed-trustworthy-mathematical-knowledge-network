## Product boundary

The new ordinary `client.serve_workspace` entry opens an existing persistent
workspace, constructs its exact typed HTTP provider, exposes the existing
read-only remote routes for a finite original-deadline-bounded interval, then
stops the listener, drains requests and closes owned storage. No mathematical
owner or certificate is constructed. Local metadata can change on opening or
querying; remote read-only service does not mean filesystem immutability.

These controls use only **fresh synthetic structural workspaces**. The one
descriptor is deliberately uncertified. A syntactically valid theorem
descriptor is not a mathematical theorem accepted by this system. All service,
discovery, fixture and final reports must preserve that distinction.

The network control makes four bounded HTTP exchanges total: offer and group
in each of two fresh lifetimes. Additional loopback listeners exist solely for
deadline/cancellation/partial-start controls, with one waiting connection in the
stop-failure control. No public endpoint is contacted.
The group route retains its inherited 16 MiB reservation, so the provider has
a 32 MiB aggregate reply allowance; each actual client response allowance is
65,536 bytes. No payload padding or large fixture is generated.
