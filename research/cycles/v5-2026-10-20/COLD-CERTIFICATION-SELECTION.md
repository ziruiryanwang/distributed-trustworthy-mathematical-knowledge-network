## Selection rule

Prefer an action that removes a central uncertainty or fixes a demonstrated
failure over another measurement of an already adequate component. A runtime
selection must name the question, alternatives changed by its result, existing
evidence it cannot replace, finite cost and exact new output. A small passing
control is not evidence of complete certification or a releasable v5.
