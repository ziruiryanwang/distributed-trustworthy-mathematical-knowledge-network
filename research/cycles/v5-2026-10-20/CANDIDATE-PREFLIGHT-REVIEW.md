## Acceptance-boundary argument

Let a trial start under the selected original candidate, acquisition, budget,
contact, profile and deadline. The source implements the following separation.

1. Trusted caller shape, expected exact-journal syntax, offer membership, byte
   limits and source origins are checked outside recoverable parsing. The scalar
   packet entry is copied before await. _trial_packet_bytes captures original
   owners and caps with the existing exact-class _binding helper, plus packet
   profile/envelope identity and contents, limits and HTTP-counter owner.
2. Exactly one original budget ticket surrounds one transfer. Its finally
   settles the original captured budget even if the acquisition's budget field
   is replaced. Unknown transfers charge the reserved reply maximum. Non200,
   cleanup, cancellation, binding and settlement errors escape; there is no retry,
   refund, new grant or replacement contact. The method itself does not parse,
   write custody or call a verifier.
3. A returned transfer has completed bounded client socket closure. HTTP/TLS,
   fixed selected Content-Length and EOF checks are preserved. writer.close or
   wait_closed failure cannot become a recoverable candidate result; timeout is
   bounded by the original deadline and one-second closure ceiling. Cancellation
   remains fatal. A closure failure can replace an earlier transport error but
   cannot convert either into success. This is not a general cleanup guarantee
   for inherited provider/search/strict transport routes.
4. The facade rechecks source origins and charges raw input before hashing or
   parsing. The pure helper checks trusted envelope/profile/summary first, then
   compares immutable byte count/digest. Its only recoverable catch encloses
   inspect_evidence, not acquisition, local custody or admission.
5. Only exact StructureError or DeltaError can be classified as policy-relative
   refusal. Their chain may contain at most16 distinct exact reviewed decoder
   types. Unexpected subclasses/types, direct or wrapped memory/recursion errors,
   cycles, excessive chains and divergent cause/context branches rethrow. This
   accounts for the existing codec's wrapped RecursionError without changing the
   codec or pretending declared structural limits prove resource exhaustion.
6. A parsed different selected target is a local policy-relative rejection.
   A parsed journal inconsistent with the caller's explicit expected J remains
   fatal before custody. An unknown schema or disabled I capability is not
   labeled mathematically false or well-formed unsupported mathematics.

Therefore a normal CANDIDATE_REJECTED_UNDER_SELECTED_POLICY return is a paid,
settled, nonauthoritative observation about one selected peer packet. It cannot
mint a warrant, bypass a verifier or certify that a theorem is false. The new
branch preserves existing work by absence of mutation, not rollback after an
error. Rejection returns requested selection, received digest/size, target and
explicit no-mathematical-acceptance labels.

## Valid paths, local failure and final liveness

Valid preflight proceeds to ordinary PacketStore.put, exact readback,
custody_ready and _admit_evidence outside the parser catch. Even StructureError
from that second local inspection is fatal. Existing positive issuer/cache/
warrant checks are unchanged. Supported mixed evidence retains the existing
UNSUPPORTED_TRIAL_FORM behavior; actual terminal verifier clean refusal remains
NOT_ADMITTED. Neither is conflated with structural preflight rejection.

Moving the existing raw-input charge earlier does not duplicate that charge on
the successful new path. Receipt/member input charges and verification
reservations remain separate and unchanged. A preflight rejection reserves no
verifier call. Strict acquire/acquire_exact, local admission and same-live cache
hits never enter the recoverable preflight branch. Corrupt local custody remains
fatal even if its bytes would have been recoverable as newly received peer data.

Original acquisition/contact/profile and quiescence are checked after transfer,
after preflight and after ordinary admission/custody. Every normal trial still
passes _terminal_final: active, census, fresh active, then scalar original
grant/issuer deadlines and controller CPU checks. The asynchronous terminal
wrapper additionally checks no pending budget ticket, structural inflight
request or reserved bytes; its strict cache branch is covered too. No new I/O
follows these final scalar checks. The local synchronous wrapper is unchanged.

The root-set lifetime interpretation is retained: the contact lease must cover
the completed transfer/triage/admission checks, but expiration during the later
purely local census need not invalidate already received local data. Final
grant/issuer deadlines, CPU and quiescence still apply. This does not promise
every contact remains live at return and introduces no lease renewal.
