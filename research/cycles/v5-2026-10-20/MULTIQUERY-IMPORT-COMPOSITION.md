## What is deduplicated, and what conflicts mean

The import merge keys proof nodes by the exact 184-byte journal J. Identical
complete representations of a shared prerequisite coalesce. Shared receipt bytes
are keyed by proof form, statement, digest and length; identical entries coalesce.
Submitted bytes/calls are still charged, and all supplying owners remain live.

Different valid proof representations of the SAME J are not semantic disagreement:
the caller must provide exactly the conflicting journals, each with existing or
incoming. Missing/extra choices refuse before publishing merged semantic state.
The chosen complete representation includes its exact parent list; parents must
be present. One directly selected journal per semantic ModuleID is required.

Semantic maps use `(ModuleID, group_index)` and `(ModuleID, universes, body_hash)`.
Repeated identical openings/bodies are retained once; unequal data for the same
key refuse. Each opening is authenticated against its selected journal summary;
bodies must match a descriptor commitment. Module-summary conflicts, a changed
foundation/policy/profile, missing authority, or unauthorized bodies do not merge.
`_build_context` checks exact external references, missing support and forbidden
inter-group cycles. Rank/parent closure remains checked by the packet relation.

Equal human-readable names in DIFFERENT modules are different formal addresses,
not conflicting declarations and not silently identified mathematics. Two forks
can coexist. A theorem over fork A cannot automatically become a theorem over
fork B; any transport needs an actual checked mathematical construction. This
is relative sound composition, not a general logical-consistency/equivalence oracle.

The new onward packet traverses the selected exact-J closure with an included
set, so a shared chosen prerequisite appears once; claim receipts also coalesce.
Interface-only mode can avoid unused ordinary definition bodies in the frontend,
but never waives required type/recursor semantics or changes final commitments.

## One unclosed efficiency seam, not a correctness gap

Semantic deduplication AFTER admission does not deduplicate native checking
ACROSS distinct incoming packets. workspace_mixed caches identical whole packets.
The selected MixedDeltaVerifier caches I/A receipt claims, but its receive loop
unconditionally invokes the native checker for every witness in every new packet.
Thus A and B sharing native prerequisites still recheck those witnesses. The
retained CONTEXT-ACTUAL-OUTCOME already records two repeated exact native inputs
across input/output admissions; no new measurement is needed to establish this.

Next high-payoff question: can the SAME live owner reuse an exact previously
checked conditional witness result while re-establishing its exact parents in
the current graph? Any successor must bind witness/checker/profile bytes, target
and ordered parents, retain original liveness/resource rules and revoke on failure.
That is not a transferable certificate or a cold-receiver ancestry solution.
Do not implement or claim it from this note; first compare its bounded source
argument/cost with the existing exact-packet cache and certificate-frontier route.
