## Source-review extension: packet members are not separate packets

Independent review found an operability gap before any helper experiment:
an ordinary contributed mixed packet already carries an I frontier and checked
delta ancestors, but the old staging preview exposes only its target module.
A dependency therefore appears to require a separate offered packet even when
the selected packet already carries all its mathematical evidence. A naked I
frontier cannot be staged through the terminal/mixed packet API. Merely
documenting this restriction would leave the intended ordinary path broken.

Select a fresh source successor at
`software/v5/staging_members/workspace_staged.py`, deployed under the existing
logical key `client/workspace_staged.py`. core_search owns this successor and
the still-unused helper; the frozen predecessor is unchanged. Reuse the exact
mixed-packet parser, its graph/closure bounds and existing expected-journal
function. Cache a bounded structural member inventory in the staged snapshot;
terminal evidence exposes only its selected member, not an aggregate's
unopened membership. Rebind the inventory to held packet bytes on the normal
pre/post-admission readbacks.

Preview may select explicit exact member journals from those inventories.
Selecting an internal member is a caller decision, not a new proof or warrant.
The helper must distinguish packet roots from selected mathematical modules:
admit each selected packet once, then pass the chosen journals and original
live warrants to unchanged import. Exact-journal/representation conflicts
must be explicit; no first-variant choice or invented standalone certificate.
Retained inventory, selected modules, packets, pending demands and repeated
work remain bounded under the original operation. The original default
staging behavior stays compatible.

core_growth extends only the new inert helper controls to cover an internal
dependency with no standalone offer, multiple selected modules with one packet
admission, and forged/conflicting member selections. core_release independently
reviews the added boundary. No actual run or installation rebuild is yet
selected. This source decision avoids a predictable failed experiment.
