## Counter diagnosis and product judgment

Frozen Job.stats directly maps QueryInformationJobObject information class1's
Accounting.TotalProcesses into total_processes, separately from ActiveProcesses.
The structure field order matches the conventional accounting layout. This is
lifetime cumulative Job accounting, not an authenticated enumeration of unique
intended Python actors. A concurrency cap constrains active processes, not the
lifetime cumulative count. The source did not justify an exact2 expectation or
record every lifetime PID/association event. The exact cause of the doubling is
not established by saved evidence; no platform probe or rerun was performed to
invent that explanation.

The exact2 harness assumption was unsupported and the source review did not
catch it. The product's positive closure decision uses the exact retained
direct handle/identity and wait plus fresh actual active0 observations, not
that total counter. The independently matching durable row supports factual
quiescence under the reviewed trusted-worker/private-root premises. This
outcome demonstrates no new product safety blocker beyond the harness
expectation, while not proving general absence of defects.

Recommended adoption is deliberately split: preserve the entire fixture FAIL
and all charges; adopt only the corroborated current-handle crashed-worker
quiescence sub-boundary. Do not rerun to obtain a preferred fixture label.
Unclean-retirement execution remains uncovered. Any genuinely new public-owner
integration must have its own selected source and finite operation; this audit
does not launch or waive that gate.
