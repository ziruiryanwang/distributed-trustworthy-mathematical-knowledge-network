savings. The current RC would need about 33.07% fewer padded rows to meet the
unchanged 75%-of-general screen on the retained exact target.

## Invariants to establish before compiling

Support metadata is derived internally from immutable children, never supplied
on the wire or trusted as an input assertion. The expression arena remains exact
structural interning, including binder origins/styles and local identities.

An `open(e,r,k)` identity shortcut must establish both absence of the target
variable and absence of any larger loose variable that the old traversal would
reject. A maximum-loose-index-plus-one bound at most k is sufficient. Under a
binder, body bounds decrease by one while domain bounds do not.

A `close(e,l,k)` shortcut must establish absence of the exact local node.
Conservative local-node-ID intervals may justify absence only outside the
interval. False positives merely cause traversal; false negatives are forbidden.
`Local`'s stored type is atomic for these traversals, as in the original code.
Support summaries must follow that same convention. Using node IDs matches
the original comparison directly; no additional serial-uniqueness assumption
is needed.

Preserve traversal-depth and memory limits, with derived height checks where
needed. Charge actual metadata construction/query work; no counter refunds or
silently enlarged budgets. Reduced actual work may avoid a former work-limit
refusal, but cannot change the accepted mathematical predicate. No shortcut may
hide unsupported syntax, unavailable signatures, a binder-capture error or an
unresolved parent receipt.
