The new theorem is justified directly. Given

\[
f:A\to B\to C\to D,\qquad
h_a:a=a',\quad h_b:b=b',\quad h_c:c=c',

the inner binary-congruence application yields
\(h:f\,a\,b=f\,a'\,b'\) in the function type \(C\to D\). The outer application
uses evaluation \((g,x)\mapsto g\,x\) with equalities \(h\) and \(h_c\), yielding
\(f\,a\,b\,c=f\,a'\,b'\,c'\). The two necessary instances have respectively
argument/codomain types \((A,B,C\to D)\) and \((C\to D,C,D)\); universe
polymorphism permits both. The template's four arbitrary sort universes and
implicit endpoint binders agree with the independently constructed
`contract(3)`. There is no assumption that the functions are extensionally equal
without proof, no new axiom, and no human-language interpretation step.
