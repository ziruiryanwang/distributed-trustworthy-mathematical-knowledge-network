- Bind exact original wire, complete byte consumption and target journals;
  identical ModuleID does not substitute a different exact journal/rank.
- Bind member order/count/commitment/openings, the maximum member rank and
  zero-assumption seal height one.

  stale/expired and closed cases, without impersonating I/A/mixed handles.
