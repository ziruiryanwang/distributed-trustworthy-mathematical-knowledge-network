## What growth can currently be claimed

The system has produced and circulated nonvacuously reusable checked artifacts:
an acquired parametric method supports a mirror/involution contribution, and a
fresh owner uses that law in a further injectivity contribution. A separate
direct-I route imports an unfamiliar certified method without its proof ancestry
and composes it into a new ternary-congruence result. These are capability and

They do **not** establish strict resource-bounded advantage against an equally
optimized isolated local policy, positive full priced cost, sustained demand,
or indefinitely renewable certified frontiers. Deductive closure is also not
the right growth measure: the newly exposed consequences may already follow
logically from the original foundations. Useful progress concerns their
accessible checked representations and the cost of obtaining/using them under
specified local policies, endowments and budgets.

The earlier full-cost inequalities are valid conditional accounting arguments.

region. In particular, a source-build timing is not a lower bound on arbitrary
local mathematical discovery, caps are not successful route costs, and low
native verification time is not the whole recipient cost. No new positive
full-cost claim follows here; neither an oracle baseline nor impossible universal
growth is required to acknowledge that remaining uncertainty.

## Why the proposed refinement is sound in principle

The memo stores a **conditional checker observation**, not durable mathematical
authority. Its exact canonical witness, returned J, ordered parents, rank and
adopted image/foundation/policy/profile bind the full relation input and output.
Exact byte equality, not merely a matching digest or journal, is essential.

On a new admission, current receipt/member premises and the complete current
topological graph are still established. A hit supplies the same previously
checked conditional relation; its parents must be established now. A miss runs
the existing native check. Induction over the packet order therefore gives the
same conclusion under the existing checker/binding assumptions. An earlier
receipt representation need not be preserved if current valid exact-J premises
are established independently. An old native result alone is never a frontier.

Only records belonging to a completely successful admission are retained. A
failed prefix or final check must not make a reusable record; revocation clears
state. Keeping only the last successfully admitted packet, at most 16 records
and 16 MiB of actually counted canonical witness bytes, avoids a growing
operation-history cache. Overflow or replacement only causes future misses.
Exact-owner state and immutable values do not imply a hostile-Python sandbox.

## Cost claim: narrowly correct, not a total-cost speedup

For a live owner admitting successive prefixes of n distinct witnesses, when
every previous prefix fits the retained limits, native invocations fall from
`n(n+1)/2` to `n`. This is a count of nonuniform-cost checker executions. More
generally, each hit removes one specific repeated native execution and its
process-related work, while adding lookup/equality/retention work.

The current delta ceiling is 16. Parsing, canonicalization, transfer, source
guards, live-premise validation and closure still process cumulative material.
This is not an unbounded linear-time growth theorem, a cold-owner optimization,
or a replacement for certifying new frontiers. The small retained workloads
spent much more CPU in controllers than native checks, so a large overall gain
cannot be inferred. A repeated mirror benchmark would not settle the broader
growth question and is not justified by this change.

Both facade and receiver conservatively reserve the full graph's CPU, even on
hits. Keeping that rule is appropriate for this first one-file refinement; it
also means the change does not admit more contributions under an unchanged
reservation grant. An equally optimized local/shared comparator gets this same
memo. Its improvement cannot be counted as a unique network-sharing advantage.

## Root-resolved evidence-layout choice

Root explicitly selected the decision's hit semantics: **no native execution
input, process report or stdout is created for a hit**. The original full packet
is retained/read back; exact immutable canonical witness equality and a distinct
reuse diagnostic support the local observation. The diagnostic must never claim
a new native execution, cryptographic proof or transferable acceptance.

A focused search over the exact current 114-source map found native `delta-N`
and `process-N` paths only in the receivers that create/read their own execution
artifacts, not a public author/import/export consumer of mixed private files.
Current import/export uses `export_packet`, live descriptions and stored packet
bytes. Thus this private evidence-layout change has no identified public SDK
dependency. It must still be checked in the new source and controls; historical
external audit fixtures expecting every native-node process log are not silently
reinterpreted as fresh execution evidence.
