# Proof-search reuse construction: retained NO-GO

2026-10-20, core_release. Source/theory decision only; no execution or implementation.

The construction recommendation in `BENEFIT-WORKLOAD-DECISION.md` is superseded
by `BENEFIT-CASE.md`, independently challenged in `BENEFIT-CASE-CHALLENGE.md`.
This follow-up supplies no concrete new mathematical family or cost fact that
defeats their falsifiers. **Do not construct or run the proposed SAT benchmark.**

The remaining decisive conditions are joint, not interchangeable:

- An exact supported theorem/proof family must reconstruct correctly. Arbitrary
  LRAT RAT additions are not implication lemmas; Boolean validity is not arbitrary
  constructive propositional validity.
- Its actual expanded types and proof rows must meet the existing codec limits.
  Naming proof steps alone does not bound repeated type/context expansion.
- A fresh consumer must derive a selective query from its own target without
  receiving the unknown answer's identities or a post hoc profile.
- The equally equipped local route retains the bundled CaDiCaL solver, direct
  proofs, generalization and caches. A benefit region must have justified costs,
  not an unspecified positive avoided-search term or repeated warm uses.

No supplied construction presently meets all four conditions. This is a decision
not to spend implementation effort on this workload, not an impossibility theorem
for useful sharing. Existing discovery/import/author/admit/publish capabilities
are not missing merely because this benefit case fails.

The numeral-support recommendation at the end of the earlier decision is
historical: subsequent work already resolved that boundary. It is not a new task.
The original failures and the general final-v5 objective remain unchanged.
