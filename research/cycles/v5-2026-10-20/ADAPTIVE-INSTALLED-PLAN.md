make two different formal queries with **no bootstrap peers and no provider**.
This closes the installed source-registration/real-child composition boundary
left intentionally inert by the five component control groups. Do not repeat

explicit owner test provisioning, not ordinary startup silently creating trust.

already covered by the coherent integration; they are not retested here.
