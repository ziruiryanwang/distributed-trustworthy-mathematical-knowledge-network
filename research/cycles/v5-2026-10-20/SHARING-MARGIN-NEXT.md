## Decision

The retained traces establish **substantive reusable capability and eliminated
work**, but do not establish a positive full-cost sharing margin. They also do
not refute such a margin on the current efficient implementation. Do not perform
another mirror/ternary/reciprocal demonstration, seal M, or build an economics
framework. The only proposed new observation is **one matched, exact-M
checked-build-versus-native-sharing pair**, ending before V/W authoring. Its
decision is whether native-first acquisition of a missing source-known method
can be recommended economically in the named finite operating case, rather than
merely as an available mechanism.

This is avoided **checked construction/materialization**, not avoided discovery.
Both recipients receive the same M source recipe. Source-build duration is not
a lower bound on mathematical ingenuity or on all possible local proof methods.
The fixed local policy uses the supported optimized Lean-to-canonical path and
persists its output after the first cold build. It never rebuilds a warm M.

## What the result may justify

For the **single cold recipient** case, all incremental service/publication cost
is paid by that recipient: n=1 is already feasible if both original grants and
deadline close successfully. Report the resource vector first. A positive CPU
coordinate with bounded bandwidth/storage is a CPU benefit, not Pareto dominance
(L may transmit no bytes). Any scalar full-resource claim must predeclare prices
or explicitly report its supported price region; never mix seconds and bytes.

If a separately identifiable positive successful-use margin remains after all
variable provider work, the existing theorem may compute an amortization threshold
for fixed setup. **Do not call that threshold feasible merely because it is finite.**
For n>1, root still needs a supported finite horizon, cold-recipient demand, storage
retention and per-queue capacity sufficient for that n, and bounds rather than an
unjustified repeated copy of a single observed CPU time. This observation does not
create those assumptions. If only n=1 is justified, make only that claim.

- Positive full finite-history margin: retain M build reuse as a witnessed narrow
  operating case; no exponential-growth or mathematical-discovery claim follows.
- Nonpositive successful-use margin: do not seek salvation through more users;
  stop this case unless a specific source-visible excess is removable.
- Positive per-use but negative full one-user margin: shared fixed setup dominates;
  expose the exact threshold, but do not schedule many copies merely to manufacture
  demand or declare an unverified service region.
- Inconclusive accounting/noise or unmatched endpoint: no margin claim, no new
  generic benchmark apparatus. Redirect to the identified central implementation
  obstacle or a genuinely better evidenced reuse case.

## Method value is separate from certificate economics

Mirror03 already proves a real methodological relationship: generic evaluator
uniqueness supports mirror involution, which supports a later independent
injectivity result after owner withdrawal. Terminal-I similarly supports two
composed uses of binary congruence. Those are warranted reusable capabilities,
not mere theorem counts, and remain valuable even if this small build-cache
comparison is unfavorable. Conversely, direct induction can prove mirror
involution and direct equality reasoning can prove ternary congruence; do not
force the local portfolio through M to claim avoided research.

M's certified-frontier production is a separate investment against ordinary
native sharing. Its cost is not justified by the 1.609375-second source worker,
by repeated warm substitutions, or by this proposed build-reuse observation.
No empirical demand threshold for that certificate is presently supplied here.
