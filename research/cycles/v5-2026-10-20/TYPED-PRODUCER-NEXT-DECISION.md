# Typed producer: next decision

## Recommendation

**Retain the qualified typed-row optimization; do not build or execute a new
guest now. Prioritize the ordinary reciprocal contribution seam.** Native
equivalence is necessary but does not make a guest measurement decision-useful.
The current bottleneck is an unfinished usable path from a discovered/imported
context to a checked, published extension; closing it needs neither a new
certificate nor another resource investigation.

The narrow selected direction is a helper composing the existing same-client
finished demand-directed context with authoring, child admission and publication.
It must preserve original grants, policy, exact dependencies and demand authority.
It must not introduce remote bulk imports, new trust, a hidden research agenda or
a fabricated context. Root selects implementation and runtime separately.

## What the optimization establishes

The eight adopted native comparisons establish unchanged selected mathematical
conclusions, premises, aggregate output and accounting, not a speedup. The exact
new path directly deserializes fourteen DAG row variants. It removes generic
suffix map/key storage and the subsequent generic-Value-to-native conversion for
supported rows. It does **not** first construct a complete generic frame and
then convert it. Unrecognized syntax returns to the old full-frame path before
semantics.

The retained two-delta input has 2,364 rows: 826 foundation rows, 1,513 supported
suffix DAG rows, and 25 declarations. The latter two generic classes, local
specifications, parent openings and bodies remain generic. Transient decoded key
strings and small typed-field vectors remain. JSON lexical work, canonical
semantic encoding/hashing, foundation checking, native DAG construction and
kernel checking also remain. These counts do not establish a runtime fraction.

If old stage work is decomposed as `W = G + R`, the changed representation removes
part of `G` and introduces typed dispatch work. There is no measured numerical
bound for the net difference, nor evidence that `G` dominates `R`. The saved PC
samples are saturated, inlined and from a failed prefix: percentages derived
from their label counts are not causal cycle shares. Do not promise a segment
reduction from the 1,513-row count.

## What remains dominant and unchanged

The newest completed selected smaller execution is BASE+FIRST: 167 full po18
segments, 43,778,048 padded rows and a 32,965,324-byte capsule, with one genuine
I premise. The older complete three-member po19 result is 113 segments; it is
not the same workload and cannot supply a matched speedup baseline.

For `s` segments and `m` assumptions, the retained staged graph has `3s+m` host
calls. Fewer segments can reduce total segment/join stages, but does not reduce
the fixed workspace of a full-size segment or the po18 recursive prover.
In particular, typed rows do not alter backend matrix geometry. The existing
preflight allocation correction is already built; re-proposing it is duplicate
work. The geometry17 snapshot host was linked but its layout was not executed:
NOW records the deferred launch and no output, not a new completed capsule.

Neither a guest-only improvement nor native correctness would establish an
affordable complete receipt, scalable receiver acceptance or net useful growth.
Those remain obligations; deferral is not their waiver.

## Three routes compared

| Route | Decision value now | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Integrate typed guest and execute once | Measures segment/cycle change but cannot remove fixed proving/recursion workspace; no presently selected proof decision depends on its result | Defer |
| Attempt another algorithmic reduction | Potentially larger benefit, but retained evidence does not identify another dominant removable stage; duplicate size scans, moves and proof memoization are already addressed | Do not speculate or repeat these changes |
| Close ordinary discovered-context → contribution → publication | Removes a known core-loop operability gap with existing checked owners; small source review and changed-boundary controls suffice initially | Select next |

## One observation that would change the producer decision

Reopen typed-guest measurement when there is a concrete production decision with
a declared segment/cost threshold, and **one matched complete typed execution on
the same BASE+FIRST input/geometry** can decide whether that threshold is met.
Its actual segment histogram and total cycles are the missing observation. A
predicted speedup, native duration, incomplete prefix or a different workload
does not answer it. Even success would select only the next production step,
not certify affordability of all stages.

changing the checker crate leaves the aggregate decoder on its old path. Fresh

This is four project compiles plus assembly and one selected execute-only run,
not a mandatory dependency/C++ rebuild. No such work is authorized by this note.
