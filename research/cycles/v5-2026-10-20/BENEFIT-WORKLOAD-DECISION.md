## Two questions, not one impossible comparison

Commons benefit compares isolated, equally equipped libraries with libraries
that share a newly discovered proof. Each isolated library may solve once,
normalize, generalize and cache; it is not forced to rediscover every instance.
Ordinary decentralized native-proof sharing counts as a successful commons route.
It need not outperform itself. The separate certification premium compares that
same sharing route with optional succinct evidence, charging its production.
Neither comparison needs a lower bound against every imaginable proof algorithm.

## Exact next mathematical construction

Create a small fresh source workload, not a new network mechanism: a propositional
resolution proof DAG whose exported result is a polymorphic implication from
explicit clauses to a conclusion. Local search is untrusted; its output must be
an ordinary Lean proof, admitted through the current author/admit/publish path.
Use named local step lemmas to share DAG nodes, never unfold a proof tree.
One module contains the proof DAG: step lemmas are not a chain of remote modules.
Stay initially inside the already supported lambda/forall/application fragment.
Church encodings avoid importing an arithmetic, SAT-checker or numeral library:
`OrC P Q := forall S : Prop, (P -> S) -> (Q -> S) -> S`,
`FalseC := forall S : Prop, S`. Resolution has the constructive proof
`fun S q r => h S (fun p => g S (fun np => np p S) r) q`, where
`h : OrC P Q` and `g : OrC (P -> FalseC) R`; its result is `OrC Q R`.
The producer must also supply/check clause-ordering and weakening steps; those
are not an oracle. No axiom, native-decide shortcut or trusted solver is added.

The artifact is the discovered implication/core, not the tiny resolution rule.
A second client applies it under a genuinely different substitution
of propositions and proves its premises; substituting arbitrary large formulas
does not require rerunning the original combinatorial search. The endpoint is
that same accepted downstream implication for both routes, not the certificate's
mere existence. Formula/certificate size, sharing and premise costs are charged.

## Honest baseline and decisive benefit observation

Before execution, fix an explicit local proof-search portfolio and initial
library/cache. Give both routes the SAME simplification, clause subsumption,
unit propagation, branching, DAG sharing, known lemmas and proof reconstruction.
Permit direct short proofs and alternative derivations; if they win, use them.
Do not choose obsolete exhaustive enumeration merely to inflate local cost.
The sharing route receives the new producer result; the isolated route does not
receive that new result for free. Its own earlier discoveries remain reusable.

Retain one productive search trace and its exact input/configuration. For a
deterministic specified solver, identical cold-library executions follow that
trace; no N-machine replay is needed. Source-level work counts can justify the
algorithmic claim; physical-cost bounds require corresponding calibrated prices
or a small actual enclosing-run record, not a made-up seconds-per-step constant.
An arbitrary agent may find a shorter proof: this is a supported-comparator result,
not a theorem that all possible mathematicians must spend the recorded effort.

Let N count independent first uses of the missing core, never warm substitutions.
With the same checked proof endpoint, common native checking cancels; avoiding
N-1 productive searches must exceed EXTRA publication, candidate inspection,
transport, producer/consumer work, failed queries, repair and finite retention.
Include any extra generalization/proof reconstruction; if routes check different
proofs, their native costs no longer cancel. Charge the first producer search
once in the sharing route and once per independently solving library otherwise.
This is a from-genesis comparison, not free historical proof production.
No numeric N or profitable horizon is presently justified. Choose a finite
support/retention horizon and bounded provider/service allocation; use the
existing operating-region test to reject demand exceeding queue capacity or
payback beyond that horizon. Query quotas only bound loss: they do not establish
successful discovery, paid retention or independent demand. Failure/withdrawal
shortens the eligible horizon; repeated hits in one warm cache add no saving.
