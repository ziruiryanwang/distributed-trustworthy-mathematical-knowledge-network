# Growth architecture challenge: earn the adoption cost

2026-10-24. Independent source/theory audit by `reference_soundness_audit`.
Canonical root: `E:\Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network`.
No native or product execution, benchmark, owner/database opening, source change,
identity migration or Git mutation was performed. This note challenges the
current architecture; it is not a selected implementation or release claim.

## Decision

The revised [practical decision](PRACTICAL-NEXT-DECISION.md) has the right causal
mechanism: independent work creates a useful method; economical discovery and
adoption let other agents exploit it sooner or more cheaply. Its conditional
accounting is sound at the stated level. It is **not evidence that our present
software has a positive margin**, or that custom protocols outperform ordinary
searchable packages. The latter remains a substantive product-design question.

Three recommendations have highest expected payoff:

1. Treat the product as an optional **checked mathematical interchange and
   adoption layer for ordinary Lean work**. Existing repositories, static object
   stores and replaceable indexes can be its infrastructure. Neither blockchain
   consensus nor a replacement for all package infrastructure follows from the
   growth argument.
2. Evaluate one independently useful structure-specific method/bridge bundle,
   including its actual applicability and compatibility costs. A generic theorem
   already supplied by the baseline library is not a new capability merely
   because our software republishes it.
3. Do not migrate theorem identities now. First distinguish an inexpensive
   advisory interface grouping from a checked, owner-local adaptation of a
   selected candidate. Neither is authority to reuse a foreign warrant.

The at-hand public workflow failed before protected registration. Its actual
init/intake and native correspondence successes are preserved in
[the independent outcome audit](ORDINARY-REFERENCE-DELTA-CONTRACT.md).
Repairing its deterministic diagnostic-file census mismatch is narrow engineering,
not growth evidence and not a reason to launch another complete campaign.

## Philosophical premise: validity can be intention-free; value is not universal

Under fixed foundation F, adding a theorem of F does not enlarge unrestricted
deductive closure. The existing [philosophy note](../v4-2026-10-02/PHILOSOPHY-OF-GROWTH.md)
already resolves this: a finite agent gains explicit, accessible, usable
capability, not new consequences of an omniscient theory. No additional metaphysical
doctrine is needed for the next architecture decision.

Keep these predicates separate:

- Derivable under the selected foundation and checking policy.
- Represented and available somewhere.
- Discoverable within a particular local resource bound.
- Applicable in a particular local formal environment.
- Beneficial for some subsequent independently specified work after full costs.

The first does not mention human intention. The last depends on opportunities,
resource prices and a reasoner policy; these need not be protocol-level goals.
Local agents may investigate entirely independently. An evaluation workload is a
scientific comparison instrument, not a mandatory network research agenda.

There can be no justified **strict positive benefit for every workload** merely
from adding valid mathematics. Choose a workload that never uses the addition:
any compulsory acquisition/indexing cost gives negative benefit. If adoption is
truly optional and cost-free to ignore, the best possible policy may recover the
old cost, but strict benefit still does not follow. For an actual bounded search
policy, additional candidates can also consume attention and worsen performance.

Consequently theorem count, byte count, citation count and syntactic path count
are not universal knowledge-growth measures. Usage is useful fallible evidence,
but is affected by exposure, ranking, duplication, fashion and the available
population. Rare complements may be valuable; popular tautological wrappers may
not be. The system can retain valid material without pretending to compute one
objective task-independent importance order.

## Audit of the causal growth argument

Use complete histories with the same independently specified endpoints:

- L: cache-aware local work with the same initial mathematics and tools.
- O: L plus plausible ordinary searchable package/source sharing.
- C: the proposed commons, possibly implemented using much of O.

L-to-O can establish a benefit of sharing. It does not establish an incremental
C-to-O benefit. Conversely C need not strictly beat an idealized O if C is a useful
implementation of O. The relevant question is whether an extra mechanism earns
its extra cost, not whether every release needs a novel economic theorem.

For an opportunity i, couple the routes to the same need and let `A_ri` be
successful applicable adoption through route r. Let `d_ri` be its conditional
resource saving relative to the same pre-adoption local route; let `q_ri`
contain other costs not already charged. A transparent model is

```text
E[C_O - C_C]
  = sum_i (Pr(A_Ci) E[d_Ci | A_Ci]
           - Pr(A_Oi) E[d_Oi | A_Oi])
    - sum_i E[q_Ci - q_Oi] - incremental_fixed_and_maintenance_cost.
```

Linearity needs no independence. Factoring a common saving `d_i` requires a
common conditional saving, or a justified coupling that supplies it; successful
adopters may otherwise have different difficulty and costs. Missing completion
must not be priced as a cheap success. Compare equal endpoints or separately
report completion and resources at the same finite budget.

The practical note's adoption-window bound is a correct sufficient case when
its assumptions hold: over a window of length w, O cannot yet supply the method,
C has conditional successful-use probability at least p per eligible arrival,
each such use saves at least d, expected eligible arrivals are at least lambda*w,
and total incremental overhead is at most H. Then

```text
E[C_O - C_C] >= lambda * w * p * d - H.
```

Here the lower bound p must hold conditionally for the counted opportunities,
including relevant exposure, compatibility, outages and capacity effects. Merely
having a marginal success probability for a differently selected population is
insufficient. No Poisson arrival or independent-agent assumption is necessary
for this conditional first-moment argument.

This is more useful than an unexplained positive coefficient because it names
where software can act: publication delay, discovery delay, successful adoption
and overhead. It is still conditional, not a proof that those advantages exist.
Git branches can publish immediately, and ordinary search can find them. If
`tau_O = tau_C`, the claimed window vanishes. C must then earn a benefit through
cheaper/safer adoption, better relevant discovery, availability, or an honestly
different supported workflow. Do not impose an artificial curated-release delay.

Likewise a known source proof gives an upper bound for one derivation route,
not a lower bound on the best possible proof. Useful theoretical comparisons
can be against specified executable, optimized policies or against a concrete
algorithmic operation count. They should not call a deliberately slow tactic an
unavoidable discovery cost. If the method was produced for the same independent
producer endpoint in both histories, common production cancels. Extra network-
induced generalization, extraction, publication, service and failed searches do
not cancel. If the network induces all creation, charge all induced creation.

Full-cost feasibility also requires demand inside the actual retention horizon
and service envelope. A positive algebraic threshold requiring more cold uses
than the service can support is not an operable growth regime. Native-first
sharing does not become cheap for a cold receiver solely because later reuse is
cheap. Keep N (global corpus), K (local warranted context), D (missing closure)
and S(q) (examined search population) separate, as in
[the practical cost audit](ORDINARY-PRACTICAL-COST.md).

## Complement bundles: a real mechanism with a narrow claim

For a fixed policy portfolio, a generic transport theorem m and a new structure-
specific bridge n can jointly enable an endpoint while neither alone helps.
This yields positive bundle synergy. Other methods may substitute for one another.
Therefore there is no general submodularity or supermodularity theorem for
mathematical libraries. The practical note correctly avoids both assumptions.

The productive object is not necessarily a new schema. An existing theorem

```text
m : forall X, Premise(X) -> Conclusion(X)
n : Premise(S)
```

already expresses a method and its structure-specific support. A client can inspect
the actual shared parameter S, remaining hypotheses and exact dependencies before
deciding whether to pay for missing evidence. An untrusted index or agent can
suggest this pairing; checked application establishes the result. False suggested
pairings consume bounded search work but cannot become mathematical authority.

The comparison must allow O the existing generic transport theorem and analogous
package tools. The possible new capability is a useful bridge for a genuinely
new relevant structure, or materially easier discovery/application of the pair,
not republishing familiar injection/fold/transport principles under new names.
The consumer endpoint must be independent of a forced citation requirement.

Do not infer that searching all useful bundles is cheap. Higher-order matching,
premise discharge and combinatorial pairings can be difficult. Bounded specialist
proposals plus ordinary proof checking are an operable partial mechanism; they
are not complete search over all mathematics. A formal applicability claim can
be exact while a relevance score remains heuristic.

## Identity audit: a real fragmentation risk, not a new discovery

Actual `ordinary_semantics.py` defines

```text
UnitID   = H(domain, exact evidence bytes)
ExportID = H(domain, semantic_id, UnitID, original_name).
```

Evidence includes full declaration bodies and exact import bindings. Thus two
proofs with identical proposition/type can yield different ExportIDs. A descendant
importing either ID can differ recursively even when its raw named proof syntax
is unchanged. This conservatively protects exact evidence identity but can reduce
the common frontier among independently evolved libraries.

It does not follow that every proof change affects every client, or that a stable
semantic-ID migration pays for itself. Shared pinned packages and unchanged
evidence avoid the conflict. The phenomenon needs one actual intended reuse case
where it is the economic bottleneck, not only an adversarial paper family.

Prior local analyses already establish the main design space:

- [CP-001F](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001F-proof-identity-composition.md) separates
  statement identity from selected proof, and rejects blanket cache portability.
- [CP-001G](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001G-proof-instance-composition.md) distinguishes
  statement descriptors, actual witness instances and grounded theorem aliases.
  A proof of P needs well-formed P, not a previous proof of P. Choosing a root
  witness as the unique representative can manufacture cycles from acyclic source.
- [CP-001H](../../../notes/analyses/CP-001H-proof-computation-and-reconstruction.md)
  and its [independent audit](../../../notes/audits/CP-001H-proof-computation-independent-audit.md)
  distinguish declarative proof irrelevance from unchanged concrete conversion.
  Their Acc example is a source-backed paper case, not an executed library test.

Prop-valued proofs of the same exact proposition are logically interchangeable
in the appropriate coherent theory. **Not every proof-bearing data computation
preserves the same successful native reduction after witness replacement.**
The Acc case has a reducible theorem proof exposing a constructor versus a theorem
backed by a genuine opaque proof. A fixed Nat-valued conversion can become stuck
after replacement. Explicit checked equality repair or a different grounded
witness can help; neither is free or guaranteed discoverable. No prior acceptance
cache is transplanted across that change.

Proof irrelevance also does not identify `opaque x : Nat := 0` with
`opaque y : Nat := 1`, merge separately nominal inductives, erase universe
differences, or make one witness's axioms/policy footprint equal another's.
Calling Lean theorem declarations simply "opaque" can obscure the exact native
distinction. Our current private/public module correspondence already checks
actual public conversion instead of assuming erased proof bodies always work.

## Two lower-migration alternatives before a new identity protocol

### Advisory interface grouping

Keep authoritative ExportIDs unchanged. An optional index key can group a strict
structural subset using selected semantics/foundation, ordered universe parameters
and encoded proposition type with exact dependency identities. The name can be
an independent search feature. The key does not warrant the proposition; actual
candidate types remain untrusted until checked. Equal syntax under an exact
dependency interpretation is a narrow comparison, not arbitrary equivalence.

This can group different direct witnesses when their type dependencies coincide.
It does **not** solve recursive fragmentation when a type's dependencies have
different proof-sensitive IDs. Normalizing those dependencies away would require
new semantic obligations. Nor does grouping alone let the current receiver skip
an unaccepted exact evidence unit. Claim discovery organization only.

### Owner-local checked adaptation, with new evidence identities

One possible later optimization requires no global representative or migration:

1. Select a finite incoming candidate and an existing warranted receiver context.
   A proposal maps selected proposition-valued dependencies to already warranted
   local proofs. Exact selected-policy types/universes and all relevant non-proof
   dependency meanings must be related, not merely printed names.
2. Construct a candidate in that receiver context, consistently substituting the
   proposed proofs and updating exact import bindings, types and bodies. Ordinary
   definitions, opaque data and inductive groups receive no proof-erasure shortcut.
3. Check every affected new declaration through the unchanged authoritative
   admission boundary. Reject unsupported groups, unresolved references, cycles,
   changed forbidden policies and failed conversion. Do not insert an assumed
   theorem to break a dependency cycle.
4. If successful, issue the actual transformed evidence's **new** UnitIDs/ExportIDs
   and retain its actual support. Never assign the original remote export ID or
   adopt its report. If a claim of exact source correspondence is needed, establish
   it separately. The actual advertised local target must bind its new meanings.

The central safety argument is simple: for valid E, a successful ordinary checked
extension by candidate d implies valid E+d under the existing checker assumptions.
The proposal algorithm need not be trusted to preserve typing; final checking
does that. Its **completeness, economy and correspondence** remain separate.
It may fail on the Acc case or require expensive repair. A failure does not show
that the remote mathematics is false.

For example, when the receiver already has `h : P`, a remote candidate deriving
Q from a different checked witness of exactly P may be re-expressed using h.
The receiver is proving a new local Q using its own warranted h, not certifying
that it downloaded or checked the remote witness. If Q's meaning depends on an
unrelated changed definition, the narrow substitution premise fails. This is
proof reuse/adaptation, not semantic identity by declaration.

This possibility can avoid some remote proof ancestry without expensive succinct
production, but only where suitable local substitutes actually exist. It is not
unconditional cold no-ancestry verification. A fully cold receiver still needs
warranted foundations and the missing proof/evidence, a different checked proof,
or an explicitly selected independent certificate/trust policy.

Neither alternative is selected for implementation by this audit. The first is
lower risk but weaker; the second attacks real interoperability but needs precise
bounded support and an economic use case. Prior concept code/schema history must
not be reintroduced wholesale merely because it already exists.

## Migration cost and a decision-changing test, on paper first

Let k be the eligible cold imports during a finite horizon, a the per-import cost
avoided by a selected compatibility mechanism, b its per-import extra checking/
adaptation/discovery cost, and F its implementation/transition/service premium in
the declared resource model. A positive region requires `k*(a-b) > F`, with
`a>b`, capacity and policy compatibility. Include unsuccessful mappings and
repair attempts. If known quantities do not support even a plausible margin,
do not spend time benchmarking a new global identity layer.

The smallest next discriminating document should pin:

- One producer method/structure bridge and one independently motivated consumer
  endpoint; the exact strongest ordinary package route to that endpoint.
- What the consumer already knows, which proof/definition interfaces actually
  disagree, and whether existing package pins already remove the mismatch.
- The call/data path avoided, versus extraction, checking, source-compatibility,
  failed-search and maintenance costs added.
- Which observation would choose package integration, advisory grouping, bounded
  adaptation, or no change. If every plausible outcome picks the same option,
  the proposed experiment is unnecessary.

A present failure in a protected-file allowlist cannot answer these questions.
Neither can a larger local peer count. Conversely a precise source argument
showing that existing APIs already carry the needed bundle may remove the need
for another component entirely.

## Deployment consequence

The defensible v5 core remains a sound local acceptance boundary, immutable
shareable evidence, compatible ordinary Lean authoring, bounded optional discovery,
continued selected custody and an operable reciprocal contribution path. Untrusted
ranking and suggestions stay outside truth. Search completeness is scoped, cold
learning is paid, and optional certificates need their own amortization case.

Worldwide availability, hostile-input isolation, concurrent service, recoverable
state, portable supported clients and release/maintenance choices remain genuine
deployment work. They should follow the selected exchange model rather than be
polished ahead of its practical value. No completed component or conditional
growth inequality makes final deployable v5 complete. The right advancement now
is a justified, useful adoption path, not another certificate or protocol by inertia.
