## Finite acquisition and shared resources

The missing-dependency loop reparses preflight after each fetched frontier. It
caps units at eight, evidence at 4 MiB and requested missing exports at eight;
repeated missing exports refuse instead of cycling. A locator selecting a unit
already in hand cannot confer an unrepresented export: preflight still requires
its actual declaration and refuses/no-progress terminates if it remains absent.
Individual evidence responses are capped at 1 MiB. Parent/selected name conflicts
and cyclic/incomplete mathematics remain the admission core's refusal cases.

Search uses one provider, one snapshot slot, two requests, one candidate page,
32 scanned records and eight returned matches. Its reply allocation is prepaid
inside the same 24-request/8 MiB acquisition budget used for later rehosting.
Provider request/reply quotas are separate and finite. Larger permitted input
ceilings do not promise success at every maximum: the aggregate budget can
correctly refuse a closure that leaves too little capacity for the final rehost.

`result.deadline = min(result.deadline, deadline)` before return. No timing test

operations are additionally enclosed by the same absolute receive deadline.

Native work is charged to the parent CPU ledger before successful continuation.
If initialization/admission raises, the workflow aborts; enclosing supervision
retains whole-process accounting even where no successful-stage CPU record was
returned. Controller/store calls are cooperatively checked and remain subject to
