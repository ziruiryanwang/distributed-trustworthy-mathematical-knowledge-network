# Central-priority challenge after the one-pass candidate

2026-09-22. Independent read-only challenge of the selected result and its next-step ordering. No product command, Lean stage, network service or test was run for this note. The only edit is this Markdown record. Its proposal is a decision gate, not an executed result or a claim that the existing candidate is generally efficient.

## Decision

The most consequential **locally testable** next obstacle is *demand-driven useful reuse at measured full-workflow cost*, ending in a second independently reusable checked contribution. Make that one bounded experiment the first central gate. Cost attribution is mandatory inside the gate; it is not a license to select a context-size or package-reader optimization beforehand. If the measured failure mode is excessive checked context work, then a separately frozen context-size discriminator becomes the next gate. Remote-discovery expansion beyond the current bounded catalog is also important, but should follow evidence of a useful selection failure at the supported bound or a separately justified coverage target.

This ordering is conditional, not a claim that retrieval is already solved, that Lean checking is cheap, or that a global two-contact/256-entry design is sufficient. It asks whether the currently selected product creates actual *net reusable mathematical capability* before polishing an isolated transport or encoding seam.

## Evidence and competing explanations

1. The [goal](../../GOAL.md) requires reliable and efficient reciprocal discovery, local verification, reuse and contribution. It explicitly rejects treating HTTP success or increasing metadata as mathematical growth. The [current design](../../CURRENT-DESIGN.md) and [selected one-pass support statement](../../deliverables/2026-09-22-onepass-peer-candidate/RESULTS.md) distinguish the complete safety demonstrations from unproved usefulness, independent-host operation and affordable scale.
2. One-pass qualification did test an ordinary A-to-B genuine contribution and receiver-local Lean admission. Its later-page fixture had 32 inert decoys, not 33 independently published results; the separate 65-entry hosting fixture was inert. The 20-step native chain took 606.786 coordinator wall seconds and 425.547 inclusive disjoint outer-Job CPU seconds. Those are a high *observed total* for a small proof, but the predecessor's different workload is not a matched speed comparator.
3. A read-only extraction from the sealed [one-pass result and per-call events](../../software/qualification/onepass-native-01/result.json) gives 54 canonical package reads and 135.300 summed package-reader wall seconds inside 352.892 summed public-call dispatcher wall seconds. `admit-a`/`admit-b` account for 55.531/43.063 outer-Job CPU seconds, `submit-a` for 118.828, `build` for 49.500 and `init-a`/`init-b` for 33.031/62.094. The package-read wall total is nested, not CPU attribution; native stages, construction, receipt checks and process startup also occur. These observations do **not** establish that Lean's kernel, context size, repeated package insertion, or networking is the dominant cause. `checking.py` does revalidate context packages and rebuild a union/plan on admission and submission, with a finite 16-package/64-MiB selected profile, making context growth a credible hypothesis, not a measured diagnosis.
4. The prior [matched consumer comparison](../../deliverables/2026-09-21-local-v1-candidate/RESULTS.md) is valuable: the peer consumer chose an append/fold law from seven entries and its checked body substantively used the received proof. Yet both one-sample arms proved the target; the peer arm had longer elapsed time (652.689 versus 375.935 seconds) despite slightly lower observed public-Job CPU. Preparation and model/operator costs were not fully comparable. This shows one feasible useful reuse, not improved productivity, robust unknown-result retrieval, sustained growth or an efficiency distribution.
5. [Indexed discovery](../../experiments/indexed-discovery/RESULTS.md) has exact-symbol query-cost evidence up to 8,192 synthetic local rows; the [native follow-through](../../experiments/indexed-discovery/NATIVE-RESULTS.md) checked a scripted Eq proof in a tiny corpus. It establishes an index/API seam, not semantic relevance or agent success on an unknown theorem. The [checked-package-insertion timing allocation](../../experiments/checked-package-insertion/TIMING-OUTCOME.md) stopped after 27/48 required reads, so its successful differential correctness tests provide no four-pair timing gate or justification to integrate that microoptimization now.

## Bounded falsifiable experiment: useful reciprocal accumulation

Freeze two or three formal target families and a corpus *before* dispatching consumers. Use genuinely checked and ordinarily published packages from independent source authors, including several plausible but non-serving mathematical candidates and at least one result relevant to each target. Do not mark inert fixtures as publications for this gate. The target types and formal library slice must be fixed independently of consumer attempts. The target is an **evaluation fixture only**; it creates no global root goal, system task queue or human-intention acceptance rule. Record how authors/target setters selected these examples, because adversarially favorable task selection would narrow the inference.

For each family, use fresh, independent consumers and otherwise matched prompts, API documentation/skills, model settings, wall/CPU/token/request/storage budgets, Lean foundation and library baseline. Predeclare randomized run order and no root mathematical repairs or replacement trials. The arms are:

- **Peer-discovery treatment:** access to the real peer catalog and its ordinary API, but no exact serving result ID, source name, proof outline or preselected package. The consumer must decide when and what to query, acquire, freshly admit, and use.
- **Withheld-result control:** same local library and similar peer-catalog load, but the relevant contribution is withheld and replaced by a predeclared unrelated valid contribution. This tests whether peer knowledge changes attainable checked work, rather than merely whether the agent can prove the target unaided.
- **Direct-local comparator:** the same relevant result is already available through the ordinary local-library path. This estimates the overhead of discovering/transporting/checking a remotely supplied candidate relative to having useful mathematics locally. It is not a second mathematical acceptance authority.

The primary endpoint is a newly checked and locally published target of the *same canonical type* in every arm. In the peer arm an independent canonical audit must show that the proof's reachable body substantively uses the selected received declaration, not merely that it was imported, named, or mentioned as inert metadata. For at least one successful peer-arm target, expose the new result through ordinary owner publication and require a fresh third consumer to discover it without a handed ID, acquire it, freshly admit it and use it in a distinct successor proof. If that return leg is absent, record useful one-step reuse but **not** a demonstrated two-generation accumulation loop.

Record all outcomes, including unsuccessful searches and source attempts: search/query path, candidates examined, acquired and admitted, false leads, duplicate handling, complete publication/serving/discovery/admission/submit cost, disjoint outer-Job CPU, coordinator/agent wall time, agent token or model cost when available, package-read counts/wall, native stage counts, retained bytes and final quiescence. Count corpus creation and supplier service separately; do not treat them as zero marginal work. The same-host experiment cannot qualify independent-host operation. Both baseline and treatment may succeed; that is not a failed mathematical result, but it may fail to show a productivity advantage. If treatment alone succeeds within the matched cap, report one bounded capability gain, not a population success-rate estimate. If peer treatment costs more than the direct-local route, quantify the remote acquisition premium. Do not infer exponential growth from two generations.

The unchanged acceptance boundary is non-negotiable: every received bundle stays untrusted until receiver-local Lean checking under the selected pinned foundation; no extra axiom, `sorry`, peer success flag, popularity score, metadata match, hash alone, or inspection reply may grant mathematical acceptance. Existing source, context, byte, process and grant limits apply. Canonical type equality and reachable proof dependency are independently audited. Invalid or misleading candidates must never bypass local checking; existing negative contracts may be reused rather than multiplying peripheral metadata tests here.

**Predeclared decision rule:** no gate pass on protocol success alone. Report each task/arm separately. A bounded positive-use result needs at least one treatment publication with substantive peer dependency and a completed third-consumer checked successor within the frozen limits. An *efficiency advantage* requires a specified paired success/cost comparison that includes complete relevant work; the result may instead be positive capability with unknown or negative efficiency. If no treatment consumer discovers a relevant result despite its presence, prioritize retrieval policy/interface within the existing catalog. If it discovers and admits but cannot construct the target, investigate agent strategy/formal affordances. If it succeeds but repeated context work dominates measured cost, run the context-size discriminator next. Do not adjust thresholds, corpus or prompts after seeing outcomes and call the same allocation a pass.

## Conditional context-cost discriminator, not a preselected optimization

If the full-workflow gate identifies repeated checking/context construction as a central cost, freeze a separate matched experiment with the **same target proof and local acceptance policy**, contrasting small and larger genuinely selected dependency closures (for example 1, 4, 8 and up to the supported 16 packages), with enough trials and ordering controls to distinguish package count from warm process/filesystem effects. Measure complete `admit` and `submit` wall/CPU and stage time, package reads/bytes, plan size and peak memory; include a direct-local baseline. A second control may compare an equivalent consolidated closure with the same mathematical declarations to isolate packaging overhead, if that consolidation can be constructed and freshly checked without changing semantics. The 16-package and 64-MiB caps are supported **limits**, not promised affordable throughput. Refusal or runaway marginal cost within the current cap would warrant an architectural context-selection/compaction change before a second reader microoptimization. Retain any failed allocation and do not enlarge a grant merely to make the result pass.

## Why the alternatives rank lower *now*

- **Another package-encoding/insertion microoptimization** might help, but the previous timing gate is incomplete and current native receipts do not identify that duplicate hash as the dominant share. A local reader ratio cannot by itself establish cheaper end-to-end knowledge reuse.
- **Another metadata or inert-view scale test** would extend already scoped parser/index/hosting evidence while leaving the central mathematical-growth question unanswered. Exact-symbol postings and 65-entry transfer are useful mechanisms, not proofs that relevant knowledge is found or reused.
- **Remote catalog/contact expansion** is a serious future issue for a very large decentralized network. Yet a larger directory is not automatically a useful one, and no current evidence shows a withheld useful theorem became unreachable because of the 256-entry/selected-contact bound. Test a concrete recall obstruction or useful-workload failure before changing the routing protocol. The independent physical-host profile remains an unexecuted deployment qualification, not something same-host tests can honestly substitute for.
- **Perfect release** has no finite verifiable meaning. A publishable v1 needs a declared support envelope and explicit unresolved conditions; licensing and independent-host qualification cannot be silently marked complete. The next experiment should decide whether the *core reciprocal-use proposition* works beyond a scripted/tiny success, not promise all mathematics or all network sizes.
