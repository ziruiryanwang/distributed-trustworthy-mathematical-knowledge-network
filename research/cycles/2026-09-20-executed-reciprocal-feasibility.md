## Exact observed claim

The selected source-derived foundation consists of 456 atomic units and 581 exported declarations. Its canonical seed is 262,520 bytes. A fresh data-only receiver checked every unit and matched every normalized generated inventory row. The source-derived exact-name interpretation, not merely a digest or familiar declaration spelling, remains the chosen bootstrap basis.

On that basis, let `f(n) := Nat.succ n`. A first finite package contains f and `ell(n): f(f(n)) = succ(succ(n))`. After actual acquisition/checking, a successor package expresses `triple(n): f(f(f(n))) = succ(succ(succ(n)))` using the acquired proof instance as `ell(f(n))`. This is checked conversion and a real instance reference, not an assertion of dependency in advisory metadata. The successor's accepted native types/bodies are re-encoded; reconstructing its canonical objects preserves all 19 identities. A third fresh process independently reconstructs and checks the re-export.

The ordinary fixture is deliberately narrow: closed forall statements, zero declaration-universe parameters, one safe definition, two proof instances and an empty semantic representative map. Full seed support is retained, but a successful ordinary path does not test arbitrary external inductive/opaque declarations, nonempty telescopes, alternative representatives, library-scale joins or all literal/computation behaviors.

## Conditional reliability argument

Let S be the exact experiment-owner-selected source-derived seed; let A be the reviewed canonical-byte/identity adapter; let K be the pinned Lean checker. The observed path supports a conditional argument, not a proof of K's consistency:

1. A rejects malformed canonical bytes and mismatched content identities, and resolves the exact selected closure into explicit terms. Its correctness and hash binding are assumptions of the composed acceptance implementation.
2. The receiver reconstructs S in an empty environment through checked declaration operations. Inert inventory records can influence planning but are never inserted as authority; actual generated output must match them. The forged Eq.refl field-count control was rejected at this comparison.
3. Each ordinary declaration references only selected seed entries or earlier actually checked contributed declarations. Generated names bind complete canonical typed identifiers. K checks every body at its stated type. A canonically encoded, correctly hashed but ill-typed proof was rejected by K.
4. Checked output is obtained from the receiving environment, not an input echo. A reconstructs its objects and verifies the exact original identities. Another fresh K execution checks the re-export, without trusting a sender's success bit.

Under correct A/K/runtime behavior, exact selected S semantics, and intact bindings, accepted terms therefore have their recorded formal types over that basis. This does not give arbitrary peer-selected axioms or kernel-sensitive names authority. The Lean JSON entry alone is not a trustless remote interface: cryptographic/object binding is currently performed by the explicitly trusted local Python adapter. Local experiment selection is not production foundation adoption.

No participant identity is required for this mathematical acceptance argument. Signatures, ledger consensus and SNARKs would not repair a wrong theorem/proof binding or an incorrect checker relation. They may serve later availability, delegation or service-accounting needs, but this experiment supplies no reason to place them ahead of the native interoperability bottleneck.

## What the result changes—and does not

The most immediate existential obstruction has narrowed: a complete selected foundation can be reconstructed from modest canonical seed bytes, and one exact reciprocal formal-use path works. This supports retaining the selected architecture while testing harder interoperability cases, rather than inventing another exchange format because native feasibility was unmeasured.

The experiment does not show that theorem generation creates useful growth. Here the successor can also be proved by reflexivity; imported proof use is not evidence of necessity, time saved or knowledge discovery. A proof's validity does not depend on a human's intended question, but a practical claim that the commons improves future work still needs a separate operational evaluation. That distinction keeps human intention out of the acceptance mechanism without substituting theorem counts for utility.

The result also does not show universal replication, sustained availability, complete indexing, network fault tolerance, a qualified hostile-input boundary or a full Lean/mathlib exporter. An index may nominate useful candidates; it cannot replace evidence retrieval and checking. A model may learn mathematical heuristics; its weights are not the canonical proof store. Retaining this separation remains justified by the successful checked path.
