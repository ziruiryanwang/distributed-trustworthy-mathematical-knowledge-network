complete genuinely useful, formally checked downstream results more cheaply
with available reusable knowledge, including search, cold verification,
contribution, failures, replication and amortized production costs. Local test
endpoints are experimental instruments, not a network task list or a dependence
on human intention. Heuristic usefulness signals remain separate from validity.

Select the smallest consequential test or implementation that can change these
decisions; reuse existing specs and evidence rather than repeating campaigns.
