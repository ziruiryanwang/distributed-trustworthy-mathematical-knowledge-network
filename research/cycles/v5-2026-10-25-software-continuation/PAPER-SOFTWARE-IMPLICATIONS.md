## Decision

**The current paper does not displace bounded unknown-provider discovery as
the next concrete software task.** Proceed with the root-selected optional
one-hop HTTP contact directory, subject to its separate source review. It repairs
a demonstrably absent investigator operation with the existing ordinary client;
it is not a new mathematical-growth mechanism or the whole large-scale solution.

The priority is conditional on that narrow implementation: no mandatory Node/DHT
service, new proof system, global concept catalog, mandatory compatibility
workbench or new research experiment follows from the paper. Finish this seam,
then reassess the next actual investigator obstruction rather than automatically
extending the directory.

The current paper is **Sharing Mathematics Without Freezing Its Concepts**,
not the earlier discovery-study design. Its [README](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/papers/PAgE-2027/paper/blue-sky-final/README.md>)
calls it a theory-led vision, not an empirical growth report. Its
[manuscript](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/papers/PAgE-2027/paper/blue-sky-final/main.tex>),
lines 91-127 and 270-365, separates warrant, adequacy, realized capability and
value, and assigns distinct responsibilities to checking, scoped relations,
discovery, custody and independent inquiry. No one responsibility entails the rest.

## Consequence: make unfamiliar providers reachable without assigning inquiry

**Actual workflow.** An investigator already has a locally chosen formal query
and a small approved set of contacts, but not the endpoint of a relevant
specialist. The current public `find` requires every selected provider to be an
explicit configured endpoint; the paper cannot make that missing step disappear.
See the adopted package's
[peer_api.py](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/experiments/v5-local-checks/fair-investigator-assembly01/assembled/package/peer_api.py>),
lines 102-120 and 180-205, and the independent
[routing audit](ORDINARY-ROUTING-GAP.md).

**Smallest change.** Permit optional bounded, advisory specialist-contact replies
from replaceable owner-selected directory contacts. Root's one-hop HTTP design
reuses the ordinary HTTP/fair-search path and pure egress policy. Bind routing
to the actual ordinary namespace and supported query features; a returned label
must not establish a new mathematical meaning, accepted source or trusted checker.
Keep directory acquisition and subsequent search inside explicit aggregate
limits, with provenance/coverage sufficient to explain what was contacted.
No recursive referral crawl, peer-granted budget, or mandatory central operator.

**Why this remains first.** The missing operation exists independently of the
paper's untested encounter-policy bet. Closing it enables exposure to work the
investigator did not preconfigure, while preserving independent choice. It is
also smaller than requiring a retained DHT runtime on each ordinary reader.
The paper itself requires replaceable incomplete specialist indexes and rejects
exhaustive concept-operation comparison (manuscript lines 361-365).

**What it does not fix.** A directory cannot make a broad posting selective,
make unknown vocabulary match known symbols, prove mathematical relevance,
ensure a referred provider is live, or establish worldwide coverage. A directory
returning no candidate is not evidence that no relevant provider or mathematics
exists. The [selectivity audit](SEARCH-LOCALITY-DECISION.md) supplies a separate
honest-provider linear-scan counterexample and an existing optional pair-feature
remedy. Retain that release obligation; do not relabel one-hop routing as global
semantic-search scalability. These limits make the selected operation narrower,
not unnecessary.

## Consequence: expose checked relations as ordinary mathematics before adding a workbench

**Actual workflow.** After encountering an interface `c` and operation `f`, a
local agent may ask whether `Stable(c,f)` holds, whether a checked obstruction
exists, or which additional semantics would settle the question. That question
is chosen locally; it is neither an acceptance condition nor a public task list.

**Reuse already present.** The adopted
[ordinary adapter](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/experiments/v5-local-checks/fair-investigator-assembly01/assembled/package/support/discovery/ordinary_export_adapter.py>)
supports exact declared-type keys, known portable type symbols, declaration kind
and result/binder heads (lines 60-92, 132-219).
[query.py](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/software/v5/ordinary_query_context/query.py>) supports manual
known bindings or an explicitly selected checked context; the
[formal query helper](<E:/Distributed Trustworthy Mathematical Knowledge Network/software/v5/ordinary_formal_query/formal_query.py>)
binds that exact query syntax to portable identities. These are sufficient
candidate-search inputs for ordinary declarations stating a particular law or
its negation. The existing preview/inspect, independent intake and approved
source-authoring path remains the way to acquire, check and extend them.

This is exact structural matching, not general unification, definitional
equivalence, theorem transport or automatic discovery of which relation to ask.
A conditional general theorem can require local instantiation and discharge of
assumptions; an exact query for one instance does not guarantee finding it.

**Minimal next action.** No new relation wire schema or automatic workbench is
justified now. If a real user interaction later needs this presentation, make it
an optional local adapter over accepted ordinary declarations. Preserve the
paper's distinction: `supported` requires an accepted exact theorem;
`obstructed` requires an accepted negation; failed tactics, unavailable bodies,
timeouts and empty search remain `unresolved` with their operational causes.
Conditional evidence retains its hypotheses and exact policy/context. A
counterexample claim is checked mathematics, not a provider's advisory label.

The paper explicitly says ordinary repositories can implement the interaction
and that there is no distinct intervention when ordinary retrieval already
provides equivalent evidence (lines 307-341). This is a reason to avoid duplicate
infrastructure, not evidence that relational discovery is already solved.

## Consequence: preserve plural concepts and enough semantics; do not score growth by refinement

**Actual workflow.** One investigator develops a richer interface supporting a
new composition. Another derives a simpler invariant suited to a different
question. Both may remain useful, and old theorems about the original interface
remain true. The paper's four-state refinement and two-state reachability
continuation demonstrate this possibility analytically; they are not an observed
agent invention or a deployed software result.

**Architectural constraint, mostly already satisfied.** Keep immutable exact
definitions and interfaces, alongside explicit checked bridges and assumptions.
Do not replace an old interface merely because another is newer, finer, more
popular or smaller. A projection alone does not prove that a new operation
descends; separately proved compatibility laws are necessary. Avoid an automatic
"latest concept" migration or a monotonically increasing refinement score as
the meaning of mathematical progress.

The existing ordinary identity/query boundaries already avoid pretending that
distinct definitions are equivalent. Retain that behavior rather than introducing
a second mandatory concept registry. Directory topic hints must stay advisory;
they cannot silently substitute one symbol identity or interface for another.

**Remaining operational obligation.** A useful public contribution must expose
enough declarations, behavioral laws or bodies for the recipient's intended
mathematical operation. A hash or checked limited interface does not supply
missing semantics, and a source offer does not establish durable custody.
Which semantics suffice depends on the local question; neither downloading all
historical proofs nor making all source executable by default follows. The
existing approved-source offers are a relevant handoff, not complete availability
or hostile-source-isolation evidence.

**Growth justification.** Directory success establishes exposure, not realized
capability or value. Compare any later claimed benefit with a persistent ordinary
library that can keep the same discoveries and derive equivalent bridges. Charge
creation, lookup, checking, adaptation, retention and failed search; count saved
acquisition at most once per capability per persistent recipient. The paper's
theorems constrain admissible architectural claims but do not prove a growth rate
or justify a new benchmark now (manuscript lines 368-417).
