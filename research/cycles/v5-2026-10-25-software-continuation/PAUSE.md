## Root synthesis

The ordinary package cannot currently discover an unknown provider endpoint;
its fair scheduler only searches explicitly supplied contacts. Generic existing
index rendezvous can use the ordinary namespace without certificate machinery.
However, importing the older high-level client would couple it to the wrong
backend. The reusable low-level routing runtime also brings a 5,896-file census,
a 192-MiB cap and a reader role configured to listen/announce as a DHT server.
Therefore an automatic wholesale routing port is not selected.

After resumption, first compare a thin optional existing-location composition
with bounded owner-approved contact federation. Decide which gives ordinary
investigators useful provider discovery at acceptable full deployment cost.
Preserve egress policy, descriptor expiry, one original resource grant/session,
finite closure and advisory-only contact authority. Provider advertisements must
actually overlap the client's route choices; hash routing alone cannot supply
relevance or coverage. No new DHT is presumed necessary.

Routing and within-provider selectivity are separate. For the current atomic
conjunction query, selected full-domain anchor work is governed by
`sum_(provider,partition) min_feature |posting|`. Perfect routing and 256 fixed
partitions do not remove this term. The recorded A-only/B-only/rare-AB family
exhibits the limitation without a large experiment. Existing explicitly selected
materialized feature pairs can reduce the relevant anchor to the intersection,
but require a new ordinary metadata namespace and real demand sufficient to pay
indexing/storage costs. Do not port them solely because a synthetic win is easy.
