## What the network is for

An agent should gain affordable, trustworthy inferential and computational
resources from others, and return resources that others can actually use.
The commons contains formal types, definitions, statements and warranted
interfaces; it need not contain every consequence, every ancestral proof at
every node, or a universally current total catalogue. Logical validity is
independent of intentions and popularity. Local usefulness is not.

The adopted epistemic stance is operational and plural: knowledge is warranted
access to mathematical structure and methods relative to an explicit formal
policy. Counting true sentences or deductive closure cannot measure practical
growth: a fixed foundation already entails many undiscovered consequences,
renaming creates arbitrary counts, and answer-containing advice trivializes
existential feasibility. Realized policy/endowment-relative capabilities avoid
that error (GROWTH-UNIFORMITY.md). This stance does not purport to settle all
philosophy of mathematics or force a single global utility measure.

## Three interacting bottlenecks

1. Finding: a local query must reach selective bounded postings without corpus
   enumeration. The actual candidate02 argument-head/wildcard streams offer
   conditional syntactic coverage and fixed-cut paging. Overlay maintenance,
   finite provider leases, custody and owner-selected participation supply the
   service; partitions and exhausted budgets still mean incomplete discovery.
2. Believing and using: certified cuts authenticate premise interfaces without
   ancestry replay. The recipient checks its newly demanded proof and semantic
   data. Actual native-fast checking now avoids the VM trace while retaining
   genuine receipt verification. It has a distinct local trust boundary, not a
   fictitious transferable certificate. Thin verification next removes unrelated
   proof-production code from its recurrent execution-integrity closure.
3. Accumulating: theorem application and transparent-definition compilation are
   existing usable mechanisms. Direct definition circulation is the newly
   selected missing interface: a type-correct body should be shareable as itself,
   not packaged as a tautology merely to pass a proposition-only transport.

These changes alter the working loop. No new reputation token, attribution
ledger, mandatory research agenda or global coordinator is required.

## Compositional obligations, with no magical inference

For each actual use, let F be the adopted mathematical policy, I the demanded
interfaces/bodies, P the new proof, and C the distinct certified frontier.
Under the stated checker/compiler/cryptographic assumptions, successful fresh
verification of every C entry plus successful checking of P relative to exactly
those interfaces yields local warrant. Hash matching alone proves neither
truth nor availability. Native and guest execution assumptions remain distinct.
Whole-workflow cost includes fixed adopted software, P,I,C, query/custody,
failures and return. The history behind C is not a mandatory recipient input.

A standalone definition is a special closed case: the exact current relation
can typecheck an F-closed transparent body with zero premise frontier/rank0.
No new succinct receipt is required for local checking of that body. This does
not waive checking, allow untracked axioms, eliminate body transfer, or give old
proposition readers authority to accept a new object kind. Acquired bytes are
untrusted until the receiving checker acts; downstream reuse may inline or
compile them under existing limits. More general definition environments remain
a separate mathematical/implementation problem, not an implicit new feature.

## What useful acceleration would establish

The equal-output squaring law supports a precise method transformation:2^r-1
multiplications versus r under a stated strict-value execution model. A named
causal route with total overhead K is beneficial when l(2^r-1)>K+ur and all
resource/service premises hold. This is a theorem about that method and route,
not about optimal mathematicians, autonomous discovery, demand or this entire
network. The current semigroup example has actual checked value evidence;
its original capacity failure remains charged. The software supports reuse of
methods; it does not establish exponential production of valuable discoveries.

Cheap trustworthy reuse can free a budget Delta for a fixed continuation.
Under observation/state/service coupling, prefix-preserving extra computation
weakly increases its opportunity to produce acceptable new work. Strict growth
requires an actual productive continuation, not just saved CPU or more objects.
The theory therefore directs engineering toward lower recurring costs, portable
definitions, maintained discoverability and affordable certified cuts, rather
than a global theorem counter. Heuristic local selection remains legitimate.

## Remaining decisive tests and decisions

- Thin verifier: exact real verification code, bounded build, genuine and altered
  receipt/journal/image controls, installed fast route and deterministic closure
  reduction; compare only complete endpoints at honestly stated measurement scope.
- Definition circulation: separate kind/domain, type-only query, actual offer and
  acquired offline custody, unchanged native definition check and actual reuse;
  reject type/body/kind/policy/binding confusion. Do not count packing as checking.
- Final coherence: one documented selected installation, current code shipped,
  exact manifest and complete delta, no unexplained source mixing. User-owned
  sealer resources remain separate and must be funded; blind READY retries are
  neither progress nor an automatic growth mechanism.

Successful focused gates justify only their implications. The much broader
Lean library boundary, supported deployment environments, owner adoption and
service envelopes must remain explicit in the final requirement audit. No
deadline or package name will convert an unsupported premise into a result.
