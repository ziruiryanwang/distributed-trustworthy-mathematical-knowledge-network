## Design discipline

Theory should eliminate bad implementation directions before benchmarking.
Source-byte counts and executable dependency proofs are stronger than blind
profiling for repeated full-install validation. Demand is unknown; growth
justification must identify a region where a real available application is net
beneficial, not assume global popularity or exponential knowledge production.
No mandatory task list, central service, natural-language intention test or
political attribution system is added. Local agents retain independent goals.

The network must distinguish correctness, availability, discoverability and
usefulness. It cannot promise complete fresh global state during partitions.
Capacity and churn assumptions must be explicit; bounded failure/INCOMPLETE
is legitimate, but an always-incomplete mechanism is not a useful scaling claim.
