# When the commons produces a net capability gain

2026-10-06 resumed v4. Consequence-bearing synthesis, not a claim that the full
goal is complete. Root owns actual integration and adoption. This note adds no
campaign and changes no frozen implementation. Numerical receiving claims are
independently reviewed; future workload bounds are explicit assumptions.

## Decision

The defensible growth mechanism is **resource-bounded access to reusable formal
capabilities**, not theorem counting or enlargement of deductive closure.
V4 should make existing mathematics cheaper to obtain and use, allow finite new
constructions from acquired mathematics, and keep this loop operable through
funded discovery, custody and occasional certified cuts. These are complementary
requirements. A successful proof checker alone supplies none of the last three.

There is already a nonempty, measured positive region: the same actual received
theorem is checked at the same assurance boundary with less known CPU; charging
the separately measured compact installation still leaves a positive saving in
this particular trace. There is also a deterministic reduction of billions of
asset-hash bytes per selected receiving operation. Neither fact proves a net
research-loop advantage, future average CPU savings, or exponential growth.

The deployable policy is therefore conditional: retain the unchanged proof
authority, expose complete costs, select only funded routes, and refuse/defer
unfunded work. Use local usefulness estimates for optional service allocation,
never for mathematical acceptance. Do not spend additional proof-campaign budget
merely to turn a one-pair measurement into an apparent universal growth law.

## The philosophical commitments that have software consequences

We need not resolve mathematical realism versus formalism to build this system.
The engineering position has three precise commitments:

1. **Structural content.** A contribution denotes an explicit formal object or
   relation relative to a foundation and checker policy. Its identity commits to
   the actual definition/type/proof data; an agent's intended question is not an
   acceptance input. A well-typed result can be accepted even if no one requested
   it, while a popular or intended result can still be false or unavailable.
2. **Fallible infrastructure.** Acceptance is conditional on the adopted formal
   foundation, checker relation, cryptographic assumptions and implementation
   boundary. Neither consistency nor complete Lean4 correspondence is inferred
   from successful receipt verification. Discovery metadata is not proof.
3. **Resource-bounded mathematical practice.** What a finite agent can discover,
   verify, compute, construct and reuse matters in addition to what follows
   abstractly. A lemma may add no consequence to deductive closure yet replace
   repeated derivation by an affordable certified use. A transparent definition
   may add no logical strength yet supply a useful reusable operation.

Thus no global utility oracle, attribution rule, human-intention interface or
unsolved-task list is required. A local evaluator may choose exact formal
endpoints and resource weights; that choice is an evaluation lens, not a field
that the network requires for truth. Different participants may disagree about
usefulness without disagreeing about accepted bytes and their formal policy.

Let K be available mathematics and service, F the adopted formal policy, b a
resource vector, e a service envelope, X a class of exact formal input/output
relations, and W a fixed uniform class of supported installed workflows. W is
held fixed in the comparison; it is not a different answer-hardcoded program
freely supplied for every endpoint. Charge description/adoption, authoring and
planning when a workflow needs newly supplied code or evidence. Define

`Cap_F^W(K,b,e) = {x in X : a workflow in W realizes x within b and e}`.

A realization includes its required discovery, acquisition, checking, application
and return, not just its final proof instruction. This is an existential
capability set; it does not promise an arbitrary agent will find the witness.
For a fixed endpoint x, its feasible resource frontier is often the more useful
measurement than the cardinality of Cap. The same verified endpoint at lower
cost is an improvement without inventing additional theorem names.

**Conditional monotonicity.** If old data/workflows/resources remain available
and new contributions are optional, every old witness can still run, so the old
Cap is a subset of the new Cap. If new data evicts old data, forces maintenance,
changes a foundation or consumes a shared budget, that proof does not apply.
This is why preservation and full accounting are substantive conditions, not
free benefits hidden in the definition.

A general complete computable semantic-equivalence test is not available for
the unbounded expressive family (for example arbitrary total Nat functions),
not literally for a fixed finite byte/step-capped search space. A usefulness
judgment additionally needs a specified workload/value relation. Exact duplicate detection, bounded
structural matching, proof-checked equivalence in named cases and local usage
signals are useful partial methods. Duplicate syntax can be waste, but redundant
custody or a shorter equivalent proof can improve capability. A system-wide
ban on all semantically redundant objects would therefore be wrong even with
an oracle.

## A complete comparison contract and usable sensitivity test

For a fixed endpoint, assurance and finite service interval, compare complete
routes, not selected attractive steps. Keep a resource vector rather than
adding seconds to bytes. A route's account contains:

| Component | What must be charged |
|---|---|
| Setup and adoption | Distribution download/copy, dependency installation, exact adoption, cold caches, later upgrades and all relevant controllers/workers |
| Agent and derivation | Search strategy, plan/definition authoring, LLM/tool costs, proof construction and unsuccessful attempts |
| Mathematical work | Parent receipt checks, local proof/definition checking, demanded conversion, preparation and every successful/failed certification |
| Demand | Each actual application and delivery under the declared demand envelope; anticipated uses are not observed uses |
| Discovery | Queries, routing maintenance, provider publication/renewal, hot-key work, retries and incomplete/failing searches |
| Custody | Actual stored bytes, replica count, retention time, serving bandwidth, repair and expiry; a provider hint is not custody |
| Failure and residual | Interrupted work, failed transfers, stale hints, monitoring, migration and any other changed cost |

Include CPU, transfer/storage traffic, peak capacity and byte-seconds of custody.
Money/energy/GPU may require additional dimensions or a declared price conversion;
they are not silently encoded as CPU. Observed nested worker costs are not added
again to encompassing Job totals. Earlier failed work is not erased when a later
route passes. Shared costs cancel in a *difference* only when the same service
contract really incurs them in both routes; cancellation does not make them free.

For migration of an existing receiver with n comparable future uses, a transparent
CPU sensitivity forecast is

`net_saving = n * theta * 18.703125 - 2.609375 - E_cpu`.

Here theta is an explicitly assumed retained fraction of this pair's per-use
saving, and E_cpu is **all** additional differential setup, acquisition,
maintenance, custody-serving, failures, planning and other CPU not already counted.
Neither theta nor E_cpu is estimated by the one pair. The equation is a forecast
under stable per-call/service assumptions, not a lower bound inferred from data.
For n=10, the remaining allowance for E_cpu before break-even is:

| Assumed retained per-use saving theta | Allowed E_cpu below break-even |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 184.421875 s |
| 0.5 | 90.90625 s |
| 0.25 | 44.1484375 s |
| 0.1 | 16.09375 s |

For example, the explicit model n=10, theta=0.25 and E_cpu<=30s leaves at least
**14.1484375s**. This is a nonempty threshold-positive model with measured
constants, **not evidence that arbitrary users satisfy its premises**. It is
operationally actionable: reserve/measure all extra work and defer when the
remaining allowance is exhausted. It would become an observed whole-route gain
only after measuring those costs and matching all endpoint/service conditions.
If theta=0, positive setup makes this model unfavorable; if demand is unknown,
economic advice must abstain. Any constrained non-CPU dimension must also fit.

This current receiving advantage is not a comparison against deriving the theorem
from scratch. The latter needs a complete derivation-cost baseline; agent reasoning
and planning costs are currently unknown. No numerical research-acceleration or
LLM-productivity factor is claimed.

## How the mechanism can continue instead of exhausting itself

### Proof work: periodic certified cuts, not free ancestry

The selected receiving path pays requested interface bytes I, local/unsealed
proof work P and distinct certified frontier F, but does not fetch or replay
ancestral proof history H behind those cuts. Large I, P or F can still make
an operation expensive or refused. In particular, a certificate does not make
a demanded definition body or its conversion computation disappear.

The [recurrent-cut theorem](../../../experiments/rollover-policy-01/THEORY.md)
has an explicit favorable region. For a sequential chain with per-step cost
C+alpha*k and a complete funded successful cut cost B0+beta*h every h steps,

`A(h) = C + beta + alpha*(h+1)/2 + B0/h`.

For alpha>0 and h>1, this improves on sealing every result exactly when
`h < 2B0/alpha`; h=2 improves when B0>alpha and the hard cap permits2. At any
fixed feasible h, total cost for N complete-epoch contributions is N*A(h),
whereas no cuts cost N*C+alpha*N*(N+1)/2. Under *this model*, fixed budget R
can support order R contributions with funded periodic cuts versus at most
order sqrt(R) in the uncapped replay chain when alpha>0. This is a meaningful
capability-throughput implication, not exponential semantic growth.

The cost model must include demand, publication, adoption, custody and failures.
Its successful finite seal bound and recurring restored conditions are premises.
One old certificate does not calibrate B0,beta for a new object; source-free
preparation is not successful certification. DAG sharing, specialization and
frontier width invalidate blindly replacing h by arbitrary object count.
The implemented rollover advisor consequently chooses among at most32complete
owner-supplied route quotes and distinguishes bounds from estimates. It uses
actual fragment metadata, abstains on unknown demand and emits nonexecuting
existing prepare-seal arguments. Its23passing focused contracts establish this
decision seam, not the supplied cost bounds or a universal economic optimum.

### Semantics: new reusable constructions, not just wrappers

The [semantic decision](SEMANTIC-GROWTH-DECISION.md) removes a concrete
constructability barrier: the untrusted compiler expands selected closed
transparent definitions into the existing foundation-closed expression DAG,
under explicit universe instantiation and finite limits. Hash-consing preserves
equal subexpressions. A fixed-type repeated-composition family can therefore
have O(n)distinct syntax nodes although fully expanded evaluation may require
2^n black-box calls. Representation compression is not computation compression.

The actual unchanged guest, not compiler success, must check the produced
definition and theorem. Compilation gives a new exact identity, not a claim
that an old identity denotes new mathematics.19pure compiler contracts passed.
The selected public D0->D1->D2 application/return now has actual operational
records, including an original final deadline-admission failure and a separate
successful receiving continuation, detailed below. Mathematical outcome review
is independent of the harness author's operational audit.

This is finite composition under the held small foundation, not new inductives,
general Lean/mathlib coverage, or an unlimited named semantic DAG. Those limits
bound the available capability set and cannot be eliminated by economic language.

### Network and retention: real recurring service

The [scaling contract](SCALING-CONTRACT.md) distinguishes participant count N,
corpus M and proof ancestry H. Bounded key-directed queries avoid mandatory
corpus enumeration; healthy maintained routing can give logarithmic expected
progress under explicit coverage/delivery/load assumptions. Fixed query caps
can instead return incomplete results. No cheap complete semantic search or
fresh global status is promised under arbitrary partitions or overload.

The actual five-peer maintained lifecycle has a separate audited success:
unknown-holder discovery, retained custody, renewal, original-node loss,
known-surviving-contact restart, charged failure and observed withdrawal.
Its20.15625known CPU seconds are the cost of that whole mechanism test, not an
amortized per-theorem production-service tariff. It is same-host evidence, not
Internet latency, independent hosting, adversarial routing or uptime evidence.

A necessary long-run average capacity condition is load<=capacity. The practical
strict-slack condition below, separately per constrained resource, reserves a
margin for extra traffic/variability; it is not logically necessary for perfectly
balanced deterministic service:

`lambda*v + (lambda/h)*c + (R_objects/tau)*u + m < capacity`.

Here v is per-result work, c a cut cost, u renewal cost, tau renewal interval,
and m other maintenance. Average slack alone is insufficient: bounded bursts,
maximum jobs, outage recovery and fair service must also meet deadlines. For
example the stated sufficient homogeneous renewal margin is
`TTL/2 > scan_delay + ceil(active_rows/slots)*job_bound + delivery_margin`.
Configuration values alone do not prove its premises.

For r copies of A provider rows, aggregate row capacity must satisfy r*A<=C;
retained object bytes require additional actual custodians. Permanent positive
incoming bytes and finite storage cannot coexist indefinitely without additional
capacity, deduplication/compression, expiry or selection. Local usage heuristics
may help select optional custody, but rare useful mathematics must not become
false because it is unpopular.

## Release consequence

The strengthened v4 argument has three distinct levels that must remain visible:

- **Established local gain:** unchanged actual theorem/checking endpoint with
  lower measured known receiving CPU in one pair, positive trace arithmetic
  even after actual compact installation, and an exact reduction in selected
  asset-hash byte work.
- **Conditional sustained gain:** funded bounded cuts can prevent quadratic
  chain replay; supported transparent composition can retain small representations;
  maintained routing/custody can keep selected capabilities reachable. Each has
  explicit resource and semantic premises with corresponding implementation.
- **Open broad claim:** net faster independent mathematical research and continued
  large-network useful accumulation across broad Lean mathematics, with all
  derivation/service costs and real demand, are not established by these facts.

Do not promise exponential knowledge from finite capacity. The actionable target
is a growing, preservable set of supported capabilities at lower full cost under
an explicit service envelope. That target can be theoretically justified in
named regimes, tested at changed boundaries, and improved without a central
server, mandatory intentions or a political attribution mechanism.
