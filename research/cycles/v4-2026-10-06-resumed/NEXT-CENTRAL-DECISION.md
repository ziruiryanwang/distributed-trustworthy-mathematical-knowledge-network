# Next central decision after the October 6 reaudit

Root, 2026-10-06. Adopted research/design direction, not a runtime selection.
Current usable development distribution: sealer02. Full goal: active, not met.

## One coherent direction

Prioritize **checked named library extensions with batch certification of an
extension**, not another standalone example or proliferation of checker images.
First support acyclic transparent definitions and opaque proposition theorems
over the existing foundation. A contributed inductive datatype is a later
discriminator when a workload needs it, not a blanket release requirement.
Keep the existing working F-closed lane immutable and usable.

This joins persistent reusable vocabulary with certification of shared context.
It does not assume that representation changes alone lower cryptographic cost.
The exact relation/migration obligations are in
[EXTENSIBLE-LIBRARY-CONTRACT](EXTENSIBLE-LIBRARY-CONTRACT.md); cost attribution and
current-policy batch arithmetic are in
[COST-FLOOR-DESIGN](../../../experiments/compact-sealer-01/COST-FLOOR-DESIGN.md).
Both received independent challenge; root read/adopts their corrected versions.
Neither proposed successor is shipped software.

## Why this, rather than the tempting alternatives

- The actual n10 pure-proposition case has no foundation declarations or parent
  proofs. A foundation-history cache cannot explain its cost. Dynamic schema
  and table construction are real, but their cost share is unmeasured. Do not
  promise a material speedup from replacing them without attribution.
- Existing compilation already shares repeated body DAGs. Exponential serialized
  inlining was an incorrect diagnosis. The identified bounded family instead has
  linear individual DAGs and quadratic aggregate logical rows across separately
  retained flattened capsules. Named imports address that specific duplication;
  requested computation may still be expensive.
- Existing two-independent-member certification can already share setup. Raising
  its cap alone does not permit sequential definitions or justify trusted
  environment installation. Independent batching and a checked module extension
  are different relations.
- Compact completion already has a reviewed conditional source-preservation
  argument and retained proving evidence. Another expensive proof is not needed
  merely to repeat the unchanged relation. Runtime should discriminate a genuinely
  changed semantic/cost boundary.

## Implementation obligations for the selected extension

1. Bind the canonical descriptor, external imports and local declaration slots,
   full types/universes, safety, opacity and reduction rules. No self-hash cycle,
   unsafe axiom substitution, name shadowing or implicit cross-policy acceptance.
2. Verify valid imported environments, not merely parent hashes. The extension
   guest verifies direct imported warrants and checks the actual extension.
   Recursive proof composition lets a cold receiver verify the terminal warrant,
   not certificate ancestry. Demanded semantic bodies remain authenticated costs.
3. Demonstrate reciprocal use: acquire a named operation, contribute a definition
   or law referring to it, then use its returned interface independently. Reject
   swapped imports/bodies, type/universe/opacity mismatches and a false law.
   Reuse qualified transfer machinery rather than repeating broad network tests.
4. Compare the same formal outputs/assurance against current flattening and
   separate cuts. Account for stored semantics, transferred bytes, checking,
   proving, receiving, failures and custody separately. Reservation savings are
   not measured CPU savings. State a funded, feasible service region.
5. Introduce an explicit new image/policy namespace. Preserve old receipts under
   their old policy; crossing requires a checked bridge or rechecking, not relabeling.

No requirement for arbitrary mathlib, autonomous discovery, inevitable exponential
growth, global semantic novelty detection or human intention is added.

## Efficient order

Specify import/extension semantics and soundness invariant first. Audit the
existing checker installation path; identify exact changes to the guest relation,
journal, native checker, application and receiver. Prove structural simulation
and extension conditions where feasible before building. Use a bounded
execute-only discriminator for genuinely unresolved cost attribution. Select
expensive proving only after a material improvement and complete original
resource/service allowance are justified.

Do not open parallel incompatible guest redesigns or cosmetic release branches.
Do not reopen today's prepared run under a new deadline: its inputs, costs,
original allowance and refusal remain fixed. This decision directs the next
implementation, not an unbounded runtime or a claim of correctness in advance.
