## What the growth argument really licenses

The adopted resource-bounded, structural/fallibilist stance is sufficient; adding
another philosophical axiom would not supply missing execution, demand or
semantic coverage. `GROWTH-UNIFORMITY.md` correctly fixes causal policy/endowment,
charges supplied advice, and denies free successful-program selection.

The named direct-versus-squaring comparison proves a property of an available
method, **not an isolated network effect**. That weakness is fatal to “the commons
beats independent mathematics,” but not to “the commons can transmit and support
checked useful methods.” The latter has actual software evidence above. An agent
already owning the same algorithm, checked law and adequate service is the
explicit null case: sharing may add cost. Keep it visible in the release guide.

The stronger same-method condition remains legitimate:

`sum_i (lower(independent_warrant_i) - upper(shared_warrant_i)) > upper(shared_setup_and_service)`.

It supports a scoped advantage when the bounds, same endpoint/assurance, actual
demand, causal endowments and funding premises hold. Certified interfaces remove
mandatory ancestral proof reads, giving a structural reason for a positive
region; they do not establish every needed CPU bound or supply future consumers.
Current measurements instantiate constituent mechanisms and one receiving gain,
not the full inequality for an optimized end-to-end research workload. This is
**theoretical conditional support**, not merely rhetoric, but its economic
premises must remain visibly conditional.

Saved resources can fund an unchanged prefix-preserving continuation. Strict new
useful outputs require a productive continuation/positive first-success mass;
more theorem names, more trials and a scripted D2 alone do not prove it. No
mandatory task list, intention field or automated agent planner follows.

## The decision-changing residual

For this release, the sharp remaining integration question is **a genuinely
available funded compact certified cut**, including final verification/commit,
not another generation or snapshot. Retained same-driver/image evidence has83
successful po18 segments under4 GiB; adopting fresh po18 policy is a defensible
capacity choice, not a new proof of worst-case memory or future success. Its
extra segmentation and unchanged128-segment cap still matter. The never-launched
po19 gate must not acquire a fabricated failure or success label.

If the new exact small fragment prepares with suitable observed n/a and original
funding/time, a separately selected completed cut closes that compact integration
claim. If it is too expensive, fails or only reaches READY, preserve the result
and narrow the claim: the packaging/advisor contribution remains real, but
affordable repeated cuts are unqualified. Neither outcome proves general
amortization. Do not spend beyond the original allocation to rescue a headline.

After that decision, the highest-value next growth requirement is the already
identified **checked contributed library extension**: one genuine datatype and
layered operation/law module outside fixed F, usable by another agent without
replaying old theorem proofs, with demanded bodies honestly charged. This is
more consequential than another finite-semigroup example or ranking score.
Discovery load/churn and public direct-host service also remain conditional;
unavailable second hardware is not by itself a reason to reject local progress.
