# U1 inference audit: what one matched reuse gate can decide

2026-09-22. Conceptual audit only. No product, U1 input, Lean source, service,
or consumer was run or changed here. This challenges the claims in the
[U1 protocol](2026-09-22-useful-reuse-next-gate.md), not its already audited
static-view preparation. The project's priority is independent agents
discovering, checking, using and returning formal mathematics at tolerable
whole-workflow cost; no global task list or human-intention test is proposed.

## Claim boundary

Let `P` be the previously checked chunk theorem available only in the
eight-entry treatment fixture, `T` the prospectively fixed triple-nested
target type, and `Q` a fresh contribution. Define:

```text
Select(P)  := agent chooses P from public returned metadata without its ID/name in the packet
Admit(P)   := receiver's ordinary fresh native check accepts the exact acquired package
Use(Q,P)   := Q's accepted reachable proof body has a proof-producing application of P
Publish(Q) := ordinary publication binds a proof whose complete canonical type is T
Return(Q)  := another fresh node obtains and admits exact ordinarily offered Q bytes
Cost(i)    := the recorded wall, disjoint Job CPU, requests, stages, bytes, memory,
              retained storage, operator work, and known/unknown agent-model cost of arm i
```

The full positive U1 chain is `Select(P) ∧ Admit(P) ∧ Use(Q,P) ∧ Publish(Q) ∧
Return(Q)` within the frozen envelope, with a valid control-arm observation.
Each conjunct is necessary for the *claimed finite trace*; none can be
replaced by a catalog hit, acquired bytes, private Lean compilation, a
quoted theorem name, or a nominal publication. The chain is an existential
**one-target, same-host engineered demonstration**. It does not imply that
`Q` is novel, logically needs `P`, will be selected again, or improves the
expected productivity of a large network. `Return(Q)` is re-entry
availability, not downstream use. A true second generation needs a fresh
agent to select, admit and substantively use `Q` in a distinct published `S`.

Formal/cryptographic checks can establish the conditional safety boundary:
typed IDs and SHA-256 bind package/view bytes; fresh Lean/native replay and
the independent canonical-type audit establish checked `T` and proof closure
under the selected foundation, assuming the trusted kernel, compiler/runtime
and stable local files; publication and receiving records bind the two nodes'
events. The reachable proof dependency is a checkable structural predicate,
not a proof of indispensability. By contrast, *usefulness* (target completion
within a budget, repeated use, relevance, acceptable cost) is an empirical
or heuristic assessment. No formal invariant turns one checked theorem into
general usefulness or exponential growth.

## What the matched pair discriminates

| Observed U1 result | Supported inference | Not supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment selects, admits and substantively uses `P`, publishes exact `Q`, and returns it | The complete finite discovery-to-re-entry path is operable on this curated corpus; a target-relevant prior result can be used without giving its identity to the agent | Autonomous growth at scale, independent-host operation, broad retrieval quality, theorem novelty, or that `P` was logically indispensable |
| Same, while control fails the exact endpoint within identical limits | One observed bounded **task advantage** of access to `P` under this frozen protocol | Population causal speedup or a general efficiency win from the peer architecture |
| Both arms publish exact `T`, treatment uses `P` | Optional checked reuse works; compare measured whole-workflow cost vectors | An advantage merely because the treatment proof is shorter or uses a peer |
| Treatment publishes without using `P` | Exact endpoint achieved, but this run does not demonstrate useful peer reuse | Any credit for `Select/Admit/Use` not present in the trace |
| Neither completes, or service expires/transport is uncertain | A classified failure or availability boundary, if receipts are complete | Mathematical uselessness of `P` or inability of independent agents in general |

The single random-byte order choice and matched packet improve the local
comparison but do not turn one stochastic agent realization into a causal
estimate. The seven/eight views differ in genuine mathematical content and
bytes, not only in a search implementation. The target was deliberately
constructed to be related to `P`; the corpus has only eight curated entries
and one page, so success is an interface/feasibility discriminator, not a
large-index or ranking validation. The target is an **evaluation fixture**;
the commons itself need not know it or rank contributions by human purpose.

## Central confounders to preserve in interpretation

1. **Selection versus proof construction.** A treatment miss may reflect
   metadata presentation, agent search decisions, page/slot exposure, finite
   service availability, package admission, Lean proof strategy, or source
   fragment limits. Classify the earliest failed edge from retained requests,
   receipts and proof evidence before choosing a repair. The treatment's
   checked use proves a real dependency in *that proof*, not that control
   cannot derive `T` directly or from equivalent library results.
2. **Availability and custody.** The eight-entry service is an evaluator
   static fixture, not an ordinary product-owner publication. A successful
   receive/admit/use path exercises the real receiving interface, but U1 does
   not yet prove the full owner/replica offering route for `P`. The 600-second
   service window sits inside a 45-minute arm; expiry can censor search or
   acquisition. Do not count a censored failure as irrelevance.
3. **One sample and shared host.** Sequential agents can differ in model
   stochasticity, cache state, host load and operator latency. Shared-host
   file isolation is cooperative; evaluator-file or prior-transcript leakage
   invalidates the comparison. Fresh node states and identical onboarding
   reduce but do not remove these confounders.
4. **Whole-workflow efficiency.** Discovery adds bytes, admission, service
   time and operator work; it may save some proof work. A claim of efficiency
   needs a predeclared comparison of the full cost vector and amortization
   scope, not just proof-source length, marginal kernel CPU, or a successful
   endpoint. Model tokens and unmetered agent cognition remain unknown, not
   zero. One pair cannot estimate a benefit distribution or scaling law.

## Smallest decision-changing continuation

If U1 has a checked `Use(Q,P) ∧ Publish(Q)`, the smallest next *reciprocity*
test is the already predeclared [U2](../../experiments/useful-reuse-u2/PLAN.md):
from a fresh node, find ordinarily offered `Q` without its ID, acquire and
admit it, then publish the distinct ordered binary-merge theorem `S` with a
reachable proof-producing application of exact `Q`. Its target, canonical
type, corpus, packet, budgets and stopping rule must be frozen independently
before seeing the U1 outcome; its current statement template is not yet a
type freeze or proof. A one-entry owner exposure would make finding `Q`
trivial, but a pass would still decide the important question U1 leaves open:
can a *newly returned* result actually seed another checked publication?
It would **not** decide scalable search quality.

If U1 cannot reach `Use(Q,P)`, U2 is premature: choose the next experiment at
the earliest observed broken edge (service/availability, discovery, fresh
admission, or proof use) and keep that change isolated. If U1 and U2 pass but
the design decision is whether to invest in search/ranking or claim efficient
network-wide growth, the smallest relevant follow-up is a pre-frozen,
multi-candidate **held-out target panel** across more than one theorem family,
with matched withheld-result/local-library arms and complete cost accounting.
Do not add inert decoys or choose targets after seeing agent behavior. Repeated
held-out success and cost distributions, then independent-host tests, are
needed before promoting the finite trace to robust decentralized growth.
