## Bounded next comparison

Use one exact admitted foundation and the same formal starting information, participants, evidence/availability requirements and resource owners. Known peers may be common starting information; do not selectively supply the useful answer's identifier or hidden source location as an oracle. Independent investigations remain outside the corpus.

| Case | Required outcome | Decision relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Compatible extension | Discover material, independently check complete support, use it in a checked Lean construction, export and independently replay a successor | Does narrowing compatibility preserve the actual cycle, and which reconstruction obligations disappear? |
| Conflicting declaration meanings | Two sound developments use the same nonreserved name for different definitions; a consumer needs both meanings in one proof | Show checked composition/transport or report unsupported service. Safe refusal is not equivalent successful reuse; renaming is not free. |
| Continued independent extension | A third agent discovers and uses the successor without adopting another owner's verdict or unrelated whole history | Separate required closure from accidental packaging/lineage requirements. |

For the selection axis, add only one concrete standalone definition/interface opportunity that proof-family-only nomination misses, if a consequential case exists. Compare against proof-root retention of its **full shared closure** and explicit collection adoption. K/M's scope does not establish missing nonproof publication. Fragments are selection conveniences, not indivisible trust, compulsory whole-library downloads, curator authority or permanent custody.

Use-frequency, estimated import savings, structural coverage, exploration or LLM nomination may guide selection. They cannot decide validity; low observed use does not establish no future value. Exact deduplication and checked relations are not a universal novelty oracle. Do not expand this into every heuristic or a global utility score.

**Decision threshold:** choose a narrower route only if it removes a consequential checking/reconstruction burden and its lost capability is deliberately acceptable; missing execution alone is insufficient. Pursue fragments only if they improve an identified core opportunity beyond existing collection/proof-closure behavior at honest resource/availability scope. Otherwise retain or defer. End with a decision or one precise unresolved discriminant, not four new mechanism specifications. Actual implemented operability remains a separately authorized evidence stage.
