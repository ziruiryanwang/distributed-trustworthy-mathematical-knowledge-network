## Candidate claims to discriminate, not adopted conclusions

1. **Priority:** automated formation of reusable mathematical theories with
   causal, full-cost improvement in later discovery. Distinguish statement
   invention, proof of supplied statements, artifact transfer, and whole-policy
   benefit. Compare a strong ordinary Lean researcher with durable library/notes
   against the selected explicit research policy; use fresh-recipient interventions
   to test artifact value separately. No topology superiority claim is required.
2. Reliable, history-local verified memory for independent reasoning agents:
   explicit grounded-interface semantics, a lifetime-cost obstruction, and a
   repair evaluated against ordinary optimized Lean batching/reuse.
3. Checked countermodels as useful negative knowledge for concept revision:
   requires causal downstream evidence beyond one scaffolded A-to-B episode.
4. Experience/negative-results report on trustworthy mathematical agent
   infrastructure: only compelling if it extracts general actionable principles
   and exposes oversight, costs and failed architectural investments honestly.

Novelty must survive comparison with LCF, OpenTheory, proof-carrying code/data,
ordinary compiled imports, modern Lean validation, proof-library-learning agents
and existing concept/theory exploration. Existing correctness and elementary
accounting lemmas are not new research merely because our names differ.

evidence cannot distinguish the claimed effect from scaffolding/leakage.
This is a quality/evidence gate, not a numerical acceptance-probability estimate.
