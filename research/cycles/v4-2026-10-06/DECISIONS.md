2026-10-06 UTC. Root-owned live decision record; not a release claim.

## Objective and scope

Deliver meaningful software advancement by October 6, 06:00 Chicago. Focus on
resource-bounded trustworthy mathematical capability, not corpus size. Mathematical
validity is checked under a declared foundation and assurance profile; usefulness
is a separate, fallible local judgment. Independent investigations need no global
goal, task list or natural-language interpretation.

Existing [growth philosophy](../v4-2026-10-02/PHILOSOPHY-OF-GROWTH.md) already
explains logical closure versus accessible capability. Existing [core model](../v4-2026-10-02/CORE-MODEL.md)
already supplies receipt locality and a net-cost identity. Repeating either is
not the intended advancement.

## Consequential missing mechanism

The current contributor separates prepare from cryptographic advance, but does
not supply a justified local decision policy for spending scarce certification,
discovery and retention resources. A proved theorem can be valid yet uneconomic
to certify immediately; rare complementary mathematics can be valuable without
high observed frequency. We must expose this distinction without weakening the
network's certified acceptance boundary or assuming unavailable proof witnesses.

## Work selected

1. Derive local capability/investment policies and their actual sufficient
   conditions, including complementary bundles and exploration limits.
2. Derive large-network resource envelopes and identify the smallest missing
   source/API mechanisms, separating conditional guarantees from deployments.
3. Independently challenge both before selecting consequential software work.
4. Use small experiments only for quantities or implementation seams the theory
   cannot settle. No expensive proof campaign selected by this record.

## Decision discipline

Each adopted claim must state: model and comparator; assumptions; derivation;
what code enforces; quantities unknown; failure cases; effect on the two central
goals. Heuristics may guide resource allocation, never proof validity. A theoretical
guarantee does not require an empirical duplicate proof; implementation still
needs focused conformance checks at changed boundaries.

## Selected central implementation: bounded proof frontiers

Source inspection established that the current pinned host already executes the
actual checking guest with exact fully verified certified parents, without
producing a new succinct proof. However unsealed outputs cannot be passed back
as certified parents. A checker-only wrapper would therefore not close growth.

Select [certified-frontier-01](../../../experiments/certified-frontier-01/CONTRACT.md):
inert raw proof fragments plus closed proof-term substitution, exact semantic
union and genuine final checking. Keep the native/guest/host/checker/image fixed.
A fresh 59-leaf source copy is verified; used predecessors stay unchanged.

This is a mechanism-level improvement: delay the expensive sealing investment
while allowing several independently chosen contributions to compose within
a bounded checking envelope. It does not presume that all proof fragments are
small, that local-execution evidence is transferable, or that the current fixed
foundation supports all mathematics. At a bound, an agent must seal, change
representation, choose another route or stop. No implicit ancestral download.

The source-level soundness argument is proof substitution followed by the same
checker against genuine certified frontier premises. The transformation is not
trusted for mathematics. Local SDK execution and cryptographic verification have
different TCBs; a receiver must explicitly adopt a policy that permits the chosen
mode. The original receipt-only route and its claims remain distinct.

Theory drafts now identify useful online promotion and protected-search policies,
plus a real vocabulary-growth barrier (only F-closed definitions, four per type).
These are being independently challenged. Current implementation selection does
not silently claim to solve arbitrary semantic dependencies or a global DHT.
