## Material change since October 2

October 2's contributor could verify a received parent but failed the protected
foundation bridge before downstream preparation. The current audited path removes
that practical reuse blocker without changing the adopted foundation or guest.

For **scalable proof and search**, v4 adds a consequential two-route boundary:
small, complete unsealed proof deltas above genuinely verified certified parents,
or final cryptographic receipts. Actual proof substitution and complete guest
checking let contributions compose without certifying every intermediate result.
Receivers do not fetch proof ancestry below the certified cut. Indexed fragment
discovery and actual TLS custody/return operate on selected objects; they do not
scan the global corpus. Source-free seal preparation makes a holder independent
of the original author's source, but its actual outcome is READY, not a certificate.

For **useful growth**, the received factor-map theorem actually contributes to a
new generic associative-square-block law, with independently audited exact type,
definitions and materialized received proof. Its output returned through real TLS.
The theorem supports a genuine conditional algorithm-family advantage:
`2^r−1` versus `r` multiplications for the two named algorithms. This replaces
artifact-count rhetoric with an explicit reusable capability and cost model.
It is not a new exponentiation discovery, evaluator speedup, demonstrated net
benefit over an agent's existing library, or exponential network-growth result.

These are mechanism-level advances, not merely additional passing tests.

## Ordered remaining obstacles and smallest discriminators

### 1. Close the current receiver/operator boundary once

Before another feature, finish the already selected relocation, launcher and
fresh check using **actual returned custody**. Otherwise the usable release still
depends on research-specific dispatch even though its components work. The
discriminator is exact installed identity, a genuine receiving check, bounded
closure and ordinary peer operations through the documented public path. A
producer PASS, mock launcher PASS or archive hash cannot substitute for it.
This is the only immediate qualification work recommended here.

### 2. Lower the cost of ordinary warranted reuse, not merely receipt verification

The actual family operation consumed 44.546875 whole-worker-Job CPU seconds;
its nested guest execution consumed 1.765625 seconds. The controller reported
32.359375 seconds, already included in that Job total. These scopes do not provide
a complete profiler, but they rule out treating guest execution alone as the
whole current cost. Optimizing cryptography indiscriminately misses this gap.

**Next discriminator:** source-count repeated profile/file validation,
serialization and semantic reconstruction within one immutable operation.
Specify an operation-local reuse invariant and derive the bytes/transformations
it removes before selecting code. Keep exact profile/input bindings and final
mathematical checking; do not introduce a transferable local-success cache.
Measure only an implementation constant or disputed bottleneck that this analysis
cannot determine. Separately preserve certification as an explicit investment:
source-free preparation did not reduce its proving price.

### 3. Make reusable mathematical vocabulary compositional

The fixed foundation, F-closed contributed definitions and small interface limits
exclude many ordinary developments. Parameterized operations with proved laws
already avoid some dependency expansion; they are not a universal replacement
for reusable types and definitions. Raising a byte cap alone does not fix this.

**Next discriminator:** specify the smallest acyclic definition-dependency graph,
including exact universe/body/type identity, required semantic closure and
profile-scoped caching. Establish conservative extension and bounded acquisition
in terms of the *demanded distinct* semantic graph, never the whole archive.
Then select one two-definition dependency that the current F-closed lane cannot
express directly. Checker interpretation and bridge preservation must be explicit
before a new profile/image is adopted. Preserve the difference between semantic
dependencies and lemma-proof ancestry; no silent all-Lean correspondence claim.

### 4. Turn known contacts into maintained discovery and availability

Current bounded local indexes are useful primitives, not a globally maintained
overlay. Sixteen owner-selected contacts do not imply that a relevant holder is
reachable. Hot keys, churn, exhausted storage and disappearing holders can defeat
growth while every returned proof remains valid. The present peer also lacks
ordinary retention/reclamation and crash-recovery maintenance.

**Next discriminator:** choose one explicit bounded-degree placement/routing
model with churn, replication leases and local admission limits. Derive its
conditional reachability and message/storage costs; make `INCOMPLETE` a valid
bounded-lookup outcome. For each service require demand below available capacity,
with explicit queue and burst assumptions—not aggregate global capacity alone.
Implement only its smallest advertisement/expiry/custody seam against the existing
namespace and object-binding API. No global ledger, perpetual-retention promise
or identity-free Sybil-resistance claim follows from adopting standard DHT ideas.

### 5. Extend the growth justification from a law to an available application

The squaring result proves a named-algorithm comparison, not that a recipient
gains over methods it already possesses. Some valid capabilities are redundant;
complementary ones can matter only together. A universal novelty/usefulness oracle
is neither available nor needed.

**Next discriminator:** select one concrete typed application procedure—ordinary
theorem application, a proof transformation, or a verified algorithm—and state
the before/after available options and assurance-equivalent endpoint. Prove its
cost bound including adoption, search, premise proofs and demanded computation.
For an executable route, connect the cost model to shared evaluation explicitly;
the law alone does not certify an evaluator. Require a nonempty stated demand
region where total savings exceed all extra costs. Unknown demand may remain a
local forecast, not a guaranteed network growth rate. This needs no compulsory
agent planner, human-intention test or shared research agenda.
