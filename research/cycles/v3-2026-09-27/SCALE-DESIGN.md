The fixed support is an explicitly installed baseline, not a global mathematical
database hidden under another name. Every supplied support record is exactly
authenticated; its earlier proof validation is part of baseline adoption.
The output is not on an allowlist. Its proof is checked by the certified
computation, and every nonbaseline premise needs its exact immediate-parent
certificate. Mathematical foundation trust and cryptographic execution trust
remain different assumptions.

For this profile, let `B` be fixed support, `w` the new proof, `k` the direct
parent count and `H` their total ancestral proof history. The intended costs
are receiver verification of one receipt plus its exposed interface, and
producer checking of `w` with selected `B` plus `k` immediate certificates.
Neither algorithm takes `H` as input. This is an architectural statement to
test against actual input access and receipts, not a claim that proving is
cheap, that interfaces have constant size, or that useful knowledge grows
exponentially. Static assets, parsing, selected definitions, prover startup,
compression and failures all remain chargeable costs.

Refusal by this finite profile means unsupported or invalid input under that
profile; it does not imply the mathematical proposition is false. A future
more expressive profile must close its own interface-realization and security
conditions rather than silently weaken this one.

## Selected direction

The commons should exchange **certified mathematical interfaces** as its
normal large-scale unit of reliance. Native proof/body archives remain separate.
Any participant can produce a certificate; no particular prover, index or
server is trusted to decide truth. Every receiver verifies its own selected
certificate under a locally adopted foundation and assurance profile.

An old theorem is used through its exact typed interface, not by replaying its
proof. A new theorem is checked against those certified interfaces. Its new
certificate composes the old certificates without reopening their ancestral
proof bodies. Definitions and inductive rules are not blindly erased: their
exact computation-relevant content is authenticated and selectively materialized.

This changes **admission, local Lean import, submission and export together**.
A networking-only receipt fast path would leave the central bottleneck intact.

## Three independent distinctions

| Question | Required evidence/data | What is not implied |
|---|---|---|
| May I rely on this exact theorem? | Locally checked native derivation, or an accepted-profile validity certificate bound to its interface | A signature, popularity, storage receipt or content hash alone |
| Can I use it in this new proof? | Exact type/universes and a certified interface import; selected computational support if needed | That every old proof script still elaborates with all old proof bodies hidden |
| Can I inspect/recheck its original proof? | Available native proof/body archive and a compatible checker | Availability follows neither from truth nor cryptographic certification |

These states must not collapse into one boolean called `verified`. A result
can be certified and usable as an opaque lemma even when its old proof archive
is temporarily unavailable. A definition can be certified but not executable
locally because a needed body is unavailable. Neither condition means false.

## Interface capsule, certificate and archive

The logical roles below need canonical encoding and typed commitments before
wire implementation. This is not an instruction to replace the frozen format.

    InterfaceCapsule {
      schema, foundation_id, semantic_profile_id,
      declaration_kind, ordered_universe_parameters,
      exact_type, closed_external_reference_bindings,
      computational_content_commitment?,
      native_artifact_commitment?, interface_id
    }

    ValidityCertificate {
      proof_system_id, relation_id, verifier_parameters_id,
      public_input, proof_bytes
    }

    public_input = Commit(
      foundation_id, assurance_policy_id, relation_id,
      output_interface_id, output_semantics_commitment,
      recursive_support_commitment, derivation_rank
    )

`interface_id` is an exact semantic-object identifier, not a display name.
Type/universe/reference/body roles are domain-separated. Existing proof-instance
CIDs remain unchanged archival/evidence identities; a new interface abstraction
does not rewrite them or claim semantically equivalent syntax has identical IDs.
Two proofs of one exact theorem may back one explicitly justified sealed
interface projection, but their old proof-instance names remain distinct unless
a checked translation is selected. No automatic identity merge is authorized,
and arbitrary equality of differently spelled statements is not inferred.

The certificate binds the *full meaning* of the interface, not only a theorem
name, root hash, status flag or arbitrary kernel input. A theorem's hidden proof
can be committed without being transmitted to its consumer. A definition's
type alone never identifies its computation: body/rules/attributes affecting
the supported semantics must be bound too.

Source annotations, readable names and human research intentions are not
mathematical authority. They may be separate advisory data. No attribution
scheme or global task selection is required.

## Exact relation to be proved

Let F denote the adopted foundation, rho the exact admitted semantic/checker
profile, and E an authenticated finite selected environment. A certificate
asserts the output interface has an admissible derivation under F/rho.
Its checking program must establish, for each extension:

1. Canonical parsing, size/reference bounds and typed commitments hold for
   the actual witness and public output; no disconnected pretty description.
2. Every external premise is either an explicitly permitted F primitive or
   the exact interface authenticated by an allowed parent certificate.
3. Every parent certificate binds the same compatible F/rho/policy, or an
   explicitly proved permitted translation. No arbitrary verifier key/image
   or trusted signer can introduce a different relation.
4. The selected interfaces form one compatible environment. Shared constants
   have identical exact type, universe and computation-relevant identity.
   An attacker cannot combine one certificate about a constant defined as0
   with another about the same constant defined as1.
5. The new proof/type/declaration checks under that environment, with no
   unauthorized axioms, sorry, unsafe declaration or foreign executable import.
   Inductive groups retain positivity/generated-rule correspondence obligations.
6. The output's full certified type and semantic identity match that checked
   extension, including references, universe order and policy constraints.
7. Recursion is grounded: a base rule uses only the permitted foundation;
   extension support has strictly smaller bounded ranks, or another independently
   proved well-founded PCD rule. Cyclic “A certifies B certifies A” is not a base.

An authenticated map is not a sound environment merely because it has a Merkle
root. Either its entries are individually certified under these conditions,
or the root has a certificate establishing the relevant invariant. Hidden
uncommitted assumptions are forbidden even if unused-looking in a supplied UI.

Ranks are local derivation structure, not a global sequence or consensus epoch.
Legitimate mutually inductive groups are checked atomic declarations, not
cycles between purported independent validity certificates.

## Logical bridge into Lean

The initial scalable import profile is **SEALED_PROP**: externally certified
theorems whose full types inhabit Prop are installed as opaque derived premises.
The pinned Lean implementation has axiom-shaped interface entries and module
theorem export machinery, but adding an axiom only checks its type; it does
not prove the proposition. Therefore an importer needs a separate, unforgeable-
by-input acceptance registry keyed by exact certificate/interface/profile.

Schematic argument, conditional on checker/encoding and certificate soundness:

    F proves T_1, ..., F proves T_k
    F + (c_1 : T_1, ..., c_k : T_k) checks p : U
    ------------------------------------------------
    the compatible realized extension of F proves U

The bottom step is justification by realization/substitution of valid derived
premises, not “Lean accepted axioms, therefore they are true.” All references
must have one compatible realization preserving shared definitions and rules.
The new certificate discharges every imported premise; an unbacked local
axiom cannot be published as a foundation-derived theorem.

The actual bridge must enumerate external dependencies from checked canonical
terms and all type/conversion support exposed or consulted by the checker,
match their exact registry entries and refuse unauthorized additions. Merely
scanning direct constants in the final proof term is insufficient; implicit
definition unfolding and transitive type support can introduce other premises.
This support traversal stops at an authenticated sealed theorem interface: it
does not reopen that theorem's hidden proof dependencies or flatten its entire
certificate ancestry into a local metadata list.
The source frontend is an untrusted authoring convenience. After elaboration,
new proof data is independently checked against the certified interface plan.
It may not capture a local helper's hidden axioms or silently import arbitrary
Init/library theorems as base trust. Existing foundation policy remains explicit.

Non-Prop definitions cannot enter this lane merely as same-typed axiom entries.
They require an exact definition/implementation interface, conservative safe
extension and authenticated required body/rules. The first implementation should
not broaden SEALED_PROP to arbitrary module images or trusted `.olean` files.

## Proof-dependent computation is a real boundary

Pinned Lean4.34.0 documents theorem unfolding in `Acc.rec`-related checking.
Therefore “theorems are opaque” is not a universal behavior-preservation proof.
The scale lane must **not automatically hydrate an entire proof history** when
one formerly transparent computation gets stuck. Its default is sealed reuse.

For a supported failed conversion, the author may instead use a certified
propositional equation/computation lemma and existing Lean rewriting/transport.
This changes the new proof term but not the intended exact theorem statement.
The kernel is not given an unproved new definitional-equality rule. Such a
rewrite is a candidate checked solution, not a guaranteed general repair.

An explicitly selected transparent/native audit mode may fetch exact old bodies
and incur their cost. It must be visibly separate from the scalable contract.
Missing support yields a precise refusal/need-data result, never success with a
placeholder proof. Genuine selected type/definition work remains charged; it
cannot be used to hide an unconditional ancestor replay under another label.

## One contribution, end to end

1. An agent discovers an untrusted offer for an exact interface. The index is
   a locator, not a validity authority or a complete view of world mathematics.
2. Its node fetches the small capsule and certificate, enforces byte/CPU limits,
   validates local profile and requested identity, and verifies the certificate.
3. It authenticates the chosen interface/type support. Bodies are fetched only
   for the explicitly selected computational requirements, not every lemma proof.
4. The certificate-aware importer creates a receiving namespace with exact
   typed bindings and registers the distinct assurance basis.
5. The agent independently authors p. The local checker checks its new delta
   against those bindings and gathers its actual external premise set.
6. Any prover constructs a compositional certificate for that exact delta,
   recursively verifying parent certificates inside the selected relation.
   The node treats the result as a candidate until it verifies the final proof.
7. The new capsule/certificate may be offered and replicated. Its native proof
   archive and supporting bodies have independent availability/custody records.
8. Another cold node repeats steps1–4 without the complete ancestral proof DAG.

The prover can be local, a cooperative peer or several independent providers.
It is an untrusted compute supplier, not a mandatory central service. Loss of
one prover affects progress, not validity. Local conditional checking before
certificate completion may help authors, but a conditional delta is not yet
a transferable foundation-grounded certificate and must not be mislabeled.

## Why recursion is central, not optional decoration

A certificate for a whole ancestry can make receipt cheap while forcing every
new prover to reconstruct the same growing ancestry. That fails reciprocal
growth. The selected architecture instead verifies already-produced parent
certificates as part of proving only the new extension.

For direct fan-in k, cost should have the shape

    new prover work = own delta check + selected interface/data work
                      + k parent-certificate verifications + proof overhead

and not “all historical proof checks again.” Aggregation/checkpoint certificates
can amortize frequently reused compatible subsets. They must support exact
subset membership without demanding one worldwide aggregate or update order.
A small node need not store an ever-growing global environment or chain.

A generic one-shot SNARK soundness claim does not by itself prove that arbitrary
self-recursion is sound. The selected PCD/recursive construction needs an
end-to-end adaptive composition theorem for its actual relation, verifier,
parameter use and supported recursion. Rank checks help exclude cycles but do
not replace that cryptographic theorem.

## Backend choice and trust accounting

### Current implementation discriminator, 2026-09-27

The actual full Lean4.34 guest is not currently build-ready for the inspected
RISC Zero backend; see [source-level feasibility](SCALE-BACKEND-FEASIBILITY.md).
Do not confuse that engineering gap with impossibility or silently substitute a
host checker-success bit. The native sealed-interface experiment separately
tests the Lean import seam. A prospective restricted equational relation is
being prepared with a Lean semantic soundness proof and genuine two-parent
recursion as its cryptographic discriminator. This is a declared supported
fragment, not all Lean mathematics. Its context hypotheses appear explicitly
in the resulting proposition; they are not new trusted foundation axioms.

The locally plausible Plonky2 research backend is deprecated and its defaults
have approximately95/100-bit security caveats in upstream documentation. A
working prototype with it would not by itself qualify a huge-network production
assurance profile. Sequential-only IVC was not selected as a substitute for
the ability to combine independently produced lemma certificates. Backend,
logical-bridge, circuit-fidelity and actual performance gates remain separate.

### Proving cost cannot disappear from the efficiency claim

For one exact supported mathematical result and Q downstream uses, compare
measured total costs, not only verifier latency. Let Pn be native production
cost, Pc certificate production cost including its native/checker work and
parent-certificate consumption, Vn a cold native use, and Vc a cold certified
use including interface/definition data and local verification/import.

    native total:     Pn + Q * Vn
    certified total:  Pc + Q * Vc

If Vn > Vc, certification saves aggregate work only when
Q > (Pc - Pn)/(Vn - Vc), subject to the measured costs' workload assumptions.
If Vn <= Vc there is no positive per-use saving established by these data.
This elementary comparison is not a forecast of theorem popularity; measured
reuse counts may guide a local production/cache policy but cannot establish
validity. Bytes, latency, peak memory and independent prover availability need
separate reporting; CPU alone is not an adequate network cost model.

The asymptotic requirement still applies to a cold certified path even when
its small-workload constant factors lose. Native replay and already-local
native receipts can remain separately labeled options. An uncertified offer
cannot be advertised as ready for history-independent reliance. No coordinator
must predict all future demand, and a node that lacks a compatible prover may
request work from any provider while retaining final local verification.

This design does not prematurely choose a branded proof system. The first
backend must pass a compatibility discriminator for the exact supported Lean
relation. Relevant candidates are a proof-oriented custom checker/circuit,
a deterministic checker guest in a general zkVM, and a recursive/IVC backend.
Domain-specific native certificates and proof simplification remain useful
alternatives where applicable, but are not a generic bound for arbitrary proofs.

The primary-source zkPi example inspected today targets Lean4.8.0-rc1; it is
not a qualified replacement for4.34.0. Likewise, proving that a Rust/C++ guest
ran is not evidence it implements our Lean semantics. A guest/compiler/image
and its relation fidelity require their own explicit trusted-base assessment.
Do not claim byte-identical execution of a Windows checker in a different ISA.

The security claim is computational and conditional: under the chosen
multi-theorem/recursive soundness and commitment assumptions, an efficient
adversary cannot cause acceptance of a false relation instance except within
the specified security bound. This supplements—not replaces—faithful encoding,
the foundation, the Lean/checker implementation and the local certificate importer.
Zero knowledge is not required; verification compression is the purpose.

Security parameters must account for large adaptive usage and composition.
This does not require a global counter of all verification calls or a consensus
ledger. The actual selected security theorem and its finite operating bounds
must be stated rather than inventing an unbounded “zero chance of error” claim.

## Availability, recovery and degradation

Native archives are valuable for independent replay, alternate checkers,
learning from proofs, and recovery after a backend flaw. Replication is a
storage policy, not a mathematical certificate. No finite proof establishes
that data will remain accessible forever.

If the capsule or needed type/body support is missing, use is unavailable. If
only the old theorem proof archive is missing, sealed certified reliance can
remain possible under its explicit profile. A verifier/profile vulnerability
can require quarantining or recertifying affected results; their mathematical
truth and their currently justified local status are different statements.

Native replay remains an optional independent verification route. A node may
refuse a crypto profile. Its resulting workload/availability limits must be
clear; such policy diversity does not authorize silently downgrading another
node's requested assurance basis.

## New release gates

1. Exact profile/relation/import format and adversarial review, including
   statement, definition, foundation, universe and verifier-key substitutions.
2. Real pinned-Lean sealed-interface use and a downstream derived theorem,
   with exhaustive imported-premise accounting and controlled conversion cases.
3. Actual compatible cryptographic prover/verifier, not a mocked pass or trusted
   signer, on the declared fragment; record all added trust assumptions.
4. A cold receiver and next prover avoid transitive proof-body downloads/replay
   as ancestor proof size and unrelated corpus size independently grow with
   fixed selected interfaces and local task. No global metadata scan is hidden
   in admission, certificate lookup, import, export or prover preparation.
5. Composition over more than one generation, arbitrary untrusted prover,
   bad/unavailable certificates/data, budget exhaustion and cleanup controls.
6. Whole-loop CPU/bytes/memory/latency and proving costs, including cold startup,
   compared to native replay on the same mathematical outcomes.

Gate2 can use an honestly labeled locally native-checked premise registry to
isolate the Lean interface question. It does not pass gate3 or4. Merely exposing
an interface called “certificate verifier” is not implementation of those gates.

The native source16 correctness/reader passes are preserved engineering gains.
They do not waive these gates. A tentative release may state completed subsets,
but may not advertise the newly requested huge-network checking capability
until the actual certified acceptance-and-reuse loop is qualified.
