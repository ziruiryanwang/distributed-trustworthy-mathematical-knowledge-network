## Growth: the exact conclusion this gate cannot supply

Keep three comparators from
[GROWTH-CRITICAL-PATH](../v5-2026-10-16/GROWTH-CRITICAL-PATH.md):

- L: optimized independent local mathematical development.
- O: ordinary shared library with proof-DAG sharing, caches and local checking.
- V: the v5 certified route, with the same mathematical methods/optimizations.

They have the same initial mathematics, representations and allowed local
optimizations. O can share the same newly contributed theorem as V. Do not
attribute fold/map evaluation savings to certificates when O also obtains them,
or give L a genuinely missing remote discovery without charging for obtaining it.

Fix exact target J, horizon T and either explicit nonnegative resource prices or
a vector/Pareto criterion; peak memory is a separate feasibility constraint.
For route j write `Cost(j) = F_j + M_j(T) + sum_i c_(j,i)`. Include creation,
certification, publication, distribution, custody/repair, failed attempts and
transition/controller costs. Truly common work cancels; historical incurred
work is not erased and is not paid again in a forward decision.

V beats both serious alternatives only if:

```text
sum_i (c_(L,i) - c_(V,i)) > (F_V-F_L) + (M_V-M_L)
sum_i (c_(O,i) - c_(V,i)) > (F_V-F_O) + (M_V-M_O)
```

If N genuinely eligible otherwise-uncached events have justified lower net
margins `d_L,d_O > 0` and additional fixed/service upper bounds `f_L,f_O >= 0`,
then `N > max(f_L/d_L, f_O/d_O)` is a sufficient nonempty benefit region.
If either margin is nonpositive or unsupported, increasing N proves nothing.
Warm events with certificate overhead must be counted, not excluded to inflate
the margin. A peer count, theorem count, fabricated cold restart or mere use
counter is not evidence of independent cold demand.

An important conditional region remains mathematically credible: an optimized
ordinary cold path genuinely requiring at least L bytes of uncached proof over
throughput at most B has transfer latency at least L/B. A justified bound v on
the **entire comparable** certified acquisition-to-ready path gives a per-event
latency improvement when `L/B > v`. Required semantics, verification, search and
controller work belong in v. A shorter alternative proof, preaccepted cache or
better ordinary compression changes the premise. A large global corpus alone
does not make L large. CPU and full-cost benefit require separate margins.

The tiny retained SECOND may have **nonpositive** certified marginal benefit
against the strong O comparator. This gate measures the newly executable route
and exact semantic endpoint; it is not a matched growth experiment and cannot
establish positive demand or amortization. The gate may rationally be a bounded
capability/assurance investment. Repeated operational sealing still needs
independently justified positive margins, eligible demand and sustainable service
capacity. No automatic sealing policy or inevitable exponential growth follows.
