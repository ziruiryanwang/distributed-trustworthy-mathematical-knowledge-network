## Approved refinement in principle: metadata-only preview

Root identified an avoidable cost in the initial implementation: each preview
round rereads/parses/charges every selected proof packet, including hex-encoded
receipt data. With r rounds and total selected packet size P, this contributes
O(r P) work and submitted-input charges although preview grants no authority.

The narrower sufficient invariant is:

1. Stage once establishes a same-client immutable structural snapshot.
2. Preview uses only that unused token's snapshot, recharges selected metadata,
   validates the pure structural closure, and checks actual held supplied
   openings/bodies. It does not promise that the evidence packet remains intact.
3. Actual admission still rereads/rebinds the exact packet and original group
   before checking, performs the original real verification, and rereads/rebinds
   again before returning a warrant. Failure revokes the operation.

Replacing per-preview `_read_stage` with `_stage(token, unused=True)` therefore
preserves acceptance soundness. A packet disappearing after staging can permit
a nonauthoritative prediction but cannot pass admission. This removes repeated
proof-packet work from preview only; it does not remove metadata/semantic scans,
fixed foundation processing, actual verifier work or required semantics.

The changed boundary should have one direct control: with held packet reads
refusing after stage, preview remains authority-free and performs no packet
read, whereas actual admission fails before a receiver and revokes the token.
This source refinement and control were requested before any test launch.
