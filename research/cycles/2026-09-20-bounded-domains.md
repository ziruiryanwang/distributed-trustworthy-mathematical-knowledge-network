- Bound actual complete traversal e/i/p/bytes, failed attempts, historical records and pinned generations—not just selected current targets.
- Count every active old domain's custody, leases, support changes, discovery and useful service. Closing new admission does not stop maintenance.
- Test exact replay closure overlap and native reconstruction separately from selected index size. Count actual per-host allocations and serving incidence; common hashes and new identities create neither shared memory nor grants.
- Test provider-key row amplification and aggregate hot-key holder caps. Existing deeper F sharding is a real alternative with real leaf/marker/traversal costs, not an absent primitive or a free escape.
- Compare finite unfamiliar-provider discovery and checked reuse; a smaller FIND domain does not make a unique useful provider appear within a fixed query budget. Direct brokers do not supply unselected broker routing.
- Publication windows must fit the original visibility/service offer and current guard-valid deadline prefixes. Urgent support invalidation cannot be postponed to manufacture a stable window.
- Distinguish exact finite record counts, conditional asymptotic families, sufficient bounds, actual lower bounds and unmeasured native/service premises.
