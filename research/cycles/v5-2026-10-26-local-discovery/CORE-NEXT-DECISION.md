## One realistic workflow and its currently missing premise

An investigator develops a new finite-map invariant, representation or theory in
an approved ordinary Lean project. Another investigator wants the selected new
definitions and laws, but will not execute that foreign project's plugins or
initializers. Both can use rich ordinary Std locally; executable approval alone
is not a commons mathematical warrant. The recipient needs an independently
grounded compatible base before it can check the unfamiliar delta and continue.

Existing public operations already supply the architecture:

1. Ordinary Lean supports new definitions, structures, models, countermodels and
   laws. Their scientific direction remains the investigator's choice.
2. An authorized producer exports inert selected declarations and their actual
   missing mathematical dependencies. Ordinary source is retained separately for
   users choosing its richer frontend, not treated as inert data.
3. `intake-baseline` checks the chosen dependency closure in bounded batches above
   an explicit local parent. A manifest or projected binding is not a warrant.
4. Above the checked parent, approved-project `contribute` can use
   `acquired_targets=[]`: it need not reconstruct the large Std signature frontier.
   Unclaimed imported names bind to that actual parent; independent new-declaration
   checking still decides acceptance. It does not warrant all of Std or the
   original author's intended interpretation.
5. Existing publication, selected export, custody and multi-source receiving can
   make the resulting work available to the next investigator.

The distinction between this concrete workflow and prior demonstrations matters.
The adopted concept episode establishes substantive concept/theory continuation;
the received-method return establishes an actual checked new consequence. Neither
establishes that the recipient can cheaply ground a realistically sized shared
base on which later unfamiliar theories depend.

The saved Std writer is not a synthetic stress fixture. It contains the actual

approved-project route is the relevant alternative, not a raised reconstruction

the aggregate intake allowance must exceed 180 seconds plus controller work.
Those are authorization reservations, not a measurement of checking time. Actual
kernel compatibility, per-batch 64-MiB snapshot fit and affordable cold grounding
remain unknown. Counts that fit a manifest are not successful native execution.

The useful theoretical point is an obligation ordering, not another payoff
inequality: the existing continuation argument is conditional on a warranted
compatible base. A new consequence over a tiny already prepared base tests the
inductive step again; it does not resolve that premise for rich mathematics.
The retained real-library closure lets us test the premise directly without
repeating concept invention or inventing a new growth theory.

Ordinary bootstrap is independent of the world's total corpus, but not of its
chosen missing closure or local parent. Choose a small eligible parent and pay
that acquisition explicitly. Durable later use can amortize it; no number of
potential future uses proves actual uptake, scientific benefit or exponential
growth. Existing source analyses already bound local history costs and explain
batching/specialty contexts. Do not reopen sessions, projection or union merely
because those optimizations could someday be useful.
