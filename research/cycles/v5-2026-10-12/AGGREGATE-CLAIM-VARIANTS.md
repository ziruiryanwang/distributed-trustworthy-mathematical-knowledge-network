## Central defect in V1

The module commitment omits theorem proof terms, while the exact legacy journal
records the rank determined by actual direct-parent journals. Hence M does not
determine J. For example, a theorem with type `forall P : Prop, P -> P` can have
one proof directly from the foundation and another through a separately named
rank0 helper theorem. Its own declared name/type/metadata can stay identical,
so M is unchanged, while the valid output ranks are0 and1.

This is a source-level constructive example, not a newly executed Lean fixture.
The crucial fact is the actual relationship of committed fields to returned
rank, independently reviewed by root/assurance. V1 graph uniqueness by M rejects
an otherwise compatible union of children referring to J0 and J1. Replacing J1
by J0 is not permitted: the existing relation and receipt assumptions bind exact
journals, and changing parents can alter required child rank and downstream J.
The defect is honest branch interoperability/efficiency, not an observed false
mathematical acceptance.
