## Decision

**The implemented changes can satisfy a completed, substantially advanced v4
milestone, provided the still-pending exact return/check/law/refusal and release
handoff gates pass.** They do not satisfy a claim that arbitrary mathematical
research now grows rapidly on an arbitrary global deployment. Those are
different assertions, and the latter is neither a plausible universal software
guarantee nor the user's original wording that v4 be substantially closer to
justifiably releasable, globally workable software.

This recommendation does not relabel rejected sealer02 or lower the bar to
component tests. There is now a different public representation and reuse
mechanism, an actual previously hidden-result discovery capability, and a
finite demonstrated capacity advantage. Calling v4 complete **before** its
remaining mathematical return and usable exact-delivery gates pass would still
be unjustified. Root owns final adoption after their independent outcomes.

## Growth: sufficient philosophy, real benefit, explicit limit

The adopted resource-sensitive account is adequate for this design. Mathematics
is represented by formal structures and warranted relationships; a commons
adds reproducible means of accessing and composing them under finite resources.
Definitions are conservative and theorems were already consequences of a sound
foundation, but finding and using their warrants need not have been affordable.
That distinction explains why a library can add knowledge without manufacturing
new truth values or consulting human intention.

The philosophy does not supply missing economic assumptions. In particular:

- Existential option-capability inclusion is not a guarantee that an actual
  heuristic agent finds a better proof. Previously supplied advice and methods
  belong to the endowment, not free unexplained discoveries.
- The finite payload theorem fixes exact endpoints and a real previous-protocol
  baseline. It does not count ModuleIDs or renamed proofs as intrinsically
  useful mathematics. Familiar preauthored operations are adequate mechanism
  witnesses but not evidence of autonomous novelty.
- Every persistent copy of a canonical capsule, even inside an evidence/native
  directory, belongs to the mathematical-payload coordinate. The one-cache
  theorem is global across locations. The audit run retains all outputs; it
  does not pretend to have exercised an eviction policy.
- Other storage, temporary materialization, checking, proving, discovery,
  maintenance and failed work are separately paid. The observed byte saving
  alone cannot determine a weighted full-cost advantage or a research-growth
  rate. This remains explicit in `GROWTH-OPERATING-REGION.md`.
- The associative-law/repeated-squaring argument provides a reusable warranted
  algorithmic transformation with a valid operation-count advantage against
  the specified naive algorithm. An efficient conformant evaluator and actual
  cost correspondence are needed before claiming executable acceleration.
  Such an evaluator is not a newly imposed requirement of a mathematical commons.

The result is more than a vacuous inequality with an unknown sign: the payload
gap and actual reconstruction are established. It is less than a general theorem
that network participation benefits every user. An already well-endowed local
library is the explicit null case; sharing may add cost for that participant.

This meets a **justified support for useful accumulation** milestone when joined
to the actual trustworthy reciprocal route. It does not mean the broader
research problem of general net knowledge-growth acceleration has been solved.

## Huge-network argument: what scales and what still does not

No selected operation inherently enumerates all global mathematics or fetches
all theorem-proof history. The relevant resources are its selected semantic
closure, missing bytes, new proof work/frontier, returned candidates and routing
operations. Total network storage/service still grows with retained knowledge.
The existing local-index and selective-custody interfaces implement this
separation; a local test alone would not establish it.

The logarithmic routing discussion is correctly conditional on maintained
healthy identifier-scale coverage, bounded delivery and adequate service.
Fixed query caps prevent runaway work; they do not prove successful lookup.
Provider renewal has an explicit service/deadline margin, and caps cannot
substitute for actual spare capacity at an indispensable provider or hot key.
Those are ordinary distributed-service assumptions, not hidden central servers.

One important central limit remains: **pagination is not a semantic search
algorithm**. A common structural type can have many irrelevant candidates;
finding a particular useful one can still require linear candidate inspection.
The fixed ninth-result defect is repaired, but relevance, broad recall and hot-key
load distribution are not thereby solved. Exact IDs and discriminating formal
keys give efficient directed reuse; broad type queries have bounded, possibly
unproductive results. The final guide must not advertise uniformly efficient
search for every mathematical interest. A better semantic index is legitimate
future central work, not a reason to secretly add an unreviewed search system
before this release.

Likewise, a 64-module selected lowering limit is not a limit on global corpus
size, but is a real limit on a single construction. Transparent semantics cannot
always be hidden behind a certificate. Re-expression/checkpointing pays body and
checking costs; it does not permit infinite compositional depth for constant
local resources. The supported declaration/foundation envelope remains visible;
the new library layer is not an unrestricted full-Lean/mathlib replacement.

## Reconciling a final v4 with honest deployment conditions

A final v4 is a fixed supported software release, not a promise that all future
research and deployment questions are solved. Its release statement should
lead with the achieved usable loop and exact capacity improvement, then state
the concrete environment: adopted Windows CPU-only/runtime/checker policies,
reachable direct-IPv4 contacts for that deployment profile, funded finite
services, selected retained custody, finite supported syntax/operation limits
and honest incomplete search. The same-host physical qualification and absence
of external publication must remain visible without pretending they negate the
actual distributed-protocol architecture.

The ultimate commons objective can remain active while this v4 milestone is
completed. Do not mark the broader objective complete merely because the package
is sealed, and do not disguise another unfinished return gate as a final release.
If the pending gates pass, describe v4 as completed within its explicit supported
contract, not as proof of global exponential mathematical growth. This is an
evidence-based release distinction, not a version-label escape from the user's
two central priorities.

### Final requirement recommendation

**Adopt this as a completed, substantially advanced supported v4 release after
the final handoff integrity gate passes.** The recommendation is based on the
joined public reciprocal-library path, actual native/cryptographic warrant
boundary, repaired continuation, finite resource-capacity advantage, and
independently usable exact package. It is not a relabeling of sealer02 or a
replacement of product requirements by the growth-accounting fixture alone.

The two central priorities have concrete software and analytical advances:
proof-history-independent selected-frontier reuse and reachable bounded
discovery; and persistent compositional semantic sharing with a nonempty,
audited payload-capacity benefit region. The larger research objectives remain:
general efficient relevance search, sustained service under global load, broader
semantic coverage and a demonstrated or theoretically warranted net useful-growth
advantage for specified realistic workloads. Common-type candidate inspection
can remain linear, demanded transparent semantics still cost resources, and the
run retained all reconstructed capsules rather than exercising a one-cache
eviction policy. These limits must remain in the final guide.

No universal exponential research growth, net total-cost saving, unrestricted
Lean/mathlib compatibility, Internet-wide deployment or independent-physical-host
test is warranted. No human-intention acceptance, mandatory research agenda or
central server has been added. The unavailable second machine and undecided
license remain the user's known conditions, not invented end-of-release excuses.
The broad goal must not be marked complete on this recommendation.
