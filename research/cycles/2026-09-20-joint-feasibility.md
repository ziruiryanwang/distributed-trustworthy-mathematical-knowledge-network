## Decision and what it changes

The envelope makes previously vague joint-fit requirements falsifiable. The specified dispersed conversion needs 245,760 calls against 196,608; holder-local transient stock can defeat dense conversion; complete per-member support work survives locator optimization; exact repeated C changes can prevent renewal despite ample daily CPU; and mandatory full rebuilds over genuinely growing current rows require the stated quadratic total traversal count.

Keep SG and the catalog unselected. Treat full enumeration as a candidate bounded reference/fallback, not an established long-term efficient update path. First compare actual bounded provider-local domains and service-permitted commit windows, including independent supply and useful-service costs. If those cannot meet the offer, investigate an exact paid incremental/change-specific currentness mechanism. Delaying publication, omitting support or inventing scoped projections is not an unchanged-service improvement. This is a research choice, not selection of an algorithm or a new network version.

The goal stays active. No implementation or qualification is authorized by this cycle.
