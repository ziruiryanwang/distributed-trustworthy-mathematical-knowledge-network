## Why the E1/E2 proposal is not yet a good expensive experiment

`SCALE-ROUTING-CHALLENGE.md` proposes two equality-only algebraic consequences
of the retained A/B capabilities. Its E1/E2 statements are not yet independently
authored, exact pinned consumer artifacts. The common context is a very small
fixed-support fragment, with no induction or substantial library search needed
by the statement syntax. A concise local derivation is a serious competing
hypothesis. This note deliberately supplies no such derivation. The absence of
one here is not evidence that the local baseline is hard.

Consequences for experimental interpretation:

- Two correlated consequences cannot justify a workload distribution, broad
  mathematical novelty, sustained throughput gain or exponential accumulation.
- A dependency on A/B shows that a particular proof used them. It does not
  show that those lemmas were necessary, hard to reconstruct, or causally saved
  work. The control must be allowed any cheaper direct proof within the same
  supported foundation. Do not force a long synthetic ancestral chain.
- E2 depends on successful prior E1 evidence if either arm elects to reuse it.
  An execution-only E1 is not a certified parent. A two-endpoint experiment
  therefore cannot silently reuse an unproved checkpoint to avoid E1's real
  certification investment.
- The older J/K plan involved a richer right-fold context and known Beta
  packages. It is not automatically compatible with this fixed-support,
  same-image Prop-only collection lane. Do not revive its old budget or dispatch
  its old consumers as if a cryptographic profile change were transparent.
- A trial comparing certified treatment against certified unseeded control
  isolates seeded reuse *within this profile*. A native-only comparison asks a
  different economic question; it must not be represented as the same formal
  assurance boundary or deprived of native caches and simpler direct proofs.

There is also a concrete source-level qualification missing from the proposal's
phrase “own helpers”: `native/ImportTail.lean:305-311` requires every proof
constant to occur in clean fixed support or the selected parents. Separate new
helper declarations are not generally admissible exports. Proof-local terms
can be used; arbitrary helper constants, new definitions and unselected Init
theorems cannot. Both arms must receive that same rule. Source elaboration in
the broad Init frontend is not permission to bypass the fresh clean recheck.

This is a prospective specification, **not launch-ready authority**. It requires
two independently authored proof sources whose pins do not yet exist; no such
sources or authors are commissioned here. If those inputs and prerequisites
are unavailable by the cutoff, the selected result is “not run,” not a substitute

duplication as a proposed new observation. Do not give a proof sketch.

- Control has the same fixed foundation with zero selected A/B parents. It may

The already selected non-oracle selectivity investigation answers that different

## Full-cost and amortization model

For scheme `s` and a fixed exact endpoint set over horizon `H`, use one primary
cost unit, such as inclusive product CPU seconds. Retain wall latency, token
effort, transferred/read/retained bytes and peak memory as separate dimensions.
Do not invent an exchange rate among them. Define:

```text
C_s(H) = B_s + P_seed,s + P_new,s(H) + F_s(H) + M_s(H)
         + sum_i [Q_s,i + X_s,i + V_s,i + I_s,i + U_s,i]
```

Here `B` is attributable build/install/profile investment; `P_seed` is seed
production; `P_new` includes every new complete producer operation and all of
its stages; `F` is failed/refused/abandoned work not already included elsewhere;
`M` is publishing, replication, index/routing maintenance and repair; `Q` is
query work; `X` is acquisition/transfer; `V` is fresh certificate/native
authentication; `I` is exact interface/materialization/import; and `U` is later
warm reuse. An event appears once: a producer's embedded final verification is
inside that producer, while a separately run receiving verification is another
real event. Enclosing and nested CPU observations are not added twice.

Use three explicitly different comparisons:

1. **Incremental, already installed:** retained seed/build investment is stated
   sunk on both relevant arms, not secretly zero. Charge all new public loop,
   preparation, certification, failed work, return and maintenance. This can
   evaluate immediate local choices but not recover original investment.
2. **New deployment/library investment:** include attributable certificates and
   backend setup once. A native-only baseline does not need the certificate
   backend; that cost cannot cancel against a nonexistent native expense.
   Missing historical/model costs remain unknown. Do not rerun the old campaign
   solely to make its ledger prettier.
3. **Cold trust-domain versus warm local benefit:** fresh independent intakes
   may benefit from history-independent receipts. A warm native node may cache
   its own exact accepted result under a pinned policy. Do not charge ancestral
   replay on every reference or treat each local cache hit as a new cold domain.

Let `DeltaP` include all incremental production and allocated setup difference,
`DeltaM(R,H)` the maintenance/failure difference, and `d_i` the matched native
minus certified query/transfer/verify/import/use cost for consumer `i`. Benefit
requires:

```text
sum_(i=1..R) d_i > DeltaP + DeltaM(R,H).
```

Only with justified stationary `d_i=d>0` and an R-independent total right-hand
side `K` is the first strictly beneficial positive integer
`R_min = max(1, floor(K/d)+1)`. If `d <= 0` and `K >= 0`, no reuse count wins in
that model. If maintenance grows, use the actual sum instead of this quotient.
No numeric `R_min` is currently defensible for E1: neither its matched native
baseline nor full certified usable-intake/producer terms have been measured.

The partial AB cost cannot simply be substituted for `DeltaP`: the baseline's
seed production and allocation policy are missing. As a transparent sensitivity
calculation only, if later evidence put net investment `K` at 30,000 CPU seconds,
then per-cold-domain savings of 0.1/1/10 seconds would require respectively
300,001/30,001/3,001 independent cold intakes for strict benefit. These are
conditional arithmetic, not measured savings or a forecast that such reuse
will occur. On tiny direct native proofs, `d` may instead be nonpositive.

Useful growth counts independently completed reusable endpoints at the same
finite resource opportunity, plus witnessed downstream reuse where claimed.
Theorems already in a fixed theory do not enlarge its deductive closure;
the relevant gain is access to consequences at lower resources. Formal validity,
library-relative new interface, proof dependency, practical reuse advantage,
mathematical novelty and global growth are different predicates. Neither a
batch member count nor this optional preparation screen identifies all of them.
