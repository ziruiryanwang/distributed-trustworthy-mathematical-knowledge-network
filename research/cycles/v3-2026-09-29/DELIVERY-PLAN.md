## Large-network decomposition

1. **Acceptance locality.** A selected member carries its formal capsule, full
   finite collection journal and final recursively discharged receipt. Cold
   verification must not fetch the transitive proof DAG. Trust assumptions
   include the exact image relation, foundation, checker correspondence and
   recursive cryptography. Selected definitions or computation explicitly
   demanded by a local proof still cost bytes/work; succinctness is not magic
   evaluation or free production.
2. **Local indexing.** Exact postings avoid scans of unrelated entries. Query
   work is bounded explicitly. Common-key collision/duplication can still
   exhaust the budget before a useful lemma; measured unrelated-row locality
   is not semantic retrieval adequacy.
3. **Holder reachability.** Known contacts provide actual exchange, not unknown
   global holder discovery. Later decentralized routing may use bounded
   referrals/rendezvous; it needs endpoint admission, churn/hotspot/eclipsing
   assumptions and maintenance accounting. Do not build a broad router before
   testing whether meaningful queries can discriminate candidates locally.
4. **Useful accumulation.** Count canonical reusable capabilities and observed
   downstream solved outputs/cost, not metadata, proofs or popular names alone.
   Certifier production/replication/maintenance and failures enter the account.
   A model may justify conditional amortization; only observed matched results
   justify an empirical benefit in that workload.
