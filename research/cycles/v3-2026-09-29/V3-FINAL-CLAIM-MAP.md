## What the two pending outcomes would add

### Actual final public child, if independently audited and adopted

A genuine final COMPLETE would add evidence that the shipped public
prepare/advance path actually finishes this exact rank1 child: all 122 stages,
actual parent-assumption discharge, a final four-file bundle and the real final
cold check, under the original operation, allowance and deadline. Clean READY
continuations alone do not supply that claim. Failure after producing a receipt
must retain its actual invocation disposition rather than be relabeled PASS.

That result would close the **public production** integration gap for this
theorem/profile. It would not show that general proof production is affordable,
that this theorem is a new scientific discovery or that certification beats
direct/native/warm-cache alternatives. Report final observed costs separately
from the base certificate campaign and preserved failures.

### Actual enveloped B-to-A-to-C return, if independently audited and adopted

The reviewed selected adapter plans ten public peer-envelope commands and two
fresh actual received-copy cold checks. B stops before A's check/service; C
acquires the child from A's custody while B stays offline. If those exact
conditions and clean closure actually pass, the result adds one **same-host,
known-contact reciprocal cycle** and real peer-sidecar TLS integration. The
already qualified unknown-holder route is separate evidence, not something
this return would newly test.

It would not establish independent physical hosts, adversarial internet
operation, retention after destruction of all copies, practical huge-network
search or useful-growth advantage. Producer service stoppage is not producer
disk destruction. A receiver must still check its actual owned copy, not adopt
the producer's success record. Neither a future child pass nor a future return
pass retroactively changes preserved original FAIL outcomes.

Even **both** future passes together would establish a bounded reciprocal
mechanism, not either complete central goal. Useful-growth qualification still
needs independent downstream outcomes and a matched full-cost comparison with
legitimate direct/native/warm-cache baselines, including failed work, production,
retrieval, transfer, checking, replication and maintenance. Reuse count, rank,
validity or file count cannot substitute for that comparison. No experiment is
selected by stating this remaining evidentiary requirement.
