## Central barriers and evidence strength

**Search:** bounded cost is not useful recall. The actual33-entry shape test
found the target with differently shaped distractors but returned sequences1..16
and no target under same-shape crowding, spending16,619 reply bytes. This
disproves a universal-success claim for that fixed query policy, even in a
small corpus. It does not prove all possible policies or workloads fail.
Unsplit partition0, finite local views and constructed closer-hop contacts
provide no demonstrated practical placement/retention/intersection under huge
hot-key load or churn.62 VM instructions do not measure physical I/O, maintenance
or useful recall. Lack of a logarithmic theorem alone is not the objection;
the observed miss and unqualified maintenance/reachability are concrete gaps.

**Proof efficiency:** receiver locality is not cheap whole-workflow proving.
The completed AB campaign consumed69,638.921875 known CPU seconds, including
retained prior work. The separate routed-copy check reports13ms inner crypto
but2.140625 known CPU seconds for its whole new allocation. These different
experiments are not a matched speedup ratio. Actual public controller cost
and repeated growing-operation scans remain producer risks. Large producer
cost does not prove negative amortization for every future workload; the
downstream savings needed to offset it have not been measured here.

**Growth:** genuine dependency use establishes reuse, not usefulness or causal
acceleration. In a fixed foundation valid theorems do not enlarge deductive
closure. Practical benefit is independently completed downstream capability
at a justified cost, including retrieval, transfer, checking, production,
failures and retention. No matched full-cost independent-agent comparison
establishes positive benefit. Warm native caches/direct proofs are legitimate
controls. Neither proof count, valid receipt, rank increase nor one successful
return supplies that missing comparison. No exponential-growth claim follows.
